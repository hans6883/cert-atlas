Create Vault policies: Historical Scope

Write and structure declarative rules using HCL or JSON to grant or forbid access to specific paths and operations, ensuring that users and machines have the correct permissions for their roles.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of 57 questions presented in a linear format. Question types include Multiple Choice, Multiple Select, and Fill-in-the-Blank, which are designed to test your knowledge of Vault operations.

What is the passing score?

The passing score for the exam is 70. Candidates must meet or exceed this score to earn the certification, demonstrating their proficiency in managing secrets and securing infrastructure.

How long is the exam?

The exam has a duration of 60 minutes. Test-takers must complete all 57 questions within this timeframe, requiring efficient time management and a strong understanding of the subject matter.

What is the price of the exam?
