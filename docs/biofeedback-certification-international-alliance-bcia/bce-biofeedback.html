What This Exam Validates

The Board Certified in Biofeedback exam, administered by the Biofeedback Certification International Alliance (BCIA), evaluates knowledge across nine domains. It covers the science, history, and theory of biofeedback, including psychophysiological recording, stress models, and specific applications like surface EMG, autonomic nervous system, and respiratory training. The exam ensures practitioners understand professional conduct and evidence-based intervention strategies for clinical practice, confirming they possess the necessary skills to provide care in various health care settings. This assessment validates the expertise required to integrate biofeedback into professional therapeutic environments effectively, ensuring that all candidates meet the standards established by the certifying body for clinical practice.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is intended for professionals who hold a BA/BS degree in a BCIA-approved health care field. Candidates must document their clinical experience or work under the legal supervision of a licensed professional when treating medical or psychological disorders.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Orientation to biofeedback principles
  • Stress, coping, and illness models
  • Psychophysiological recording techniques
  • Research methodology and efficacy
  • Surface EMG applications
  • Autonomic nervous system training
  • Respiratory application protocols

How to Prepare

Review the Blueprint of Knowledge Statements and the Core Reading List to prepare for the 100 multiple-choice questions. Candidates should engage in formal didactic education and clinical mentoring to build expertise. Utilize the online practice exam to gain familiarity with the question format and testing environment. Ensure all application requirements and the required certification fees are submitted well in advance of your preferred exam date to ensure your registration is processed correctly by the certifying body.

Domain Study Guidance

ORIENTATION TO BIOFEEDBACK: Study Guidance

This domain covers the formal definition of biofeedback, its history, and the pioneers who shaped the field. It explores human learning principles and the core assumptions underlying self-regulation for practitioners seeking to master the basics of the field.

  • AAPB definition of biofeedback
  • Pioneers and seminal studies

STRESS, COPING, AND ILLNESS: Study Guidance

This section focuses on models of stress and illness, including biopsychosocial and allostatic load theories. It examines psychophysiological reactions to acute and chronic stress to help practitioners understand patient responses in clinical settings through various theoretical frameworks and models.

  • Biopsychosocial and transactional models
  • Fight-or-flight and polyvagal theory

PSYCHOPHYSIOLOGICAL RECORDING: Study Guidance

This domain covers the technical aspects of psychophysiological recording, including sensors, signal characteristics, and electronic concepts. It ensures practitioners can manage equipment and signal data effectively for accurate patient monitoring and assessment during their daily clinical work and practice.

  • Common biofeedback modalities
  • Electronic concepts and signal artifacts

RESEARCH METHODOLOGY: Study Guidance

This area addresses research methodology, including experimental designs, statistical concepts, and evidence-based practice criteria for evaluating treatment efficacy. It helps practitioners interpret scientific literature to support their clinical decision-making processes and outcomes in a professional health environment for patients.

  • Experimental vs. non-experimental designs
  • Descriptive and inferential statistics

SURFACE EMG (SEMG) APPLICATIONS: Study Guidance

This domain explores skeletal muscle anatomy and physiology, focusing on SEMG applications for pain disorders and tension recognition training. It provides the basis for using surface electromyography to improve patient muscle control and overall physical health outcomes in clinical settings.

  • Motor unit functions
  • SEMG assessment and training strategies

AUTONOMIC NERVOUS SYSTEM (ANS) APPLICATIONS: Study Guidance

This section examines the autonomic nervous system, including anatomy, assessment strategies, and training protocols for conditions like anxiety and hypertension. It focuses on how practitioners can influence autonomic balance through specific biofeedback techniques and targeted clinical interventions for patients.

  • Sympathetic and parasympathetic branches
  • HRV biofeedback mechanisms

RESPIRATORY APPLICATIONS: Study Guidance

This domain covers respiratory anatomy, physiology, and breathing patterns. It details assessment and training techniques to address dysfunctional breathing, helping practitioners guide patients toward healthier physiological states through controlled respiratory intervention protocols and evidence-based clinical breathing exercises for better health.

  • Respiratory muscles and gas exchange
  • Healthy vs. dysfunctional breathing patterns

INTERVENTION STRATEGIES: Study Guidance

This section details the practical application of biofeedback, including intake procedures, treatment planning, session structure, and client graduation strategies. It ensures practitioners can manage the entire lifecycle of a patient's therapeutic journey with professional care and oversight in practice.

  • Intake and symptom assessment
  • Establishing baselines and goals

PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on professional conduct, ethical principles, client rights, record keeping, and the requirements for supervision and consultation. It ensures that all certified practitioners maintain the highest standards of professional integrity and follow established ethical guidelines for clinical practice.

  • Certification vs. licensure
  • Informed consent and confidentiality

Exam-Day Guidance

The exam is delivered via an online proctoring service using your own computer. You must complete the 100 multiple-choice items within the allotted time. Ensure your environment is secure and that you have communicated any schedule changes to the proctoring service and BCIA.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The certification exam consists of exactly 100 multiple-choice items. Each item has only one correct answer, and there is no penalty for guessing, so you should answer every question to maximize your score and demonstrate your knowledge of the 9 domains.

How long is the exam?

There is a specific time limit for the exam. If you require additional time due to identified special needs or because English is not your first language, you must write to BCIA to request consideration for your specific testing situation and requirements.

What is the cost of the exam?

The certification exam fee is set by the board. Additionally, there is a separate fee paid directly to the proctoring service. A filing fee is also required at the time of application to process your request for the 9 domains.

Where can I find the current certification path?

You should check with Biofeedback Certification International Alliance (BCIA) for the current certification path and any updates to requirements or procedures to ensure you have the latest information for your application and to verify the 100 questions format.
