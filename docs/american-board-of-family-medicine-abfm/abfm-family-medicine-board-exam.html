What This Exam Validates

The ABFM Family Medicine Board Exam is officially certified by the American Board of Family Medicine (ABFM). This test contains 300 questions and spans 380 minutes of total testing time. It is designed to evaluate clinical competencies across five distinct practice domains, including acute care, chronic management, emergent situations, preventive care, and medical knowledge required for board certification.

Who Should Take This Exam

Family medicine practitioners and physicians seeking board certification or maintenance of certification through the American Board of Family Medicine (ABFM) complete this evaluation to demonstrate their clinical knowledge and patient care abilities.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Acute Care and Diagnosis
  • Chronic Care Management
  • Emergent and Urgent Care
  • Preventive Care
  • Foundations of Care

How to Prepare

Review clinical guidelines and diagnostic criteria across all five domains. Focus study time on high-weight areas such as acute care and chronic care management. Utilize official content outlines and relevant medical literature to guide your preparation for the 300 questions included in this board exam to ensure you are ready for the testing experience.

Domain Study Guidance

Acute Care and Diagnosis: Study Guidance

This domain covers acute care and diagnosis, which accounts for 35.0 percent of the total exam content. Candidates should prioritize understanding acute presentation evaluation and the appropriate selection of diagnostic tests for various clinical scenarios.

  • Acute presentation evaluation
  • Diagnostic test selection

Chronic Care Management: Study Guidance

This section focuses on chronic care management, representing 25.0 percent of the exam. Candidates must demonstrate proficiency in long-term disease management strategies and the implementation of effective patient follow-up protocols for managing various chronic conditions.

  • Long-term disease management
  • Patient follow-up protocols

Emergent and Urgent Care: Study Guidance

This domain addresses emergent and urgent care, comprising 20.0 percent of the exam. It requires candidates to be proficient in urgent triage procedures and the stabilization of patients during emergency medical situations encountered in practice.

  • Urgent triage procedures
  • Emergency stabilization

Preventive Care: Study Guidance

This area centers on preventive care, making up 15.0 percent of the total content. Candidates are expected to master health maintenance techniques and evidence-based disease prevention strategies for diverse patient populations seen in family medicine.

  • Health maintenance
  • Disease prevention strategies

Foundations of Care: Study Guidance

This domain covers foundations of care, holding a 5.0 percent weight on the exam. It focuses on core clinical principles and the application of basic medical knowledge within a family medicine context for all board candidates.

  • Core clinical principles
  • Basic medical knowledge

Exam-Day Guidance

Manage your time effectively across the 380-minute duration to complete all 300 questions. Read each question carefully and pace yourself to ensure all sections are finished within the allotted time frame provided by the testing center during your appointment.
