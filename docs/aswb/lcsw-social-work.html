What This Exam Validates

The ASWB Licensed Clinical Social Work Exam is a professional certification assessment administered by the ASWB. It covers four distinct domains, including human development, assessment and diagnosis, clinical interventions, and professional ethics. This exam measures the clinical knowledge and skills required for independent social work practice, ensuring that practitioners meet the standards set by the certifying body for clinical social work. Candidates must demonstrate proficiency across these core areas to achieve their professional goals and maintain high standards of practice within the social work field.

Who Should Take This Exam

This exam is intended for social workers seeking clinical licensure. Candidates should possess the professional experience and academic background required by their jurisdiction to sit for the ASWB clinical level examination and demonstrate readiness for independent clinical practice.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Human development and diversity
  • Assessment and diagnosis
  • Treatment planning
  • Clinical interventions
  • Case management
  • Professional values and ethics

How to Prepare

Review the four domains outlined by the ASWB to understand the distribution of the 170 questions. Focus on the 30% weight assigned to assessment and diagnosis, and the 27% weight for clinical interventions. Utilize official ASWB study resources to familiarize yourself with the exam structure and content areas before your scheduled test date. Consistent review of these specific domains will help you prepare effectively for the examination requirements.

Domain Study Guidance

I. HUMAN DEVELOPMENT, DIVERSITY, AND BEHAVIOR IN THE ENVIRONMENT: Study Guidance

This domain covers human development, diversity, and behavior in the environment. It accounts for 24% of the exam and focuses on how environmental factors and individual differences influence human behavior and development across the lifespan.

  • Human development stages
  • Diversity and social environment

II. ASSESSMENT, DIAGNOSIS, AND TREATMENT PLANNING: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on assessment, diagnosis, and treatment planning, representing 30% of the exam. It addresses the clinical processes involved in identifying mental health conditions and creating effective, evidence-based plans for patient care.

  • Diagnostic criteria
  • Treatment planning

III. PSYCHOTHERAPY, CLINICAL INTERVENTIONS, AND CASE MANAGEMENT: Study Guidance

This domain covers psychotherapy, clinical interventions, and case management, making up 27% of the exam. It involves the application of therapeutic techniques and the coordination of care to support patient outcomes.

  • Psychotherapy methods
  • Clinical interventions

IV. PROFESSIONAL VALUES AND ETHICS: Study Guidance

This domain addresses professional values and ethics, comprising 19% of the exam. It focuses on the ethical standards, legal requirements, and professional responsibilities that guide clinical social work practice.

  • Professional ethics
  • Legal standards

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive at the testing center prepared for a 240-minute session. Manage your time effectively to complete the 170 questions. Ensure you have all required identification and follow the instructions provided by the testing center staff for a smooth check-in process.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of 170 questions in total. These questions are distributed across four domains, with weights ranging from 19% to 30% of the total content, ensuring an evaluation of your clinical knowledge and professional readiness for independent social work practice.

What is the passing score?

The exam is pass/fail. The specific number of questions needed to pass varies by the specific exam form administered to the candidate, as the difficulty and content distribution can differ between versions to maintain the integrity of the certification process for all test takers.

How long is the exam?

The total duration for the exam is 240 minutes. Candidates should plan their time carefully to address all 170 questions within this period, ensuring they have sufficient time to review their answers and complete the assessment before the allotted time expires at the testing center.

Where can I find the current certification path?

For information regarding the long-term certification path or specific jurisdictional requirements, please check with ASWB for the current certification path. They provide the most accurate and up-to-date guidance for candidates navigating the requirements for clinical licensure and professional certification in their respective regions.
