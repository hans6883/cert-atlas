What This Exam Validates

The API 570: Piping Inspector Certification, administered by the American Petroleum Institute (API), evaluates technical proficiency in piping inspection. The exam assesses knowledge across five domains, including thickness measurement calculations, welding procedures, and nondestructive examination techniques. It is a 450-minute exam consisting of 170 questions, designed for professionals working in the petroleum and chemical process industries to ensure the integrity and safety of piping systems.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is for inspectors, engineers, and technical personnel working in the petroleum and chemical process industries. Candidates should have practical experience in piping inspection, maintenance, and the application of industry codes to ensure system integrity and regulatory compliance.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Evaluating piping thickness measurements
  • Calculating inspection intervals
  • Assessing piping integrity
  • Reviewing welding procedures
  • Applying nondestructive examination methods
  • Executing general and specific inspection tasks

How to Prepare

Review the official API 570 body of knowledge and publications effectivity sheet. Focus on mastering the calculation methods for piping integrity and inspection intervals. Utilize practice tests to familiarize yourself with the 170-question format. Ensure you understand the application of welding standards and nondestructive testing techniques. Allocate your study time to cover all five domains effectively before the 450-minute exam.

This domain focuses on the evaluation of welding procedures and the qualification requirements for welding personnel. It ensures that inspectors can verify that welding activities comply with established industry standards and safety codes.

This domain addresses the application and interpretation of various nondestructive examination methods used in piping inspection. Candidates are tested on their ability to select appropriate testing techniques and interpret the resulting data accurately.
