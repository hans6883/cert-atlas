What This Exam Validates

The AANP Family Nurse Practitioner Certification Exam is officially administered by the American Academy of Nurse Practitioners Certification Board to ensure high standards of excellence. This standardized test covers four distinct clinical domains including assessment, diagnosis, planning, and evaluation across various healthcare scenarios to validate professional nurse practitioner competence and clinical readiness.

Who Should Take This Exam

Family nurse practitioners seeking professional board certification must fulfill all established eligibility requirements set by the certifying body before applying to sit for the 150-question examination.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Assess patient health status
  • Diagnose clinical conditions
  • Plan patient interventions
  • Evaluate clinical outcomes

How to Prepare

Review clinical practice guidelines and study the four individual domain weightings carefully. Focus your daily study efforts on systematic patient assessment, advanced diagnostic reasoning, management planning, and precise outcome evaluation. Utilize official candidate handbooks and official practice resources provided by the American Academy of Nurse Practitioners Certification Board.

Arrive at the test center early with required identification. Manage your time effectively across the 150 questions to ensure completion within the 180-minute testing limit.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of 150 questions delivered across a 180-minute duration. Candidates must manage their time efficiently to complete all items within the allocated period.

What is the passing score?

Specific passing score thresholds are determined by the American Academy of Nurse Practitioners Certification Board. Check with the board directly for current score validation metrics.

How long is the exam?

The certification examination has a strictly enforced total duration of exactly 180 minutes. Test-takers should pace themselves carefully to successfully answer all 150 items well within this allotted timeframe.

Who administers the certification?

The American Academy of Nurse Practitioners Certification Board administers this family nurse practitioner certification. Check with American Academy of Nurse Practitioners Certification Board for the current certification path.
