Historical AAPA Scope

The Associate, Accredited Pension Administrator (AAPA) designation was a professional certification program formerly offered by LOMA. It covered topics including annuity products, contract administration, tax and regulatory considerations, and customer service operations. This exam is officially retired as of 2015-12-31. LOMA does not provide a successor designation for this program. Candidates seeking current professional credentials should consult LOMA directly for information regarding their active certification offerings and updated educational requirements for industry professionals who are looking to advance their careers in the financial services sector.

Who AAPA Was For

This retired designation was intended for professionals working in pension administration, annuity product management, and retirement plan operations. It provided a framework for understanding the regulatory and administrative requirements of various tax-sheltered retirement products within the financial services sector.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions were on the exam?

The retired AAPA exam consisted of 100 multiple choice questions. This total count was designed to test a candidate's knowledge across all four domains of the pension administration curriculum before the program was officially discontinued by the organization.

What was the passing score?

The passing score for the AAPA exam was 70. This threshold was established to ensure that all individuals who earned the designation demonstrated a level of proficiency in the core concepts of retirement planning and pension contract administration.

How long was the exam?

The exam was a 120-minute, linear assessment. Candidates were required to complete the entire set of questions within this specific duration to be considered for the designation, as the time limit was strictly enforced during the testing process.

Was there a fee for the exam?

The price was $130 per exam, with LOMA member discounts available for those who qualified. Please note that this fee structure is no longer applicable as the exam is retired and is no longer available for registration or purchase.
