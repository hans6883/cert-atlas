What This Exam Validates

The Association of Water Technologies (AWT) Certified Water Technologist (CWT) exam covers general water treatment, external treatment, boiler water treatment, cooling water and closed system treatment, and health, safety, and environment. The Association of Water Technologies (AWT) sponsors this certification to validate candidate knowledge in industrial water treatment science and technology.

Who Should Take This Exam

Industrial water treatment professionals looking to validate their knowledge. Candidates prepare through work experience in direct sales, service, technical support, or technical consulting related to boiler and cooling water systems.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Basic Water Treatment Chemistry and Terminology
  • Zeolite and Boiler Feedwater Deaeration
  • Boiler Water System Design and Corrosion
  • Cooling Water Microbiological and Fouling
  • Closed Loop Systems
  • Federal Regulations and Environmental Affairs

How to Prepare

Review the detailed exam content outline and selected sample questions to understand the format. Utilize the suggested preparation materials available through the Association of Water Technologies bookstore. Practice pacing yourself steadily for the complete test and answer all multiple-choice questions since there is no penalty for guessing.

Domain Study Guidance

General Water Treatment Knowledge: Study Guidance

This general domain addresses basic water treatment chemistry, metallurgy, feed control equipment, heat transfer principles, system monitoring procedures, and federal environmental affairs that impact daily industrial water management operations across facilities.

  • Basic Water Treatment Chemistry and Terminology
  • Metallurgy
  • Feed Control Equipment and Instrumentation
  • Boiler and Cooling Water System Monitoring

External Treatment: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on zeolite softeners, boiler feedwater deaeration processes, external treatment terminology, and fundamental water chemistry principles required to properly condition and prepare industrial water supplies before they enter sensitive systems.

  • Zeolite
  • Boiler Feedwater Deaeration
  • External Treatment Terminology
  • Basic Water Chemistry

Boiler Water Treatment: Study Guidance

This section addresses intricate boiler system design, inspection procedures, accurate sampling and analytical testing methodologies, potential corrosion and deposit problems, and the operational limitations of specific internal treatment programs.

  • System Design
  • System Inspection Procedures
  • Sampling and Analytical Testing Procedures
  • Corrosion and Deposit Problems and Treatments

Cooling Water and Closed System Treatment: Study Guidance

Candidates explore cooling water system knowledge, precise sampling techniques, analytical testing protocols, corrosion control strategies, deposit and scale management, microbiological fouling prevention, and closed loop system maintenance procedures.

  • System Knowledge
  • Cooling Water Corrosion
  • Cooling Water Deposits and Scale
  • Cooling Water Microbiological and Fouling

Health, Safety, and Environment: Study Guidance

This final domain covers maintaining personnel and physical plant safety standards, accurate identification of federal environmental regulations, and the correct procedures for the safe disposal of treated waters in industrial settings.

  • Maintaining Personnel and Physical Plant Safety
  • Identification of Federal Regulations
  • Proper Disposal of Treated Waters

Exam-Day Guidance

Bring the required forms of signature identification with a photo matching your registration name to your designated test center. Arrive early and follow all instructions carefully.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of exactly 200 multiple-choice questions, each featuring multiple possible answers with only one correct choice available for the candidate to select during the testing period.

What is the passing score requirement?

Candidates taking this certification test need to meet the established grading criteria across all sections of the examination to achieve a successful passing result on the exam.

How long is the exam?

Candidates registered for this industrial water treatment evaluation are given a total of 240 minutes to complete the computer-based examination at an authorized testing center location.

What are the exam registration policies?

The candidate registration process requires strict adherence to all Association of Water Technologies guidelines and administrative policies established for individuals seeking to obtain professional certification.
