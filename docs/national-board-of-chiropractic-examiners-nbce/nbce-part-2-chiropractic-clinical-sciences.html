What This Exam Validates

The NBCE Part II Clinical Sciences Chiropractic Board Examination is a standard assessment administered to candidates seeking licensure in the chiropractic profession. This important test features exactly 255 questions spanning multiple standard clinical domains, requiring examinees to demonstrate broad proficiency across general diagnosis, neuromusculoskeletal assessment, diagnostic imaging, chiropractic principles, chiropractic practice, and associated clinical sciences in order to meet official certification requirements successfully.

Who Should Take This Exam

This examination is intended for chiropractic students and candidates seeking licensure. Examinees must demonstrate clinical competency across multiple diagnostic and treatment domains to satisfy board requirements set by the NBCE.

How to Prepare

To successfully prepare for the assessment containing 255 questions with a passing score of 375, candidates must review core topics across all six domains, focusing heavily on clinical sciences and diagnostic procedures. Practice interpreting diagnostic imaging findings and studying standard chiropractic principles to ensure complete mastery of the test plan.

Domain Study Guidance

General Diagnosis: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on general diagnostic procedures and patient assessment strategies relevant to professional clinical practice settings, ensuring candidates understand fundamental evaluation methods and general diagnostic techniques required for safe patient care.

  • Patient evaluation
  • General diagnosis procedures

Neuromusculoskeletal Diagnosis: Study Guidance

This section covers complex neuromusculoskeletal conditions, specific examination techniques, and related evaluation protocols designed to test a candidate's ability to accurately diagnose disorders affecting the nervous, muscular, and skeletal systems during clinical encounters.

  • Neuromusculoskeletal assessment
  • Diagnosis of musculoskeletal conditions

Diagnostic Imaging: Study Guidance

Candidates will examine diagnostic imaging techniques, radiograph evaluation methodologies, and the identification of clinical abnormalities to demonstrate proficiency in interpreting visual diagnostic records for effective patient management and treatment planning.

  • Radiograph interpretation
  • Diagnostic imaging evaluation

Principles of Chiropractic: Study Guidance

This domain addresses the core principles and underlying theories of chiropractic care and practice, evaluating how candidates integrate traditional philosophies and theoretical frameworks into modern clinical decision-making environments.

  • Chiropractic principles
  • Clinical philosophy

Chiropractic Practice: Study Guidance

Reviews practical applications, standard patient management strategies, and procedural protocols utilized within professional chiropractic practices to ensure safe, effective, and ethical delivery of chiropractic healthcare services to the public.

  • Practice management
  • Patient care protocols

Associated Clinical Sciences: Study Guidance

Covers associated clinical sciences that supplement core chiropractic education and patient care, ensuring that candidates possess a well-rounded understanding of related medical and scientific disciplines necessary for standard clinical practice.

  • Associated clinical sciences
  • Supplementary medical knowledge

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive at the testing center prepared to complete 255 questions. Manage your time efficiently across all sections to ensure every domain is answered before submission.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of a total of exactly 255 questions covering six distinct clinical domains. Candidates must carefully answer these questions within the provided testing framework.

What is the passing score?

The passing score for this board examination is established at 375. Candidates must achieve this specific score or higher to successfully pass the clinical board assessment.

Who certifies the exam?

This examination is officially certified by the National Board of Chiropractic Examiners. Check with NBCE for the current certification path and administrative details regarding your testing.

What topics are covered in the domains?

The exam covers general diagnosis, neuromusculoskeletal diagnosis, diagnostic imaging, principles of chiropractic, chiropractic practice, and associated clinical sciences, with each area weighted across the test plan.
