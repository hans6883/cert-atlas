What This Exam Validates

The Praxis Core: Academic Skills for Educators - Mathematics exam, administered by ETS, evaluates college and career readiness in quantitative reasoning for prospective educators. Over a timed 90-minute testing session, test takers complete exactly 56 questions spanning number and quantity, data interpretation and statistics, and algebra and geometry to prove their foundational teaching competencies.

Who Should Take This Exam

This exam is intended for candidates preparing for a career in education who need to demonstrate quantitative skills required for educator preparation programs and teacher certification.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Integers, decimals, and fractions
  • Ratios, proportions, and percents
  • Measures of central tendency
  • Probability and statistics
  • Algebraic expressions and equations
  • Geometric shapes and formulas

How to Prepare

Review arithmetic, algebraic procedures, and geometric formulas carefully before test day. Practice solving multi-step word problems, working with various data displays, and calculating statistical measures. Familiarize yourself well with the provided on-screen four-function calculator to manage your time effectively during the complete 90-minute testing session.

Domain Study Guidance

Number and Quantity: Study Guidance

This domain covers integers, decimals, fractions, ratios, proportions, percents, and constant rates. It assumes a solid knowledge of basic U.S. customary and metric systems of measurement for solving daily quantitative problems.

  • Solve problems involving integers, decimals, and fractions
  • Solve problems involving ratios and proportions
  • Solve problems involving percent
  • Solve problems involving constant rates

Data Interpretation and Representation, Statistics, and Probability: Study Guidance

This domain assesses the ability to read and interpret visual displays of quantitative information accurately, compute various measures of central tendency, and assign simple mathematical probabilities to events.

  • Work with data and data representations to solve problems
  • Solve problems involving measures of central tendency and spread
  • Use data from a random sample to draw inferences
  • Compute simple probabilities and use them to solve problems

Algebra and Geometry: Study Guidance

This specific section focuses heavily on manipulating algebraic expressions, following standard mathematical procedures, solving complex word problems and equations, and successfully applying basic geometric properties and relevant formulas to complete problems.

  • Use properties of operations to identify or generate equivalent algebraic expressions
  • Solve word problems involving linear relationships
  • Utilize basic properties of common two-dimensional shapes
  • Use formulas for area, circumference, perimeter, and volume

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive fully prepared for a computer-delivered test featuring exactly 56 questions. An on-screen four-function calculator is provided during the complete 90-minute testing session for all registered candidates.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam contains exactly 56 questions in total, which include both scored operational items and unscored pretest questions distributed across three distinct content domains for educators.

What is the passing score?

The official passing score for the exam varies significantly by state and certifying agency. You should always check directly with ETS or your local certification office for specific requirements.

How long is the exam?

The exam is exactly 90 minutes long in total duration and is delivered entirely via computer at designated testing centers for all registered candidates seeking certification.

What calculator is allowed?

An on-screen four-function calculator is officially provided directly within the testing software interface during the test for all test takers to use for calculations during the session.
