What This Exam Validates

The Praxis Audiology Examination, certified by ETS, measures the knowledge, clinical judgment, and analytical capabilities required for independent practice as a professional audiologist. The exam contains exactly 120 selected-response questions and is delivered on a computer over a structured duration of 120 minutes, evaluating core competencies across five distinct content domains.

Who Should Take This Exam

Examinees who are currently enrolled in or have successfully completed a doctoral degree program preparing them for professional practice in audiology should take this exam. It serves as an important credentialing requirement for ASHA certification and state licensure.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Foundations of Audiology
  • Prevention and Screening
  • Assessment
  • Intervention
  • Professional and Ethical Responsibilities

How to Prepare

Review the official study guide in its entirety and build a personalized study schedule based on your available preparation time before test day. Practice with representative sample questions, carefully examine specific topic objectives, and utilize all available resource materials to solidify your understanding of core clinical concepts.

Domain Study Guidance

Foundations of Audiology: Study Guidance

This core domain focuses extensively on the underlying physics of sound, the detailed anatomy and physiology of the auditory and vestibular systems, associated pathophysiology, and complex psychometric principles required for accurate clinical evaluation.

  • Acoustics and Psychoacoustics
  • Auditory and Vestibular Anatomy
  • Pathophysiology and Pharmacology

Prevention and Screening: Study Guidance

This domain covers standard clinical education strategies, detailed exposure assessment techniques, risk mitigation protocols, standardized screening procedures, and continuous program performance monitoring designed specifically for hearing and balance disorders.

  • Education and Prevention
  • Screening and Risk Identification
  • Program Performance

Assessment: Study Guidance

This assessment domain addresses thorough clinical preparation, advanced behavioral and physiologic audiological evaluations, focused vestibular assessments, and the systematic clinical integration of diagnostic results for diverse patient populations.

  • Audiological Evaluation Behavioral
  • Audiological Evaluation Physiologic
  • Balance and Vestibular Assessment

Intervention: Study Guidance

This intervention domain covers systematic treatment planning, advanced device selection, detailed device verification procedures, wide-ranging audiological rehabilitation, tinnitus management strategies, and specific vestibular rehabilitation methodologies to support patients.

  • Treatment Planning and Device Selection
  • Device Verification and Validation
  • Audiological Rehabilitation and Intervention

Professional and Ethical Responsibilities: Study Guidance

This professional responsibility domain evaluates complex professional practice models, regulatory compliance frameworks, and ethical responsibilities across a wide variety of clinical, healthcare, and educational service delivery settings for audiologists.

  • Professional Practice and Service Delivery Models
  • Ethical and Legal Responsibilities

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive at the Prometric testing center or designated testing location well in advance with proper, government-issued identification. You will receive time to familiarize yourself with the computer interface before your 120-minute testing session officially begins.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of exactly 120 selected-response questions distributed across five content domains, covering clinical topics such as foundations, prevention, assessment, intervention, and professional responsibilities.

What is the passing score?

The exact passing score varies by state and certifying agency. You should always check directly with ETS or your specific regional licensing board to confirm your local score requirements before registering.

How long is the exam?

The examination has a total duration of 120 minutes and is administered securely on a computer at authorized test centers located worldwide for all registered candidates.

Where can I take the Praxis Audiology Examination?

You can take the test at any convenient test center within an international network that includes Prometric Testing Centers, select universities, and other approved professional testing locations worldwide.
