What This Exam Validates

The SAP Certified Professional - SAP Enterprise Architect certification validates your professional-level expertise in enterprise architecture frameworks, business architecture, roadmap development, and technical solutions. Certified by SAP, this scenario-based assessment evaluates your ability to correctly interpret complex IT requirements and apply reference architectures effectively across modern enterprise systems during the 120-minute assessment.

Who Should Take This Exam

Enterprise architects, solution architects, and senior consultants with experience in SAP technologies should take this 120-minute professional-level assessment to demonstrate expertise in designing architectures and roadmaps.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Enterprise Architecture Framework and Toolset
  • Architecture Vision and Roadmap
  • Business Architecture
  • Data, Application, and Technology Architecture

How to Prepare

To adequately prepare for this professional-level assessment, review the official exam guide and study all related architecture framework documentation. Focus specifically on understanding how to map business capabilities, interpret IT requirements, and structure enterprise architecture models using the available reference content and frameworks provided by SAP.

Domain Study Guidance

SAP Enterprise Architecture Framework and Toolset: Study Guidance

This specific domain focuses heavily on analyzing business strategies, interpreting IT requirements accurately, and applying reference architecture content from SAP in order to align technical solutions successfully and efficiently across modern architectures.

  • Analyze, assess, and understand a company’s business strategies
  • Apply Reference Architecture Content from SAP
  • Interpret IT requirements

Architecture Vision and Roadmap: Study Guidance

This domain covers developing architecture visions and broad roadmaps, capturing existing and future business models, and defining target business and solution architecture roadmaps for complex enterprise environments and stakeholders.

  • Develop the architecture vision and roadmap for a company
  • Capture existing and future business models and capabilities
  • Define a target business/solution architecture roadmap

Business Architecture: Study Guidance

This domain centers entirely on recommending business capability mappings and modeling end-to-end processes to support enterprise architecture designs effectively across various business units, functional areas, and technical domains.

  • Recommend a business capability mapping
  • Model end-to-end processes

Data, Application, and Technology Architecture: Study Guidance

This domain explores data, application, and technology architecture, describing artifact usage, identifying key stakeholders, and applying best practices of reference architecture content provided by SAP to maintain high quality.

  • Describe/explain artifact content, usage, and stakeholders
  • Use artifacts
  • Apply best practices of Reference Architecture Content from SAP

Exam-Day Guidance

Manage your time carefully during the 120-minute duration. Read each professional-level scenario carefully before selecting your answers to ensure you meet the 60% passing score.

Frequently asked questions

How many domains are covered on the exam?

The certification exam covers a total of four distinct domains, which include the SAP Enterprise Architecture Framework and Toolset, Architecture Vision and Roadmap, Business Architecture, and Data, Application, and Technology Architecture.

What is the passing score?

The passing score for the SAP Certified Professional - SAP Enterprise Architect assessment is set at precisely 60 percent to successfully demonstrate professional competency and architectural knowledge.

How long is the exam?

The exam has a total duration of 120 minutes, giving all candidates sufficient time to complete every professional-level scenario question presented during the evaluation process successfully.

Is this exam currently active?

Yes, this exam is current and active for professionals seeking certification. If you need any further information regarding long-term certification pathways and official updates, check with SAP directly.
