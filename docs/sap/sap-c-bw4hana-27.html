Historical C_BW4HANA_27 Scope

The SAP Certified Application Associate — Reporting, Modeling and Data Acquisition with SAP BW/4HANA 2.x exam, certified by SAP, covers data modeling and InfoProviders, data acquisition and transformation, reporting and analysis, planning functions, administration and monitoring, and SAP HANA integration. Candidates must master BW Modeling Tools in Eclipse, including InfoObjects, DataStore Objects, CompositeProviders, and Open ODS Views across the architecture.

Who C_BW4HANA_27 Was For

Application associates and technical consultants working with SAP BW/4HANA modeling tools in Eclipse, data acquisition pipelines, and reporting who need to validate their knowledge of enterprise data warehousing concepts.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Data Modeling and InfoProviders
  • Data Acquisition and Transformation
  • Reporting and Analysis
  • Planning Functions
  • Administration and Monitoring
  • SAP HANA Integration

How to Reuse Your Preparation

Review official SAP study documentation and practice modeling tasks in Eclipse. Focus heavily on configuring DataStore Objects, defining CompositeProviders, managing data flows, understanding Open ODS Views, and mastering transformation routines using ABAP or SAP HANA SQLScript. Work through practical exercises covering data tiering and query properties.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of exactly 80 questions in total, presented in various formats such as multiple choice, multiple select, drag and drop, and matching types.

What is the passing score?

To successfully pass this certification exam, candidates must achieve a minimum passing score of 66 out of the total available points provided on the test.

How long is the exam?

You are given a total duration of 180 minutes to complete all questions included on the certification exam session without any exceptions or extensions allowed.

What is the exam price?

The official exam registration fee is priced at 562.0 USD. Please check directly with SAP for any updates or regional pricing variations before booking.
