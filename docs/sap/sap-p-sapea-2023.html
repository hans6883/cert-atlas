Historical P_SAPEA_2023 Scope

The SAP Certified Professional — SAP Enterprise Architect certification evaluates enterprise architecture concepts and SAP frameworks. Certified by SAP, this historical exam is retired and has been directly replaced by P_SAPEA. Candidates must direct their certification efforts toward the active successor path to validate their skills and professional expertise effectively. This transition ensures that all certified professionals maintain alignment with the most current industry standards and methodologies required for modern enterprise architecture roles within the global SAP ecosystem today.

Who P_SAPEA_2023 Was For

Experienced enterprise architects, solution architects, and consultants with deep technical backgrounds who want to validate their expertise in enterprise architecture frameworks, business architecture, solution design, and integration methodologies within the SAP ecosystem.

How to Reuse Your Preparation

Prepare exclusively for the successor P_SAPEA by studying the required reference materials. Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of exactly 80 questions presented in multiple choice, multiple select, drag and drop, and matching formats to evaluate candidate knowledge of architecture.

What is the passing score?

The passing score for the exam is set at 60 percent, requiring candidates to answer the necessary proportion of questions correctly to achieve their certification status.

How long is the exam?

The exam has a duration of 180 minutes, giving candidates three full hours to carefully read, analyze, and complete all questions presented during the testing session.

What is the price of the exam?

The cost of the exam is 562.0 USD when registered through official Pearson VUE channels or online proctoring options provided by the certification vendor for candidates.
