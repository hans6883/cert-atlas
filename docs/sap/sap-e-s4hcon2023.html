Historical E_S4HCON2023 Scope

The SAP Certified Specialist — SAP S/4HANA Conversion and SAP System Upgrade examination, certified by SAP, tests technical proficiency in converting existing enterprise business suite systems to SAP S/4HANA. Candidates are evaluated across five domains, including conversion approaches, project planning, technical execution using the Software Update Manager, post-conversion activities, and troubleshooting to achieve professional certification.

Who E_S4HCON2023 Was For

Technical consultants and system administrators who are responsible for planning, executing, and troubleshooting system conversions and upgrades to SAP S/4HANA should take this professional certification exam.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The official examination consists of a total of 40 questions presented across multiple formats including Multiple Choice, Multiple Select, Drag and Drop, and Matching to evaluate candidates.

What is the passing score?

The passing score required to successfully pass the examination is set at 61 out of the total available points provided on the test to ensure proper competency.

How long is the exam?

Candidates are given a total duration of 90 minutes to complete all questions within the official examination session without any interruption from start to finish.

How much does the exam cost?

The standard exam registration price is set at 562.0 USD for candidates attempting to register for the official SAP certification test through authorized channels.
