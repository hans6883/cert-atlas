What This Exam Validates

The SAP Certified Associate — Implementation Consultant — SAP Customer Experience certification examination validates professional knowledge and technical skills across six functional domains, including SAP Sales Cloud and SAP Service Cloud. Certified directly by SAP, this proctored assessment tests practical abilities through exactly 80 questions delivered during a time limit of 180 minutes to demonstrate professional implementation expertise.

Who Should Take This Exam

Implementation consultants, technical specialists, and professionals with hands-on experience deploying SAP Customer Experience solutions who want to formally validate their configuration, administration, and integration skills.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • SAP Sales Cloud configuration
  • SAP Service Cloud deployment
  • SAP Marketing Cloud setup
  • SAP Commerce Cloud management
  • CX platform integration
  • Analytics and reporting

How to Prepare

Review the official SAP study documentation and domain weightings very carefully before your testing appointment. Focus intensely on configuration settings, system administration requirements, and integration methods across sales, service, marketing, and commerce solutions. Practice navigating administrative settings, user management, and document flows to ensure you are well prepared for all linear format question types.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The certification examination consists of exactly 80 questions presented in linear formats including multiple choice, multiple select, drag and drop, and matching to test your knowledge completely.

What is the passing score?

You need a minimum passing score of 70 to successfully obtain the SAP Certified Associate Implementation Consultant SAP Customer Experience credential upon completion of the proctored assessment.

How long is the exam?

The total duration of the exam is 180 minutes, giving you ample time to carefully read and complete all 80 questions within the allotted schedule.

What is the exam price?

The official exam registration fee is priced at $562.0 USD. Check directly with SAP for current registration procedures, policy updates, and available testing center options.
