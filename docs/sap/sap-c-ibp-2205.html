What This Exam Validates

The SAP Certified Application Associate — SAP IBP for Supply Chain certification validates expertise across 6 core domains. Candidates are evaluated on their ability to manage supply chain planning concepts, demand planning, inventory optimization, sales and operations planning, supply planning, and SAP IBP configuration and analytics. The exam comprises 80 questions and requires a passing score of 70 percent during the 180 minutes session.

Who Should Take This Exam

Supply chain professionals and application consultants with practical experience configuring and managing SAP Integrated Business Planning solutions should pursue this certification to demonstrate their technical knowledge.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Supply Chain Planning Concepts
  • Demand Planning
  • Inventory Optimization
  • Sales and Operations Planning (S&OP)
  • Supply Planning
  • SAP IBP Configuration and Analytics

How to Prepare

Master the core functionalities of SAP Integrated Business Planning by reviewing official SAP study documentation and help portal guides. Focus your study sessions on planning areas, key figure calculations, data integration, and configuration settings to handle the 80 multiple choice, multiple select, drag and drop, and matching questions.

Domain Study Guidance

Supply Chain Planning Concepts: Study Guidance

This domain covers fundamental supply chain planning concepts and overarching system principles required within the SAP IBP platform to ensure that candidates understand core architectural models and primary planning frameworks effectively.

  • Review core supply chain planning models
  • Understand overarching system principles

Demand Planning: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on demand planning processes, forecasting algorithms, and statistical tools used to predict future product requirements accurately while evaluating historical consumption patterns and leveraging advanced calculation engines.

  • Examine forecasting algorithms
  • Analyze statistical forecasting tools

Inventory Optimization: Study Guidance

This domain evaluates inventory optimization techniques, safety stock calculations, and methods for managing stock levels across multi-stage networks to reduce holding costs while maintaining optimal service levels for customers.

  • Study safety stock calculations
  • Review multi-stage network optimization

Sales and Operations Planning (S&OP): Study Guidance

This domain addresses sales and operations planning processes, scenario management, and collaborative planning workflows within the application to align executive decision-making with operational execution across global supply chains.

  • Explore scenario management
  • Understand collaborative planning workflows

Supply Planning: Study Guidance

This domain covers supply planning mechanics, heuristic calculations, and supply optimization features designed to balance material and capacity constraints efficiently across complex manufacturing and distribution networks during daily operations.

  • Analyze supply planning heuristics
  • Review optimization features

SAP IBP Configuration and Analytics: Study Guidance

This domain tests SAP IBP configuration, analytics features, dashboard setup, and administrative tools utilized for system maintenance, reporting, and customization to ensure proper daily performance across the enterprise software environment.

  • Practice analytics and dashboard setup

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of a total of 80 questions delivered in a linear format using multiple choice, multiple select, drag and drop, and matching question types.

What is the passing score?

Candidates must achieve a passing score of exactly 70 percent in order to successfully pass the examination and earn the professional certification credential issued by SAP.

How long is the exam?

You are given a total duration of 180 minutes to complete the test during your scheduled online proctored or in-person Pearson VUE testing center examination session.

What is the exam price?

The price for the exam is set at 562.0 USD. Candidates can register through official SAP learning channels or directly through Pearson VUE for their test.
