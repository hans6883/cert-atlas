Prepare for JN0-106: Junos, Associate (JNCIA-Junos)

Historical JN0-105 Scope

The JNCIA-Junos exam, certified by Juniper Networks, covered Junos OS fundamentals, user interfaces, configuration, and routing. This exam is retired as of 2026-04-05. The official successor is JN0-106 (Junos, Associate (JNCIA-Junos)). All candidates seeking certification must now focus on the requirements for the JN0-106 exam, as the retired JN0-105 is no longer available for testing or certification purposes. This transition ensures that all certified professionals maintain current knowledge of Juniper networking technologies and standards.

Who JN0-105 Was For

This certification path is for networking professionals who manage Juniper Networks devices. It targets individuals with experience in Junos OS operations, configuration, and monitoring who want to validate their technical skills through the current JN0-106 exam and demonstrate proficiency in modern network infrastructure management.

  • Junos OS system fundamentals
  • CLI user interface navigation
  • Device configuration management
  • Operational monitoring tasks
  • Routing protocol implementation

How to Reuse Your Preparation

Focus your study on the objectives for the JN0-106 exam. Knowledge of Junos OS fundamentals, CLI operations, and routing basics from the retired JN0-105 remains relevant. Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The current certification exam consists of 65 multiple choice questions that test your knowledge of Junos OS fundamentals, routing, and device management, requiring you to demonstrate your technical proficiency within the allotted time frame.

What is the passing score?

The passing score for the exam is 70 percentage, which candidates must achieve to successfully earn their certification and demonstrate their competency in managing Juniper Networks devices and Junos OS operational environments.

How long is the exam?

The exam duration is 90 minutes, providing candidates with sufficient time to answer all 65 questions and review their responses before submitting the final exam for grading by the official testing authority.

Is the certification valid for life?

No, the certification is valid for 3 years, after which renewal is required to maintain your status as a certified professional and ensure your skills remain aligned with the latest Juniper Networks technologies.
