What This Exam Validates

The CRCST (Certified Registered Central Service Technician) examination evaluates knowledge across seven distinct content domains, including departmental considerations, cleaning, decontamination, sterilization processes, and inventory management. The Healthcare Sterile Processing Association (HSPA) administers this 180-minute evaluation to ensure candidates possess the competencies required for professional central service technician roles across healthcare facilities.

Who Should Take This Exam

Candidates seeking professional credentialing as central service technicians. This credential validates knowledge in sterile processing, decontamination, and sterile storage within modern healthcare facilities for aspiring professionals.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The CRCST examination contains a total of 150 questions distributed across seven distinct content domains covering sterile processing practices, equipment management, and departmental procedures for candidates.

How long is the exam?

Candidates are given a total duration of exactly 180 minutes to complete all questions on the professional examination administered by the Healthcare Sterile Processing Association.

Who administers the CRCST exam?

The official certification examination is administered, maintained, and fully managed directly by the Healthcare Sterile Processing Association for individuals pursuing official central service technician professional certification credentials.

What are the primary domains covered?

The exam covers seven domains with varying weights: departmental considerations, cleaning and decontamination, preparation and packaging, sterilization processes, sterile storage and transport, patient care equipment, and professional development.
