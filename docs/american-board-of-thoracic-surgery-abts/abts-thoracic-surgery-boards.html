What This Exam Validates

The American Board of Thoracic Surgery (ABTS) administers this board certification to assess clinical proficiency in thoracic surgery. The exam covers 11 distinct domains, ranging from general thoracic and lung procedures to complex cardiac and congenital heart disease management. It serves as a formal evaluation of a surgeon's expertise in the field, ensuring practitioners meet the standards required for professional certification in thoracic surgery. Candidates must demonstrate knowledge across the full spectrum of surgical interventions to successfully achieve this professional designation.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is intended for surgeons seeking formal recognition of their expertise in thoracic surgery. Candidates should possess clinical experience in both cardiac and general thoracic procedures to address the breadth of the 11 domains covered in the exam.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • General thoracic surgical procedures
  • Lung and airway management
  • Pleural and mediastinal interventions
  • Esophageal and chest wall surgery
  • Cardiac and acquired heart disease
  • Congenital heart disease treatment
  • General surgical management

How to Prepare

Review clinical guidelines and surgical literature relevant to the 11 domains. Focus on diagnostic and management protocols for cardiac and thoracic conditions. Utilize official ABTS resources to understand the scope of the 250 questions. Practice time management to complete the 330-minute exam effectively, ensuring familiarity with the breadth of topics from general thoracic surgery to congenital heart disease. Candidates should prioritize understanding patient management scenarios and surgical decision-making processes.

Domain Study Guidance

General Thoracic: Study Guidance

This domain covers the fundamental principles and clinical practices required for general thoracic surgery, focusing on the techniques and patient care standards necessary for outcomes in a variety of thoracic surgical procedures.

  • General thoracic surgical techniques
  • Preoperative patient assessment
  • Surgical safety protocols

Lung: Study Guidance

This section focuses on the diagnosis and surgical management of various lung-related pathologies, requiring candidates to demonstrate expertise in identifying disease states and applying surgical interventions for patient recovery and health.

  • Lung disease interventions
  • Pulmonary surgical diagnostics
  • Post-procedural lung care

Trachea/Bronchi: Study Guidance

This domain addresses the surgical conditions affecting the trachea and bronchial structures, emphasizing the anatomical knowledge and technical skills needed to manage airway obstructions and other related structural diseases within the thoracic cavity.

  • Tracheal and bronchial surgery
  • Airway reconstruction techniques
  • Structural disease management

Pleura: Study Guidance

This area examines surgical approaches to pleural diseases and conditions, covering the management of pleural effusions, empyema, and other space-occupying lesions that require surgical intervention to restore normal thoracic function and patient comfort.

  • Pleural space procedures
  • Management of pleural effusions
  • Empyema treatment

Mediastinum: Study Guidance

This domain covers the surgical management of mediastinal masses and related conditions, requiring an understanding of mediastinal anatomy and the diagnostic workup necessary to determine the effective surgical approach for various types of tumors.

  • Mediastinal surgery
  • Tumor resection techniques
  • Anatomical assessment

Esophagus: Study Guidance

This section focuses on surgical interventions for esophageal disorders and diseases, including the management of motility disorders, malignancies, and structural abnormalities that require precise surgical techniques to ensure proper esophageal function and patient nutrition.

  • Esophageal surgical techniques
  • Management of esophageal motility
  • Malignancy resection

Chest Wall/Diaphragm: Study Guidance

This domain addresses the surgical repair and management of chest wall and diaphragm issues, focusing on the reconstruction of chest wall defects and the treatment of diaphragmatic hernias or injuries to maintain thoracic integrity.

  • Chest wall and diaphragm repair
  • Diaphragmatic hernia management
  • Reconstructive techniques

Cardiac: Study Guidance

This domain covers the core principles of cardiac surgical practice, including the physiological considerations and technical requirements for performing cardiac procedures safely and effectively in a variety of clinical settings and patient populations.

  • Cardiac surgical procedures
  • Cardiac physiology principles
  • Clinical setting adaptation

Acquired Heart Disease: Study Guidance

This section focuses on the management of acquired heart disease in surgical patients, emphasizing the diagnostic evaluation and surgical treatment of conditions such as coronary artery disease and valvular heart disease in adult patients.

  • Acquired heart disease management
  • Valvular heart disease repair
  • Coronary artery diagnostics

Congenital Heart Disease: Study Guidance

This domain addresses the surgical treatment of congenital heart disease, requiring knowledge of pediatric cardiac anatomy and the complex surgical techniques used to correct structural heart defects present from birth in various patients.

  • Congenital heart disease repair
  • Pediatric cardiac surgical techniques
  • Structural defect correction

General Management: Study Guidance

This domain covers general management principles applicable to thoracic surgery patients, including postoperative care, pain management, and the coordination of multidisciplinary teams to ensure recovery and long-term health for patients undergoing thoracic surgical procedures.

  • General patient management
  • Postoperative care protocols
  • Multidisciplinary team coordination

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive prepared for a 330-minute session. You will encounter 250 questions covering 11 domains. Manage your time carefully across the entire exam duration to ensure all sections are addressed within the allotted time frame, as pacing is important for success.
