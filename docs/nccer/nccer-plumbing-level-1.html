What This Exam Validates

The NCCER Plumbing Level 1 certification exam evaluates a candidate's knowledge of the plumbing profession across exactly 100 questions. Certified by NCCER, the assessment covers twelve distinct domains including trade history, safety procedures, trade tools, mathematics, architectural drawings, plastic pipe, copper tube, cast iron pipe, steel pipe, plumbing fixtures, drain and waste systems, and water distribution systems to ensure competence.

Who Should Take This Exam

Apprentice plumbers and entry-level trade trainees seeking professional certification should take this exam. Candidates must understand basic plumbing concepts, hand and power tools, and pipe installation requirements.

Complete practice questions to test your knowledge across all twelve domains before test day to ensure you are fully prepared for the 180 minutes duration.

Domain Study Guidance

Introduction to the Plumbing Profession: Study Guidance

This domain covers the plumbing profession, including trade history, current emerging technologies, and the primary human relations skills that are required for successful apprentices entering the modern workforce today.

  • Plumbing history
  • Trade technologies
  • Human relations

Plumbing Safety: Study Guidance

This domain focuses intensely on plumbing hazards, examining both direct and indirect costs of accidents, proper personal protective equipment usage, and safety guidelines for all apprentice plumbers working on active job sites.

  • Personal protective equipment
  • Confined-space safety
  • Hand and power tool hazards

Tools of the Plumbing Trade: Study Guidance

This domain reviews the hand and power tools used daily by professional plumbers to accurately measure, lay out, level, cut, drill, and join various types of pipes during everyday field installations.

  • Tool selection
  • Power tool hazards
  • Tool care and maintenance

Introduction to Plumbing Math: Study Guidance

This domain covers fundamental mathematical concepts such as fractions, decimals, and squares, applying them directly to practical on-the-job measuring tasks encountered during everyday plumbing installations across various construction projects.

  • Whole numbers and fractions
  • Decimals and squares
  • Pipe length measurement

Introduction to Plumbing Drawings: Study Guidance

This domain teaches trainees how to correctly interpret plumbing and mechanical drawings, standard symbols, and technical schematics required for accurate residential or commercial system installations in the field.

  • Plumbing symbols
  • Isometric and orthographic drawings
  • Code requirements

Plastic Pipe and Fittings: Study Guidance

This domain explains different plastic pipe materials in detail and outlines the correct methods for measuring, cutting, joining, supporting, and pressure testing them safely within active job sites.

  • Plastic pipe types
  • Cutting and joining
  • Pressure testing

Copper Tube and Fittings: Study Guidance

This domain addresses copper tube sizing, standard labeling, typical applications, various valves, and the proper industry-standard methods for cutting, joining, and insulating copper materials during installation for residential and commercial projects.

  • Copper sizing and labeling
  • Cutting and joining methods
  • Insulation and seismic codes

Cast Iron Pipe and Fittings: Study Guidance

This domain covers hub-and-spigot and no-hub cast iron pipe and fittings extensively, focusing on their specific uses and installation requirements in modern drain, waste, and vent piping systems for buildings.

  • Cast iron sizing
  • Cutting techniques
  • Joining and hanging

Steel Pipe and Fittings: Study Guidance

This domain presents core concepts for steel pipe threading, material labeling, accurate sizing, field measuring, cutting, joining, and hanging techniques utilized by trade workers on various commercial and residential projects.

  • Steel pipe threading
  • Valves and elbows
  • Corrugated stainless steel tubing

Introduction to Plumbing Fixtures: Study Guidance

This domain introduces primary plumbing fixture types, their underlying materials, and their overall functionality when installed in typical residential or commercial building applications by certified plumbing professionals across the industry.

  • Fixture materials
  • Bathroom and kitchen fixtures
  • Faucet functionality

Introduction to Drain, Waste, and Vent (DWV) Systems: Study Guidance

This domain explains how drain, waste, and vent systems operate to safely remove liquid and solid waste from buildings directly to municipal or private sewer systems without compromising indoor air quality.

  • DWV components
  • Drains, traps, and vents
  • Sewer system connections

Introduction to Water Distribution Systems: Study Guidance

This domain identifies the major features and functions of water distribution system components, including various valves, pipe fittings, and water heaters used to supply clean water throughout structures.

  • Water distribution components
  • Valves and fittings
  • Public and private water sources

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive early at the testing center with your identification. Pace yourself during the 180 minutes allowed to answer all 100 questions. Read each question carefully before selecting your answer.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The certification exam consists of exactly 100 multiple-choice questions covering twelve distinct plumbing domains outlined in the official NCCER curriculum and candidate preparation guidelines for trade professionals.

What is the passing score?

The established passing score for the NCCER Plumbing Level 1 certification examination is 70, meaning candidates must achieve this minimum benchmark to successfully earn their professional credential.

How long is the exam?

Candidates are given a total duration of 180 minutes to carefully read, analyze, and complete all questions presented on the official certification examination administered at authorized testing centers.

What materials are covered in the training guide?

The trainee guide covers twelve modules ranging from plumbing safety and standard tools to math, drawings, plastic pipe, copper tube, cast iron, steel pipe, fixtures, DWV systems, and water distribution systems.
