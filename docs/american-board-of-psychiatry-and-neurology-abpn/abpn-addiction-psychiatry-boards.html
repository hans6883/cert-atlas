What This Exam Validates

The ABPN Addiction Psychiatry Board Certification is administered by the American Board of Psychiatry and Neurology (ABPN). Candidates must answer exactly 220 total questions during a strict duration of 240 minutes to successfully demonstrate professional competence across all fourteen distinct substance use and addictive disorders domains tested on this thorough board examination.

Who Should Take This Exam

Psychiatrists, medical practitioners, and other qualified healthcare professionals seeking official board certification in addiction psychiatry from the American Board of Psychiatry and Neurology (ABPN) as part of their career.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Alcohol-related disorders assessment
  • Opioid-related disorders treatment
  • Stimulant-related disorders analysis
  • Gambling disorder evaluation
  • Forensic issues in substance use
  • Tobacco-related disorders care

How to Prepare

Review the official exam guide published by the American Board of Psychiatry and Neurology (ABPN) very carefully. Focus your study on the 14 specified content domains, covering each substance-related disorder and related forensic topics in depth to prepare thoroughly for the 220 questions during the 240 minutes examination period.
