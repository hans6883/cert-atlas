What This Exam Validates

The Certified Professional in Healthcare Quality (CPHQ) is a professional credential offered by the National Association for Healthcare Quality (NAHQ). It measures extensive knowledge in healthcare quality, including leadership, performance improvement, and patient safety. The exam consists of 140 questions, including scored items and pretest questions, designed to assess competency in managing quality systems across diverse healthcare settings and organizational structures. Candidates must demonstrate proficiency in seven distinct domains to earn this certification, ensuring they meet the standards required for professional excellence in the healthcare quality field.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is intended for healthcare quality professionals with experience performing tasks related to the exam content outline. It is suitable for clinical and nonclinical staff involved in quality, risk management, and process improvement who seek to validate their expertise.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Quality leadership and integration
  • Performance and process improvement
  • Population health and care transitions
  • Health data analytics
  • Patient safety
  • Quality review and accountability
  • Regulatory and accreditation

How to Prepare

Review the official candidate handbook to understand the exam content outline. Focus on the available question types such as recall, application, and analysis. Use any available practice examination to familiarize yourself with the computerized format. Ensure you have adequate practical experience in the field, as the exam tests applied knowledge. Schedule your exam appropriately to maintain your eligibility window and ensure you are prepared for the testing environment, as the 140 questions require focused attention.

Domain Study Guidance

Quality Leadership and Integration: Study Guidance

This domain covers the integration of quality principles into organizational leadership and management structures to ensure effective system performance and alignment with strategic goals across the entire healthcare enterprise and its various departments.

  • Organizational quality strategy
  • Leadership roles in quality
  • Strategic planning

Performance and Process Improvement: Study Guidance

This section focuses on the methodologies and tools used to monitor, analyze, and improve healthcare processes and clinical outcomes, ensuring that quality improvement initiatives are data-driven, sustainable, and aligned with best practices in the field.

  • Process improvement models
  • Performance measurement
  • Quality improvement tools

Population Health and Care Transitions: Study Guidance

This domain addresses the management of patient populations and the coordination of care as individuals transition between different healthcare settings, emphasizing the importance of continuity, communication, and patient-centered approaches to improve overall health outcomes.

  • Care coordination
  • Population health management
  • Transition of care

Health Data Analytics: Study Guidance

This area covers the collection, analysis, and interpretation of healthcare data to support decision-making and quality improvement initiatives, ensuring that practitioners can effectively utilize statistical methods to drive meaningful changes in clinical and operational performance.

  • Data collection methods
  • Statistical analysis
  • Data reporting

Patient Safety: Study Guidance

This domain centers on the identification and mitigation of risks to patient safety and the implementation of safety-focused clinical practices, aiming to reduce medical errors and foster a culture of safety throughout the healthcare organization.

  • Risk management
  • Patient safety standards
  • Error prevention

Quality Review and Accountability: Study Guidance

This section involves the systematic review of healthcare services to ensure accountability and adherence to established quality standards, focusing on internal audits, peer review processes, and the maintenance of high-quality care delivery across all clinical services.

  • Quality review processes
  • Accountability frameworks
  • Peer review

Regulatory and Accreditation: Study Guidance

This domain covers the requirements for maintaining compliance with healthcare regulations and achieving accreditation from relevant bodies, ensuring that the organization meets all legal and professional standards necessary for safe and effective healthcare delivery operations.

  • Regulatory standards
  • Accreditation survey preparation
  • Compliance monitoring

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive at your scheduled test center with proper identification. You will be monitored during the exam. Ensure you understand the security rules regarding personal belongings and prohibited items before your appointment to avoid any potential issues during the check-in process.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The CPHQ examination consists of 140 multiple-choice questions. This total includes scored questions used to determine your final result and pretest questions that are not included in your final score, but are used for statistical analysis purposes.

What is the passing score?

The passing score for the CPHQ examination is 600 out of a possible 800 points. Candidates must meet this specific standard to earn the certification and demonstrate their competency in healthcare quality management.

How long is the exam?

The exam is a timed, computerized test administered at authorized centers. Candidates are given specific duration limits for the testing session, which are provided during the scheduling process at the testing center according to official guidelines.

How do I maintain my CPHQ status?

To maintain active CPHQ status, you must fulfill continuing education requirements by obtaining the required continuing education credits over your recertification cycle and paying the fee. Check with National Association for Healthcare Quality (NAHQ) for the current certification path and specific renewal requirements.
