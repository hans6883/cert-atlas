What This Exam Validates

The American Board of Foot and Ankle Surgery Certification validates professional competence in podiatric surgery. Administered by the American Board of Foot and Ankle Surgery, this exam assesses knowledge across 11 domains, including surgical techniques, perioperative management, and general medicine. It ensures that practitioners demonstrate the clinical proficiency required to provide safe and effective surgical care for complex foot and ankle conditions. Candidates must demonstrate mastery of these core areas to achieve professional board certification status through this assessment of 80 questions, which requires a passing score of 500 to successfully earn the credential.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is intended for podiatric surgeons seeking formal board certification. Candidates should possess extensive clinical experience in surgical procedures, perioperative management, and diagnostic imaging to demonstrate their ability to manage complex foot and ankle pathologies effectively in a professional clinical setting.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Diagnostic imaging interpretation
  • Surgical principles application
  • Procedural technique execution
  • Perioperative patient management
  • Complication identification
  • General medical assessment
