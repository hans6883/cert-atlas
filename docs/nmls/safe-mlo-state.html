Historical State-MLO Scope

The SAFE MLO Test — State Component, certified by NMLS, covered state licensing laws, state mortgage laws, and state-specific regulatory requirements for aspiring mortgage professionals. This particular examination is officially retired as of 2013-04-01. Its verified successor is National Test with UST, which serves as the required replacement pathway for all candidates seeking proper licensing credentials today. Candidates must understand that this specific exam is no longer available for registration or completion, as the industry has transitioned to a unified testing model to ensure consistent standards across all jurisdictions for mortgage loan originators.

Who State-MLO Was For

Individuals seeking mortgage loan originator licensing who must complete state-specific educational and testing requirements. Because this particular examination is retired, all candidates should pursue the National Test with UST instead to satisfy their professional licensing obligations.

Frequently asked questions

What is the passing score?

The established passing score is a percentage of 75% which is strictly required for all SAFE MLO test components to demonstrate adequate competency before receiving any professional mortgage licensing credentials in the industry.

How much does the exam cost?

The price is $69 USD in addition to the national component known as UST, and this specific fee payment is required in some states for proper registration and processing of your licensing application.

When was the exam retired?

The exam is officially retired as of 2013-04-01, with National Test with UST currently serving as the verified successor for all licensing candidates seeking to enter the mortgage industry and obtain professional credentials.

What format is used for testing?

Testing follows a linear format administered through Pearson VUE in-person testing centers or convenient OnVUE online proctored options depending on the preference of the individual candidate seeking to complete their required mortgage certification.
