What This Exam Validates

The SAFE MLO National Test with Uniform State Test, certified by NMLS, covers federal mortgage laws, uniform state content, general mortgage knowledge, loan origination activities, and ethics. This assessment evaluates candidates seeking state-licensed mortgage loan originator status across participating jurisdictions as part of the official licensing and regulatory framework with an exam price of 110.0 dollars.

Who Should Take This Exam

Individuals seeking state-licensed mortgage loan originator status through NMLS must take this assessment to demonstrate their knowledge of mortgage regulations, state content, and ethical standards.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Federal Mortgage Laws
  • Uniform State Content
  • General Mortgage Knowledge
  • Mortgage Loan Origination
  • Ethics in Lending
