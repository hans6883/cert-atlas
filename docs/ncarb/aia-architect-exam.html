What This Exam Validates

The ARE 5.0 Architect Registration Examination is a multi-part, national exam developed by NCARB. It evaluates the knowledge and skills required for architectural licensure, focusing on professional services that affect the public's health, safety, and welfare. The exam consists of six divisions, totaling 490 questions across a 1190-minute duration, and is used by U.S. jurisdictions to grant initial licensure to practice architecture.

Who Should Take This Exam

Candidates seeking architectural licensure in a U.S. jurisdiction should take this exam. It is for individuals who have developed the knowledge and skills to practice architecture safely and competently, often following completion of the Architectural Experience Program.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Architectural business practices
  • Legal and contractual standards
  • Project team coordination
  • Programmatic and regulatory requirements
  • Building systems and materials
  • Construction phase services

How to Prepare

Review the official NCARB exam guide and guidelines to understand policies and procedures. Utilize the provided practice exams and reference materials to familiarize yourself with the testing interface, calculator, and whiteboard tools. Ensure your NCARB Record is accurate and matches your identification before scheduling. Plan your study schedule across the six divisions, as you must pay for and schedule each division separately.

Domain Study Guidance

Practice Management: Study Guidance

This initial domain covers the business side of architecture, including the practical application of business practices, professional ethics, firm operations, and the overall management of professional services within the architecture industry.

  • Business operations
  • Professional ethics

Project Management: Study Guidance

This project management domain focuses heavily on the careful coordination of project team activities, resource allocation, project scheduling, and the effective management of various administrative and technical project-related tasks from inception to completion.

  • Project team coordination
  • Project scheduling

Programming & Analysis: Study Guidance

This analytical domain addresses the proper establishment of programmatic requirements, environmental analysis, and regulatory compliance for architectural projects, ensuring that all initial planning phases align with building codes and client specifications.

  • Programmatic requirements
  • Regulatory compliance

Project Planning & Design: Study Guidance

This specific design-focused domain involves providing creative design alternatives, evaluating physical site conditions, and incorporating appropriate materials, structural considerations, and building systems into structured and cohesive architectural project plans.

  • Design alternatives
  • Building systems

Project Development & Documentation: Study Guidance

This technical documentation domain covers the precise provision and detailed coordination of project documentation, construction drawings, specifications, and site development details required for successful building execution and regulatory review.

  • Project documentation
  • Site development

Construction & Evaluation: Study Guidance

This final domain focuses entirely on construction phase services, administration, and evaluating projects during all phases of physical development, submittal reviews, and post-construction evaluations to ensure design intent is fully achieved.

  • Construction phase services
  • Project assessment

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive at your PSI test center or launch your online-proctored exam with your valid identification. Ensure your testing space meets all security requirements. You may take breaks during the exam, but follow all conduct policies to avoid security violations.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The Architect Registration Examination features a detailed structure consisting of a total of 490 questions distributed across all six distinct divisions of the overall testing program.

What is the passing score?

NCARB does not publish a specific numerical passing score. Instead, the examination utilizes an established passing standard to evaluate candidate competence in the areas of health, safety, and welfare.

How long is the exam?

The total duration for all divisions combined is 1190 minutes. Each division has a specific duration determined by the content and complexity of the material covered in that section.

Can I take the exam online?

Candidates have the flexibility to take the examination online through the PSI Proctoring Platform or in person at any authorized physical PSI test center location of their choice.

What is the next step?

After completing the exam, check with NCARB for the current certification path regarding your licensure status and any additional requirements needed to practice in your specific jurisdiction.
