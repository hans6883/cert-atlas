What This Exam Validates

The PSP Physical Security Professional examination is certified by ASIS International and tests candidates across three domains: Physical Security Assessment, Application, Design, and Integration of Physical Security Systems, and Implementation of Physical Security Measures, providing an extensive validation of professional security knowledge and vital practitioner competencies across the industry today.

Who Should Take This Exam

Physical security managers, security professionals, and practitioners with experience in risk assessment, system design, and security measure implementation should take this certification exam to validate their competencies.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Physical Security Assessment
  • System Design and Integration
  • Implementation Measures
  • Risk Analysis
  • Countermeasures
  • Procurement Plan

How to Prepare

Carefully and completely review the official ASIS International Body of Knowledge before attempting the test. Study risk assessment models, physical security countermeasures, bidding processes, and project management functions to ensure absolute readiness for all three exam domains without exception during your preparation process.

Domain Study Guidance

Physical Security Assessment: Study Guidance

This domain focuses intensely on physical security assessment plans, asset valuation, threat identification, vulnerability quantification, and detailed risk analysis strategies required for professional security practitioners during evaluations and thorough facility reviews.

  • Key area and important asset identification
  • Risk assessment models and considerations
  • Qualitative and quantitative assessment methods

Application, Design, and Integration of Physical Security Systems: Study Guidance

This domain covers security program performance requirements, appropriate physical security countermeasures, and system design with extensive project documentation to ensure effective physical security planning and operational integration across various organizational environments.

  • Design constraints and performance requirements
  • Structural and electronic security measures
  • Systems integration and project management concepts

Implementation of Physical Security Measures: Study Guidance

This domain addresses criteria for pre-bid meetings, procurement plans, implementation management, personnel requirements, and ongoing program evaluation to guarantee successful deployment of security measures across facilities and operational sites.

  • Bid process and package types
  • Vendor evaluation and selection
  • Installation and inspection techniques

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive at the testing center prepared for a 150-minute exam consisting of 140 multiple-choice questions. Select one answer from the four choices offered for each question.

Frequently asked questions

The official examination consists of a total of 140 multiple-choice questions designed to test your knowledge and expertise across all designated subject areas of the program.

What is the passing score?

Check with ASIS International for the current certification path and passing score details, as specific passing scores are managed directly by the official certifying body.

How long is the exam?

The exam provides candidates with a total duration of 150 minutes to carefully read, evaluate, and complete all of the assigned multiple-choice questions during testing.

Who certifies the PSP exam?

The PSP Physical Security Professional examination is officially certified and administered by ASIS International, which maintains the extensive Body of Knowledge and job analysis studies.
