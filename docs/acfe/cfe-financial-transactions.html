Historical CFE-FT Scope

The CFE Part 1: Financial Transactions and Fraud Schemes exam, administered by the ACFE, covered methods of occupational fraud. This exam is retired as of 2026-06-02. The registry names no successor for this specific exam. Candidates seeking the CFE credential should contact the ACFE directly to determine the current requirements and path for certification, as this specific exam is no longer available for registration or completion by any candidate.

Who CFE-FT Was For

This exam was intended for anti-fraud professionals, auditors, and investigators. It required knowledge of financial crime schemes and internal control systems. As the exam is retired, individuals should consult the ACFE for the current requirements for the CFE credential.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Detecting financial statement fraud
  • Analyzing corruption schemes
  • Recognizing revenue fraud
  • Investigating payroll fraud

How to Reuse Your Preparation

Since this exam is retired and has no successor, do not prepare for it. Focus on current ACFE materials and the latest Report to the Nations to understand fraud schemes. Historical Domain Guide

Asset Misappropriation: Historical Scope

This domain covers the theft or misuse of organizational resources by employees. It focuses on identifying how assets are taken and the methods used to conceal these actions within an organization during daily operations.

  • Cash larceny
  • Skimming

Financial Statement Fraud: Historical Scope

This domain addresses intentional material misstatements or omissions in financial statements. It examines how perpetrators manipulate net income or net worth to deceive stakeholders and hide financial irregularities within the reporting process of an organization.

  • Fictitious revenues
  • Concealed liabilities

Corruption: Historical Scope

This domain covers the abuse of power for personal gain. It examines schemes where employees or third parties use their influence to obtain improper benefits or payments from an organization through various corrupt business practices.

  • Bribery
  • Conflicts of interest

Revenue Fraud: Historical Scope

This domain focuses on fraudulent activities related to the recognition and reporting of revenue. It covers the methods used to inflate sales figures or misrepresent the financial health of an organization to external parties and investors.

  • Timing differences
  • Fictitious sales

Payroll Fraud: Historical Scope

This domain addresses fraud involving employee compensation systems. It covers the creation of fake employees or the falsification of wages to misappropriate funds from the organization's payroll through various deceptive and unauthorized internal schemes.

  • Ghost employees
  • Falsified wages

Frequently asked questions

How many questions were on the exam?

The exam consisted of 125 multiple choice questions in total. Candidates were expected to complete these within the allotted time frame before the exam was officially retired by the governing body.

What was the passing score?

A passing score of 75% was required for this exam. This percentage was the standard benchmark for candidates to demonstrate sufficient knowledge of the financial transactions and fraud schemes covered in the curriculum.

How long was the exam?

The exam had a duration of 135 minutes. This time limit was strictly enforced for all candidates during the testing period to ensure that the assessment of their anti-fraud knowledge remained consistent and fair.

Is there a successor to this exam?

No, the ACFE has retired this exam and has not named a successor. Candidates should reach out to the organization directly for guidance on the current path to achieving the professional CFE credential.
