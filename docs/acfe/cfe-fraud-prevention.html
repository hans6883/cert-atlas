What This Exam Validates

The CFE Part 4 Fraud Prevention and Deterrence examination comprehensively evaluates candidate proficiency across five distinct domains. Candidates are given a strict duration of 135 minutes to answer 125 multiple-choice questions delivered in a linear format through Pearson VUE or online proctored testing. A passing score of 75 percent is required.

Who Should Take This Exam

Certified Fraud Examiner candidates and anti-fraud professionals studying fraud prevention, deterrence principles, anti-corruption compliance frameworks, and corporate governance standards required in the modern anti-fraud industry today.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Why Fraud Occurs (Fraud Triangle)
  • Fraud Risk Management
  • Ethics Programs
  • FCPA and Anti-Corruption Laws
  • Criminology Theory

How to Prepare

Carefully review the official candidate handbooks and detailed content outlines concerning the Foreign Corrupt Practices Act, anti-bribery provisions, accounting rules, and compliance program standards. Understand statutory definitions, regulatory enforcement practices, and corporate liability principles across all assigned domains for the test to ensure comprehensive mastery of the subject matter.

Domain Study Guidance

Why Fraud Occurs (Fraud Triangle): Study Guidance

Examines the core concepts of why fraud occurs through the Fraud Triangle framework, analyzing motives, pressures, and opportunities within organizational environments to understand illicit behavior and deter workplace schemes.

  • Fraud Triangle concepts
  • Motives and pressures

Fraud Risk Management: Study Guidance

Covers fraud risk management strategies, focusing on identifying, assessing, and mitigating organizational fraud risks through internal controls, governance policies, and structured evaluation methodologies designed to protect corporate assets.

  • Risk identification
  • Mitigation strategies

Ethics Programs: Study Guidance

Focuses on ethics programs, corporate codes of conduct, and establishing an organizational culture that promotes integrity and discourages illicit behavior through clear administrative policies and internal reporting systems.

  • Codes of conduct
  • Integrity policies

FCPA and Anti-Corruption Laws: Study Guidance

Explores the Foreign Corrupt Practices Act and related anti-corruption laws, detailing anti-bribery provisions, accounting controls, and jurisdictional conduct applicable to multinational corporate entities operating in global business environments.

  • Anti-bribery provisions
  • Accounting controls

Criminology Theory: Study Guidance

Analyzes criminology theory to understand the underlying behavioral and sociological factors that drive individuals to commit white-collar crimes, occupational fraud, and various organizational financial offenses against their employers.

  • Behavioral factors
  • Sociological theories

Exam-Day Guidance

Review all testing rules before your appointment, ensuring you arrive on time at the Pearson VUE center or set up your online proctored workstation to complete your examination.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The certification examination contains a total of 125 multiple-choice questions structured in a fixed, linear format that candidates must complete during their scheduled testing session.

What is the passing score?

A passing score of 75 percent is required per part on the certification exam to demonstrate adequate competency across all tested knowledge domains and subject areas.

How long is the exam?

Registered candidates are given a total duration of exactly 135 minutes to complete all of the multiple-choice questions included in the official test session successfully and efficiently.

What question format is used on the test?

The test utilizes multiple-choice questions delivered securely via Pearson VUE test centers or through convenient online proctored testing options that are available to all qualified applicants.
