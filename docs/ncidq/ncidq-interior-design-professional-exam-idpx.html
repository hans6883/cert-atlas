What This Exam Validates

The NCIDQ IDPX - Interior Design Professional Exam is administered by CIDQ (NCIDQ). It evaluates professional practice knowledge across five distinct domains, covering feasibility studies, scheduling, budgeting, contracts, procurement, permitting, the construction process, site observation, project close-out, and overall project management coordination to ensure candidates demonstrate complete competence in the field.

Who Should Take This Exam

Interior design professionals who have met the education and work experience requirements and are approved to sit for Route 1 or Route 2, Part 2 exams.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Feasibility Studies
  • Scheduling and Budgeting
  • Contracts, Procurement, and Permitting
  • Construction Process
  • Site Observation and Close-Out

How to Prepare

Review the official Candidate Handbook and blueprints provided by CIDQ to prepare properly for your test. Ensure transcripts and work experience verifications are submitted through your MyNCIDQ account. Schedule your exam appointment through Prometric early during the payment and scheduling period to secure your preferred testing time.

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive at the Prometric test center or ready your remote proctoring environment according to Prometric guidelines. Bring valid government-issued photo identification containing your signature for verification purposes.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The standard exam consists of 115 total questions administered professionally by Prometric, carefully covering all five required content domains to test your professional interior design knowledge completely.

What is the passing score?

The established passing score for the NCIDQ IDPX exam is 500, which candidates must achieve to successfully demonstrate their competency in interior design professional practice.

How long is the exam?

The total duration allotted for completing the exam is 180 minutes, providing candidates sufficient time to address all the multiple-choice questions within the test center.

How often is the exam offered?

The exam scheduling frequency and specific testing windows are managed directly by CIDQ, so always check with them for the latest registration dates and application deadlines.
