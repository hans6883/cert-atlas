Historical SASM Scope

The SAFe Advanced Scrum Master (SASM) exam, certified by Scaled Agile, covered advanced team facilitation and flow optimization. This exam is retired as of 2025-06-30. The verified successor is SAFe Advanced Scrum Master (SASM). Candidates seeking certification must now pursue the successor exam, which maintains the focus on scaling Agile practices and improving team performance within the Scaled Agile Framework. This transition ensures that all practitioners are evaluated against the most current standards for leadership and team facilitation in complex environments.

Who SASM Was For

This certification is intended for experienced Scrum Masters and team coaches working within an Agile Release Train. Candidates should have practical experience facilitating team events and managing flow to improve delivery outcomes in a scaled environment.

Focus your study on the objectives of the successor SASM. Review SAFe principles, flow metrics, and team coaching techniques. Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of 45 multiple-choice questions designed to test your knowledge of the Scaled Agile Framework and the responsibilities of an Advanced Scrum Master in a professional setting.

What is the passing score?

To pass the exam, you must achieve a score of 77. This requirement applies to the standard certification assessment provided by Scaled Agile for all candidates seeking the credential.

How long is the exam?

The exam is a 90-minute assessment. Candidates must complete all 45 questions within this duration to qualify for certification and demonstrate their proficiency in the Scaled Agile Framework.

What is the cost of the exam?

The exam price is $1095. This fee is typically included as part of the official training provided by Scaled Agile, covering the assessment and the certification process for participants.
