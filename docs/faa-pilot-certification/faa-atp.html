What This Exam Validates

The FAA Airline Transport Pilot Knowledge Test is a computer-based examination administered at authorized PSI or CATS testing centers. Candidates must answer exactly 125 multiple choice questions during a linear test session. The assessment covers professional aviation operations, regulations, meteorology, and navigation systems for transport category aircraft, requiring a passing score of 70 percent.

Who Should Take This Exam

Experienced professional pilots seeking airline transport pilot certification under official federal aviation regulations and transport category aircraft operating rules within commercial aviation environments across the nation.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Advanced Aerodynamics and High-Altitude Operations
  • Transport Category Aircraft Systems
  • Air Carrier Operations and CRM
  • High-Altitude Meteorology

How to Prepare

Carefully review official pilot handbooks, federal aviation regulations, navigation principles, and high-altitude meteorology guides in absolute detail. Ensure you understand all core domains completely to achieve the minimum passing score required for pilot certification during your computer-based testing appointment, keeping the 180-minute duration limit firmly in mind.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of exactly 125 multiple choice questions delivered in a linear computer-based format at authorized PSI or CATS testing centers across the country for all candidates.

What is the passing score?

The passing score is 70, which aligns with the standard percentage passing requirement established for all pilot knowledge tests administered under FAA Pilot Certification guidelines and official standards.

How long is the exam?

The exam has a maximum duration of 180 minutes, giving you sufficient time to carefully complete all questions at an authorized computer-based testing center location without feeling rushed during your appointment.

What is the exam price?

The exam price is $175.0 for candidates registering and taking the computer-based knowledge test at approved PSI or CATS testing facilities across the nation under standard registration policies.

Online ProctoringNot available
ID RequirementsGovernment-issued photo ID required. Student pilot certificate or instructor endorsement required for some tests. Name on ID must match registration exactly.
RenewalRequired -- FAA Airman certificates do not expire, but proficiency requirements apply. Airman Knowledge Test result valid for 24 months for scheduling the practical test (checkride).
