What This Exam Validates

The ABA Anesthesiology Board Certification is administered by the American Board of Anesthesiology. Candidates must complete a formal assessment consisting of 200 questions delivered over 240 minutes to demonstrate their understanding of anesthesiology, including basic sciences, clinical sciences, organ-based basic and clinical sciences, clinical subspecialties, and special problems or issues within a strict timeframe.

Who Should Take This Exam

Anesthesiology candidates seeking initial board certification or maintenance of certification through the American Board of Anesthesiology (ABA) must complete this evaluation to demonstrate professional clinical and scientific knowledge.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Basic Sciences
  • Clinical Sciences
  • Organ-based Sciences
  • Clinical Subspecialties
  • Special Problems or Issues

How to Prepare

Review the official American Board of Anesthesiology content outlines carefully and study basic anatomy, pharmacology, organ systems, and subspecialty procedures in detail. Practice answering multiple-choice questions within the 240 minutes allotted to build exam-day stamina and time management skills for the 200 total questions.

Domain Study Guidance

Basic Sciences: Study Guidance

This domain covers baseline principles including topographical and clinical anatomy, anesthesia machine physics, monitoring equipment operations, and simple mathematical or statistical applications relevant to clinical practice during surgical procedures.

  • Topographical, radiological, and clinical anatomy landmarks
  • Physics of anesthesia machines, monitoring equipment, and delivery devices
  • Simple math and statistics applied to clinical scenarios

Clinical Sciences: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on routine procedures, preoperative patient evaluations, fasting guidelines, regional anesthesia techniques, potential complications, general anesthesia management, monitoring standards, and airway management methods required for patient safety.

  • Patient evaluation, preoperative preparation, and fasting guidelines
  • Regional anesthesia techniques and potential complications
  • General anesthesia management, monitoring standards, and airway techniques

Organ-based Basic & Clinical Sciences: Study Guidance

This domain examines physiological functions and clinical applications across major human organ systems, including the central and peripheral nervous systems, respiratory system, cardiovascular system, renal, hepatic, hematologic, and endocrine systems.

  • Central and peripheral nervous system physiology and anatomy
  • Respiratory and cardiovascular system function under anesthesia
  • Renal, hepatic, hematologic, and endocrine systems

Clinical Subspecialties: Study Guidance

This domain addresses advanced clinical subspecialties, pediatric and obstetric anesthesia, painful disease states, intensive care management, and general procedures associated with orthopedic, trauma, and ambulatory surgical environments during daily practice.

  • Pediatric and obstetric anesthesia
  • Painful disease states and intensive care management
  • Subspecialty procedures including orthopedic, trauma, and ambulatory surgery

Special Problems or Issues in Anesthesiology: Study Guidance

This domain explores complex ethical dilemmas, physician impairment, substance abuse, clinician well-being, practice management, medicolegal issues, quality improvement, patient safety initiatives, and disparities within healthcare delivery systems nationwide.

  • Physician impairment, substance abuse, and clinician well-being
  • Ethics, practice management, and medicolegal issues
  • Quality improvement, patient safety, and healthcare disparities

Exam-Day Guidance

Manage your pacing carefully across the 200 questions to ensure you finish within the 240 minutes. Check with American Board of Anesthesiology (ABA) for specific testing center policies and arrival times.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of 200 total questions covering multiple domains of anesthesiology, including basic sciences, clinical sciences, organ-based topics, subspecialties, and special problems or issues.

What is the passing score?

You should check directly with the American Board of Anesthesiology regarding specific score requirements, grading criteria, and official thresholds necessary to achieve successful certification outcomes on this evaluation.

How long is the exam?

The total duration of the official examination is strictly 240 minutes long, during which candidates must complete all 200 questions within the allotted timeframe for board certification.

Who administers the exam?

The entire certification process, along with its associated medical evaluations, is officially administered and overseen directly by mission-driven leadership from the American Board of Anesthesiology for all participating medical professionals.
