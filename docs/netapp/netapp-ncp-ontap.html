What This Exam Validates

The NetApp Certified Professional ONTAP exam, administered by NetApp, evaluates technical proficiency in managing complex ONTAP storage environments. It covers six distinct domains, including data management, storage architecture, SAN/NAS configuration, data protection, cloud integration, and performance troubleshooting. This certification confirms an individual's ability to implement and manage storage solutions effectively within NetApp ecosystems, ensuring they meet industry standards for storage administration, infrastructure design, and reliable data handling in modern enterprise environments.

Who Should Take This Exam

This exam targets storage administrators and architects with significant experience in NetApp ONTAP environments. Candidates should possess practical knowledge of storage configuration, data protection workflows, and cloud integration to successfully manage, maintain, and troubleshoot storage systems in demanding enterprise settings.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • ONTAP data management
  • Storage architecture design
  • SAN and NAS configuration
  • Data protection and SnapVault
  • Cloud integration for AWS, Azure, and GCP
  • Performance and troubleshooting

How to Prepare

Review the official NetApp study documentation and exam guides to understand the six domains. Focus on practical configuration tasks for SAN/NAS and data protection. Use the provided resources to practice cluster setup and management workflows. Ensure you are familiar with the specific features of ONTAP versions mentioned in the documentation. Consistent hands-on practice with the CLI and System Manager is necessary for success. Candidates should dedicate time to mastering the nuances of cluster storage management and replication policies to ensure readiness for the exam.

Exam-Day Guidance

The exam is a linear, fixed-form test delivered via Pearson VUE. It consists of multiple choice and multiple response questions. Manage your time effectively across all six domains to ensure you address every section before the time expires during your session.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam format is linear and fixed-form, consisting of multiple choice and multiple response questions. Specific question counts are not provided in the official documentation, so candidates should focus on mastering all six domains to ensure preparation for the test.

What is the passing score?

The exam uses a scaled score system to evaluate candidate performance. The passing requirement is approximately 70%. Candidates should aim for high proficiency across all domains to ensure they meet this threshold and successfully achieve their certification status.

How much does the exam cost?

The registration price for this exam is 250 dollars. This fee covers the cost of the exam delivery through Pearson VUE. Candidates should ensure they are fully prepared before scheduling their appointment to maximize the value of their investment in this professional certification.

How long is the certification valid?

The certification is valid for 2 years from the date of passing. After this period, a renewal is required to maintain your status. Keeping your certification current ensures that your skills remain aligned with the latest NetApp ONTAP technologies and industry best practices.
