What This Exam Validates

The NetApp NCIE Data Protection certification exam evaluates candidates on ONTAP Data Management, Storage Architecture and Design, SAN/NAS Configuration, Data Protection and SnapVault, Cloud Integration across AWS, Azure, and GCP, alongside Performance and Troubleshooting. Candidates must complete a total of 65 questions at authorized Pearson VUE testing centers to successfully earn this professional credential. This exam validates the technical proficiency required to manage complex storage environments effectively.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification explicitly targets enterprise storage professionals who design, configure, and manage complex storage architectures, data protection workflows, and cloud-integrated infrastructures using advanced NetApp technologies in professional environments.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The certification exam consists of exactly 65 questions presented in a linear, fixed-form test format delivered securely through Pearson VUE testing centers across the globe for all candidates.

What is the passing score?

The exam utilizes a scaled score system where approximately 70% passing performance is required to successfully achieve certification for standard candidates taking the test at authorized centers.

How long is the certification validity?

The earned professional certification remains valid for a total duration of 2 years, after which a renewal or recertification process is required to maintain active status for professionals.

How much does the exam cost?

The standard exam registration price is set at $250 USD when registering directly through official NetApp certification booking channels and authorized partner portals for all global test takers.
