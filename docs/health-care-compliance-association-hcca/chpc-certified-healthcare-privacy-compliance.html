What This Exam Validates

The Certified in Healthcare Privacy Compliance (CHPC) designation is offered by the Health Care Compliance Association (HCCA). This professional certification validates knowledge of healthcare privacy regulations, industry standards, and operational practices. It covers seven distinct domains, including privacy program oversight, employee screening, communication, monitoring, and investigations, ensuring that certified professionals can manage and maintain privacy compliance programs within complex healthcare organizations. This credential demonstrates a commitment to maintaining the highest standards of patient data protection.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is intended for professionals currently working in healthcare compliance, privacy, or legal roles. Candidates should possess practical experience in managing privacy programs, addressing regulatory obligations, and overseeing internal reporting systems to safeguard sensitive patient information effectively.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Implementing privacy standards and policies
  • Managing compliance program oversight
  • Screening physicians and vendors
  • Delivering compliance training
  • Conducting privacy monitoring and audits
  • Enforcing discipline for non-compliance
  • Executing investigations and remediation

How to Prepare

Candidates should focus on their professional compliance experience to prepare for the assessment. Review the official Health Care Compliance Association (HCCA) exam guide to identify specific subject areas for testing. Attend healthcare compliance events, engage with industry experts, and participate in the HCCA net social network to discuss regulations. Use the official exam guide to familiarize yourself with the testing environment before your scheduled appointment.

Domain Study Guidance

Privacy Standards, Policies, and Procedures: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the development and implementation of privacy standards, policies, and procedures within a healthcare organization to ensure that all regulatory requirements are met consistently across the entire facility for patient data.

  • Privacy policy creation
  • Regulatory standard implementation

Privacy Compliance Program Oversight: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the oversight and management of privacy compliance programs to ensure organizational integrity and adherence to legal mandates while maintaining the standards of patient data protection and security across the enterprise.

  • Compliance program oversight
  • Privacy program management

Screening and Evaluation of Employees, Physicians, Vendors, and Other Agents: Study Guidance

This domain addresses the processes for screening and evaluating employees, physicians, vendors, and other agents to ensure they meet the necessary privacy standards and do not pose a risk to the organization or patient data.

  • Vendor screening procedures
  • Employee evaluation protocols

Communication, Education, and Training on Compliance Issues: Study Guidance

This domain involves the communication, education, and training strategies required to address compliance issues, ensuring that all staff members understand their roles and responsibilities regarding the protection of sensitive patient health information in daily operations.

  • Compliance training delivery
  • Communication of privacy issues

Privacy Monitoring, Auditing, and Internal Reporting Systems: Study Guidance

This domain covers the systems and processes for monitoring, auditing, and internal reporting of privacy compliance to identify potential vulnerabilities and ensure that all organizational privacy policies are being followed by staff at all levels.

  • Internal reporting systems
  • Privacy auditing techniques

Discipline for Non-Compliance: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the establishment and enforcement of clear disciplinary actions for instances of non-compliance, ensuring that all staff understand the consequences of failing to adhere to established privacy policies and procedures within the organization.

  • Disciplinary policy enforcement
  • Non-compliance response

Investigations and Remedial Measures: Study Guidance

This domain covers the formal procedures for conducting investigations and implementing necessary remedial measures following identified compliance breaches to prevent future occurrences and maintain the trust of patients and regulatory bodies alike in the organization.

  • Investigation procedures
  • Remedial measure implementation

Exam-Day Guidance

The exam consists of 120 questions. Manage your time effectively during the 120-minute session. Use the official exam guide provided by the Health Care Compliance Association (HCCA) beforehand to build familiarity with the testing environment and reduce stress on the day of your exam.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The CHPC exam consists of 120 questions that cover seven distinct domains of healthcare privacy compliance, requiring candidates to demonstrate their knowledge across a range of regulatory and operational topics.

What is the passing score?

The exam uses a criterion-referenced scaled passing score to determine if a candidate has met the required standard for certification, ensuring that all successful individuals possess the necessary professional competency levels.

How long is the exam?

Candidates are given a total of 120 minutes to complete the 120 questions on the exam, which requires careful time management to ensure all sections are addressed within the allotted period.

Is there an official study guide available?

Yes, the Health Care Compliance Association (HCCA) provides an official exam guide that candidates should review to understand the content outline and prepare for the certification assessment effectively.
