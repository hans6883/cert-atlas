No direct replacement is named in the reviewed official sources.

Historical Associate-MD Scope

The OutSystems Associate Mobile Developer certification, offered by OutSystems, covered mobile application development topics across five specific domains. This certification officially retired on 2020-08-10. It did not replace itself, and the verified successor path forward is Mobile Developer Specialist (O11 and ODC). Candidates previously tested on 60 questions within a duration of 90 minutes, requiring a passing score of 70 to certify successfully.

Who Associate-MD Was For

Developers seeking credentials in OutSystems mobile application development can no longer register for this retired exam and should instead pursue Mobile Developer Specialist (O11 and ODC).

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The professional examination consists of exactly 60 total questions presented in a linear format using multiple choice and multiple select question types to test candidates on their technical knowledge.

What is the passing score?

Candidates must achieve a verified passing score of 70 to successfully complete the examination and earn the professional certification credential directly from the provider without exception.

How long is the exam?

The permitted duration of the examination is precisely 90 minutes, administered as a linear, online proctored test requiring careful time management from all registered participants during their testing session.

What is the successor to this retired exam?

The official verified successor is Mobile Developer Specialist (O11 and ODC). The Associate Mobile Developer exam did not replace itself upon reaching its designated official retirement date.
