Professional-RD

Professional-RD was retired on March 3, 2025

OutSystems retired PROFESSIONAL-RD on 2025-03-03. Expert Developer is the named replacement path.

No direct replacement is named in the reviewed official sources.

Historical Professional-RD Scope

The OutSystems Professional Reactive Developer certification, certified by OutSystems, evaluated technical knowledge across advanced application architecture, performance, scalability, integration patterns, security best practices, and DevOps. Please note that this certification is retired as of 2025-03-03. Its verified successor is Expert Developer (OutSystems Expert Developer Certification), which serves as the designated path forward.

Who Professional-RD Was For

This retired exam was intended for experienced developers building reactive applications on the OutSystems platform who needed to validate their advanced technical skills across multiple domains.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Advanced Application Architecture
  • Performance and Scalability
  • Advanced Integration Patterns
  • Security Best Practices
  • DevOps and Lifecycle Management

How to Reuse Your Preparation

Because this professional level exam is permanently retired, candidates should instead focus their preparation entirely on the official successor path named Expert Developer by reviewing documentation covering architecture canvas, reactive web security, deployment plans, and standard REST integration patterns to successfully build required platform expertise during their studies.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of exactly 60 total questions presented in a linear online proctored format. Question types include multiple choice and multiple select items to test candidate knowledge across all domains.

What is the passing score?

The passing score required for the exam is established at 73 percent. Candidates must successfully achieve this score or higher across all questions to pass.

How long is the exam?

The official exam duration is precisely 90 minutes. Candidates must complete all required questions within this allocated time limit during their scheduled linear online proctored testing session.

What is the current status of the exam?

The exam is retired as of 2025-03-03. OutSystems has named Expert Developer as the verified successor path for candidates looking to advance their certification credentials.
