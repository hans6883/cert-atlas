What This Exam Validates

The Nurse Executive Advanced, Board Certified (NEA-BC) exam, administered by the ANCC, validates senior nursing leaders’ expertise in strategic, financial, human‑resource, and technology management. It consists of 175 multiple‑choice items delivered in a linear format over 210 minutes. Candidates must achieve a scaled score of 350 (on a 200‑500 scale) to earn the five‑year certification.

Who Should Take This Exam

Current nurse executives, directors of nursing, chief nursing officers, and senior managers with at least several years of leadership experience should pursue NEA‑BC to demonstrate advanced executive competence in healthcare.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Strategic planning and policy development
  • Budgeting and financial analysis
  • Workforce development and talent management
  • Health‑IT and data analytics

How to Prepare

Review the ANCC handbook to understand exam format and content areas. Build a study schedule that allocates time to each of the four domains, using current nursing leadership literature and the IOM report on education transformation. Practice with multiple‑choice questions, focusing on scenario‑based items that reflect strategic, financial, HR, and technology decisions. Join a peer study group or online forum for discussion and feedback. Take at least one full‑length practice test under timed conditions.

Domain Study Guidance

Strategic Management: Study Guidance

Strategic Management focuses on aligning nursing initiatives with organizational vision, developing long‑term plans, and evaluating outcomes to drive improvement. Candidates must understand environmental analysis, change leadership, and the role of nursing executives in shaping health policy.

  • Environmental analysis
  • Change leadership
  • Policy alignment
  • Outcome evaluation

Financial Management: Study Guidance

Financial Management assesses the ability to oversee budgets, interpret financial statements, and allocate resources effectively. Test‑takers need competence in cost‑benefit analysis, revenue cycle oversight, and fiscal decision‑making that supports nursing operations.

  • Budget oversight
  • Cost‑benefit analysis
  • Revenue cycle management
  • Fiscal decision‑making

Human Resource Management: Study Guidance

Human Resource Management covers workforce planning, talent acquisition, performance evaluation, and staff development. Candidates should master strategies for building high‑performing teams, managing labor relations, and fostering a culture of continuous learning.

  • Workforce planning
  • Talent acquisition
  • Performance evaluation
  • Staff development

Technology Management: Study Guidance

Technology Management evaluates proficiency with health‑information systems, data analytics, and emerging digital tools that improve patient care. Examinees must understand implementation, cybersecurity basics, and how technology supports strategic nursing objectives.

  • Health‑IT systems
  • Data analytics
  • Implementation planning
  • Cybersecurity basics

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive early, bring a valid photo ID and your registration confirmation. Bring only approved calculators if needed; the exam is computer‑based and linear, so you cannot return to previous questions. Manage time to answer all 175 items within the 210‑minute window.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The NEA‑BC exam contains 175 multiple‑choice questions presented in a linear format. All items must be answered in the order they appear, and you cannot return to earlier questions once you advance.

What is the passing score?

A scaled score of 350 on the 200‑to‑500 range is required to pass. Scores below 350 are considered failing, while scores above indicate varying levels of proficiency.

How long is the exam?

The exam lasts 210 minutes, giving you just over three hours to complete all 175 items. Manage your time carefully to ensure you answer each question within the allotted period.

What is the cost for non‑members?

Non‑members pay $520 for the NEA‑BC exam, while ANA members receive a reduced price of $395. Prices are listed in the ANCC exam guide and must be paid at registration.
