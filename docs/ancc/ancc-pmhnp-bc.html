What This Exam Validates

The PMHNP-BC credential is issued by the ANCC and certifies psychiatric mental health nurse practitioners. The 175-question multiple-choice exam covers five domains: Assessment, Diagnosis, Treatment Planning, Psychopharmacology, and Evidence-Based Practice. It is a linear-format test administered in a 210-minute session. Candidates must achieve a scaled passing score of 350 on the 200‑500 scale, and the certification remains valid for five years, after which renewal through the ANCC is required.

Who Should Take This Exam

Licensed nurse practitioners who have completed or are completing a psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner program and want board certification through the ANCC. It also suits practicing PMHNPs whose certification is due for renewal.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Conduct psychiatric assessments including mental status examinations
  • Formulate differential diagnoses across mental health conditions
  • Develop treatment plans integrating therapy and medication
  • Apply psychopharmacology principles and monitor side effects
  • Use evidence-based practice to guide clinical decisions

How to Prepare

Build a study plan that maps each of the five domains to specific review tasks, such as practicing mental status examination documentation and working through case-based pharmacology questions. Take timed 175-question practice tests to build pacing for the 210-minute session. Review current ANCC practice standards and renewal requirements alongside clinical study.

Domain Study Guidance

Assessment: Study Guidance

The Assessment domain covers gathering psychiatric history, performing mental status examinations, and evaluating risk. Candidates should know how to elicit appearance, behavior, motor activity, speech, mood, affect, thought process, thought content, perception, cognition, insight, and judgment.

  • Mental status examination components
  • Suicide and homicide risk assessment
  • Cultural considerations in evaluation

Diagnosis: Study Guidance

The Diagnosis domain focuses on forming differential diagnoses from clinical data, recognizing symptom clusters, and applying diagnostic criteria. It includes identifying when altered mental status reflects medical rather than primary psychiatric causes.

  • Differential diagnosis formulation
  • Delirium versus primary psychiatric illness
  • Distinguishing neurocognitive and psychotic disorders

Treatment Planning: Study Guidance

The Treatment Planning domain addresses building care plans that combine psychotherapy, pharmacotherapy, and interprofessional coordination. It includes planning for safety, monitoring response, and adjusting interventions effectively across care settings.

  • Integrated psychotherapy and medication planning
  • Interprofessional team collaboration
  • Monitoring treatment response and progression

Psychopharmacology: Study Guidance

The Psychopharmacology domain covers mechanism of action, indication, side effect profiles, and toxicity recognition for psychiatric medications. It includes monitoring extrapyramidal symptoms, tardive dyskinesia, and severe acute dystonic reactions.

  • Antipsychotic side effects and movement disorders
  • Mood stabilizer and antidepressant pharmacology
  • Recognizing toxicity and adverse reactions

Evidence-Based Practice: Study Guidance

The Evidence-Based Practice domain tests the ability to locate, appraise, and apply current research to psychiatric care decisions. It connects clinical guidelines, outcome data, and patient values to the treatment plan.

  • Appraising clinical research
  • Applying clinical practice guidelines
  • Integrating patient values with evidence

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive early with required ID and any permitted items. Budget time across 175 questions in a 210-minute linear format, flagging uncertain items to revisit. Read each question carefully before selecting a response.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the PMHNP-BC exam?

The PMHNP-BC exam contains 175 questions in a linear multiple-choice format. Candidates answer all items within the allotted testing time without skipping sections or returning to earlier items.

What is the passing score for the PMHNP-BC?

The PMHNP-BC uses a scaled score range of 200 to 500. A scaled score of 350 is required to pass the examination, regardless of which form a candidate receives.

How long is the PMHNP-BC exam?

The PMHNP-BC exam allows 210 minutes of testing time. Candidates should pace themselves across 175 questions, leaving room to address flagged items within the linear format.

How much does the PMHNP-BC exam cost?

The PMHNP-BC exam costs $395 for ANA members and $520 for non-members. Pricing is set by the ANCC and applies to each exam attempt at registration.

How long is PMHNP-BC certification valid?

PMHNP-BC certification is valid for 5 years from the date it is awarded. Renewal is required and is administered through the ANCC before the certification end date.

What domains does the PMHNP-BC exam cover?

The PMHNP-BC exam covers five domains: Assessment, Diagnosis, Treatment Planning, Psychopharmacology, and Evidence-Based Practice. Each domain contributes to the overall 175-question linear examination and is weighted to assess core clinical competencies used for advanced psychiatric care.
