What This Exam Validates

The SQF Practitioner Food Safety Certification, administered by SQFI (Safe Quality Food Institute), evaluates professional competencies across 3 distinct parts. Candidates must complete 60 questions within 60 minutes to achieve the required passing score. The test evaluates knowledge across Part 1, Part 2, and Part 3 to confirm candidates reach the 70% passing threshold.
