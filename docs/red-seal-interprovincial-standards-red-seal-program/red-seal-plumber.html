What This Exam Validates

The Red Seal Plumber Exam is formally administered through the Interprovincial Standards Red Seal Program to evaluate required competencies for professional plumbing across Canada. Candidates must complete a set of 125 multiple-choice questions within a strict time limit of 240 minutes to successfully achieve the national passing score of 70% required for full trade certification.

Who Should Take This Exam

Journeypersons and experienced apprentices seeking trade certification. Candidates must have practical work experience across the full scope of plumbing trade activities as determined by their provincial or territorial apprenticeship and certification authority.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Performs common occupational skills
  • Prepares and assembles tube, tubing and pipe
  • Installs and services drainage, waste and vent systems
  • Installs and services water service and distribution
  • Installs fixtures and appliances
  • Services low-pressure steam and hydronic systems
  • Installs specialized systems

How to Prepare

Review the Red Seal Occupational Standard very carefully before your test date. Candidates should use sticky notes for important plumbing procedures and study all national mathematical formulas alongside code book layouts. Check your knowledge effectively using the official self-assessment tool and focus your preparation exclusively on areas with lower confidence levels before finally writing.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of 125 multiple-choice questions. Each question is worth one mark, and candidates have a total writing time of 240 minutes to complete the test booklet and bubble sheet.

What is the passing score?

The passing score for the exam is 70%. Candidates must meet or exceed this percentage to achieve a passing grade and obtain trade certification through their provincial authority.

How long is the exam?

The exam allows for a writing time of 240 minutes. Candidates should pace themselves across the 125 questions to ensure they have enough time to review their bubble sheet.

What should I bring to the exam room?

Candidates must bring required items such as government-issued photo ID and proof of apprenticeship. Check with your provincial or territorial apprenticeship office for specific administrative rules before exam day.
