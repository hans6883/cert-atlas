What This Exam Validates

The Red Seal Carpenter Exam assesses the knowledge and abilities of journeyperson carpenters across Canada. Administered by the Red Seal (Interprovincial Standards Red Seal Program), the exam covers seven major work activities, including framing, concrete work, and interior and exterior finishing. It ensures that tradespeople meet a national standard for construction, renovation, and repair of residential, commercial, and industrial structures. This evaluation confirms that candidates possess the expertise to perform work in diverse construction environments throughout the country, maintaining consistency in trade practices.

Who Should Take This Exam

This exam is for experienced carpenters who have completed their apprenticeship or have equivalent trade experience. It is intended for those seeking to demonstrate their professional competence and mobility as a journeyperson within the Canadian construction industry.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Common occupational skills
  • Planning and layout
  • Concrete work
  • Framing
  • Exterior finishing
  • Interior finishing

How to Prepare

Review the Red Seal Occupational Standard to understand the seven major work activities and their weightings. Focus your study on the common core sub-tasks identified in the standard, as these are the basis for exam questions. Use the provided self-assessment tools to identify knowledge gaps in areas like framing or concrete work. Practice applying mathematical formulas for geometry and trigonometry to solve trade-related problems. Ensure you are familiar with all safety protocols and tool maintenance procedures required for professional carpentry work.

Domain Study Guidance

A – Performs common occupational skills: Study Guidance

This domain covers the use and maintenance of various hand, power, and pneumatic tools, alongside safety procedures and the construction of temporary access structures like scaffolding for safe work environments.

  • Hand, power, and pneumatic tools
  • Personal protective equipment
  • Scaffolding erection

B – Performs planning and layout: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the technical skills required for interpreting project documentation and specifications, as well as performing accurate site layout for various building components to ensure structural integrity and compliance.

  • Project drawings and specifications
  • Site preparation
  • Layout of concrete forms and walls

C – Performs concrete work: Study Guidance

This domain involves the construction of formwork systems and the installation of concrete and cement-based products, including the management of reinforcements and the proper placement and curing of concrete materials.

  • Footing and wall form systems
  • Embedded reinforcements
  • Concrete placement and curing

D – Performs framing: Study Guidance

This domain covers the construction of structural systems, including the assembly of engineered floor systems, dimensional lumber wall framing, and the installation of roof and ceiling framing components for residential and commercial buildings.

  • Engineered floor systems
  • Dimensional lumber wall framing
  • Roof and ceiling framing

E – Performs exterior finishing: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the installation of exterior components, including exterior door and window hardware, roof coverings, and various exterior wall coverings, ensuring that the building envelope is properly sealed and protected.

  • Exterior door and window hardware
  • Roof coverings
  • Exterior wall coverings

F – Performs interior finishing: Study Guidance

This domain covers interior finishing tasks, including the application of wallboard and compound, the installation of suspended ceilings, and the fitting of interior doors and windows to meet aesthetic and functional standards.

  • Wallboard and compound application
  • Suspended ceilings
  • Interior door and window installation

G – Performs renovations: Study Guidance

This domain addresses the specific requirements and technical tasks associated with performing renovations in existing structures, including renovation planning and the structural assessment of buildings to ensure safety during the modification process.

  • Renovation planning
  • Structural assessment for renovations

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive prepared to complete the 100-question exam within the 240-minute time limit. Ensure you have all required identification and follow the instructions provided by your testing center. Manage your time effectively across the seven domains to ensure all questions are addressed.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The Red Seal Carpenter Exam consists of exactly 100 questions. These questions are distributed across seven major work activities, with framing representing the largest portion of the total exam content to ensure coverage of the trade.

What is the passing score?

To successfully pass the Red Seal Carpenter Exam and demonstrate your professional competence as a journeyperson, you must achieve a minimum passing score of 70% on the final assessment.

How long is the exam?

The exam is a 240-minute assessment. You should plan to manage your time carefully throughout the entire session to ensure you have sufficient opportunity to complete all 100 questions accurately.

Where can I find the current certification path?

For information regarding the current certification path or any updates to the trade requirements, check with Red Seal (Interprovincial Standards Red Seal Program) for the current certification path to ensure you have the most accurate and official documentation available.
