What This Exam Validates

The CMPE: Certified Medical Practice Executive certification is offered by MGMA / ACMPE. The test evaluates expertise across six specific management domains, covering operations, financial administration, human resources, risk management, transformative healthcare delivery, and organizational governance to validate professional management standards across a total of 265 questions during a duration of 315 minutes.

Who Should Take This Exam

Medical practice managers, healthcare administrators, and clinical leaders seeking formal recognition of their executive skills in managing clinical practices and healthcare delivery systems with detailed focus.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Operations Management
  • Financial Management
  • Human Resource Management
  • Risk and Compliance Management
  • Transformative Healthcare Delivery
  • Organizational Governance

How to Prepare

Review the six distinct domain areas systematically to build profound mastery. Focus your daily study sessions extensively on financial administration, operations, and compliance requirements. Utilize official MGMA / ACMPE reference materials and practice resources to prepare very carefully for the 265 total questions across the 315 minutes.

Domain Study Guidance

Operations Management: Study Guidance

Covers core operational principles and practices required to manage day-to-day medical facility workflows and administrative efficiency within your organization while supporting overall clinical practice excellence and patient service delivery standards.

  • Facility workflow design
  • Operational efficiency
  • Resource allocation

Financial Management: Study Guidance

Focuses on financial management, budgeting, revenue cycle practices, and monetary oversight within medical practice environments to ensure long-term economic viability, accurate fiscal reporting, and optimal fiscal responsibility across all administrative units.

  • Budget planning
  • Revenue cycle management
  • Financial reporting

Human Resource Management: Study Guidance

Addresses human resource management principles including staff hiring, retention, performance evaluation, and personnel relations to foster a productive workplace culture, maintain compliance with labor standards, and support professional development.

  • Staff recruitment
  • Personnel policies
  • Performance evaluations

Risk and Compliance Management: Study Guidance

Examines risk and compliance management strategies, adherence to federal regulations, and internal auditing practices designed to protect the medical practice from legal liabilities, security breaches, and operational disruptions effectively.

  • Regulatory compliance
  • Internal audits
  • Risk mitigation

Transformative Healthcare Delivery: Study Guidance

Explores transformative healthcare delivery models, modern care delivery trends, and changes in patient care strategies to help executives adapt to evolving industry standards, value-based reimbursement frameworks, and patient-centered innovations.

  • Care delivery models
  • Healthcare trends
  • Patient care strategies

Organizational Governance: Study Guidance

Reviews organizational governance structures, leadership ethics, board relations, and institutional policy creation to ensure transparent decision-making, ethical leadership, and alignment with the strategic vision of the medical practice organization.

  • Governance structures
  • Leadership ethics
  • Board relations

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive early at your assigned testing center and manage your time effectively across the 315 minutes allocated for the 265 questions during the test session.
