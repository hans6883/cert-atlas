Historical 100-105 Scope

The Cisco 100-105 certification exam covers five core networking domains, including Network Fundamentals, LAN Switching Fundamentals, Routing Fundamentals, Infrastructure Services, and Infrastructure Maintenance. Administered directly by Cisco, this examination presents a total of 74 multiple choice questions during a duration of 90 minutes to test candidate knowledge across all defined domains.

Who 100-105 Was For

Networking professionals and technicians seeking to validate their skills in network fundamentals, routing, and switching. Candidates should possess hands-on experience implementing core Cisco infrastructure services and maintenance procedures.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Network Fundamentals
  • LAN Switching Fundamentals
  • Routing Fundamentals
  • Infrastructure Services
  • Infrastructure Maintenance
