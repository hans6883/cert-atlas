What This Exam Validates

The ABP Pediatric Cardiology certification test spans 21 distinct domains administered by the American Board of Pediatrics. Candidates must demonstrate proficiency across numerous clinical subjects, ranging from normal cardiovascular structure and basic pharmacology to complex congenital heart defects, arrhythmias, single-ventricle lesions, intensive care management, and adult congenital heart disease.

Who Should Take This Exam

Pediatric cardiology fellows and practicing physicians seeking formal board certification from the American Board of Pediatrics. Candidates must demonstrate extensive clinical competence across all 21 specific cardiovascular domains outlined in the official exam guide.
