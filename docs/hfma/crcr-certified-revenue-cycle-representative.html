What This Exam Validates

The HFMA Certified Revenue Cycle Representative (CRCR) program provides a professional designation for those managing healthcare revenue cycles. The certification covers four distinct units: patient-centric revenue cycles, pre-service financial care, point-of-service financial care, and post-service financial care. This program serves as a benchmark for revenue cycle knowledge and expertise within the healthcare industry, helping professionals demonstrate their proficiency in managing financial outcomes for their organizations. Candidates gain a deep understanding of the entire cycle, ensuring they are prepared to handle the complexities of modern healthcare financial management and patient interactions effectively.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is for revenue cycle staff and leadership seeking to demonstrate their knowledge of healthcare financial processes. It is suitable for those working in patient access, billing, or financial counseling who want to advance their professional development within the healthcare revenue cycle.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Patient-centric revenue cycle management
  • Pre-service financial care processes
  • Point-of-service financial care
  • Post-service financial care and resolution
  • Healthcare compliance and HIPAA regulations
  • Medicare and value-based payment models

How to Prepare

Review the official online study materials provided by HFMA, as the assessment questions tie directly to this content. Use the key concepts guide to capture important ideas while working through the four units of the online program. Since the assessment covers content from all four units, ensure you have a clear understanding of each area before sitting for the 90-minute exam. Consistent study of the provided materials will help you master the concepts required to pass the 75 questions presented on the final assessment.

Domain Study Guidance

Patient Centric Revenue Cycle: Study Guidance

This specific domain covers the overarching revenue cycle, patient experience principles, and compliance regulations including HIPAA guidelines and specific Medicare requirements for healthcare professionals working in the industry today.

  • Revenue cycle overview and financial reporting
  • HIPAA and Medicare compliance regulations

Pre-Service Financial Care: Study Guidance

This specific domain focuses on pre-service operational activities, including patient scheduling workflows, detailed insurance verification procedures, and effective financial communication strategies used to assist patients before their scheduled medical services.

  • Scheduling and pre-registration processes
  • Insurance verification and health plan types

Point of Service Financial Care: Study Guidance

This specific domain addresses point-of-service financial care elements, which include patient arrival and intake procedures, medical coding practices, and billing rules used during the actual delivery of healthcare services.

  • Patient arrival and intake procedures
  • Revenue capture and coding

Post Service Financial Care: Study Guidance

This specific domain covers post-service financial care operations, including cash posting, electronic remittance advice, denied claims management, and compliance with patient debt regulations following the completion of medical care services.

  • Cash posting and electronic remittance
  • Denied claim and non-paid processing

Exam-Day Guidance

The exam consists of 75 questions to be completed in 90 minutes. Ensure you have reviewed all four units of the online material, as the assessment covers content from each section to verify your professional knowledge.
