What This Exam Validates

The CPRP Certified Park and Recreation Professional examination thoroughly assesses the essential knowledge and critical competencies required for modern professionals in the field. Certified by the National Recreation and Park Association and the National Certification Board, it covers communication, finance, human resources, operations, and programming strategies to evaluate candidates effectively and comprehensively.

Who Should Take This Exam

The examination is structured for professionals and practitioners who have practical experience working within parks and recreation, delivering public services, managing resources, and leading personnel.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Communication
  • Finance
  • Human Resources
  • Operations
  • Programming

How to Prepare

Plan your study schedule well in advance of your test date. Find a quiet place to study and concentrate your efforts on accredited parks and recreation baccalaureate textbooks, the Official Study Guide, practice examinations, and the CPRP Basic Learning Pack available through the NRPA Store.

Domain Study Guidance

Communication: Study Guidance

This domain covers communication tasks and related competencies, accounting for 25.0 percent of the examination content to ensure candidates can effectively engage with stakeholders, the public, and colleagues.

  • Professional communication methods
  • Public relations practices

Finance: Study Guidance

This domain covers finance tasks and related competencies, accounting for 10.0 percent of the examination content to ensure candidates can effectively handle budgeting, resource management, and financial planning for agencies.

  • Budgeting and financial resource management
  • Revenue sources

Human Resources: Study Guidance

This domain covers human resources tasks and related competencies, accounting for 10.0 percent of the examination content to ensure candidates can effectively manage personnel policies, staff leadership, and team development.

  • Personnel policies
  • Staff management and leadership

Operations: Study Guidance

This domain covers operations tasks and related competencies, accounting for 30.0 percent of the examination content to ensure candidates can properly manage facility maintenance, safety protocols, and risk management.

  • Facility maintenance
  • Safety and risk management

Programming: Study Guidance

This domain covers programming tasks and related competencies, accounting for 25.0 percent of the examination content to ensure candidates can effectively plan, execute, evaluate, and deliver recreation services to communities.

  • Program planning and evaluation
  • Recreation service delivery

Exam-Day Guidance

Report to the assessment center in advance of your scheduled testing time with the required identification credentials. Arriving late may result in the complete denial of admission to the testing session.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The examination contains exactly 150 questions presented one at a time on a computer screen. Candidates have 180 minutes to complete the test successfully at the designated testing center facility.

What is the passing score?

The passing score reflects the amount of knowledge determined appropriate by a committee of experts using a strict criterion-referenced standard setting procedure and expert judgment.

How long is the exam?

Candidates are allotted a total of 180 minutes to complete the computer-delivered examination at a designated official PSI Test Center location during their scheduled session time slot.

How do I schedule my exam?

After receiving an official email confirmation of eligibility from the NRPA Certification Division containing scheduling directions, candidates can schedule their examination online through the PSI scheduling portal.
