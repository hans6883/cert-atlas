What This Exam Validates

The Aquatic Facility Operator certification examination comprehensively details swimming pool and spa management, mechanical concepts, risk management, and overall aquatic facility operations. Certified by the National Recreation and Park Association, it carefully assesses the technical knowledge required for managing safe aquatic facilities across the industry for professionals seeking certification to demonstrate competence.

Who Should Take This Exam

Designed for owners, operators, and managers of municipal pools, school facilities, water parks, resort pools, and community associations who require verified knowledge in aquatic safety and facility management.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Swimming Pool and Spa Management
  • Mechanical Concepts and Systems
  • Risk Management
  • Aquatic Facility Operations

How to Prepare

Carefully review the official Candidate Handbook and study guide materials provided by the organization during your preparation phase. Practice calculations regarding pool volume, turnover rates, and chemical dosages using the permitted slide rule and manual during your structured preparation to ensure complete success.

Domain Study Guidance

Swimming Pool and Spa Management: Study Guidance

This domain covers testing methods, water treatment, pool water balance, acid-base chemistry, and sanitation and oxidation processes necessary for maintaining safe pool water conditions during daily operations throughout the year.

  • Testing Methods and Calculations
  • Pool Water Treatment
  • Pool Water Balance

Mechanical Concepts and Systems: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on facility environments, water collection, circulation, mechanical devices such as pumps and heaters, filtration systems, and preventative maintenance procedures required for proper system functioning across all managed aquatic properties.

  • Facility Environment
  • Water Collection and Circulation
  • Mechanical Devices

Risk Management: Study Guidance

This domain addresses risk management principles, prevention of recreational water illness and injury, record keeping, signage, and national standards including ADA and the Model Aquatic Health Code to ensure high safety levels.

  • Risk Management
  • National Standards and Organizations

Aquatic Facility Operations: Study Guidance

This domain examines daily routine operations, air quality, air circulation, weatherizing, facility maintenance, renovation, and management of various aquatic facility types and alternate spas found across the industry.

  • Daily/Routine Operations
  • Air Quality & Air Circulation
  • Weatherizing

Exam-Day Guidance

Bring a government-issued photo ID, an approved handheld calculator, the manual, and the slide rule. Cell phones and electronic devices are strictly prohibited during testing.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The certification examination consists of exactly 50 multiple-choice questions designed to evaluate your knowledge across swimming pool management, mechanical systems, risk management, and facility operations.

What is the passing score?

Candidates must achieve a passing score of 78%, which requires successfully answering at least 39 of the 50 questions correctly on the official test administered by NRPA.

How long is the exam?

Candidates are given a total duration of 180 minutes to complete the open-book examination under the direct supervision of an approved professional proctor at the testing site.

What materials are permitted during the exam?

You are fully permitted to use the official Aquatic Facility Operator Manual, the slide rule that comes packaged with the manual, a handheld calculator, and scratch paper during the test.
