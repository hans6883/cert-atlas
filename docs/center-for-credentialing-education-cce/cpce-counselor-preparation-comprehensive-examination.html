What This Exam Validates

The Counselor Preparation Comprehensive Examination is administered by the Center for Credentialing & Education (CCE). It evaluates student knowledge across eight core counseling areas. The exam contains exactly 160 multiple-choice questions divided across all domains, each carrying a 12.5 weight percent. Programs use results to assess curriculum effectiveness and compare student data to national averages for academic standards.

Who Should Take This Exam

Candidates are graduate-level students enrolled in counseling programs accredited by the Council for Accreditation of Counseling and Related Educational Programs or institutionally accredited colleges and universities who need to complete their program testing requirements.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Professional counseling orientation and ethical practice
  • Social and cultural diversity
  • Human growth and development
  • Career development
  • Counseling and helping relationships
  • Group counseling and group work
  • Assessment and testing
  • Research and program evaluation

How to Prepare

Review course materials covering all eight CACREP subject areas in detail. Familiarize yourself with the exact 160-item format and practice answering questions across all domains carefully. Check registration procedures with your university program coordinator and Pearson VUE to complete the process successfully within your assigned test authorization window.

Domain Study Guidance

Professional Counseling Orientation and Ethical Practice: Study Guidance

This specific domain reviews professional counseling orientation and ethical practice standards that all practicing clinicians must understand during their clinical training experiences, accounting for a 12.5 weight percent.

  • Counseling ethics and professional standards
  • Orientation and legal guidelines

Social and Cultural Diversity: Study Guidance

This section covers social and cultural diversity factors in counseling settings, ensuring candidates can competently address multicultural issues and diverse populations within various therapeutic environments and modern community structures.

  • Multicultural counseling competencies
  • Diversity and social systems

Human Growth and Development: Study Guidance

Candidates examine human growth and development across the entire lifespan, evaluating psychological, emotional, and physical milestones that influence client behaviors from early childhood through late adulthood and older age categories.

  • Developmental theories
  • Lifespan growth milestones

Career Development: Study Guidance

This crucial examination area addresses career development theories and decision-making models, assisting candidates to guide clients successfully through occupational exploration, lifestyle choices, and educational planning throughout different life stages.

  • Career development theories
  • Occupational information and planning

Counseling and Helping Relationships: Study Guidance

This designated section evaluates counseling and helping relationships theories and skills, building the basic competencies required to establish strong therapeutic alliances, utilize effective intervention techniques, and facilitate positive client change processes.

  • Helping relationship theories
  • Counseling techniques and processes

Group Counseling and Group Work: Study Guidance

This domain covers group counseling and group work dynamics and leadership, instructing students how to manage group development stages, therapeutic factors, and various leadership styles in clinical or institutional settings.

  • Group dynamics and development
  • Group leadership styles

Assessment and Testing: Study Guidance

This specific domain examines assessment and testing principles, instruments, and evaluation methods, assisting candidates to understand psychometric properties so they can select, administer, and interpret standardized tests appropriately.

  • Psychometric properties
  • Test selection and interpretation

Research and Program Evaluation: Study Guidance

Addresses research and program evaluation methodologies and statistics, providing students with the analytical skills needed to review scientific literature, understand quantitative and qualitative designs, and assess counseling program outcomes effectively.

  • Research design methods
  • Program evaluation practices

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive at the testing center with two forms of proper identification. Follow all security rules, return all writing materials at the end of testing, and manage your time effectively during the assessment session.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The comprehensive examination consists of exactly 160 multiple-choice questions divided across 8 distinct content areas, ensuring a balanced evaluation of all core counseling subjects required by participating academic programs.

What is the passing score?

Rather than utilizing a fixed national passing grade or uniform cutoff score across all institutions, the local university or academic program determines the minimum cut or passing score required for their specific students.

How long is the exam?

Candidates should check with their local testing coordinators and official guidelines regarding the exact time limits and scheduling parameters associated with their specific administration window of the assessment.

Who administers the exam?

The Center for Credentialing & Education offers this assessment, utilizing Pearson VUE to deliver computer-based testing and secure online administration formats for candidates completing their academic requirements worldwide.
