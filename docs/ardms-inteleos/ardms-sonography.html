What This Exam Validates

The ARDMS Diagnostic Medical Sonography exam is a certification assessment provided by ARDMS (Inteleos). It covers five key domains: performing ultrasound examinations (23%), managing transducers (7%), optimizing images (26%), applying Doppler concepts (34%), and ensuring clinical safety and quality assurance (10%). This exam validates the core knowledge and skills required for sonography professionals to perform diagnostic procedures safely in a clinical setting.

Who Should Take This Exam

This exam is designed for sonography professionals who perform ultrasound examinations in clinical settings. Candidates should have practical experience in sonography and a firm grasp of the principles and instrumentation required to maintain high standards of patient care and diagnostic accuracy.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Performing ultrasound examinations
  • Managing ultrasound transducers
  • Optimizing sonographic images
  • Applying Doppler concepts
  • Providing clinical safety and quality assurance

How to Prepare

Review the official content outline to understand the weighting of each domain. Focus your study on the 34% weight assigned to Doppler concepts and the 26% weight for image optimization. Use the provided task inventory to identify areas for improvement. Practice applying sonographic principles to clinical scenarios and ensure you are familiar with safety protocols and quality assurance testing procedures to succeed.

Domain Study Guidance

Perform Ultrasound Examinations: Study Guidance

This domain covers the practical aspects of performing ultrasound examinations, including patient care, communication, and the application of sonographic techniques to ensure that diagnostic procedures are conducted efficiently and safely within the clinical environment.

  • Patient care and communication skills
  • Interaction of sound and matter
  • Imaging protocols and artifacts

Manage Ultrasound Transducers: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the selection and management of ultrasound transducers used during diagnostic procedures, ensuring that the correct equipment is utilized to achieve the best possible imaging results for various clinical patient scenarios.

  • Transducer selection and frequency adjustment
  • Transducer components and integrity

Optimize Sonographic Images: Study Guidance

This domain involves the technical optimization of sonographic images to ensure diagnostic quality, requiring a deep understanding of resolution, gain settings, and advanced imaging modes to produce clear and accurate diagnostic data for interpretation.

  • Axial, lateral, and temporal resolution
  • Brightness and gain settings
  • Harmonic and spatial compounding imaging

Apply Doppler Concepts: Study Guidance

This domain addresses the application of Doppler concepts to evaluate blood flow and hemodynamic principles, which is vital for assessing vascular health and identifying potential abnormalities during routine diagnostic ultrasound examinations in clinical practice.

  • Doppler shift and angle concepts
  • Spectral Doppler waveforms
  • Aliasing and color Doppler mapping

Provide Clinical Safety & Quality Assurance: Study Guidance

This domain covers clinical safety, infection control, and quality assurance protocols for ultrasound equipment, ensuring that all diagnostic procedures are performed in a safe environment while maintaining the highest standards of equipment performance and accuracy.

  • Infection control protocols
  • Quality assurance testing with phantoms
  • Statistical parameter evaluation

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive prepared to complete 110 questions within the 120-minute time limit. Manage your time effectively across the five domains, keeping in mind the specific weightings for each section to ensure you address the most heavily weighted topics during your session.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The ARDMS Diagnostic Medical Sonography exam consists of 110 questions in total, which are designed to test your baseline knowledge of sonographic principles and instrumentation throughout the entire testing session.

What is the passing score?

The passing score for the examination is 555 out of a total of 700 points, and candidates must meet this threshold to successfully earn their professional certification through the ARDMS.

How long is the exam?

The duration of the examination is 120 minutes, providing candidates with sufficient time to carefully read and answer all questions while maintaining focus on the core sonography principles being tested.

Where can I find the current certification path?

For information regarding the current certification path and any updates to the exam requirements, please check with ARDMS (Inteleos) to ensure you have the most accurate and up-to-date guidance available.
