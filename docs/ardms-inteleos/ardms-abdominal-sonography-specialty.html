What This Exam Validates

The ARDMS Abdominal Sonography Specialty certification is an evaluation covering standard clinical concepts including normal anatomy, perfusion, function, pathology, vascular abnormalities, trauma, postoperative anatomy, abdominal physics, and clinical care. It is officially certified by ARDMS (Inteleos) and is evaluated through an official 180-minute examination consisting of 165 questions designed to validate professional sonography competency.

Who Should Take This Exam

Sonographers seeking professional credentialing in abdominal sonography who regularly perform diagnostic examinations in clinical settings and require formal validation of their diagnostic skills through ARDMS (Inteleos).

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Assess physical characteristics of anatomic structures
  • Assess perfusion and function of anatomic structures
  • Assess anatomic structures for pathology
  • Assess anatomic structures for vascular abnormalities
  • Assess anatomic structures for trauma-related abnormalities
  • Apply concepts of equipment/image optimization
  • Incorporate clinical data with performed study

How to Prepare

Review the complete test content outline across all four distinct domains. Focus heavily on pathology, vascular abnormalities, trauma, and postoperative anatomy, which accounts for forty-two percent of the overall exam content. Practice evaluating clinical data and applying equipment optimization concepts diligently before taking the 165 questions.

Domain Study Guidance

Anatomy, Perfusion, and Function: Study Guidance

This specific domain covers normal anatomy, anatomic variants, and congenital anomalies for major structures like the biliary system, liver, and pancreas while carefully evaluating blood flow patterns and perfusion characteristics during scans.

  • Assess physical characteristics of anatomic structures
  • Assess perfusion and function of anatomic structures

Pathology, Vascular Abnormalities, Trauma, and Postoperative Anatomy: Study Guidance

This section focuses intensely on identifying pathology, vascular abnormalities, trauma-related changes, and postoperative anatomy including transplanted organs across various abdominal structures and bodily systems during examinations and clinical evaluations.

  • Assess anatomic structures for pathology
  • Assess anatomic structures for vascular abnormalities
  • Assess anatomic structures for trauma-related abnormalities
  • Assess aspects related to postoperative anatomy

Abdominal Physics: Study Guidance

Candidates study how to apply equipment and image optimization techniques, precise transducer selection, appropriate Doppler settings, and imaging artifacts to gray-scale and Doppler ultrasound imaging applications during diagnostic procedures.

  • Apply concepts of equipment/image optimization
  • Apply concepts of imaging artifacts

Clinical Care, Practice, and Quality Assurance: Study Guidance

This area involves incorporating clinical data and standards, maintaining effective patient communication, obtaining accurate diagnostic measurements, and assisting or supporting interventional procedures and ultrasound guidance during daily clinical practice.

  • Incorporate clinical data with performed study
  • Incorporate clinical standard/guidelines with performed study
  • Obtain accurate measurements
  • Assist/support during procedures

Exam-Day Guidance

Manage your testing time effectively throughout the entire 180-minute duration to successfully complete all 165 questions while carefully analyzing complex clinical scenarios and sonographic findings.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The official certification examination consists of a total of 165 questions covering four distinct content domains related to abdominal sonography practice, physics, and patient care standards.

What is the passing score?

The passing score required to successfully achieve the Abdominal Sonography Specialty certification credential is officially established at exactly 555 out of 700 possible total points.

How long is the exam?

The specialty exam is administered with a total time duration of 180 minutes to ensure all candidates have adequate time to complete all of the questions.

Who certifies the abdominal sonography specialty?

The professional certification is offered and administered by ARDMS (Inteleos), which provides valid and reliable examinations designed specifically for qualified ultrasound and sonography professionals worldwide.
