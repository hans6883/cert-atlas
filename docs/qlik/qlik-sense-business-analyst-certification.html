What This Exam Validates

The Qlik Sense Business Analyst Certification is a formal evaluation designed to thoroughly test your ability to correctly identify business requirements, design functional applications, prepare and load data, and develop engaging applications. Certified by Qlik, this platform-neutral examination covers practical content applicable to both client-managed environments and Qlik Cloud deployments.

Who Should Take This Exam

Business analysts and developers with practical experience building production-quality applications in Qlik Sense. Candidates should know how to create dimensions, measures, master library items, and understand user experience design best practices.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Identify business requirements and capabilities
  • Design functional applications and visualizations
  • Prepare, load, and manage data models
  • Develop and refine complete applications

How to Prepare

Review all relevant topics under creating apps and visualizations within the online help documentation. Practice building complete applications, constructing data models using Data Manager, and managing master items effectively. Check with Qlik for the official learning path and use the available preparation questions to accurately gauge your readiness.

Domain Study Guidance

Identify Requirements: Study Guidance

This domain focuses heavily on identifying product capabilities, recommending key performance indicators along with dimensions and measures, discussing user needs, and carefully interpreting results generated by the Qlik Associative Engine during your analysis.

  • Determine Qlik Sense product capabilities
  • Recommend KPIs, dimensions, and measures
  • Interpret results from Qlik Associations

Design Applications: Study Guidance

This domain covers determining appropriate visualization layouts and visual characteristics, evolving application designs over time, and aligning your overall creation closely with the specific consumption needs of business users.

  • Determine visualization layout and characteristics
  • Evolve and enhance application design

Prepare and Load Data: Study Guidance

This domain involves using the Data Manager tool to create basic data models, enhancing existing data sets through proper transformation, and verifying the integrity of your data models using the built-in data model viewer.

  • Use Data Manager to create a basic data model
  • Enhance and augment the data model
  • Verify the data model using the data model viewer

Develop Applications: Study Guidance

This domain centers entirely on building, refining, and finalizing applications using various visualization bundles, advanced authoring features, custom variables, and tailored styling options to deliver a polished end product.

  • Build an application
  • Refine and finalize an application

Exam-Day Guidance

Plan for 120 minutes to answer 50 questions. Answer questions you know right away first, flag remaining items for review, and ensure you select the exact number of required answers for multi-select items.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The Qlik Sense Business Analyst Certification exam consists of exactly 50 multiple-choice questions that you must complete within the allotted time frame provided by the testing administrator.

What is the passing score?

The passing score for the Qlik Sense Business Analyst Certification exam is 62%. Please note that this passing percentage may be adjusted periodically to maintain a consistent global standard across test takers.

How long is the exam?

The examination duration is 120 minutes in total. Candidates should pace themselves carefully across all 50 questions and utilize the review feature for any flagged items before submitting their final answers.

Is the exam platform-neutral?

Yes, the examination is completely platform-neutral, meaning the tested content applies equally to both traditional client-managed deployments and modern Qlik Cloud environments without any distinction in the tested objectives.
