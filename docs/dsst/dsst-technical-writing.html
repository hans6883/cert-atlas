What This Exam Validates

The DSST Technical Writing exam certifies competency in professional communication by testing various aspects of modern workplace documentation. Administered by DSST, the computer-based multiple-choice exam features 100 questions and a duration of 120 minutes. It evaluates proficiency in workplace communication, document structure, writing mechanics, research, and design methodologies for professional environments. This assessment ensures that candidates possess the necessary skills to create effective, clear, and accurate technical documents suitable for diverse business and industrial settings.

Who Should Take This Exam

Candidates seeking college credit for professional writing skills. This exam suits students and professionals who prepare technical documents, manage workplace correspondence, and edit business reports to ensure clarity and accuracy in professional settings.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Process management
  • Audience analysis
  • Research methods
  • Document design
  • Common document types
  • Editing techniques

How to Prepare

Review core writing principles, document design standards, and editing rules before your test date. Practice analyzing target audiences, conducting research, and structuring various business documents effectively. Check with DSST for the current certification path and any updates to the testing requirements to ensure your study plan remains aligned with the latest exam content. Consistent practice with professional writing samples will help you master the necessary communication standards required for success.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of exactly 100 multiple-choice questions administered in a secure, computer-based format to test your professional communication skills and technical writing knowledge effectively.

What is the passing score?

The official passing score required to successfully pass this certification exam is set at 400 points, which candidates must achieve to earn their college credit.

How long is the exam?

Test takers are given a total duration of 120 minutes to complete all questions included in the evaluation, so careful time management is very important.

How much does the exam cost?

The exam price is $85 for regular civilian candidates, but it is completely free for eligible military personnel who utilize the available DANTES funding options.
