What This Exam Validates

The Principles of Advanced English Composition exam is certified by DSST to rigorously evaluate knowledge across five diverse and standard domains, including Rhetoric, Writing Process, Research and Documentation, Style, and Grammar and Usage. Candidates encounter multiple-choice questions on this computer-based test designed to accurately measure college-level proficiency.

Who Should Take This Exam

Students seeking college credit for advanced English composition can take this test. It suits individuals with strong writing skills, research abilities, and a firm grasp of grammar and usage rules.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Rhetorical analysis
  • Writing process steps
  • Research and documentation
  • Style guidelines
  • Grammar and usage

How to Prepare

Review academic writing resources, style guides for citation formats like MLA and APA, and strict grammar rules thoroughly before taking the test. Candidates should carefully practice drafting, organizing documents, and refining arguments to ensure they are fully prepared for every domain covered on the test.

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive at the testing center completely prepared for a 120-minute computer-based exam consisting of 100 multiple-choice questions while managing your time carefully across all questions.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of a total of 100 multiple-choice questions administered via a computer-based format, giving candidates ample opportunity to demonstrate their composition knowledge during the test.

What is the passing score?

The official passing score required for this certification exam is set at 400 points, ensuring that successful candidates display adequate mastery of advanced writing principles and composition rules.

How long is the exam?

The exam has a strict duration of 120 minutes, requiring test-takers to pace themselves appropriately while answering all questions within the designated testing window provided at the center.

How much does the exam cost?

The exam price is $85 for civilian test-takers, while eligible military candidates can take the test for free via DANTES. Check with DSST for more details.
