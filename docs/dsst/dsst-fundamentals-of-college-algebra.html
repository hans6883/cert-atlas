What This Exam Validates

The DSST Fundamentals of College Algebra computer-based assessment consists of 100 multiple-choice questions aimed at measuring a candidate's grasp of introductory algebraic concepts. Candidates are given a total duration of 120 minutes to complete the test, which covers real numbers, equations and inequalities, functions, polynomial functions, and exponentials and logarithms.

Who Should Take This Exam

Civilians and eligible military personnel seeking college credit can take this exam. It suits students who have mastered high school algebra concepts and want to validate their knowledge for undergraduate programs.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Real numbers
  • Equations & inequalities
  • Functions
  • Polynomial functions
  • Exponentials & logarithms

How to Prepare

To prepare effectively for the assessment, candidates should review core algebra concepts across all five content domains. Practice solving mathematical equations, manipulating polynomials, and working with logarithmic functions regularly. Utilize official study materials and practice tests to build confidence for answering the 100 multiple-choice questions before entering the testing center.

Domain Study Guidance

Real Numbers: Study Guidance

This domain covers standard real numbers and basic algebra principles, focusing heavily on standard mathematical operations and number properties that serve as the base for all subsequent advanced algebraic topics encountered throughout the entire assessment.

  • Real numbers
  • Operations

Equations & Inequalities: Study Guidance

Focuses extensively on solving diverse mathematical equations and various inequalities, requiring candidates to demonstrate strong problem-solving skills when manipulating algebraic expressions to find unknown values and correctly interpret mathematical relationships.

  • Equations
  • Inequalities

Functions: Study Guidance

Explores function definitions, characteristics, and evaluations, ensuring that test-takers understand how to analyze graphical representations, determine domain and range limitations, and effectively perform various algebraic operations involving standard mathematical functions.

  • Functions
  • Evaluations

Polynomial Functions: Study Guidance

Deals deeply with polynomial functions, their graphical behaviors, factoring techniques, and complex arithmetic operations, challenging candidates to accurately interpret polynomial roots, degrees, and behaviors across a wide variety of standard mathematical problems.

  • Polynomial functions
  • Graphs

Exponentials & Logarithms: Study Guidance

Covers exponential rules and advanced logarithmic calculations, requiring students to skillfully transform exponential equations into logarithmic forms and solve complex logarithmic equations typically encountered in higher-level college mathematics coursework.

  • Exponentials
  • Logarithms

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive at the testing center ready for a 120-minute computer-based session. Manage your time carefully across the 100 questions to ensure you complete all sections before time expires.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of 100 multiple-choice questions delivered via a computer-based format. You must carefully answer these questions within the strict 120-minute time limit provided at the test center.

What is the passing score?

The official passing score for the exam is 400. You need to meet or exceed this established numerical score in order to successfully earn college credit for your performance.

How long is the exam?

The total duration for this computer-based assessment is 120 minutes. Candidates are required to finish answering all 100 multiple-choice questions within this fixed time window.

How much does the exam cost?

The standard exam price is $85 for civilian test-takers. However, the test is free for eligible military personnel through DANTES funding programs. Always check with DSST for current institutional policies.
