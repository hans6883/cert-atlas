What This Exam Validates

The DSST Organizational Behavior exam assesses knowledge of workplace behavior, managed by DSST. It features a total of 100 multiple-choice questions administered in a convenient computer-based format. Candidates must achieve a passing score of 400 to earn academic college credit recommendations, making targeted preparation important for success on test day. The standard registration fee is set at $85 for civilians, while eligible military personnel can take the assessment for free through DANTES funding. You will have a total duration of 120 minutes to complete all items.

Who Should Take This Exam

Students seeking college credit for organizational behavior coursework, military personnel utilizing DANTES benefits, and adult learners aiming to demonstrate professional management competencies through standardized testing.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Individual Behavior
  • Group Dynamics
  • Organizational Structure
  • Motivation
  • Leadership
  • Culture

How to Prepare

Review core textbooks and academic resources aligned with organizational psychology and management theory. Focus your study efforts extensively on employee motivation theories, leadership styles, group behaviors, and structural frameworks. Take multiple practice assessments to build accurate pacing and confidence for the computer-based testing format within the designated duration.

Domain Study Guidance

Individual Behavior: Study Guidance

This domain carefully covers individual behavior within the workplace, focusing specifically on psychological factors, perception mechanisms, learning processes, and various employee attitudes that directly influence overall professional performance outcomes on the test.

  • Personality traits and values
  • Perception and individual decision making
  • Work attitudes and job satisfaction

Group Dynamics: Study Guidance

This domain carefully examines group dynamics, team development stages, communication patterns, conflict resolution methods, and the interactive processes that significantly shape team productivity and collaboration in corporate environments.

  • Group roles and norms
  • Team development stages
  • Conflict management strategies

Organizational Structure: Study Guidance

This domain focuses heavily on organizational structure, design elements, bureaucracy, and how various structural frameworks directly impact operational efficiency, communication channels, and daily workflow across enterprises during evaluations.

  • Span of control and centralization
  • Departmentalization types
  • Mechanistic and organic designs

Motivation: Study Guidance

This domain explores core motivation theories and drive mechanisms that heavily influence worker effort levels, goal-setting behaviors, and overall performance outcomes within various organizational settings covered during the evaluation process.

  • Content and process theories of motivation
  • Goal-setting application
  • Reward systems

Leadership: Study Guidance

This domain addresses specific leadership theories, behavioral approaches, power dynamics, and political strategies utilized by managers to effectively guide teams toward achieving strategic business goals within modern workplace environments.

  • Trait and behavioral leadership models
  • Power and influence tactics
  • Organizational politics

Culture: Study Guidance

This domain investigates organizational culture, shared core values, socialization processes, and how cultural environments profoundly affect employee behavior, retention, and long-term institutional success across various professional corporate settings today.

  • Culture creation and sustenance
  • Employee socialization
  • Managing cultural change

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive at the computer-based testing center with proper identification. Manage your time across the multiple-choice questions so that all items receive a response before the duration expires.
