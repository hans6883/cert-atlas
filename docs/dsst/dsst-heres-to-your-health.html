Historical Here's to Your Health Scope

The Here's to Your Health exam, certified by DSST, covered wellness, nutrition, fitness, mental health, disease, consumer health, and environmental health. This exam is now retired and has been replaced by the Health & Human Development exam. Candidates seeking certification in these health-related areas should focus their efforts on the current successor, which serves as the direct path forward for those pursuing this credential in the field of health studies. The transition ensures that all testing remains aligned with current academic standards and modern health science practices for all students.

Who Here's to Your Health Was For

This exam was intended for students and professionals seeking to demonstrate knowledge in health and wellness. As the exam is retired, individuals should now pursue the Health & Human Development exam to validate their expertise in these subject areas.

How to Reuse Your Preparation

Focus your preparation on the Health & Human Development exam. Much of the content regarding wellness, nutrition, and fitness transfers from the retired exam. You must learn new material specific to the current curriculum requirements. Use official DSST resources to align your study plan with the successor exam's objectives. Do not prepare for the retired exam, as it is no longer available for testing. Ensure you review all updated materials to succeed in your academic goals.

Disease: Historical Scope

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The official exam consists of exactly 100 multiple-choice questions. Candidates should be prepared to answer all of these items within the allotted time frame to ensure they have the best opportunity to demonstrate their knowledge and achieve a passing result.

What is the passing score?

The required passing score for the exam is 400. This score represents the minimum level of proficiency needed to demonstrate sufficient understanding of the subject matter and to earn the associated credit for the health and human development course.

How long is the exam?

The total duration for the exam is 120 minutes. This time limit is designed to allow candidates to carefully read and respond to all questions, ensuring that they can complete the assessment within the standard testing period provided by the center.

What is the cost of the exam?

The exam price is $85 for civilians. It is free for eligible military personnel via DANTES. This fee structure is standard for DSST exams, and candidates should verify their eligibility status before scheduling their testing appointment at an authorized center.
