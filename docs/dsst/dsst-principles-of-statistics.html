What This Exam Validates

The Principles of Statistics exam, certified by DSST, evaluates knowledge across six distinct domains: Descriptive Statistics, Probability, Distributions, Hypothesis Testing, Regression, and ANOVA. This computer-based, multiple-choice exam consists of 100 questions. It provides an opportunity for students to earn college credit by demonstrating proficiency in statistical concepts and their practical applications, rather than focusing on advanced mathematical theory or complex derivations.

Who Should Take This Exam

This exam is intended for students seeking college credit for an introductory statistics course. It is suitable for those majoring in fields other than mathematics or engineering who possess knowledge of intermediate algebra and wish to demonstrate their grasp of statistical application.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Analyzing descriptive statistics
  • Calculating probability
  • Interpreting data distributions
  • Performing hypothesis testing
  • Applying regression models
  • Executing ANOVA analysis

How to Prepare

Review the six domains of the exam to ensure coverage of all required topics. Utilize textbooks that focus on statistical application over abstract theory. Practice with problems involving real-world scenarios to build comfort with the material. Since the exam is computer-based, familiarize yourself with the types of questions presented. Check with DSST for additional resources and ensure you understand the scope of the 100 questions included in the assessment.

Domain Study Guidance

Descriptive Statistics: Study Guidance

This domain covers the organization and summarization of data sets to describe their main features, including the use of various graphical and numerical methods to represent data accurately for analysis and interpretation in statistical studies.

  • Data sampling methods
  • Measures of central tendency
  • Data visualization

Probability: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the mathematical study of likelihood and the rules governing random events, providing the framework for understanding how to calculate probabilities and analyze the behavior of random variables in various contexts.

  • Probability rules
  • Random variables

Distributions: Study Guidance

This domain addresses how data values are spread or clustered across a range of outcomes, focusing on the properties of the normal distribution and the application of the Central Limit Theorem in statistical inference processes.

  • Normal distribution
  • Central Limit Theorem

Hypothesis Testing: Study Guidance

This domain involves making inferences about populations based on sample data through statistical testing, covering the calculation of confidence intervals and the execution of one-sample and two-sample testing to draw valid conclusions from data.

  • Confidence intervals
  • One-sample testing
  • Two-sample testing

Regression: Study Guidance

This domain covers the relationship between variables and the use of models to predict outcomes, emphasizing the application of linear regression techniques and correlation analysis to understand how different variables interact within a given data set.

  • Linear regression
  • Correlation analysis

ANOVA: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on comparing means across multiple groups to determine if observed differences are statistically significant, utilizing the F distribution and one-way ANOVA techniques to evaluate variance between different categories of data effectively.

  • F distribution
  • One-way ANOVA

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive prepared for a 120-minute session. The exam is computer-based and contains 100 multiple-choice questions. Manage your time effectively across the six domains to ensure you address all questions within the allotted duration to maximize your potential for success.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The Principles of Statistics exam consists of 100 multiple-choice questions. These questions are designed to test your understanding of statistical concepts and their practical applications across the six domains covered in the official exam guide.

What is the passing score?

The passing score for this exam is 400. You must achieve at least this score to demonstrate the required proficiency in statistical principles and earn college credit for your introductory statistics course requirements.

How long is the exam?

The exam duration is 120 minutes. This time is provided for you to complete all 100 multiple-choice questions, so it is important to manage your time carefully throughout the testing session to ensure you finish.

How much does the exam cost?

The exam price is $85 for civilians. It is free for eligible military personnel via DANTES. Please check with your local testing center or the official DSST website for the most current payment and registration information.
