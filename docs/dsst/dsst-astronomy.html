What This Exam Validates

The Astronomy certification examination is administered by DSST to evaluate thorough understanding across 8 distinct content domains. Candidates must demonstrate proficiency in astrophysics, celestial systems, the science of light, planetary systems, the Sun and stars, galaxies, and the overall structure and evolution of the universe during the computer-based assessment containing 100 multiple-choice questions.

Who Should Take This Exam

Students seeking college credit for astronomy knowledge, as well as military personnel and civilian candidates preparing to demonstrate proficiency in celestial and planetary sciences during testing.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The examination consists of exactly 100 multiple-choice questions delivered in a computer-based format, carefully testing your knowledge across 8 distinct subject domains during the test session to evaluate overall competence.

What is the passing score?

The official passing score required to earn college credit for this specific exam is set at 400 points, which candidates must achieve successfully during their evaluation.

How long is the exam?

The total exam duration is 120 minutes, giving you adequate time to complete all multiple-choice questions before the official testing timer expires completely on test day.

How much does the exam cost?

The standard exam price is $85 for regular civilian candidates who register for the test, while eligible military personnel can take the examination completely free via DANTES administration services.
