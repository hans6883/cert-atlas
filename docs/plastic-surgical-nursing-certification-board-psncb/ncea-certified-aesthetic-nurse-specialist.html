What This Exam Validates

The NCEA Certified Aesthetic Nurse Specialist exam, administered by the Plastic Surgical Nursing Certification Board (PSNCB), evaluates professional competency in aesthetic nursing. The assessment covers three primary domains: aesthetic injectables, light, laser, and energy-based therapies, and clinical skin care. It serves as a formal validation of clinical knowledge for nurses working within the aesthetic medicine field, ensuring practitioners meet established standards for patient care and procedural safety. This evaluation ensures that nurses possess the necessary skills to perform aesthetic procedures safely and effectively in a clinical environment.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is intended for registered nurses who perform aesthetic procedures. Candidates should have practical experience in clinical settings involving injectables, laser therapies, and skin care treatments to demonstrate proficiency in the clinical tasks required by the Plastic Surgical Nursing Certification Board (PSNCB).

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Administering aesthetic injectables
  • Managing light-based therapies
  • Operating laser energy devices
  • Providing clinical skin care
  • Evaluating patient skin conditions

How to Prepare

Utilize all available resources to master the technical requirements and safety protocols necessary for success on the examination.

Domain Study Guidance

Aesthetic Injectables: Study Guidance

This domain covers the administration and clinical application of aesthetic injectables. It accounts for 55% of the exam content and focuses on the technical skills and safety protocols required for injectable procedures in a clinical environment.

  • Injectable safety protocols
  • Clinical application techniques

Light, Laser, and Energy-based therapies: Study Guidance

This domain addresses light, laser, and energy-based therapies, representing 30% of the exam. It focuses on the operation of energy-based devices and the management of patients undergoing these specific dermatological procedures to ensure safety and efficacy.

  • Laser device operation
  • Energy-based therapy safety

Clinical Skin Care: Study Guidance

This domain covers clinical skin care, comprising 15% of the exam. It involves the assessment of skin conditions and the application of clinical treatments to maintain skin health and address common dermatological concerns for diverse patient populations.

  • Skin condition assessment
  • Clinical skin care treatments

Exam-Day Guidance

You will have 240 minutes to complete the 150 questions. Manage your time effectively across the three domains, noting that injectables carry the highest weight. Ensure you are prepared to answer questions on clinical skin care and energy-based therapies within the allotted time.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The NCEA Certified Aesthetic Nurse Specialist exam consists of 150 questions covering three distinct domains of aesthetic nursing practice. Candidates must be prepared to address these questions within the designated time frame to demonstrate their clinical proficiency and knowledge.

What is the passing score?

To achieve certification, candidates must reach a passing score of about 75% correct on the total examination. This benchmark ensures that all certified nurses have met the standards set by the board for safe and effective aesthetic practice.

How long is the exam?

The total duration for the exam is 240 minutes, providing sufficient time to address all 150 questions. Candidates should pace themselves carefully throughout the session to ensure they have adequate time to review their answers before the final submission.
