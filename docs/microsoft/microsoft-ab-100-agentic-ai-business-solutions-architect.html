What This Exam Validates

The official certification program evaluates solution architects on planning, designing, and deploying AI-powered business solutions across modern enterprise platforms. Candidates prove thorough expertise in managing autonomous agents, enterprise copilots, advanced data grounding techniques, and cross-platform artificial intelligence workloads to support vital business operations and digital transformation objectives in modern environments.

Who Should Take This Exam

Accomplished solution architects, technical leaders, and senior consultants with extensive hands-on experience designing secure, scalable, and integrated AI-driven business solutions using advanced Microsoft services and tools.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Plan AI-powered business solutions
  • Design AI-powered business solutions
  • Deploy AI-powered business solutions
  • Assess agents for task automation
  • Design customizations for Copilot
  • Analyze backlog and user feedback

How to Prepare

Review the official exam guide and complete extensive hands-on practice sessions. Study grounding data requirements, multi-agent orchestration, agent monitoring tools, and application lifecycle management strategies in detail. Gain practical, working experience with Microsoft Copilot Studio and Azure AI Foundry tools before sitting for the examination.

Domain Study Guidance

Plan AI-powered business solutions: Study Guidance

This domain covers planning AI-powered business solutions by assessing agents in task automation and data analytics, reviewing data for grounding, and organizing business solution data for other artificial intelligence systems.

  • Assess agents in task automation, data analytics, and decision-making
  • Review data for grounding, including accuracy, relevance, timeliness, cleanliness, and availability
  • Organize business solution data to be available for other AI systems

Design AI-powered business solutions: Study Guidance

This domain focuses heavily on designing AI-powered business solutions by establishing business terms for Copilot in Dynamics 365 apps, designing app customizations, and building effective connectors for Dynamics 365 Sales.

  • Design business terms for Copilot in Dynamics 365 apps for customer experience and service
  • Design customizations of Copilot in Dynamics 365 apps for customer experience and service
  • Design connectors for Copilot in Dynamics 365 Sales

Deploy AI-powered business solutions: Study Guidance

This domain involves deploying AI-powered business solutions through recommending tools for monitoring agents, analyzing user feedback and backlogs, and applying artificial intelligence tools to identify issues and perform system tuning.

  • Recommend the process and tools required for monitoring agents
  • Analyze backlog and user feedback of AI and agent usage
  • Apply AI-based tools to analyze and identify issues and perform tuning

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive prepared to complete the proctored exam. Register using a personal account so exam records remain secure, and check with Microsoft for the exact price based on your region.

Frequently asked questions

What is the passing score?

Candidates must achieve a minimum score of 700 to successfully pass the examination on a scaling system ranging from 1 to 1000 for all test takers.

How much does the exam cost?

The exact pricing is determined directly by the specific country or region in which the exam is proctored, as Microsoft does not publish a standard global list price.

Is this exam currently active?

Yes, it is currently active. You should always check directly with Microsoft for the latest updates regarding certification paths and long-term requirements before scheduling your test.

What are the domain weights for the exam?

The exam features three domains: planning AI-powered business solutions weighted at 27.5 percent, designing them weighted at 27.5 percent, and deploying them weighted at 42.5 percent.
