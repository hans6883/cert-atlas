Historical MB-210 Scope

The Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales Functional Consultant exam, known as MB-210, was designed to evaluate a candidate's proficiency in configuring user interfaces, managing core sales features, and integrating various sales applications. This exam is now retired, and Microsoft has not named a successor. Candidates should check official Microsoft resources for current certification paths. This reference remains available for historical study of the final published objectives, which included 40 questions and a 100-minute duration for all test takers.

Who MB-210 Was For

This exam was intended for functional consultants responsible for configuring, customizing, and expanding Dynamics 365 Sales functionality. It required professional experience in automating and accelerating sales processes to create effective business solutions for diverse organizational needs.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Configuring user interfaces
  • Creating and managing accounts and contacts
  • Creating and managing leads and opportunities
  • Creating and managing quotes, orders, and invoices
  • Integrating sales applications

Historical Domain Guide

Configure Dynamics 365 Sales: Historical Scope

This domain focused on the technical setup and configuration of the user interface within the Dynamics 365 Sales environment to ensure that sales teams could effectively navigate and utilize the platform features for their daily operations.

  • Configuring user interface elements
  • Customizing system forms and views

Manage core sales features: Historical Scope

This domain covered the management of primary sales entities and the full lifecycle of sales documentation, including the creation and maintenance of accounts, contacts, leads, opportunities, quotes, orders, and invoices for various business requirements.

  • Managing accounts and contacts
  • Managing leads and opportunities
  • Managing quotes, orders, and invoices

Configure additional tools and services: Historical Scope

This domain addressed the integration of external sales applications and services with the Dynamics 365 platform to enhance functionality and provide a more cohesive experience for users managing complex sales workflows and data.

  • Integrating sales applications
  • Configuring external service connections

Frequently asked questions

How many questions were on the exam?

The exam consisted of 40 questions in total. These questions were designed to test the functional consultant's ability to configure and manage the Dynamics 365 Sales environment effectively during the allotted testing time.

What was the passing score?

The passing score for the exam was 700 on a scale of 1-1000. Candidates were required to demonstrate a high level of proficiency to meet this standard and earn the associated professional certification.

How long was the exam?

The exam had a total duration of 100 minutes. This time limit was intended to allow candidates to complete all sections and review their answers before final submission at the authorized testing center.

What was the cost of the exam?

The price for the exam was 165 dollars. This fee covered the registration and administration of the test at authorized testing centers or through online proctoring services for all eligible candidates who registered.
