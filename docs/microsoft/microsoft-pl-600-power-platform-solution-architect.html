PL-600 Retired: What Replaced It and What Carries Over

PL-600

Microsoft

Retired exam

PL-600 was retired on June 30, 2026

Microsoft retired PL-600 and the Power Platform Solution Architect Expert certification on June 30, 2026. Microsoft describes AB-100 as a collective and partial replacement path that expands solution architecture into agentic AI business systems.

Explore the broader replacement path AB-100: Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect

Historical PL-600 Scope

PL-600 was Microsoft's Microsoft Power Platform Solution Architect exam until its retirement on 2026-06-30. Its final published scope covered Perform solution envisioning and requirement analysis (45–50%), Architect a solution (35–40%), Implement the solution (15–20%). This page preserves that dated outline for historical comparison and curriculum cleanup. It is not a schedulable exam, and its prices, delivery details, registration links, and practice calls to action must not be used for a new certification plan.

Who PL-600 Was For

Nobody should start a new certification plan around PL-600. This page serves former candidates, credential holders, training teams removing stale material, and practitioners deciding whether prior preparation is reusable for AB-100. New candidates should verify the active Microsoft page and current dated study guide before booking.

  • Preserve practical experience from Architect a solution (35–40%) only where the current guide shows meaningful overlap
  • Preserve practical experience from Implement the solution (15–20%) only where the current guide shows meaningful overlap

How to Reuse Your Preparation

Do not rename an old PL-600 checklist or question bank as AB-100. Compare the two official guides objective by objective and classify each historical skill as transferable, changed, or absent. Use transferable experience as a diagnostic baseline, then rebuild labs and review around AB-100's current domain weights, products, role expectations, security controls, and operational tasks. Because Microsoft identifies AB-100 as the broader replacement path, review the skill map below before assuming equivalence.
