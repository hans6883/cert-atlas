Historical MB-300 Scope

Microsoft officially retired the MB-300 exam on 2024-02-29. This professional assessment was designed to evaluate a candidate's proficiency in core finance and operations functionality, security configuration, data management, and solution validation. The exam consisted of 40 questions in total. As there is no successor, candidates should consult Microsoft for current certification paths.

Who MB-300 Was For

This exam was intended for functional consultants, system administrators, and technical professionals who worked with Microsoft Dynamics 365 finance and operations applications and needed to validate their core configuration and implementation skills.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions were on the exam?

The exam contained 40 questions presented in a linear format using various question types such as multiple choice, multiple response, drag and drop, case study, build list, hot area, and repeated answer choices.

What was the passing score?

The passing score for this certification exam was 700 on a scale of 1-1000. Candidates were required to meet or exceed this threshold to demonstrate their proficiency in the core finance and operations subject matter.

How long was the exam?

The total duration of the exam was 100 minutes. This time limit was provided to allow candidates to complete all 40 questions, including complex case studies and various interactive question types found within the assessment.

What was the exam price?

The price of the exam was 165.0 USD. This fee covered the cost of the assessment at the time it was available for scheduling through the official Microsoft certification portal before the retirement date.
