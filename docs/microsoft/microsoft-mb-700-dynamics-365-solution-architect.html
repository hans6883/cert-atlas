Historical MB-700 Scope

MB-700 was Microsoft's Microsoft Dynamics 365: Finance and Operations Apps Solution Architect exam until its retirement on 2026-06-30. Its final published scope covered Architect solutions, Define solution strategies, Manage implementations, Manage testing. This page preserves that dated outline for historical comparison and curriculum cleanup. It is not a schedulable exam, and its prices, delivery details, registration links, and practice calls to action must not be used for a new certification plan.
