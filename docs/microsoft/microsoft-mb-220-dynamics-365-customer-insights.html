Historical MB-220 Scope

This exam covered the configuration and management of Dynamics 365 Customer Insights - Journeys. Certified by Microsoft, it evaluated skills in segment management, marketing channels, and journey orchestration. This exam is retired, and Microsoft has named no successor. Candidates should review official Microsoft documentation for current certification paths and learning opportunities related to Dynamics 365 applications. The exam consisted of 40 questions and required a passing score of 700 to achieve certification, which remained valid for 1 year.

Who MB-220 Was For

This exam was intended for functional consultants responsible for configuring and managing customer journeys. It required experience in Dynamics 365 environments, specifically focusing on segment creation, marketing channel distribution, and event management to support business marketing goals.
