What This Exam Validates

The ISO 45001 Lead Auditor Certification, certified by PECB, covers occupational health and safety principles, audit concepts, preparation, execution, closing, and program management. Candidates must complete an 80-question exam to demonstrate their competence in managing ISO 45001 audits. This certification validates the ability to lead audit teams while ensuring compliance with international standards for occupational health and safety management systems within various organizational environments.

Who Should Take This Exam

Professionals seeking to lead audit teams for occupational health and safety management systems. This is ideal for auditors, consultants, and safety managers with relevant workplace experience needing formal certification from PECB to advance their careers.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • OH&S MS principles
  • Audit concepts
  • Audit preparation
  • Conducting audits
  • Audit closing
  • Program management

How to Prepare

Review the seven domains, focusing on audit conduct, preparation steps, and management principles. Practice applying the 70% passing threshold criteria to sample questions to gauge your readiness. Additionally, check with PECB for any available official study materials to supplement your learning and ensure you are prepared for the exam format and the 80-question structure.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of 80 questions that cover seven distinct domains of occupational health and safety auditing, requiring a clear understanding of the material to succeed in the assessment.

What is the passing score?

Candidates must achieve a passing score of 70% to successfully pass the ISO 45001 Lead Auditor Certification exam and earn their professional credential from the certifying body.

How long is the exam?

Please check with PECB for specific duration details regarding the exam delivery and scheduling, as these parameters can vary based on the testing center and the specific delivery method chosen.

Who certifies this exam?

This certification is administered and certified by PECB based on ISO 45001 audit standards, ensuring that all successful candidates meet the requirements set for lead auditors globally.

What is the next step?

If you are looking for a successor exam, check with PECB for the current certification path, as they maintain the standards for all professional occupational health and safety audits.
