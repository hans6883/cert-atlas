What This Exam Validates

The ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Implementer Certification, administered by PECB, comprehensively evaluates your capability to plan, implement, manage, and continuously improve an information security management system. The rigorous evaluation consists of exactly 80 questions and requires achieving a passing score of 70% to successfully certify under standard professional guidelines.

Who Should Take This Exam

Professionals responsible for information security management, risk management, or regulatory compliance within their organizations who need to establish and maintain an information security management system.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • ISMS fundamental principles
  • Management system requirements
  • Implementation planning
  • ISMS implementation
  • Monitoring and measurement
  • Continual improvement
  • Audit preparation

How to Prepare

Review each domain very carefully according to its specified weight, focusing heavily on implementation planning at 22.5% and fundamental principles at 18.75%. Practice applying the management system requirements and study measurement techniques carefully. Always check with PECB for the current certification path.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The certification exam consists of a total of 80 questions covering all seven domains of information security management systems according to ISO/IEC 27001 standards.

What is the passing score?

The passing score required for the PECB ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Implementer Certification is set at 70%. Be sure to check with PECB for the current certification path.

Who administers the certification?

The certification is administered by PECB. It covers topics ranging from fundamental principles to audit preparation across all 7 distinct domains of the evaluation framework.

What is the highest weighted domain on the exam?

The highest weighted domain is the planning of an ISMS implementation based on ISO/IEC 27001, which accounts for 22.5% of the total evaluation.
