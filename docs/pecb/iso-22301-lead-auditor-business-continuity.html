What This Exam Validates

The ISO 22301 Lead Auditor Certification exam, certified by PECB, evaluates candidate proficiency in planning, conducting, and managing business continuity audits. Covering 7 distinct domains, the test assesses understanding of business continuity management system requirements, core audit concepts, preparation, execution, closing activities, and audit program management. Candidates face 80 questions and must achieve a minimum passing score of 70% to earn certification.

Who Should Take This Exam

This test suits business continuity professionals, risk managers, audit managers, and consultants responsible for auditing business continuity management systems. It is intended for individuals overseeing compliance with ISO 22301 standards or leading audit teams within public or private organizations.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Understanding BCMS principles and concepts
  • Evaluating business continuity system requirements
  • Applying core audit concepts and principles
  • Preparing ISO 22301 audit activities
  • Conducting ISO 22301 audits
  • Closing and reporting ISO 22301 audits
  • Managing a business continuity audit program

How to Prepare

Begin preparation by reviewing the 7 exam domains, placing heavy emphasis on conducting audits, which accounts for 25.0% of the scoring weight, and audit principles at 17.5%. Study ISO 22301 requirements and standard audit methodologies. Practice evaluating non-conformities, drafting audit reports, and managing audit programs. Allocate study time according to domain weightings to ensure effective coverage across all 80 questions.

Domain Study Guidance

Fundamental principles and concepts of a business continuity management system: Study Guidance

This domain addresses core concepts of business continuity management systems, weighting 16.25% of the exam. Focus on understanding key terminology, business continuity frameworks, organizational resilience concepts, and how business continuity integrates with standard organizational management processes.

  • Business continuity concepts
  • Management system frameworks
  • Organizational resilience principles

Business continuity management system requirements: Study Guidance

Representing 10.0% of the exam, this domain covers specific requirements established by the ISO 22301 standard. Study organizational context, leadership commitment, planning, support structures, operation, performance evaluation, and continual improvement within a business continuity system.

  • ISO 22301 standard requirements
  • Context and leadership obligations
  • Performance evaluation and improvement

Fundamental audit concepts and principles: Study Guidance

Accounting for 17.5% of the evaluation, this domain focuses on fundamental auditing principles. Review professional auditor ethics, audit evidence collection criteria, risk-based audit approaches, regulatory compliance considerations, and objective evaluation techniques used during management system audits.

  • Auditing principles and ethics
  • Audit evidence collection
  • Risk-based audit methodology

Preparing an ISO 22301 audit: Study Guidance

Carrying a 12.5% weighting, this section covers initial audit planning and preparation. Focus on establishing audit scope, defining criteria, creating audit plans, reviewing preliminary documentation, and assigning responsibilities to audit team members prior to onsite activities.

  • Audit scope definition
  • Document review procedures
  • Audit plan creation

Conducting an ISO 22301 audit: Study Guidance

As the largest domain at 25.0%, this area tests on-site audit execution. Key topics include conducting opening meetings, gathering evidence through interviews and observations, evaluating audit findings, identifying non-conformities, and preparing initial audit conclusions.

  • On-site evidence gathering
  • Finding evaluation and non-conformities
  • Interview techniques and observations

Closing an ISO 22301 audit: Study Guidance

This domain represents 6.25% of the exam and covers final audit activities. Study procedures for conducting closing meetings, presenting audit findings to leadership, drafting the formal audit report, and verifying corrective action plans.

  • Closing meeting execution
  • Audit report preparation
  • Corrective action review

Managing an ISO 22301 audit program: Study Guidance

Weighted at 12.5%, this domain covers long-term audit program administration. Focus on establishing audit program objectives, managing program risks, selecting qualified auditors, monitoring program performance, and maintaining audit records across an organization.

  • Audit program objectives
  • Auditor competence and selection
  • Program risk management

Exam-Day Guidance

On exam day, ensure you have a stable internet connection and quiet environment if taking the test online. Carefully review all 80 multiple-choice questions, monitor your pace across the test, and verify your answers before final submission.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The ISO 22301 Lead Auditor Certification exam consists of 80 questions in total. These questions cover all 7 specified domains, testing candidate knowledge on business continuity management systems and auditing concepts.

What is the passing score?

To pass the PECB ISO 22301 Lead Auditor exam, candidates must achieve a minimum score of 70%. Your total score reflects performance across all 7 exam domains, which carry varying percent weights.

How long is the exam?

Specific time limits for taking the exam are established by PECB during the exam scheduling process. Candidates should verify current scheduling details and exam administration policies directly with PECB prior to test day.

Who certifies the ISO 22301 Lead Auditor exam?

The ISO 22301 Lead Auditor Certification is issued and administered by PECB. PECB provides certification for individuals seeking to validate their expertise in business continuity management system auditing.
