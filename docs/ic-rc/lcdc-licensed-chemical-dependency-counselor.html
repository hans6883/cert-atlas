What This Exam Validates

The IC&RC Licensed Chemical Dependency Counselor certification exam assesses practitioner knowledge across four distinct and detailed domains. Certified by IC&RC, this evaluation covers the scientific principles of substance use, evidence-based screening procedures, advanced treatment practices, and professional responsibilities to ensure qualified care delivery and proper clinical practice.

Who Should Take This Exam

Practitioners seeking professional certification in substance use disorder treatment. Candidates need preparation in clinical evaluation, treatment planning, counseling, service coordination, and adherence to legal and ethical standards.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Scientific principles of substance use
  • Co-occurring disorders
  • Evidence-based screening
  • Assessment methods
  • Evidence-based treatment
  • Counseling and referral
  • Professional and ethical responsibilities
  • Legal responsibilities

How to Prepare

Review the four domains very carefully and align your daily study habits with the precise weighted distribution specified by the official guide. Focus on scientific principles, screening methods, treatment methodologies, and legal duties. Practice answering questions under timed conditions to manage the 180-minute duration effectively.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The official certification examination consists of a total of 150 questions covering four distinct domains weighted across scientific principles, screening methods, treatment strategies, and professional ethics to test knowledge.

What is the passing score?

The established passing score required to successfully pass this professional certification examination is officially set at 500/800 points according to the established guidelines for candidates seeking credentials.

How long is the exam?

Test takers are given a total duration of 180 minutes to complete all questions included in the evaluation and demonstrate their professional competency across all mandatory core subjects.

Who certifies the LCDC credential?

The credential certification is officially issued by IC&RC. For long-term certification path details, check with IC&RC directly regarding current requirements and policies for all future applicants.
