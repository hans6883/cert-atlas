What This Exam Validates

The CSCMP Supply Chain Management Professional (SCPro) certification is an established credential offered by CSCMP (Council of Supply Chain Management Professionals). It comprehensively evaluates candidate knowledge across core supply chain management principles, demand planning, procurement, manufacturing operations, inventory control, transportation logistics, warehousing, and customer service operations during the official 240-minute examination duration.

Who Should Take This Exam

Supply chain professionals, logicians, and experienced operational managers aiming to formally validate their extensive expertise across multiple functional areas of logistics, procurement, and enterprise operations.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Supply Chain Management Principles
  • Demand Planning
  • Supply Management and Procurement
  • Manufacturing and Service Operations
  • Inventory Management
  • Transportation Operations
  • Warehousing Operations
  • Customer Service Operations

How to Prepare

Carefully review all official exam domains and test your knowledge across all eight topics provided by CSCMP (Council of Supply Chain Management Professionals). Focus intently on mastering fundamental supply chain principles, planning demand accurately, managing supply relationships effectively, and handling operations, inventory systems, and transportation logistics.

Domain Study Guidance

Supply Chain Management Principles: Study Guidance

This domain covers fundamental supply chain management principles, core concepts, and structural frameworks helpful for understanding end-to-end logistics operations across various enterprise environments, organizational networks, and complex commercial distribution structures.

  • Supply Chain Management Principles
  • Frameworks

Demand Planning: Study Guidance

This domain specifically focuses on demand planning processes, forecasting techniques, and effectively managing customer requirements to align inventory levels with actual market consumption patterns and verified historical data.

  • Demand Planning
  • Forecasting Techniques

Supply Management and Procurement: Study Guidance

This domain directly addresses supply management and procurement strategies, purchasing methodologies, vendor evaluation protocols, and maintaining productive supplier relations across the entire operational network of the enterprise organization.

  • Supply Management and Procurement
  • Strategic Purchasing

Manufacturing and Service Operations: Study Guidance

This domain deals extensively with manufacturing and service operations, production processes, capacity planning, quality control measures, and efficient service delivery methods within modern operational facilities and active manufacturing plants.

  • Manufacturing and Service Operations
  • Production Processes

Inventory Management: Study Guidance

This domain critically examines inventory management methodologies, stock control procedures, carrying cost optimizations, and effective storage strategies designed to minimize holding expenses while maximizing product availability for customers.

  • Inventory Management
  • Stock Control Procedures

Transportation Operations: Study Guidance

This domain comprehensively covers transportation operations, freight routing optimization, carrier management, modal selection criteria, and regulatory compliance protocols necessary for moving goods safely and efficiently between different geographical locations.

  • Transportation Operations
  • Carrier Management

Warehousing Operations: Study Guidance

This domain focuses heavily on warehousing operations, facility layout optimization, material handling equipment, inventory tracking technologies, and streamlined order fulfillment processes within modern distribution centers and active storage hubs.

  • Warehousing Operations
  • Order Fulfillment Processes

Customer Service Operations: Study Guidance

This domain directly addresses customer service operations, satisfaction performance metrics, complaint resolution procedures, and accurate order handling techniques to ensure consistently high levels of client retention and total operational success.

  • Customer Service Operations
  • Satisfaction Performance Metrics

Exam-Day Guidance

Manage your testing time effectively across all 160 questions during the allocated 240-minute exam duration. Read each multiple-choice question very carefully before selecting and submitting your final answer choice.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The certification exam consists of a total of 160 questions covering the eight distinct core domains of professional supply chain management as officially outlined by CSCMP.

What is the passing score?

Candidates must achieve a minimum passing score of 65% in order to successfully pass the examination and earn the official professional certification credential from CSCMP.

How long is the exam?

Test-takers are given a total duration of 240 minutes to review, process, and complete all questions presented on the official certification exam administered by the organization.

What is the certification path?

There is no replacement exam code specified for this test. Candidates should check with CSCMP (Council of Supply Chain Management Professionals) for the current certification path and program updates.
