Historical CHRP Scope

The CIPD Certificate in HR Practice (CHRP) was a professional credential covering four specific domains certified by the CIPD. It officially retired on 2023-07-31. Candidates seeking this credential must now pursue the Foundation Certificate L3, officially known as the CIPD Foundation Certificate in People Practice (Level 3), as the verified successor path for professional human resources development and career progression within the industry.

Who CHRP Was For

Human resources practitioners and professionals looking to develop core operational skills through assignment-based assessments. Candidates seeking this retired qualification should instead target the Foundation Certificate L3 for their professional certification needs.

How to Reuse Your Preparation

Because the Certificate in HR Practice is retired, candidates should focus preparation entirely on the Foundation Certificate L3. Skills from the four domains transfer safely to the new syllabus, but candidates must learn current assignment-based assessment requirements fresh through CIPD-approved training providers offering the updated qualifications for human resources practitioners. This ensures full alignment with current industry standards and professional expectations.

Frequently asked questions

How many domains are on the exam?

The curriculum features exactly 4 distinct domains, with each individual domain weighted at 25.0 percent of the total content for candidates pursuing the professional qualification today. This structure ensures a balanced approach to learning core human resources competencies required for success in the modern workplace environment.

Is the CIPD Certificate in HR Practice still active?

No, it is not active. The CIPD officially retired the CHRP credential on 2023-07-31, establishing the Foundation Certificate L3 as the official named replacement path for practitioners everywhere. Candidates should transition their studies to this current qualification to maintain their professional standing and ensure their skills remain relevant.

What is the format of the assessment?

The assessment format relies on assignment-based evaluations delivered exclusively through CIPD-approved training providers, utilising written assignments, case study analysis, and workplace-based projects for grading. This practical approach ensures that all candidates demonstrate their ability to apply theoretical knowledge to real-world human resources scenarios effectively and professionally.

What is the price range for the qualification?

The qualification typically costs between £800 and £1,800. It is a short qualification that is less extensive than the full Foundation Certificate available today. Candidates should verify current pricing directly with their chosen training provider to ensure they have the most accurate financial information before enrolling in any course.
