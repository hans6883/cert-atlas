Historical Advanced Diploma OD&D L7 Scope

Certified by CIPD, the CIPD Advanced Diploma in Organisational Design and Development (Level 7) covered advanced organization design, development, change, leadership, business research, and systems thinking across 5 detailed domains. Please note that this credential is retired as of 2023-07-31, and its verified successor is Advanced Diploma SPM L7 for strategic people management.

Who Advanced Diploma OD&D L7 Was For
