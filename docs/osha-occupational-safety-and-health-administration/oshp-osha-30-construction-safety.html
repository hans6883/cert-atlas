OSHA 30-Hour Construction Industry Safety Outreach Credential

OSHA

Not an examination

OSHA does not offer an examination for OSHA 30-Hour Construction Industry Safety Outreach: it is a training programme. No exam blueprint, domain weights, exam code or practice test apply. Verified on the issuer's site September 19, 2026. issuer page

What This Exam Validates

The OSHA 30-Hour Construction Industry Safety Outreach certification, administered by OSHA, covers safety standards for the construction industry. The program requires 1800 minutes of training across eight specific domains, focusing on hazard recognition, personal protective equipment, health controls, and safety management procedures to ensure workers remain safe on job sites during their daily operations.

Who Should Take This Exam

Construction workers, site supervisors, and safety personnel with professional safety responsibilities should take this training to understand hazard identification, regulatory compliance requirements, and best practices on active construction job sites.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Introduction to OSHA
  • Managing Safety and Health
  • OSHA Focus Four Hazards
  • Personal Protective Equipment
  • Health Hazards in Construction
  • Stairways and Ladders

How to Prepare

To prepare effectively, review the 1800 minutes of course material across all eight domains. Focus heavily on construction safety standards, hazard identification, and safety management principles as outlined in the official documentation. Ensure you understand the specific subparts related to construction safety to successfully navigate the program requirements and improve your workplace safety knowledge.

Ensure you have completed the required 1800 minutes of training time. Review all 8 domain topics and understand the specific subparts related to construction safety standards before concluding the program to ensure full compliance with the 30-hour requirement.
