What This Exam Validates

The Certified Construction Manager exam is an assessment designed for professionals seeking to validate their expertise in the construction industry. The exam evaluates candidates across ten distinct domains, including project management, cost, time, and quality management. By mastering these areas, candidates demonstrate their ability to lead complex projects effectively. The exam consists of 175 questions that must be completed within a 240 minute timeframe. Achieving a passing score of 72% or higher is required to earn this professional designation, which signifies a level of competence and adherence to industry standards.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is intended for experienced construction managers who wish to formally validate their professional skills, demonstrate their commitment to industry standards, and advance their careers through a recognized credential in the field.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Project lifecycle management
  • Cost and time estimation
  • Contractual compliance oversight
  • Quality and safety assurance
  • Risk management
  • Professional ethics

How to Prepare

Candidates should review the official CMAA handbook to understand the scope of the ten domains. Utilize practice materials to familiarize yourself with the 175 question format and the 240 minute time limit. Focus on identifying knowledge gaps in cost, time, and quality management to ensure you meet the 72% passing threshold.

Domain Study Guidance

Program & Project Management: Study Guidance

This domain covers the principles of project management, requiring candidates to understand how to lead construction projects effectively while maintaining alignment with organizational goals and stakeholder expectations throughout the entire project lifecycle.

  • Project planning
  • Leadership
  • Stakeholder management

Cost Management: Study Guidance

Candidates must demonstrate proficiency in managing construction budgets, tracking expenditures, and implementing cost control measures to ensure that projects remain financially viable and adhere to the established fiscal constraints of the project.

  • Budgeting
  • Cost tracking
  • Financial reporting

Time Management: Study Guidance

This section emphasizes the importance of scheduling and time management, requiring knowledge of path methods and techniques to ensure that all project milestones are achieved within the designated project timeline for the client.

  • Scheduling
  • Time management
  • Milestone tracking

Contract Administration: Study Guidance

Focuses on the legal and administrative aspects of construction contracts, ensuring that managers can interpret, negotiate, and enforce contract terms to protect the interests of all parties involved in the project delivery process.

  • Contract law
  • Negotiation
  • Administration

Quality Management: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on establishing, monitoring, and maintaining quality control and assurance standards throughout the construction lifecycle, ensuring that completed work complies with design specifications and industry requirements for the project.

  • Quality control
  • Standards of practice
  • Compliance

Safety Management: Study Guidance

Candidates are evaluated on their ability to implement and oversee safety programs, ensuring that all site activities comply with regulatory requirements and minimize risks to personnel and the public during construction operations.

  • Safety regulations
  • Risk mitigation
  • Site safety

Risk Management: Study Guidance

This domain requires an understanding of risk identification, assessment, and mitigation strategies, enabling managers to address potential project threats and uncertainties that could impact project success or safety during the construction phase.

  • Risk assessment
  • Contingency planning
  • Threat analysis

Professional Practice: Study Guidance

Covers the ethical and professional responsibilities of construction managers, emphasizing the importance of integrity, professional conduct, and adherence to the established standards of practice within the construction industry for all certified professionals.

  • Ethics
  • Professional conduct
  • Industry standards

Sustainability: Study Guidance

Focuses on the integration of sustainable building practices and environmental stewardship, requiring knowledge of green building standards and techniques that reduce the environmental impact of construction projects over the entire project duration.

  • Sustainability
  • Green building
  • Environmental impact

Technology: Study Guidance

This domain explores the application of modern construction technologies and digital tools, ensuring that managers can leverage software and hardware solutions to improve project efficiency, communication, and overall data management for stakeholders.

  • Construction software
  • Digital tools
  • Data management

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive early at the testing center with your required identification. Manage your time effectively during the 240 minute exam to ensure you answer all 175 questions, prioritizing accuracy to reach the 72% passing score required for certification.

Frequently asked questions

What is the passing score?

To successfully earn the certification, candidates must achieve a passing score of 72% or higher on the examination, reflecting an understanding of the core construction management principles required for professional practice.

How many questions are on the exam?

The official examination consists of 175 questions, which are designed to test the candidate's knowledge across all ten domains of construction management as outlined in the official handbook provided by the association.

What is the exam duration?

Candidates are provided with a total duration of 240 minutes to complete the examination, allowing sufficient time to address all 175 questions and demonstrate their professional competency in the field of construction management.

Is there a replacement exam?

Please check with CMAA for the current certification path, as no specific successor exam code is provided in the current documentation for this professional construction management credential at this time.
