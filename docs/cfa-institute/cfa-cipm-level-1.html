What This Exam Validates

The CIPM Level I is a professional certification program administered by the CFA Institute, designed specifically to evaluate and validate professional competency across five distinct domains of investment management, including performance measurement, attribution, appraisal, benchmarking, and Global Investment Performance Standards (GIPS), ensuring candidates meet the established global benchmarks for ethical and professional practice.

Who Should Take This Exam

Investment professionals, asset managers, and financial analysts seeking to validate their expertise in performance evaluation and measurement principles. Candidates build skills required for accurate reporting and compliance.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Performance Measurement
  • Performance Attribution
  • Performance Appraisal
  • Benchmarking
  • Performance Presentation Standards

How to Prepare

To prepare effectively for this examination, candidates should review the official CFA Institute study documentation and practice questions provided in the curriculum. Focus heavily on understanding calculation methodologies, composite maintenance rules, and Global Investment Performance Standards requirements. Ensure you allocate adequate study time for each of the specific domains based on the official examination weighting percentages to maximize your overall retention and exam readiness.

Domain Study Guidance

Performance Measurement: Study Guidance

This domain covers core concepts in performance measurement principles, input data integrity, and quantitative calculation methodologies that form the primary foundation for all subsequent portfolio analysis within the curriculum.

  • Input data quality
  • Calculation methodology

Performance Attribution: Study Guidance

This domain focuses intensively on the advanced methods and analytical tools used for conducting detailed performance attribution analysis within professional portfolio management and asset allocation strategies for clients.

  • Attribution methodologies
  • Portfolio return analysis

Performance Appraisal: Study Guidance

This domain carefully examines advanced performance appraisal techniques, risk-adjusted return measures, and detailed evaluation frameworks necessary for assessing investment manager skill and portfolio outcomes across various market cycles successfully.

  • Appraisal measures
  • Manager evaluation

Benchmarking: Study Guidance

This domain addresses benchmarking principles, asset class characteristics, and the careful selection of appropriate comparison standards required to evaluate portfolio manager performance accurately according to established institutional standards.

  • Benchmark selection
  • Comparison standards

Performance Presentation Standards (GIPS): Study Guidance

This domain explores complex Global Investment Performance Standards requirements, compliance fundamentals, ethical considerations, verification guidelines, and detailed report presentation rules for investment firms operating in competitive global markets today.

  • GIPS compliance
  • Composite reporting

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive at the computer-based testing center early with your required identification documents ready. Complete the 100 questions within the 120 minutes allocated for the linear examination format.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The examination consists of a total of exactly 100 multiple choice questions delivered in a linear, computer-based testing format at authorized testing centers globally for candidates.

What is the passing score?

CFA Institute does not publish a specific passing score or minimum passing percentage threshold for this professional certification examination under their current grading policies and procedures.

How long is the exam?

The official exam duration is exactly 120 minutes in total, during which candidates must complete all portions of the computer-based testing requirements successfully to finish.

What is the cost of the exam?

The program features a one-time CIPM Program enrollment fee of $250 plus a per-exam fee of $625, resulting in a combined total cost of $875 for the standard fee structure.
