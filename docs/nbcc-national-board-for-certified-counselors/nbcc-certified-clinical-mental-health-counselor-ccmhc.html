What This Exam Validates

The Certified Clinical Mental Health Counselor credential examination is administered by the National Board for Certified Counselors. It comprehensively evaluates professional competencies across six clinical domains, including professional practice, intake, assessment, treatment planning, counseling skills, and core attributes to ensure high standards in mental health care delivery for all candidates.

Who Should Take This Exam

Candidates seeking national certification as clinical mental health counselors through the NBCC. Applicants must meet specific education, supervision, and ethical requirements set by the certifying body.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Professional Practice and Ethics
  • Intake, Assessment, and Diagnosis
  • Areas of Clinical Focus
  • Treatment Planning
  • Counseling Skills and Interventions
  • Core Counseling Attributes

How to Prepare

Review the official NBCC exam handbooks and code of ethics carefully before your scheduled test date. Focus your daily study efforts on the six distinct content domains, paying close attention to assessment, diagnosis, and treatment planning weights to ensure adequate preparation across all required topics.

Domain Study Guidance

Professional Practice and Ethics: Study Guidance

This initial domain covers professional practice and ethics standards with a designated weight of 15.0 percent on the overall examination, ensuring candidates understand ethical obligations and professional responsibilities.

  • NBCC Code of Ethics compliance
  • Professional credentialing and qualifications

Intake, Assessment, and Diagnosis: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on intake, assessment, and diagnosis, accounting for 25.0 percent of the examination to test a candidate's ability to evaluate client needs and establish proper diagnostic profiles.

  • Client assessment protocols
  • Diagnosis and testing standards

Areas of Clinical Focus: Study Guidance

This domain addresses specific areas of clinical focus, weighted at 1.0 percent of the total exam content, directing attention toward particular situations and maintaining client welfare during the therapeutic process.

  • Specific clinical focus areas
  • Client welfare maintenance

Treatment Planning: Study Guidance

This domain encompasses treatment planning responsibilities with a weighted value of 15.0 percent, ensuring counselors can formulate structured, collaborative, and effective interventions tailored to individual client goals.

  • Collaborative treatment plan creation
  • Goal updates and modifications

Counseling Skills and Interventions: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on counseling skills and interventions, representing the largest domain weight at 30.0 percent, which measures the practitioner's direct ability to manage therapeutic relationships and apply evidence-based techniques.

  • Counseling techniques and interventions
  • Therapeutic relationship management

Core Counseling Attributes: Study Guidance

This final domain evaluates core counseling attributes, contributing 15.0 percent to the examination score by focusing on counselor values, beliefs, and multicultural competence necessary for effective practice.

  • Core values and beliefs
  • Multicultural counseling competence

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive well-prepared for a 255-minute testing session. Manage your time effectively across the 130-150 questions administered on the official test form during your scheduled testing appointment.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The examination consists of 130-150 questions designed to test your knowledge across six distinct domains of clinical mental health counseling and professional practice standards established by the national board.

What is the passing score?

The passing score varies by test form depending on the specific version of the examination you receive. Check with the NBCC for specific scoring guidelines and certification path details before your test date.

How long is the exam?

The examination has a total duration of 255 minutes, providing adequate time for candidates to complete all 130-150 questions included on the test form during the administration period.

What body certifies this credential?

The National Board for Certified Counselors administers the CCMHC certification and establishes all official standards for eligibility, examination content, and professional ethics required for practicing clinical mental health counselors.
