Historical PAS-C01 Scope

The AWS Certified SAP on AWS – Specialty exam, certified by AWS, covered the design, implementation, migration, and maintenance of SAP solutions on the AWS cloud. This exam officially retired on 2024-04-30. AWS has not designated a successor for this certification. Candidates interested in current SAP-related cloud certifications should consult the official AWS certification website for the most up-to-date information regarding available paths and professional credentials. This credential previously validated expertise in managing complex SAP environments within the AWS ecosystem.

Who PAS-C01 Was For

This retired exam targeted professionals with experience managing SAP workloads on AWS. It required technical knowledge of SAP HANA, NetWeaver, and cloud infrastructure to support complex enterprise environments and database migrations for organizations utilizing SAP software on AWS platforms.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions were on the exam?

The exam consisted of 65 questions in a linear format. Candidates were required to navigate through these questions within the allotted time to demonstrate their technical proficiency in managing SAP workloads on the AWS cloud platform.

What was the passing score?

The passing score for the exam was 750 on a scale of 100-1000. This score represented the minimum level of competency required to achieve the certification before the exam was officially retired by the AWS organization.

How long was the exam?

The exam had a duration of 170 minutes. This time frame was designed to allow candidates sufficient opportunity to answer all 65 questions while carefully evaluating the complex technical scenarios presented during the testing session.

What was the exam price?

The cost of the exam was 300 dollars. This fee was standard for the certification level and covered the administrative and technical requirements associated with delivering the exam through authorized testing centers globally.
