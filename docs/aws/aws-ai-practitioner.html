What This Exam Validates

The AWS Certified AI Practitioner exam, certified by AWS, evaluates candidate knowledge across five core domains. Over 90 minutes, candidates answer 65 questions to demonstrate proficiency in AI and machine learning concepts, generative AI principles, foundation model applications, guidelines for responsible AI, and security, compliance, and governance for AI solutions.

Who Should Take This Exam

Technology professionals, developers, and architects working with cloud workloads should take this exam. Candidates require familiarity with artificial intelligence concepts, machine learning fundamentals, and practical cloud deployments to demonstrate readiness for professional AI tasks.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
  • Generative AI Principles
  • Foundation Model Applications
  • Responsible AI Guidelines
  • Security and Compliance Governance

How to Prepare

Review official AWS documentation and domain objectives to prepare effectively for the certification exam. Focus your study time extensively on foundation model applications, generative AI fundamentals, and responsible AI guidelines. Practice with sample questions to build familiarity with the 65-question format and manage the 90-minute limit efficiently.

Domain Study Guidance

Fundamentals of AI and ML: Study Guidance

This domain covers the core concepts and fundamental principles of artificial intelligence and machine learning technologies, establishing a solid baseline of knowledge regarding data patterns, machine learning algorithms, and predictive modeling techniques required for cloud implementations.

  • AI and ML concepts
  • Data patterns
  • Machine learning algorithms

Fundamentals of GenAI: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the fundamentals of generative AI, covering advanced models, underlying architectures, and text generation concepts that drive modern applications, helping candidates understand how content creation systems operate within cloud environments.

  • Generative AI principles
  • Model selection
  • Content generation

Applications of Foundation Models: Study Guidance

This domain addresses the practical applications and implementations of foundation models across various use cases, ensuring candidates can successfully deploy model development pipelines, execute fine-tuning procedures, and configure retrieval-augmented generation architectures.

  • Foundation model development
  • Fine-tuning
  • RAG architectures

Guidelines for Responsible AI: Study Guidance

This domain examines the guidelines and architectural principles required for building responsible artificial intelligence solutions, emphasizing ethical design, fairness, accuracy, and the mitigation of bias across all deployed services and automated workflows.

  • Responsible AI design
  • Fair and accurate services
  • Ethical guidelines

Security, Compliance, and Governance for AI Solutions: Study Guidance

This domain covers security, compliance, and governance frameworks for managing artificial intelligence and machine learning solutions on AWS, detailing the implementation of security guardrails, regulatory compliance standards, and organizational governance challenges.

  • Security guardrails
  • Governance challenges
  • Compliance standards

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

There are 65 questions on the exam, covering five distinct domains including AI and machine learning fundamentals, generative AI, foundation models, responsible AI, and security governance.

What is the passing score?

The passing score for the exam is 700. Candidates must meet or exceed this exact numerical score to successfully earn the certification credential directly from AWS.

How long is the exam?

The certification exam has a strict duration of exactly 90 minutes, during which candidates must complete all 65 total questions within the officially allotted testing time limit.
