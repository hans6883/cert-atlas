What This Exam Validates

The FCC GROL General Radiotelephone Operator License is certified by the Federal Communications Commission. It validates knowledge and skills required for commercial radio operators, covering basic radio law, operating practices, and electronic fundamentals necessary to properly adjust, repair, and maintain radio transmitters and receivers across various regulated telecommunications services with a total of 124 questions.

Who Should Take This Exam

Radio operators, professional technicians, and engineering personnel working in aviation and maritime radio services who need to service, maintain, or operate regulated radio transmitting equipment.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Radio law and regulations
  • Electronic fundamentals
  • Transmitter and receiver maintenance
  • Operating practices

How to Prepare

Carefully review current Federal Communications Commission question pools and official documentation for Element 1 and Element 3. Study electronic fundamentals, radio laws, treaties, regulations, and operating practices for maritime and aviation services extensively before scheduling your test to achieve the passing score of 18 out of 24 on Element 1.

How many questions are on the exam?

The examination consists of a total of 124 questions distributed across the designated written examination elements required by the Federal Communications Commission for commercial operators.

What is the passing score?

The passing score for the certification requires achieving 18 out of 24 on Element 1 and successfully scoring 75 out of 100 on Element 3.
