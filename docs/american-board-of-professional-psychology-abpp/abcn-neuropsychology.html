What This Exam Validates

The ABCN Neuropsychology Board Certification, administered by the American Board of Professional Psychology (ABPP), evaluates professional competence in clinical neuropsychology. The exam covers five domains, including general clinical psychology, psychopathology, neuropathology, and brain-behavior relationships. It assesses the candidate's ability to integrate psychological and neurological knowledge within the practice of clinical neuropsychology, ensuring practitioners meet the standards required for board certification in this area of psychological practice.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is intended for licensed psychologists seeking board recognition in clinical neuropsychology. Candidates should possess training and experience in assessing brain-behavior relationships and managing clinical cases involving neuropathology and psychopathology within a professional practice setting.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • General psychology and statistics
  • General clinical psychology practice
  • Psychopathology assessment
  • Neuropathology assessment
  • Brain-behavior relationship analysis
  • Clinical neuropsychology practice

How to Prepare

Review the five domains of the exam, focusing on the intersection of clinical psychology and neuropathology. Utilize the official exam guides provided by the American Board of Professional Psychology (ABPP) to understand the scope of the 125 questions. Practice applying statistical methodology to clinical data and ensure familiarity with standards for the practice of clinical neuropsychology. Allocate time to study brain-behavior relationships and diagnostic criteria for various neuropathological conditions to prepare for the 180 minutes of testing.

Domain Study Guidance

General Psychology (including statistics and methodology): Study Guidance

This domain covers the application of statistical methods and research methodology within the context of psychological practice and evaluation, ensuring that candidates can interpret data accurately and apply scientific methods to their professional clinical work.

  • Statistical analysis in psychology
  • Research methodology

General Clinical Psychology: Study Guidance

This domain addresses the core principles of general clinical psychology and their application to patient assessment and treatment, focusing on the knowledge required for effective psychological practice across diverse clinical populations and settings.

  • Clinical assessment techniques
  • General psychological practice

General Psychopathology/Neuropathology: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the identification and understanding of psychopathology and neuropathology in clinical populations, requiring candidates to demonstrate proficiency in diagnosing conditions and understanding the underlying manifestations of various neurological and psychological disorders.

  • Psychopathology diagnostic criteria
  • Neuropathology manifestations

Brain-Behavior Relationships: Study Guidance

This domain examines the functional and structural relationships between the brain and human behavior, emphasizing the link between neuroanatomy and observable psychological functioning in both healthy individuals and those with clinical impairments.

  • Brain-behavior correlations
  • Functional neuroanatomy

Practice of Clinical Neuropsychology: Study Guidance

This domain covers the practical application of clinical neuropsychology, including assessment, diagnosis, and intervention strategies, ensuring that practitioners can effectively apply their knowledge to improve patient outcomes in a professional clinical environment.

  • Neuropsychological assessment
  • Clinical practice standards
