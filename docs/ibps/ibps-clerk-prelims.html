What This Exam Validates

The IBPS Clerk Preliminary Examination is administered by IBPS and carefully evaluates candidates across three distinct test domains within a strict sixty-minute timeframe. It features a total of one hundred questions constructed specifically to measure candidate proficiency and capability across core competency areas required for prospective banking clerk roles in various institutions.

Who Should Take This Exam

Candidates seeking banking roles who want to demonstrate standard skills in language, mathematics, and logic should take this exam. It serves as the preliminary stage for aspiring bank clerks.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • English Language proficiency
  • Numerical Ability calculations
  • Simplification and number series
  • Arithmetic problem solving
  • Reasoning Ability logic
  • Inequalities and syllogisms
  • Alphabet and number tests
  • Puzzle solving

How to Prepare

To prepare successfully, practice sample problems across all three subject domains regularly every single week without fail. Focus heavily on managing your time effectively to successfully complete all one hundred questions within the sixty-minute testing limit. Review core concepts in English, arithmetic, and reasoning daily.

Domain Study Guidance

English Language: Study Guidance

This specific domain measures your command of the English Language, assessing reading comprehension abilities, grammatical rules, and vocabulary skills required for effective professional communication within modern banking institutions.

  • Practice reading comprehension and vocabulary exercises
  • Review grammar rules and sentence correction
  • Take practice tests on verbal sections

Numerical Ability (Simplification, Number Series, Arithmetic): Study Guidance

This specific domain tests Numerical Ability, covering simplification exercises, number series sequences, and general arithmetic calculations relevant to performing quantitative tasks accurately and efficiently under strict time pressure.

  • Solve simplification and approximation problems
  • Practice number series and arithmetic word problems
  • Work on data interpretation sets

Reasoning Ability (Inequalities, Syllogism, Alphabet/Number Tests, Puzzles): Study Guidance

This specific domain evaluates Reasoning Ability through logical inequalities, syllogism rules, alphabet tests, number tests, and complex logical puzzles designed to test analytical thinking capabilities and logical deduction skills.

  • Practice syllogism and inequality problems
  • Solve alphabet tests, number tests, and logical puzzles
  • Review coding and decoding concepts

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive at the designated testing center early with your required identification documents. Manage your time carefully across the 100 questions to ensure completion within the 60-minute duration.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

There are exactly 100 total questions distributed across 3 distinct subject domains on the IBPS Clerk Preliminary Examination, which must all be completed within 60 minutes.

What is the passing score?

Passing score criteria and cut-offs are determined directly by IBPS based on overall test performance and available vacancies. Check with IBPS for specific score requirements.

How long is the exam?

The official examination duration is precisely sixty minutes for all one hundred questions across the English Language, Numerical Ability, and Reasoning Ability subject domains during your test attempt.

What is the exam price?

The exam price is ₹850 or approximately $10 USD for general candidates, and ₹175 or approximately $2 USD for SC, ST, PwBD, and Ex-Servicemen candidates.
