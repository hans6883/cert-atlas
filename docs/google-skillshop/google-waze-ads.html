Historical Google-WazeAds Scope

The Waze Ads Fundamentals exam, certified by Google Skillshop, covered Waze platform features, ad formats, campaign targeting, and reporting metrics. This certification exam is retired as of 2023-08-31. There is no official successor exam provided by the certifying body. Learners seeking current information on advertising with Google tools should visit the official Google Skillshop platform to explore available learning paths and current training resources for professional development.

Who Google-WazeAds Was For

This exam was intended for digital marketers, media planners, and advertisers managing location-based campaigns. It required familiarity with mobile advertising strategies and the Waze ecosystem. Current professionals should focus on updated Google advertising training available through official channels.

How to Reuse Your Preparation

Since this exam is retired and has no successor, do not prepare for it. Focus instead on current Google advertising documentation. Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The official exam consisted of a total of 30 questions designed to test your knowledge of advertising concepts, platform features, targeting, and reporting metrics within the Waze ecosystem before it was retired.

What is the passing score?

The required passing score for this certification assessment was set at 80, meaning test-takers needed to achieve this percentage or higher to successfully demonstrate their proficiency in managing campaigns on the platform.

How long is the exam?

The assessment had a total duration of 45 minutes, providing candidates with a strict time limit to complete all of the questions presented during the testing session at the Google Skillshop platform.

Is there a fee to take the exam?

The certification exam was completely free to take for all users, although completing the assessment and accessing the required learning content did require registering and signing into a valid Google account.
