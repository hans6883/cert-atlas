What This Exam Validates

The CMA Intermediate Group II exam is a professional certification administered by ICMAI that evaluates candidates across 4 distinct domains. This assessment covers business areas including operations management, strategic management, cost and management accounting, financial management, indirect taxation, corporate accounting, and auditing. It is designed to test the technical proficiency and analytical capabilities of aspiring management accountants in complex organizational environments while ensuring they meet the standards required for professional practice in the field.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is intended for candidates actively pursuing the CMA designation who have successfully completed Group I and are now advancing their professional accounting and management education through the structured curriculum provided by ICMAI.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Operations management
  • Strategic management
  • Cost accounting
  • Financial management
  • Indirect taxation
  • Corporate accounting
  • Auditing

How to Prepare

To prepare effectively, candidates should review all study materials and official notes provided by ICMAI across the 4 domains. Focus your efforts on mastering operations management, strategic planning, taxation principles, and corporate auditing requirements. Regularly check with ICMAI for any curriculum updates and complete all available practice problems for each subject area to ensure readiness for the testing environment and the 50 point passing score requirement.

Domain Study Guidance

Paper 9: Operations Management and Strategic Management: Study Guidance

This domain covers operations management and strategic management principles, focusing on production planning, operational systems, and strategy implementation to ensure candidates understand the aspects of modern business operations and organizational planning for professional accounting.

  • Operations Management – Introduction
  • Production Planning and Control
  • Strategic Management

Paper 10: Cost and Management Accounting and Financial Management: Study Guidance

This section focuses on cost and management accounting alongside financial management tools for organizational decision making, ensuring that candidates can apply financial theories to real-world business scenarios and corporate financial reporting requirements within the professional accounting sector.

  • Cost and Management Accounting
  • Financial Management
  • Decision Making Tools

Paper 11: Indirect Taxation (GST, Customs, FTP): Study Guidance

This domain addresses indirect taxation including GST, Customs, and Foreign Trade Policy regulations, providing candidates with the knowledge to navigate complex tax landscapes and international trade compliance requirements within the professional accounting field and regulatory frameworks.

  • GST
  • Customs
  • Foreign Trade Policy

Paper 12: Corporate Accounting and Auditing: Study Guidance

This area covers corporate accounting standards and auditing principles required for professional accountants, ensuring that candidates possess the technical expertise to maintain accurate financial records and perform audits in accordance with established standards and corporate governance.

  • Corporate Accounting
  • Auditing
  • Accounting Standards

Exam-Day Guidance

Candidates must manage their time effectively across the 720 minutes allowed for the exam. Ensure you meet the passing score requirement of 50 to successfully achieve your certification and advance your professional career in management accounting.

Frequently asked questions

How many domains are on the exam?

The exam features 4 distinct domains, each carrying a 25.0 percent weight across operations management, financial management, taxation, and corporate accounting to ensure a balanced assessment of your professional knowledge and technical skills.

What is the passing score?

The official passing score for the CMA Intermediate Group II exam is 50, which candidates must achieve to demonstrate their competency and successfully earn their professional certification through the ICMAI examination process.

How long is the exam?

The total duration for the exam is 720 minutes, providing candidates with sufficient time to address all questions across the four core domains covered in the official ICMAI curriculum and testing requirements.

What is the exam fee?

The group exam fee is ₹1,800, which is approximately $22 USD, and candidates should verify these payment details directly through the official ICMAI portal before registering for their scheduled examination session.
