What This Exam Validates

The CGEIT exam thoroughly certifies qualified professionals in enterprise IT governance, managed directly by ISACA. Candidates are evaluated across four distinct domains encompassing governance, IT resources, benefits realization, and risk optimization through a total of 150 multiple choice questions administered during a standard 240-minute testing session.

Who Should Take This Exam

Professionals managing, advising, or assuring enterprise IT governance frameworks. Suitable for managers and practitioners aiming to validate expertise in aligning IT with enterprise goals and optimizing resources.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Governance of Enterprise IT
  • IT Resources
  • Benefits Realization
  • Risk Optimization

How to Prepare

Review the official exam guide and certification page in detail well before your scheduled test date. Focus your daily preparation efforts on the four defined domains, carefully noting their respective weight percentages. Practice multiple choice questions regularly and check with ISACA for the current certification path.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The official exam contains a total of 150 multiple choice questions delivered in a standard linear format designed to evaluate your professional knowledge completely across all required topics.

What is the passing score?

Candidates must achieve a scaled score of 450 out of a scale ranging from 200 to 800 to successfully pass the examination and earn the credential.

How long is the exam?

The exam has a strict total duration of 240 minutes, giving candidates ample time to answer all required questions carefully and thoughtfully during the official testing session.

What does the exam cost?

The standard exam price is $760 for non-members, while active ISACA members pay a reduced preferential price of $575 when registering for the official test.
