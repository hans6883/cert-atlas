What This Exam Validates

The Salesforce Certified Pardot Consultant exam, certified by Salesforce, covers the implementation and management of Account Engagement solutions. It evaluates your ability to configure accounts, automate business processes, manage leads, and personalize prospect experiences. The exam also tests your knowledge of email marketing standards, reporting metrics, and sales alerts. It is a linear exam consisting of 60 questions that assess your technical and strategic proficiency in the platform, ensuring you can effectively align marketing initiatives with business objectives.

Who Should Take This Exam

This exam is designed for consultants who design and implement Account Engagement strategies. Candidates should have experience with Salesforce and Account Engagement landscapes, including data migration, lead qualification, and automation tools, and be responsible for aligning marketing initiatives with business objectives.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Analyzing Salesforce and Account Engagement landscapes
  • Developing data migration and integrity plans
  • Designing lead nurturing and qualification strategies
  • Configuring automation rules and segmentation
  • Implementing email marketing and consent management
  • Setting up reporting and analytics
