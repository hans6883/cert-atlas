What This Exam Validates

The ASE A8 Engine Performance exam certifies professional automotive technicians on complex automotive engine systems and diagnostic procedures. Administered by ASE, it carefully evaluates candidate knowledge across four distinct domains, including ignition systems, fuel and air induction, emissions controls, and detailed engine diagnosis, ensuring technicians meet high industry standards during everyday operations.

Who Should Take This Exam

Automotive technicians seeking professional certification in engine performance repair, analysis, and vehicle diagnosis should take this exam. Candidates require relevant professional experience working with vehicle engines, advanced fuel systems, and emissions equipment.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Ignition system testing
  • Fuel and air induction service
  • Emissions control inspection
  • Engine diagnosis and repair

How to Prepare

Review vehicle emission standards, standard testing procedures, and advanced diagnostic strategies carefully and repeatedly across all designated subjects to ensure success. Practice using the process of elimination on multiple choice questions, study the official blueprint closely, and check with ASE for the current certification path.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

There are exactly 60 questions on the exam, which are delivered in a linear computer-based format. The test includes traditional multiple choice questions alongside complex composite vehicle scenarios.

What is the passing score?

You should check directly with ASE for specific passing score details and official registration requirements for the certification process and subsequent recertification timelines required for maintaining active credentials.

How long is the exam?

The maximum duration of the exam is 75 minutes. Test takers must carefully complete all questions within this allocated and monitored timeframe to ensure proper completion.

How much does the exam cost?

The test fee is approximately $50 plus an additional ASE registration fee of about $36, though exact fees may vary depending on the chosen testing window.
