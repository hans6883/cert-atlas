What This Exam Validates

The Certified Sports Management Professional certification, offered by the Global Association for Quality Management (GAQM), validates expertise in managing sports organizations, facilities, and events. The exam covers five modules, including sports business administration, communication, fitness participation, marketing, and governance. It is designed for professionals seeking to improve their management effectiveness in the sports and recreation industry by mastering operational, administrative, and strategic leadership skills required for success in this competitive global field.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is for sport team, event, tournament, and facility managers. It also suits gym managers, volunteer coordinators, and entry-level practitioners or students interested in building a career in sports management and project leadership within the professional recreation sector.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Sports business administration
  • Communication and customer care
  • Fitness participation and disability management
  • Marketing and tournament planning
  • Governance and ethical behavior

How to Prepare

Candidates should prioritize the official E-Course, as the majority of the 100 questions are drawn directly from this material. While there are no formal prerequisites, completing the E-Course provides the necessary technical know-how. Utilize the provided sample exam to practice the multiple-choice format. Ensure you understand the operational context of each module to apply your knowledge effectively during the 120-minute testing session. Reviewing all module content will help you achieve the 70% passing score required for certification.

Domain Study Guidance

Module 1 - Sports Business Administration: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the administrative requirements of sports organizations, including financial management, record keeping, and facility maintenance. It covers the operational aspects of maintaining health, safety, and security within a sports or recreation facility.

  • Record keeping systems
  • Project finance management
  • Facility health and safety

Module 2 - Communication, Customer Care and Self-development: Study Guidance

This domain addresses communication strategies within business environments and the importance of customer care. It also covers personal development methodologies and techniques for facilitating learning in a professional sports context to improve overall organizational performance.

  • Business communication
  • Customer care practices
  • Personal growth training

Module 3 - Fitness Participation and Disabilities: Study Guidance

This domain covers the management of fitness and sports participation for diverse groups, including individuals with disabilities and those living with HIV/AIDS. It explains physical activity principles relevant to sports and fitness environments for inclusive programming.

  • Managing participants with disabilities
  • Supporting HIV/AIDS awareness in fitness
  • Physical activity principles

Module 4 - Marketing, volunteer management and tournament planning: Study Guidance

This domain explores marketing principles applied to sports, the management of volunteers, and the administration of tournaments. It provides the framework for organizing and executing sports events and promotional activities to ensure successful community engagement and participation.

  • Sports marketing principles
  • Volunteer management
  • Tournament organization

Module 5 - Governance, Structures and Ethical Behaviour in Sport: Study Guidance

This domain covers the governance structures of sports organizations and the implementation of ethical behavior. It emphasizes the promotion of diversity, equity, and discipline within sports and fitness teams to maintain high standards of professional conduct.

  • Governance structures
  • Diversity and equity promotion
  • Team ethical behavior

Exam-Day Guidance

The exam is conducted in an AI-proctored format and can be taken anytime. Ensure you have your voucher code ready to apply through your profile. You have 120 minutes to complete the 100 multiple-choice questions provided during your session.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of 100 multiple-choice questions. Candidates are expected to answer these within the allotted time to demonstrate their competency in sports management and earn their professional certification.

What is the passing score?

The passing score for the exam is 70%. This means you must correctly answer at least 70 out of the 100 questions provided to earn your certification and demonstrate your mastery of the material.

How long is the exam?

The total duration of the exam is 120 minutes. This time limit applies to the completion of all 100 multiple-choice questions in the AI-proctored environment, requiring efficient time management from all candidates.

Is the certification valid for life?

Yes, the Certified Sports Manager certificate is valid for life. Once you pass the exam, you do not need to renew your certification, as it remains a permanent professional credential for your career.
