What This Exam Validates

The American Board of Surgery administers the ABVS Vascular Surgery Board Certification. This exam assesses clinical knowledge across 19 distinct domains, including carotid, aortic, and venous systems, as well as trauma and hemodialysis access. It serves as the standard for verifying professional competence in vascular surgery, ensuring surgeons meet the requirements for practice through a structured evaluation of their diagnostic and procedural expertise during the 360-minute session.

Who Should Take This Exam

Vascular surgeons seeking board certification should take this exam. It is intended for qualified medical professionals who have completed their surgical training and are prepared to demonstrate their clinical proficiency in managing complex vascular diseases and patient care.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Carotid and vertebral artery management
  • Aortic and visceral vascular repair
  • Lower and upper extremity revascularization
  • Venous disease treatment
  • Trauma and hemodialysis access
  • Vascular imaging and medical therapy

How to Prepare

Review the 19 domains to understand the weight of each topic, such as the 12% focus on lower extremity and 11% on aorto-iliac and venous systems. Utilize official content outlines from the American Board of Surgery. Practice with clinical scenarios that mirror the 250 questions on the exam. Focus on high-weight areas while maintaining a broad understanding of vascular medicine, research, and practice management to ensure readiness for the testing environment.
