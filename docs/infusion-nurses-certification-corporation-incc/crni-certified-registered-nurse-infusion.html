What This Exam Validates

The CRNI Certified Registered Nurse Infusion examination is administered by the Infusion Nurses Certification Corporation (INCC). It validates professional knowledge across principles of practice, access devices, and infusion therapies, helping registered nurses demonstrate clinical proficiency by completing a total of 140 questions during a thorough 150-minute testing window established for candidates.

Who Should Take This Exam

Registered nurses working in infusion therapy who want to validate their clinical expertise. Candidates build on professional experience to demonstrate mastery of infusion nursing principles, access device management, and therapy administration.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Principles of practice
  • Access devices
  • Infusion therapies
  • Clinical management
  • Patient care

How to Prepare

Review the official exam guides and reference materials published by the Infusion Nurses Certification Corporation (INCC). Focus your study plan on principles of practice, access devices, and infusion therapies. Complete practice questions to assess your pacing for the 150-minute time limit across all 140 questions.

Domain Study Guidance

Principles of Practice: Study Guidance

Covers the core standards, legal aspects, and standard guidelines governing professional infusion nursing practice while establishing standard benchmarks for quality patient care and professional accountability in modern healthcare settings today.

  • Practice standards
  • Legal considerations

Access Devices: Study Guidance

Focuses on the selection, insertion, maintenance, and troubleshooting of vascular access devices used in patient care, ensuring registered nurses understand proper management protocols and safety procedures during every clinical procedure.

  • Device selection
  • Maintenance procedures

Infusion Therapies: Study Guidance

Addresses the administration, management, and clinical monitoring associated with various complex infusion therapies, preparing clinicians to handle medications, fluids, and patient responses safely throughout the entire course of treatment.

  • Clinical monitoring

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive early for your test appointment with required identification. Manage your time carefully across the 140 questions during the 150-minute testing window to ensure successful completion.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The examination consists of a total of 140 questions covering multiple domains of infusion nursing, including principles of practice, access devices, and infusion therapies for clinical candidates.

What is the passing score?

The initial base form passing point uses the Angoff method to be approved by September 2025. Future passing points are established through pre-equating procedures by the organization.

How long is the exam?

You are given a total duration of 150 minutes to complete all of the questions presented on the official certification test administered by the governing board.

Who administers the examination?

The certification is administered by the Infusion Nurses Certification Corporation (INCC), which provides official guides and references for candidates preparing to test during the scheduled window.
