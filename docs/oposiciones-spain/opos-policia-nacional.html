What This Exam Validates

The Oposiciones Policía Nacional Escala Básica certification examination is administered by Oposiciones Spain to evaluate prospective candidates. This evaluation covers Spanish Constitutional Law, Criminal Law and Procedure, Administrative Law and Police Regulations, English Language proficiency, IT and Computing Basics, and the rich Geography, History, and Culture of Spain across multiple domains.

Who Should Take This Exam

Candidates aiming to join the National Police force in Spain through the Escala Básica entry path. Applicants must prepare for the application fee and the multi-domain syllabus.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Constitutional Law
  • Criminal Law and Procedure
  • Administrative Law
  • English Language B1
  • IT and Computing Basics
  • Geography and History

How to Prepare

Review the six distinct examination domains carefully, focusing equal attention on law topics, administrative regulations, and general knowledge. Practice reading comprehension and translation exercises for English, and review basic computing principles. Check with Oposiciones Spain for the current certification path and official updates regularly to ensure full alignment with all published requirements.

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive fully prepared for a 90-minute examination containing exactly 100 questions. Manage your time effectively across all six domains to achieve the required passing score of 5.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The official examination consists of exactly 100 questions distributed across the six distinct domains. Candidates are given a total of 90 minutes to complete the entire test.

What is the passing score?

The established passing score for the Oposiciones Policía Nacional — Escala Básica certification exam is 5. Candidates must meet or exceed this score to successfully pass the examination process.

How long is the exam?

The official examination duration is exactly 90 minutes for answering all questions across the entire multi-domain syllabus provided in the official documentation for applicants taking this test.

What is the application fee for the exam?

The required application fee is €24.40, which is approximately $27 USD, as specifically detailed and specified in the official convocatoria documentation published for applicants.
