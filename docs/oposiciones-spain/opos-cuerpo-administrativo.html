What This Exam Validates

The Cuerpo Administrativo de la Administración del Estado certification program is managed by Oposiciones Spain to evaluate candidates across 5 core domains. These domains cover administrative topics including constitutional law, administrative procedures, information technology systems, European Union policies, and Spanish language spelling and document drafting skills necessary for public service roles.

Who Should Take This Exam

Candidates seeking administrative roles within the Spanish State Administration should take this exam. It requires preparation across public law, IT systems, European institutions, and document drafting.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Constitutional Law
  • Administrative Procedure
  • Information Technology
  • European Union Policies
  • Spanish Language Proficiency

How to Prepare

To prepare for this certification exam, candidates must review Spanish constitutional law, administrative procedures, and official European Union institutions. Furthermore, you should dedicate significant time to practicing the drafting of administrative documents in Spanish while studying information technology regulations, office software systems, and cybersecurity principles.

This specific domain covers Spanish Constitutional Law and Administrative Organization, including an examination of the Constitución, the LRJSP framework, and the Statute of the Administration to ensure legal compliance.

Information Technology (Office 365, LOPD/RGPD, e-Government, Cybersecurity): Study Guidance

European Union: Institutions, Treaties, Policies: Study Guidance

This specific international domain examines the European Union regarding its governing institutions, treaties, and cooperative policy initiatives that impact national administrative procedures and public sector legal frameworks across member states.

This specific linguistic domain extensively tests Spanish Language proficiency regarding fundamental spelling rules, grammatical correctness, and the formal drafting procedures required for official administrative documents within state administration public offices.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The official exam contains a total of 100 questions covering 5 distinct domains of law, technology, European policies, and Spanish language skills to assess candidate competency during the evaluation.

What is the passing score?

The established passing score required to successfully clear the Cuerpo Administrativo de la Administración del Estado certification evaluation is precisely 5 points according to official guidelines provided by the administrators.

How long is the exam?

The official exam duration is strictly 90 minutes, giving candidates a limited time frame to complete and submit all 100 questions successfully before the timer expires during the testing session.

What is the application price?

The standard exam application price notes are €18.06, which is approximately equivalent to $20 USD per individual application submitted through the designated official processing channels managed by the organization.
