What This Exam Validates

The Oposiciones Guardia Civil — Escala de Cabos y Guardias certification is certified by Oposiciones Spain for aspiring personnel. Candidates tackle a detailed 90-minute exam consisting of 100 questions. The thorough test evaluates core competencies across 6 domains covering Spanish Constitutional Law, Civil Guard Organic Law, Criminal Law, Road Traffic Law, geography, history, and physical tests.

Who Should Take This Exam

Candidates seeking a role as a Cabo or Guardia in the Spanish Civil Guard must take this test. Applicants prepare for the official convocatoria with an application fee of €0 while demonstrating proficiency in law, geography, and physical conditioning.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Spanish Constitutional Law
  • Civil Guard Organic Law
  • Criminal Law Offences
  • Road Traffic Law
  • Spanish Geography and History
  • First Aid and Physical Tests

How to Prepare

Review Spanish Constitutional Law and Fundamental Rights alongside the Civil Guard Organic Law and administrative structure carefully. Study criminal offences and road traffic regulations in detail before the test date. Train extensively for the physical tests, including the Cooper test, swimming, chin-ups, and sprint, while maintaining physical readiness for the examination.

Domain Study Guidance

Spanish Constitutional Law and Fundamental Rights (Art. 1–55 CE): Study Guidance

This primary domain covers Articles 1 through 55 of the Spanish Constitution, focusing heavily on fundamental rights, public liberties, and constitutional principles governing the State and its primary democratic institutions.

  • Articles 1 to 55 CE
  • Fundamental rights and public liberties

Civil Guard Organic Law and Structure (Ley Orgánica 11/2007, LOFCS 2/1986): Study Guidance

This domain focuses intensely on the organizational framework, operational guidelines, and legal foundations of the Civil Guard under Ley Orgánica 11/2007 and LOFCS 2/1986 for all prospective officers.

  • Ley Orgánica 11/2007
  • LOFCS 2/1986

Criminal Law: Offences Against Persons, Property, Public Order (Código Penal): Study Guidance

This specific segment examines criminal legislation addressing specific offences against persons, property, and public order as clearly outlined in the official Código Penal provisions established for law enforcement candidates.

  • Offences against persons
  • Offences against property and public order

Road Traffic Law (Ley de Tráfico, Reglamento General de Circulación): Study Guidance

This area pertains directly to road safety regulations, traffic legislation, and the general circulation rules governing vehicle and pedestrian movement across public roadways as mandated by national transit laws.

  • Ley de Tráfico
  • Reglamento General de Circulación

Spanish Geography, History and Democratic Values: Study Guidance

This subject focuses completely on the national territory, historical milestones, and democratic values of Spain as a vital component of the general knowledge requirements mandated for all candidates taking the test.

  • Spanish geography
  • Spanish history and democratic values

First Aid, Physical Tests (Cooper Test, Swimming, Chin-ups, Sprint): Study Guidance

This specific module evaluates medical response techniques and athletic performance through detailed physical assessments including sprinting, swimming, chin-ups, and the Cooper test to ensure optimal candidate fitness levels for the corps.

  • First aid procedures
  • Cooper test, swimming, chin-ups, and sprint

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive prepared to complete 100 questions within the 90 minutes allowed. Manage your time effectively across all subjects to secure a passing score of 5.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The official test contains exactly 100 questions drawn across 6 weighted domains covering constitutional law, criminal law, traffic law, geography, and physical tests for candidates.

What is the passing score?

Candidates must achieve a minimum passing score of 5 in order to successfully complete the 90-minute examination administered by Oposiciones Spain during the convocatoria process.

How long is the exam?

The official exam duration is strictly set to 90 minutes, during which candidates must answer all 100 questions within the specified time limit for the test.

Is there an application fee for the exam?

There is no application fee for the Guardia Civil convocatoria, listed as €0 under current practice. Check with Oposiciones Spain for further details on the certification path.
