What This Exam Validates

The PSI Contractor License Exam — General Building B (PSI-GCB), certified by NASCLA, professionally tests competence across eight distinct domains of construction practice. Candidates sit for a 100-question computer-based examination lasting 180 minutes at authorized PSI testing centers, evaluating key expertise across business administration, project management, estimating, building codes, materials, safety, contract law, and financial management principles.

Who Should Take This Exam

General building contractors and construction professionals pursuing licensure in jurisdictions requiring the PSI-GCB credential. Candidates must demonstrate practical knowledge in project management, legal compliance, and field safety practices.

How to Prepare

Review official NASCLA study documentation and relevant code sections extensively. Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The computer-based examination consists of exactly 100 multiple-choice questions that are delivered directly via computer terminals located at authorized PSI testing center locations nationwide for candidates.

What is the passing score?

The required passing score is established as a percentage, specifically set at 70% for most contractor exams, although exact state licensing requirements may vary slightly depending on jurisdiction.

How long is the exam?

Registered candidates are officially allotted a total duration of 180 minutes to complete all questions during their scheduled testing appointment at an authorized PSI testing center facility.

What is the cost to take the exam?

The standard exam price is $55.0, which must be paid directly when registering for your computer-based appointment at a designated testing facility before taking the test.
