What This Exam Validates

The ACA Professional Level: Business Planning: Taxation exam, certified by ICAEW, covers five key tax domains. It evaluates technical knowledge across corporation tax, VAT, income tax, capital gains tax, and stamp taxes. This written paper is a component of the professional qualification, requiring candidates to demonstrate application of tax rules to business scenarios. The exam is scheduled for retirement on 2026-09-30, with the Fundamental Case Study serving as the official successor. Candidates must demonstrate proficiency across these areas to progress in their professional development journey.

Who Should Take This Exam

This exam is for professional-level candidates pursuing the ACA qualification. It is intended for those who have completed earlier stages and are ready to apply tax legislation to business planning and owner-managed entity scenarios.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Corporation tax compliance
  • VAT rules and procedures
  • Income tax for owner-managed businesses
  • Capital gains tax application
  • Stamp taxes and other levies

How to Prepare Before Retirement

Review the official ICAEW study documentation to understand the tax rules and procedures. Practice applying these regulations to business scenarios, as the exam is a written paper. Ensure familiarity with the five domains, focusing on the 35% weight assigned to Corporation Tax. Candidates should prepare for the 210-minute duration and aim to meet the 55 passing score. Direct your future studies toward the Fundamental Case Study to ensure your certification path remains aligned with current ICAEW requirements.

Domain Study Guidance

Corporation Tax: Study Guidance

This domain covers the taxation of companies, including self-assessment, distributions, and group structures. It represents 35% of the exam content, focusing on the application of tax rules to corporate entities and their financial activities during the assessment.

  • Corporation Tax self-assessment
  • Group and consortia tax rules

Value Added Tax (VAT): Study Guidance

This domain addresses VAT rules and procedures, accounting for 20% of the exam. It focuses on the mechanics of VAT, including registration, taxable supplies, and the administration of the tax system for businesses within the current regulatory framework.

  • VAT registration requirements
  • Input and output tax accounting

Income Tax and National Insurance (for owner-managed businesses): Study Guidance

Focusing on owner-managed businesses, this domain accounts for 20% of the exam. It covers the interaction between business income and personal tax liabilities, including National Insurance contributions for business owners operating within the professional tax environment.

  • Income tax for business owners
  • National Insurance contributions

Capital Gains Tax (CGT) for individuals and companies: Study Guidance

This domain covers Capital Gains Tax for individuals and companies, representing 15% of the exam. It involves calculating gains and losses and applying relevant reliefs and exemptions in a business context to ensure accurate tax reporting and compliance.

  • Individual capital gains
  • Corporate capital gains

Stamp Taxes and Other Taxes: Study Guidance

This domain accounts for 10% of the exam and covers Stamp Taxes and other miscellaneous taxes. It requires knowledge of when these taxes apply to business transactions and property dealings to maintain full compliance with current legislation.

  • Stamp Duty application
  • Other relevant business taxes

Exam-Day Guidance

The exam is a written paper lasting 210 minutes. Candidates must manage their time effectively across the five domains. Ensure you have your identification and required materials ready for the session, which costs approximately £300–£500 per sitting.
