What This Exam Validates

The FEMA NIMS ICS-300 Intermediate ICS for Expanding Incidents exam, certified by the FEMA Emergency Management Institute, evaluates knowledge of incident management systems. It covers the application of the Incident Command System for expanding incidents, including resource management, planning processes, and unified command structures. This certification ensures personnel can coordinate efforts effectively during complex incidents by utilizing standardized protocols and procedures established by the FEMA Emergency Management Institute for all emergency responders across the 8 domains of the course, which requires mastery of 4 distinct source guides to ensure operational success.

Who Should Take This Exam

This exam is intended for emergency responders, emergency management personnel, and officials responsible for incident decision-making. Candidates should have experience in incident management and a working knowledge of NIMS fundamentals to effectively apply these concepts to expanding incidents.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Course introduction and overview
  • ICS fundamentals review
  • Unified command initial actions
  • Operational process implementation
  • Planning and IAP development
  • Incident resource management
  • Demobilization and recovery transition
