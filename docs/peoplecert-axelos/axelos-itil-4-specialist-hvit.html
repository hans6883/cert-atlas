What This Exam Validates

The ITIL 4 Specialist: High Velocity IT (HVIT) exam, certified by PeopleCert (AXELOS), focuses on the digital product lifecycle and the high-velocity nature of modern organizations. It covers four distinct domains, including approaches, models, and safety culture. This linear, 40-question exam is part of the ITIL 4 framework, though it is currently scheduled for retirement on 2027-12-31, with ITIL (Version 5) modules serving as the official successor path for all current and future certification candidates.

Who Should Take This Exam

This exam is intended for IT professionals managing digital products and services. It is particularly suitable for those working in high-velocity environments who need to understand technical practices, safety culture, and the digital product lifecycle. Candidates should have experience with ITIL 4 concepts before attempting this certification.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Digital organization dynamics
  • High-velocity models
  • Safety culture implementation
  • Technical practice integration
  • Digital product lifecycle management

How to Prepare Before Retirement

Review the official PeopleCert (AXELOS) study documentation to master the four domains. Since this is a 40-question linear exam, practice applying concepts to real-world scenarios. You have 90 minutes to complete the test, so focus on time management. Ensure you are prepared for the 70 percent passing score requirement. As the exam retires on 2027-12-31, prioritize your studies or consider the ITIL (Version 5) modules as your next step.
