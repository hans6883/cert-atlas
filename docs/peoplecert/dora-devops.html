What This Exam Validates

The DORA DevOps certification exam extensively certifies your professional knowledge of DevOps capabilities, practices, and related frameworks. Administered by PeopleCert, the test evaluates important concepts across eight distinct domains, ensuring candidates understand core principles, automation mechanisms, continuous integration models, and organizational cultural frameworks required for modern software delivery across diverse enterprise environments.

Who Should Take This Exam

Professionals involved in software delivery, operations, or development who want to validate their knowledge of DORA practices, continuous integration, and deployment automation. Candidates should prepare to answer 40 questions in 60 minutes.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Exploring DevOps
  • Core DevOps Principles
  • Key DevOps Practices
  • Business and Technology Frameworks
  • Culture, Behaviours, Operating Models
  • Automation, Architecting DevOps Toolchains

How to Prepare

Review all eight exam domains with absolute diligence to master the core concepts. Focus your study efforts on continuous integration, deployment automation, and trunk-based development principles derived directly from the official PeopleCert materials. Practice answering sample questions under timed conditions to successfully complete the 40 questions within the allocated 60 minutes.

This particular domain examines key DevOps practices such as continuous integration, deployment automation, and trunk-based development designed to significantly improve software delivery stability and speed across engineering pipelines.

This informative domain explores vital business and technology frameworks that support organizational goals, governance models, and align technology capabilities directly with overall software delivery performance and enterprise value.

  • Framework integration

  • Team collaboration

Automation, Architecting DevOps Toolchains: Study Guidance

This technical domain details advanced automation strategies and the architecture of DevOps toolchains designed to eliminate manual steps, reduce bottlenecks, and accelerate overall software delivery cycles efficiently across systems.

This analytical domain focuses heavily on measurement, key metrics, and reporting mechanisms used to track delivery speed, software stability, and operational performance across enterprise systems in the industry.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The official certification exam contains exactly 40 questions in total. Candidates must complete all of these multiple-choice questions within the allocated 60 minutes while successfully meeting the passing score requirement of 65%.

What is the passing score?

The official passing score required to pass the exam is 65%. Candidates must achieve this specific percentage across the 40 test questions to successfully earn the DORA DevOps certification credential.

How long is the exam?

The total exam duration is strictly set to 60 minutes. During this time window, candidates are required to answer 40 multiple-choice questions covering all eight distinct domains of the syllabus.

What certifying body administers the exam?

The certification exam is officially administered by PeopleCert. For long-term certification path details, rules, and renewal requirements, check directly with PeopleCert for the current path.
