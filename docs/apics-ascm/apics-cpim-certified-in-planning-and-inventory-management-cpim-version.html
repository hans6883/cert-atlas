What This Exam Validates

The APICS Certified in Planning and Inventory Management certification evaluates professional knowledge across nine domains in supply chain and operations. Certified by APICS (ASCM), this credential focuses on operational functions including demand planning, master scheduling, material requirements planning, inventory control, distribution management, and continuous improvement strategies across modern enterprise networks.

Who Should Take This Exam

Supply chain professionals, inventory managers, material planners, and operations personnel seeking to validate their knowledge of planning, scheduling, procurement, and inventory control processes within organizational operations.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Supply Chains and Strategy
  • Sales and Operations Planning
  • Demand Management
  • Internal Supply
  • External Supply
  • Inventory Planning
  • Detailed Schedules
  • Distribution

How to Prepare

Review the official content outline carefully to define the full scope of the material before testing. Study each content area thoroughly using suggested references, master key terminology, and apply outlined tools, processes, and techniques to your preparation routine prior to taking the official certification examination to ensure complete readiness across all required subjects.

Domain Study Guidance

Supply Chains and Strategy: Study Guidance

This specific domain covers organizational supply chain environments, environmental analysis, strategic scope, strategy development processes, overall performance monitoring, and key performance indicators for business success in operational environments.

  • Supply chains, environment, and strategy
  • Environmental analysis and strategic scope
  • Developing and managing organizational strategy
  • Performance monitoring and KPIs

Sales and Operations Planning (S&OP): Study Guidance

This domain focuses directly upon sales and operations planning purposes, aggregate demand plans, aggregate supply plans, and effectively reconciling sales and operations plans across your entire organization to achieve strategic alignment.

  • S&OP purpose and process
  • Aggregate demand and supply plans
  • Reconciling S&OP plans

Demand: Study Guidance

This section addresses demand management processes, various sources of demand, advanced forecasting methods, and evaluating overall forecast performance within the operational framework established by industry standards to support planning.

  • Demand management
  • Sources of demand and forecasting
  • Forecast performance

Internal Supply: Study Guidance

This segment covers functional operational strategies, facility environments and layouts, master scheduling, rough-cut capacity planning, material requirements planning, and additional capacity requirements planning principles for modern manufacturing organizations.

  • Functional and operational strategies
  • Environments, types, and layouts
  • Master scheduling
  • Material requirements planning

External Supply and Key Support Functions: Study Guidance

Candidates examine suppliers, procurement practices, purchasing maintenance, risk management, sustainability strategies, the waste hierarchy, and reverse logistics operations required for supply chain resilience and modern business continuity planning throughout every organizational level.

  • Suppliers and procurement
  • Risk management
  • Sustainability strategies
  • Waste hierarchy and reverse logistics

Inventory: Study Guidance

This module focuses extensively on inventory planning, inventory costs, basic accounting principles, costing metrics, inventory management techniques, and effective inventory control systems utilized within warehouse and operational facilities.

  • Inventory planning
  • Inventory costs and basic accounting
  • Inventory management
  • Inventory control

Detailed Schedules: Study Guidance

This content covers planning detailed schedules, scheduling and production activity control methods, creating reliable production and service schedules, and scheduling materials efficiently to support manufacturing and delivery timelines.

  • Planning detailed schedules
  • Scheduling and PAC methods
  • Creating production and service schedules
  • Managing detailed schedules

Distribution: Study Guidance

This domain addresses planning distribution operations, replenishment strategies, order management, tracking procedures, tracing methods, and distribution key performance indicators to optimize logistics networks and fulfill customer orders efficiently.

  • Planning distribution
  • Replenishment
  • Order management
  • Trace, track, and KPIs

Quality, Business Investments and Continuous Improvement: Study Guidance

This final extensive domain examines quality control, capital equipment investments, facilities management, advanced technology adoption strategies, and continuous improvement methodologies to drive operational excellence across the entire supply chain enterprise network.

  • Quality
  • Capital equipment and facilities
  • Technology
  • Continuous improvement

Exam-Day Guidance

Read all directions carefully before answering questions. Approach questions in order, utilize the provided time limit efficiently, and answer every question since there is no penalty for incorrect answers.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The examination consists of a total of 150 questions, which includes pretest questions used for statistical purposes that do not contribute to your final score.

What is the passing score?

The minimum required passing score to successfully pass this certification test is 300 points, measured on a scaled score range extending from 200 to 350 points.

How long is the exam?

Candidates taking this certification are given a strict 210-minute time limit to complete all portions of the official examination according to the established testing guidelines.

What version of the exam content manual applies?

The examination follows version 9.0 of the APICS exam content manual, which features nine content domains spanning supply chain strategy, planning, inventory, and operations.
