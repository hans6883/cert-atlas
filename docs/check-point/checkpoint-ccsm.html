What This Exam Validates

The CCSM Elite certification exam, provided by Check Point, is designed to evaluate the advanced technical proficiency of security professionals. This extensive assessment covers domains including complex firewall administration, high availability configurations, advanced threat prevention strategies, and performance tuning. Candidates must demonstrate deep expertise in managing enterprise-grade security environments to successfully achieve this industry certification.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is intended for experienced security engineers and network administrators who are responsible for managing complex Check Point security environments and need to validate their advanced configuration, high availability, and troubleshooting capabilities in real-world scenarios.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Advanced Firewall Administration
  • Advanced VPN Configuration
  • ClusterXL and High Availability
  • Advanced Threat Prevention
  • Performance Tuning and Troubleshooting

How to Prepare

To prepare effectively, candidates should carefully review official Check Point administration guides that detail ClusterXL, CoreXL, and advanced gateway deployments. It is highly recommended to practice configuring virtual MAC modes, state synchronization, and performance tuning commands on actual equipment. Ensure you are comfortable with the command line interface and troubleshooting methodologies before attempting the 90-minute exam to maximize your chances of success.

Domain Study Guidance

Advanced Firewall Administration: Study Guidance

This domain covers advanced firewall administration principles, focusing on managing complex rulebases and inspecting traffic through multi-core instances to ensure optimal security posture and efficient packet processing across the entire network infrastructure.

  • CoreXL firewall instances
  • Secure Network Distributor settings

Advanced VPN Configuration: Study Guidance

This section focuses on advanced VPN configurations, handling third-party peers in hub and spoke deployments, and managing site-to-site connectivity to maintain secure and reliable communication channels across diverse and geographically distributed enterprise network environments.

  • VPN tunnels with third-party peers
  • Hub and spoke VPN deployments

ClusterXL and High Availability: Study Guidance

This domain addresses ClusterXL and high availability deployments, synchronization mechanisms, and cluster failover processes, ensuring that candidates understand how to maintain continuous network availability and service reliability during hardware or software failure events.

  • Cluster Control Protocol settings
  • High availability and load sharing modes

Advanced Threat Prevention: Study Guidance

This area encompasses advanced threat prevention mechanisms, software blade inspections, and network protection configurations, providing the knowledge required to identify, analyze, and mitigate sophisticated cyber threats in real-time within a protected network environment.

  • Threat prevention administration
  • Real-time malware detection

Performance Tuning and Troubleshooting: Study Guidance

This domain deals with performance tuning, monitoring tools, and troubleshooting cluster issues using command line references, enabling administrators to optimize system throughput and resolve complex technical problems that may arise during daily operations.

  • ClusterXL monitoring commands
  • Troubleshooting bond interfaces

Exam-Day Guidance

Manage your time effectively across the 60 multiple choice items during the 90-minute appointment. Read each prompt carefully and utilize your knowledge of Check Point CLI tools and administration guides to ensure accurate responses.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The certification exam consists of exactly 60 multiple choice questions that are delivered in a linear format, requiring candidates to answer each question sequentially during the allotted time.

What is the passing score?

To successfully earn the certification, candidates must achieve a passing score of 70% or higher on the exam, reflecting a strong mastery of the required Check Point security technologies.

How long is the exam?

You will have a total of 90 minutes to complete all of the questions presented on the examination, so it is important to pace yourself throughout the session.

How much does the exam cost?

The standard price for this certification exam is 350.0 USD, which covers the cost of the assessment and the associated administrative processing for your professional certification record.
