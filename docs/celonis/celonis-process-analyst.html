Historical CCPA Scope

The Celonis Certified Process Analyst certification, offered by Celonis, validates your technical knowledge and practical skills across five distinct domains. Candidates must answer a total of 60 questions within a timeframe of 90 minutes. The assessment covers advanced process analysis, Process Query Language queries, process conformance evaluation, business transformation cases, and data integration capabilities.

Who CCPA Was For

This certification suits process analysts, data analysts, and technical professionals working with the Celonis platform who need to demonstrate proficiency in querying and transforming business processes.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Advanced Process Analysis
  • Process Query Language (PQL)
  • Process Conformance and Variants
  • Business Transformation Cases
  • Data Connectivity and Integration

How to Reuse Your Preparation

To prepare successfully for your scheduled test date, review the official Celonis documentation regarding advanced Process Query Language functions, data integration connectors, and process conformance rules. Practice building queries and analyzing workflow data models regularly to meet the required 72 percent passing score on your exam day.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The certification exam consists of 60 multiple choice and multiple select questions delivered in a linear online proctored format for registered candidates participating in the assessment program.

What is the passing score?

Candidates must achieve a minimum passing score of 72 percent to successfully earn the Celonis Certified Process Analyst certification credential upon completing the test successfully.

How long is the exam?

You are given a total duration of 90 minutes to complete all 60 questions presented on the linear online proctored certification exam without any exceptions permitted.

How much does the exam cost?

The standard exam registration fee is 150.0 dollars, and the obtained certification requires renewal after a period of 2 years to maintain an active status.
