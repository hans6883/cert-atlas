Historical CCAPA Scope

The Celonis Certified Associate Process Analyst certification exam evaluates candidate competence across process mining fundamentals, Celonis platform navigation, data models, OLAP cubes, analysis, KPI building, action flows, and recommendations. Certified by Celonis, this online proctored exam consists of exactly 60 questions and requires candidates to demonstrate practical knowledge across five distinct certification domains.

Who CCAPA Was For

This certification suits data analysts, process analysts, and professionals working with process mining who need to validate their skills in navigating the Celonis platform and building analytics.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam contains a total of 60 questions formatted as multiple choice and multiple select items, covering all five designated domains across the Celonis platform.

What is the passing score?

The passing score required to pass the exam is 70. Candidates must successfully meet or exceed this established numerical score across all questions to earn the credential.

How long is the exam?

The duration of the online proctored exam is exactly 90 minutes. You must complete all multiple choice and multiple select questions within this strict time frame.

How much does the exam cost?

The exam price is USD 150.0. Certification validity is 2 years, after which renewal is required. Check with Celonis for any updates regarding the current certification path.
