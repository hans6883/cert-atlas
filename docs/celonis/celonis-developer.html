Historical CCD Scope

The Celonis Certified Developer exam, certified by Celonis, evaluates technical expertise across 5 distinct domains. Candidates face 60 questions within a 90 minute window. The assessment requires a passing score of 72 percent and tests core competencies in platform architecture, data extraction, app building, advanced PQL queries, and action automation workflows.

Who CCD Was For

Technical professionals and developers working with the Celonis platform who design data pipelines, build custom applications, construct advanced PQL expressions, and implement process automation solutions.

  • Data Connectivity and Extraction

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The certification exam consists of a total of 60 questions presented in a linear online proctored format utilizing multiple choice and multiple select question types to test candidates comprehensively.

What is the passing score?

Candidates must achieve a minimum passing score of 72 percent in order to successfully pass the certification exam and demonstrate technical competency across all domains assessed by Celonis.

How long is the exam?

You are given a generous period of 90 minutes to complete all questions within the online proctored exam environment before the testing session officially comes to an end.

What is the cost of the exam?

The exam registration fee is priced at 150.0 US dollars. Please note that certification validity lasts for a period of 2 years, after which a formal renewal is required.
