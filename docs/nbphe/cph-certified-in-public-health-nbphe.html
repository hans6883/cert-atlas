What This Exam Validates

The CPH: Certified in Public Health examination is administered by the National Board of Public Health Examiners. It measures professional proficiency across 10 distinct domains, assessing public health knowledge, methodologies, and practical applications through an assessment consisting of exactly 200 questions administered within a total time limit of 240 minutes.

Who Should Take This Exam

Public health professionals, students, and practitioners seeking certification in public health principles, practices, and policies. It is suitable for individuals with experience across public health domains looking to validate their expertise.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Data, Analytics, and Informatics
  • Communication
  • Leadership
  • Law and Ethics
  • Disease and Injury Prevention
  • Community Engagement and Partnerships

  • Health Equity and Social Justice

How to Prepare

Review all domain content outlines carefully and focus your study time on specific knowledge areas requiring improvement. Utilize practice exams and available official study resources to prepare for the format, pacing, and overall structure of the 240-minute examination covering public health topics and ensuring success.

Domain Study Guidance

Data, Analytics, and Informatics: Study Guidance

This specific domain comprehensively covers statistical and methodology tools used for data collection, quantitative analysis, professional interpretation, and accurate presentation of public health information across diverse community populations and settings.

  • Differentiate between primary and secondary data
  • Conduct descriptive statistics commonly used to summarize data
  • Create and interpret data visualizations

Communication: Study Guidance

This domain addresses effective communication strategies and methods for interacting clearly with varied populations, enhancing public understanding, and improving overall health literacy within different community stakeholder groups.

  • Assess health literacy of populations served
  • Incorporate culturally appropriate approaches into communications
  • Translate data for dissemination across audiences

Leadership: Study Guidance

This specific domain focuses on strategic planning concepts, organizational decision-making frameworks, worker motivation techniques, and effective team building practices necessary for successful public health management and general operational success.

  • Prioritize and justify allocation of resources
  • Apply conflict management skills
  • Implement team building skills and strategies

Law and Ethics: Study Guidance

This domain carefully considers ethical and legal principles and their direct application to professional practice, scientific research, and population-based policy development within the broader public health sector across many jurisdictions.

  • Identify regulations regarding privacy and security
  • Apply ethical analysis and principles to research
  • Identify and manage conflicts of interest

Disease and Injury Prevention: Study Guidance

This domain covers evidenced-based biological concepts and important multifactorial parameters related to both infectious and non-infectious diseases affecting community health and population safety across various global regions and health agency frameworks.

  • Identify health hazards and assess frequency and impact
  • Identify risk and protective factors for infectious diseases
  • Utilize public health emergency management principles

Community Engagement and Partnerships: Study Guidance

This domain involves systematically identifying community stakeholders and developing strategic partnerships to address specific population health needs and foster collaborative environments for effective disease prevention initiatives and ongoing health programs.

  • Identify opportunities to partner across sectors
  • Engage key stakeholders for implementation
  • Engage the community in collective actions to improve health

Program Planning and Evaluation: Study Guidance

This domain addresses evaluation plans, quantitative and qualitative research methods, and structured program implementation frameworks designed specifically for evaluating health policies and community interventions across various populations and geographical regions.

  • Develop and conduct formative, process, and outcome evaluations
  • Apply project management principles
  • Design public health interventions with a health equity lens

Program and Resource Management: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on developing organizational budgets, securing diverse financial resources, and successfully implementing structured community health plans to maximize operational efficiency and overall program sustainability in public health organizations.

  • Develop budgets with justifications
  • Manage operations with current resources
  • Implement a Community Health Assessment

Policy and Advocacy: Study Guidance

This domain covers developing and defending public policy positions based on scientific evidence, stakeholder input, and existing regulatory frameworks relevant to modern public health legislation and governance structures across multiple jurisdictions.

  • Navigate the governmental policy-making process
  • Defend existing health policies and programs
  • Analyze political and economic policies for impact

Health Equity and Social Justice: Study Guidance

This domain applies a social-ecological model to analyze population health issues and systematically address persistent health disparities and systemic inequities across diverse communities using established public health frameworks and equity principles.

  • Design culturally appropriate needs assessments
  • Identify and address causes of systemic racism
  • Apply social justice principles

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive prepared for a 240-minute testing session. Manage your time efficiently across the 200 questions to ensure completion of all sections within the allotted period.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The certification examination consists of exactly 200 questions covering the 10 defined domains of public health practice, theory, and administrative strategy in great detail for candidates.

What is the passing score?

Candidates should check directly with NBPHE for specific details regarding the established passing score and related scoring policies for this particular professional certification examination during their application.

How long is the exam?

The examination has a total duration of 240 minutes, providing candidates with sufficient time to thoughtfully complete all multiple-choice questions included in the test format.

What body administers the certification?

The professional certification is administered by NBPHE, which routinely conducts regular job task analyses to update and maintain the relevant content outline for all potential certification candidates.
