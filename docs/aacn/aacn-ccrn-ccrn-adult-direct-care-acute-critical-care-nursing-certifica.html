What This Exam Validates

The American Association of Critical-Care Nurses administers the adult direct care certification examination specifically for registered nurses providing direct care to acutely and critically ill adult patients. The test evaluates clinical judgment across multiple complex body systems and professional caring and ethical practice during a total duration of 180 minutes.

Who Should Take This Exam

Registered nurses and advanced practice registered nurses providing direct care to acutely and critically ill adult patients who meet specific clinical practice hour requirements established by the American Association of Critical-Care Nurses.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Clinical Judgment
  • Professional Caring and Ethical Practice
  • Multisystem Care Management
  • Patient Advocacy and Moral Agency

How to Prepare

Carefully review the official American Association of Critical-Care Nurses exam handbook and test plan to understand all requirements before your scheduled test date. Utilize the available online practice examination resources for a realistic test-taking experience and consult the detailed exam bibliography for all reference materials necessary for success.

Domain Study Guidance

I. Clinical Judgment — Cardiovascular: Study Guidance

This domain covers cardiovascular clinical judgment topics including acute coronary syndromes, thoracic and abdominal aortic aneurysms, dissections, ruptures, and various cardiac infection and inflammatory diseases managed by nurses providing care to adult patients.

  • Acute coronary syndrome
  • Aortic aneurysm, dissection, rupture
  • Cardiac infection and inflammatory diseases

I. Clinical Judgment — Respiratory: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on critical respiratory clinical judgment such as acute pulmonary edema, acute pulmonary embolus, and acute respiratory distress syndrome to ensure proper evaluation of patient respiratory status in acute care settings.

  • Acute pulmonary edema
  • Acute pulmonary embolus
  • Acute respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS)

I. Clinical Judgment — Endocrine, Hematology/Immunology, GI, Renal/GU, Integumentary: Study Guidance

This domain addresses complex endocrine, hematology, immunology, gastrointestinal, renal, and integumentary clinical judgment concepts required for managing severely ill adult patients in high-acuity healthcare environments based on the official test plan.

  • Endocrine disorders and diabetes
  • Hematology and transfusion reactions
  • Gastrointestinal surgeries and hepatic failure

I. Clinical Judgment — Musculoskeletal, Neurological, Behavioral/Psychosocial: Study Guidance

This domain evaluates musculoskeletal, neurological, and behavioral or psychosocial clinical judgment, encompassing critical conditions like compartment syndrome, acute spinal cord injuries, stroke, delirium, and substance use disorders for adult patient populations.

  • Musculoskeletal trauma and compartment syndrome
  • Neurological injuries and stroke
  • Behavioral health and substance use disorder

I. Clinical Judgment — Multisystem: Study Guidance

This domain covers vital multisystem clinical judgment including acid-base imbalances, anoxic brain injuries, and severe burn management strategies necessary for critical care nursing practice according to the published guidelines and test specifications.

  • Acid-base imbalance
  • Anoxic injury
  • Burns

II. Professional Caring & Ethical Practice: Study Guidance

This domain emphasizes professional caring and ethical practice, covering patient advocacy, moral agency, caring practices, and clinical inquiry principles for critical care nurses who manage complex adult patients throughout their hospital stays.

  • Advocacy/Moral Agency
  • Caring Practices
  • Clinical Inquiry

Exam-Day Guidance

Always bring official photo identification that matches your legal name on the application. For computer-based testing, your official results will appear directly on-screen immediately upon completion.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of multiple-choice items, of which a portion are scored and others are used to gather statistical data on item performance for future exams.

What is the passing score?

The passing point is determined using a criterion-referenced standard setting process known as the modified Angoff, measured against a predetermined standard rather than a fixed percentage.

How long is the exam?

The computer-based certification exam has a total duration of 180 minutes, providing candidates with adequate time to complete all required multiple-choice questions during the test session.

How much does the exam cost?

The nonmember computer-based exam fee is 375 dollars, while American Association of Critical-Care Nurses members pay a reduced rate for their certification application and testing fees.
