CCRN-K was retired on November 14, 2023

AACN retired CCRN-K on 2023-11-14. CCRN is the named replacement path.

Prepare for CCRN: CCRN (Adult) Direct Care — Acute/Critical Care Nursing Certification Exam

Historical CCRN-K Scope

The CCRN-K examination, previously certified by the American Association of Critical-Care Nurses, evaluated clinical judgment and professional caring and ethical practice knowledge across multiple testing domains. AACN officially retired this program on 2023-11-14. The CCRN (Adult) Direct Care — Acute/Critical Care Nursing Certification Exam serves as the designated successor replacement path for all candidates seeking certification. This certification maintains a 5-year validity period and requires renewal to ensure ongoing professional standards for all practicing nurses.

Who CCRN-K Was For

Because the CCRN-K examination is no longer active or administered, acute and critical care nurses seeking professional certification must register for the CCRN (Adult) Direct Care — Acute/Critical Care Nursing Certification Exam replacement pathway through the American Association of Critical-Care Nurses.

  • Clinical judgment

  • Acute and critical care nursing

How to Reuse Your Preparation

Preparation for the replacement assessment requires a detailed review of clinical judgment and professional caring domains. Candidates should study acute and critical care nursing principles to prepare for the linear computer-based format. Reviewing the core domains ensures that individuals are ready for the 150 questions presented on the exam. Understanding the testing structure and the specific focus areas remains necessary for those pursuing this certification path to ensure they meet the required standards.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The examination consists of a total of 150 multiple choice questions distributed across the clinical judgment and professional caring and ethical practice domains. These questions are designed to evaluate the knowledge and skills of nursing candidates seeking to demonstrate their expertise in acute and critical care settings.

What is the passing score?

The established passing score required to pass the examination is set at a value of 87. Candidates must achieve this score or higher to earn their professional certification through the American Association of Critical-Care Nurses and maintain their standing within the professional nursing community.

How long is the exam?

The linear computer-based examination has an official duration of 180 minutes. This timeframe is provided to ensure that all candidates have the opportunity to address the questions presented during the testing session in a professional manner and demonstrate their clinical knowledge effectively.

What is the exam price?

The applicable AACN member price for the examination is $280. For those who are not members of the organization, the non-member price for taking the examination is $430, as established by the current policy guidelines for all candidates pursuing this specific certification path.
