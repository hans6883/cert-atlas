What This Exam Validates

The Principles and Practice of Engineering PE Mining and Mineral Processing exam evaluates professional engineering competency across five core technical domains required for professional licensure. Certified by NCEES, the linear computer-based assessment comprises exactly 80 multiple-choice questions administered continuously over a total test duration of 480 minutes for candidates.

Who Should Take This Exam

Engineers with experience in mine operations, mineral processing, and safety control. Candidates typically hold engineering degrees and seek professional licensure in the mining and mineral processing disciplines.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Mine Development and Operations
  • Mine Safety and Environmental Control
  • Rock Mechanics and Ground Control
  • Mine Ventilation
  • Mineral Processing

How to Prepare

Review official NCEES documentation alongside comprehensive study guides covering ventilation plan review procedures, applicable mining regulations, and standard industry handbooks. Focus your preparation efforts heavily on operational safety standards, diesel equipment ventilation requirements, precise environmental particulate sampling guidelines, and standard mathematical calculations to achieve success.

Domain Study Guidance

Mine Development and Operations: Study Guidance

This specific domain covers standard engineering practices related to overall mine development, project execution, and day-to-day operational activities across various mining environments and active project lifecycles to ensure proper production scheduling.

  • Operational planning
  • Equipment deployment
  • Production scheduling

Mine Safety and Environmental Control: Study Guidance

This domain focuses heavily on major mine safety protocols, mandatory regulatory compliance, and necessary environmental control measures designed to protect workers and maintain operational sustainability through effective contaminant sampling and safety practices.

  • MSHA regulations
  • Contaminant sampling
  • Safety compliance

Rock Mechanics and Ground Control: Study Guidance

This area addresses fundamental rock mechanics principles and standard ground control techniques required to ensure long-term underground stability, structural integrity, and worker safety during active operations by implementing proper underground strata management.

  • Ground support
  • Rock stability
  • Underground strata management

Mine Ventilation: Study Guidance

This technical section examines complex mine ventilation systems, precise air quantity calculations, and necessary regulatory plan approvals to maintain a safe working atmosphere underground for personnel through proper ventilation plan submittals and diesel equipment controls.

  • Ventilation plan submittals
  • Air quantity requirements
  • Diesel equipment ventilation

Mineral Processing: Study Guidance

This domain pertains directly to mineral processing operations, facility design parameters, and extractive metallurgy methods used to recover valuable minerals efficiently from mined ores during production utilizing advanced material separation circuits and techniques.

  • Processing circuits
  • Material separation
  • Extractive techniques

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive at the testing center early with valid identification for your linear computer-based test. Manage your time across all multiple-choice items during the 480-minute duration.
