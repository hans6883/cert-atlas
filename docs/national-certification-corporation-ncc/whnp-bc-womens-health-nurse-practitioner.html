What This Exam Validates

The WHNP-BC: Women's Health NP Certification is an advanced practice credential administered by the National Certification Corporation (NCC). The examination evaluates professional clinical knowledge across six distinct domains, featuring exactly 175 total questions and allowing candidates a duration of 180 minutes to complete the entire assessment successfully without unnecessary delays.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is intended for qualified nurse practitioners seeking formal credentialing in women's health care. Candidates demonstrate clinical proficiency across gynecologic, reproductive, and obstetric practice domains.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Assessment and diagnostics
  • Primary care delivery
  • Gynecologic health management
  • Reproductive health care
  • Obstetric clinical practice
  • Pharmacology applications

How to Prepare

Candidates should review the six core domains in complete detail, focusing on assessment methods, primary care principles, gynecologic conditions, obstetric protocols, pharmacologic treatments, and professional practice issues. Use official guidelines from the National Certification Corporation (NCC) to direct study sessions and prepare for the 175 total questions.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The certification exam consists of exactly 175 total questions covering assessment, primary care, gynecologic health, obstetrics, pharmacology, and professional practice issues during the official testing period for candidates.

What is the passing score?

A candidate must obtain a score equal to or higher than the passing score established by the certifying body in order to pass the examination successfully.

How long is the exam?

Candidates are given a total duration of 180 minutes to complete all questions on the assessment administered directly by the National Certification Corporation as part of their professional testing procedures.

Who administers the WHNP-BC certification?

The professional certification is administered and issued by the National Certification Corporation (NCC). Check with National Certification Corporation (NCC) for the current certification path details.
