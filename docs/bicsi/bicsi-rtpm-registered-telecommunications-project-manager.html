What This Exam Validates

The BICSI Registered Telecommunications Project Manager (RTPM) certification validates mastery of project management principles within the information and communications technology (ICT) industry. Certified by BICSI, this program confirms an individual's ability to oversee and coordinate complex ICT projects, from initial budget estimation and proposal development to final project closure and stakeholder communication, ensuring adherence to industry standards and best practices throughout the entire project lifecycle.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is designed for project managers, project coordinators, and lead technicians working in the ICT field. Candidates should possess relevant industry experience in managing or supervising technology projects, including pathways, spaces, and infrastructure systems, to meet the eligibility requirements for this professional designation.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Project initiation and information gathering
  • Budget estimation and financial management
  • Proposal development and contract management
  • Deliverable assembly and quality planning
  • Administrative project oversight
  • Project launch and implementation
  • Stakeholder communication planning
  • Project closure and documentation

How to Prepare

Candidates should dedicate time to independent study to master the material. Review the Telecommunications Project Management Reference Manual and the Foundations of Project Management course materials. Focus on each functional area of the exam content outline. Because knowledge bases vary, study the specific project phases and management principles outlined in the handbook to prepare for the 100 questions on the exam.

Domain Study Guidance

Gather Specific Project Information: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the initial phase of project management, specifically gathering and reviewing project information to determine feasibility. Candidates must demonstrate the ability to create project file repositories, perform site surveys, and identify client requirements accurately.

  • Project file repository creation
  • Site survey performance

Create Initial Project Budget Estimate: Study Guidance

This domain covers the financial aspects of project initiation, including the quantification of resources and the creation of initial budget estimates. Candidates learn to evaluate value engineering options and manage insurance and bonding requirements for complex telecommunications projects.

  • Resource quantification
  • Value engineering evaluation

Orchestrate Project Proposal: Study Guidance

This domain involves the development of the project proposal, including defining the statement of work and obtaining approval to proceed. Candidates must understand how to establish vendor qualification criteria and define the elements of a notice to proceed.

  • Statement of Work preparation
  • Vendor qualification criteria

Project Deliverables: Study Guidance

This domain addresses the creation and assembly of project deliverables, including safety, quality, and risk management plans. Candidates are expected to master the development of work breakdown structures, risk management plans, and the preparation of necessary project submittals.

  • Work breakdown structure
  • Risk management plan

Administrative: Study Guidance

This domain covers administrative project activities, including managing financials, purchase orders, and compliance with codes and standards. Candidates learn to facilitate project kick-off meetings, track financial and purchase order data, and monitor ongoing compliance with relevant industry codes.

  • Kick-off meeting management
  • Financial and purchase order tracking

Project Launch: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the project launch and implementation phase, including resource mobilization and trade coordination. Candidates must demonstrate proficiency in coordinating various trades, mobilizing necessary resources, and monitoring actual costs against the project budget throughout the implementation process.

  • Resource mobilization
  • Trade coordination

Communication: Study Guidance

This domain covers the communication plan, ensuring effective status updates and meeting facilitation for all project stakeholders. Candidates learn to manage stakeholder engagement, determine the appropriate frequency for status communications, and facilitate productive project meetings throughout the project lifecycle.

  • Meeting facilitation
  • Stakeholder engagement

Project Closure: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on project closure, including acceptance reviews, final documentation, and financial reconciliation. Candidates must understand the process for conducting final acceptance reviews, managing punch lists, submitting final project documentation, and completing the formal financial close-out of the project.

  • Acceptance review and punch list
  • Financial close-out

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive at the testing center with approved identification. The exam consists of 100 questions to be completed within 120 minutes. Ensure all contact information in your BICSI profile is current before scheduling your appointment to avoid any potential delays.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The BICSI RTPM certification exam is comprised of exactly 100 questions. Candidates are expected to demonstrate their knowledge across all project management domains covered in the official reference materials during this testing session.

What is the passing score?

Information regarding the specific passing score is maintained internally by BICSI. You should check directly with BICSI for the current certification requirements and scoring policies to ensure you are fully prepared for your scheduled examination date.

How long is the exam?

The exam is a 120-minute session. You must manage your time effectively during this period to ensure you have sufficient opportunity to answer all 100 questions presented in the exam format provided by the testing center.

How do I maintain my certification?

You are required to follow the official BICSI recertification policies to maintain your credential. Please consult the official BICSI documentation to track your progress and ensure all requirements are met before your current certification status expires.
