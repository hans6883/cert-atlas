Historical ACP-Security Scope

The ACP Security Professional certification, managed by Alibaba Cloud, focused on security architecture, threat detection, compliance, IAM, and security operations. This exam is retired as of 2025-03-31. There is no official successor exam provided by the certifying body. Candidates seeking to validate their cloud security expertise should monitor the official Alibaba Cloud certification portal for updates on current professional-level offerings and training paths to ensure they remain aligned with industry standards and requirements.

Who ACP-Security Was For

This retired exam targeted architects, developers, and operations personnel responsible for managing security products and cloud resources. It required practical experience with Alibaba Cloud security, monitoring, and management tools to address infrastructure requirements and maintain protection for enterprise-level cloud deployments.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Advanced security architecture design
  • Threat detection and analysis
  • Regulatory compliance management
  • Advanced identity and access management
  • Security operations and monitoring

How to Reuse Your Preparation

Since this exam is retired and lacks a successor, do not prepare for it. Focus your efforts on current Alibaba Cloud documentation and security whitepapers. Review the latest product guides for Security Center, RAM, and Cloud Firewall to maintain relevant skills. Consult the official Alibaba Cloud Academy portal to identify active certifications that align with your professional goals and current cloud security standards. Always prioritize learning paths that are currently supported by the vendor to ensure your knowledge remains applicable to modern cloud environments.

Historical Domain Guide

Advanced Security Architecture: Historical Scope

This domain covers the design and implementation of secure cloud architectures using Alibaba Cloud services, focusing on the principles required to build protected and scalable infrastructure environments for various business applications and diverse enterprise workloads.

  • Secure network design
  • Architecture best practices

Threat Detection: Historical Scope

This domain focuses on identifying, analyzing, and responding to security threats within the cloud environment, ensuring that administrators can effectively detect malicious activities and mitigate potential risks before they impact business operations or data integrity.

  • Threat identification
  • Security monitoring

Compliance: Historical Scope

This domain addresses the alignment of cloud deployments with regulatory and organizational security requirements, ensuring that all infrastructure configurations adhere to necessary compliance standards and internal governance policies for data protection and privacy within the cloud.

  • Compliance auditing
  • Policy enforcement

This domain covers the management of user identities, permissions, and access control policies, emphasizing the importance of the principle of least privilege when configuring access to sensitive cloud resources and administrative management consoles for authorized users.

  • RAM user management
  • Permission policies

Security Operations: Historical Scope

This domain involves the day-to-day operational tasks required to maintain a secure cloud environment, including the continuous monitoring of system logs and the execution of incident response procedures to maintain high availability and system performance.

  • Security operations

Frequently asked questions

How many questions were on the exam?

The exam consisted of 70 questions in total. These questions were designed to test the candidate's practical knowledge of security architecture and threat management within the Alibaba Cloud ecosystem during the active period of the certification program.

What was the passing score?

The exam used a pass/fail scale, and the specific passing threshold was not publicly disclosed by the certifying body. Candidates were expected to demonstrate knowledge across all domains to achieve a successful result on the assessment during the active period.

How long was the exam?

The exam was a 120-minute, linear, computer-based test. Candidates were required to manage their time effectively across the various sections to ensure all questions were addressed within the allotted duration provided for the professional-level certification assessment.

Is the certification still valid?

The certification had a validity period of 3 years, after which renewal was required to maintain status. However, because the exam itself is now retired, no new certifications are being issued, and existing holders should check their status.
