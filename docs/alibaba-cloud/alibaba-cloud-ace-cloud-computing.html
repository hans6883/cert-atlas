Historical ACE-Cloud Scope

The ACE Cloud Computing Expert certification from Alibaba Cloud covered enterprise architecture, multi-cloud strategy, networking, security, and cost optimization. This exam is retired as of 2025-05-13. Because no successor exists, candidates should consult the official Alibaba Cloud education portal to identify current professional development opportunities. The certification previously required a 500 USD investment for a 120-minute multiple-choice exam and a 4-hour hands-on scenario assessment. This program was designed to validate expertise in managing cloud environments.

Who ACE-Cloud Was For

This retired certification was intended for experienced cloud architects and engineers. It required technical knowledge of large-scale infrastructure, networking, and security compliance within the Alibaba Cloud ecosystem to manage enterprise-level cloud environments and ensure operational excellence.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consisted of 60 questions in total, presented in a linear format. It included both multiple-choice and multiple-response question types, along with scenario-based questions to test practical application of cloud knowledge.

What is the passing score?

The exam was graded on a pass/fail basis. The specific passing threshold was not publicly disclosed by Alibaba Cloud, meaning candidates were required to demonstrate mastery of the subject matter to achieve certification.

How long is the exam?

The multiple-choice portion of the exam was 120 minutes long. Additionally, the certification process included a separate 4-hour hands-on scenario exam, requiring a significant time commitment from candidates to complete both assessment components successfully.

What was the cost of the exam?

The total price for the certification was 500 USD. This fee covered both the 120-minute multiple-choice exam and the 4-hour hands-on scenario assessment, providing an evaluation of the candidate's theoretical and practical cloud computing skills.
