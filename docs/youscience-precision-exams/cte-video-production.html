What This Exam Validates

The CTE Video Production Industry Certification, administered by YouScience (Precision Exams), evaluates technical proficiency across seven domains. The exam covers the full lifecycle of video creation, including planning, production techniques, post-production editing, and equipment management. It validates the knowledge required for professional roles in the media production sector, ensuring students can apply industry-standard practices to real-world projects throughout their media production career path.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is for students in media production programs who have completed their standards-related instruction. It is suitable for those seeking to demonstrate their mastery of video editing, motion graphics, and production coordination to potential employers through a verified industry credential.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Pre-production planning and storyboarding
  • Video production and camera operation
  • Post-production editing and sound mixing
  • Legal and ethical responsibilities
  • Equipment maintenance and management
  • Career preparation and professional readiness

How to Prepare

Review the seven domain areas to ensure mastery of the required skills. Utilize curriculum materials and textbooks to reinforce knowledge of production standards. Practice with sample questions to become familiar with the format. The CareerTech Testing Center recommends taking the assessment as soon as you finish your instruction rather than waiting until the end of the school year to ensure your knowledge remains fresh.

Domain Study Guidance

Pre-Production: Study Guidance

This domain accounts for 18% of the exam and covers the initial stages of a video project, focusing on script analysis, concept development, storyboard creation, and overall production planning requirements for various media projects.

  • Script study and concept development
  • Production team coordination

Production: Study Guidance

Representing 23% of the total exam weight, this domain addresses the active filming and recording phase of video production, including proper camera operation, shot composition, lighting design, and on-set management techniques for professional results.

  • Camera operation and composition
  • Lighting and set management

Post-Production: Study Guidance

Comprising 32% of the test, this domain focuses on the technical aspects of assembling and refining the final video product, encompassing digital editing workflows, timeline organization, audio mixing, and visual effects for high-quality output.

  • Video editing and timeline management
  • Audio mixing and effects application

Ethics & Legal Responsibilities: Study Guidance

Accounting for 5% of the evaluation, this domain covers the legal and moral obligations inherent in media production, such as copyright law, intellectual property rights, release forms, and licensing compliance for all creative content.

  • Copyright and intellectual property
  • Compliance and licensing

Equipment Management: Study Guidance

Making up 5% of the overall score, this domain involves the logistical management of production hardware, focusing on proper asset tracking, equipment organization, inventory control, and secure storage practices for all production gear.

  • Equipment organization
  • Asset tracking

Safety and Maintenance: Study Guidance

Carrying a 5% exam weight, this domain focuses on maintaining a safe working environment and keeping production equipment in optimal condition through routine cleaning, proper cabling procedures, and strict safety guidelines for all users.

  • Routine equipment cleaning
  • Safety and maintenance procedures

Career Preparation: Study Guidance

Representing 12% of the assessment, this domain prepares students for the transition from the classroom to the workforce by emphasizing professional roles, team dynamic structures, portfolio development, and workplace expectations for media professionals.

  • Professional role identification
  • Project structure and team roles

Exam-Day Guidance

The exam is web-based and delivered by a proctor. You may use scratch paper and calculators provided by the proctor. You can flag questions to revisit later, but all 38 questions must be answered before submission to ensure a complete score.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The CTE Video Production Industry Certification exam consists of exactly 38 questions. Candidates must respond to every question delivered on the web-based assessment platform prior to submitting their completed examination for final scoring by the system.

Is the exam timed?

The exam is not timed. Most students complete the assessment at their own pace, but you may take as much time as needed to finish in a single testing session without any pressure to rush through the content.

Can I retake the exam if I do not pass?

Yes, you may retake the test unless your school or state testing policies prohibit it. You must wait for the required period between test attempts to allow for additional study and preparation before your next attempt.

What is the current certification path?

This certification remains active in current career pathways. Candidates seeking details regarding long-term recertification options, updated blueprints, or broader career cluster pathways should check with YouScience (Precision Exams) for the current certification path.
