What This Exam Validates

The BCIDP Infectious Disease Pharmacist certification is administered by the Board of Pharmacy Specialties. This exam evaluates a pharmacist's knowledge across three primary domains: Pathophysiology and Epidemiology of Infections, Therapeutics and Patient Management, and Professional Practice. It ensures that practitioners demonstrate the necessary expertise to manage complex infectious disease cases and contribute to effective patient care outcomes in various clinical settings. The certification process validates the professional skills required to navigate the challenges of modern infectious disease pharmacy practice effectively and maintain high standards of care.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is for pharmacists who manage infectious diseases in clinical practice. Candidates should have experience in patient management, therapeutic decision-making, and the application of epidemiological principles to improve health outcomes for patients with various infections.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Pathophysiology of infections
  • Epidemiology of infectious diseases
  • Therapeutic patient management
  • Professional practice standards
  • Clinical decision-making
  • Patient care planning

How to Prepare
