What This Exam Validates

The BCCCP Board Certified Critical Care Pharmacist examination is officially administered by the Board of Pharmacy Specialties. It covers critical care, therapeutics and patient management, and professional practice across exactly 150 questions during a 225-minute testing window for qualified candidates seeking professional recognition and validating advanced clinical expertise in the field.

Who Should Take This Exam

Licensed pharmacists specializing in critical care who seek professional certification through the Board of Pharmacy Specialties to demonstrate advanced expertise in patient management, clinical practice, and specialized therapeutic interventions within acute care settings.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Critical care concepts
  • Therapeutics and patient management
  • Professional practice guidelines
  • Clinical decision-making
  • Pharmacotherapy interventions

How to Prepare
