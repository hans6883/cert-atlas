What This Exam Validates

The BCPP Psychiatric Pharmacy Specialist certification is officially administered by the Board of Pharmacy Specialties. The professional evaluation carefully measures candidates across mental health, therapeutics, patient management, and professional practice domains, featuring a total of 150 questions over a duration of 225 minutes for all participants to demonstrate their clinical expertise.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is intended for pharmacy professionals seeking specialization in psychiatric care. Candidates build competence in managing patient therapeutics, handling complex mental health treatments, and addressing clinical challenges in professional practice settings.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Mental Health
  • Therapeutics
  • Patient Management
  • Professional Practice

How to Prepare

Review core psychiatric conditions including depression, bipolar disorder, and schizophrenia by thoroughly studying official guides. Focus meticulously on treatment guidelines, comprehensive medication management strategies, and patient care methods outlined in official source documentation to successfully prepare for answering the 150 questions during the official certification examination.
