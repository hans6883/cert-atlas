What This Exam Validates

The BRCGS Internal Auditor Food Safety Certification, managed by BRCGS, evaluates your proficiency in internal auditing for food safety. The exam covers auditor roles, audit planning, report writing, and follow-up activities. It ensures you can maintain compliance and safety standards within food industry operations, confirming your ability to execute audit procedures effectively according to BRCGS requirements. This assessment ensures that candidates possess the necessary skills to uphold food safety standards through systematic internal evaluation and professional reporting practices within their respective organizations, requiring a 75% passing score to achieve certification.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is intended for food safety professionals, quality assurance staff, and internal auditors working in food manufacturing or retail environments. It is suitable for those responsible for maintaining internal audit programs and ensuring ongoing compliance with established food safety management standards.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Defining auditor roles and responsibilities
  • Planning internal audit activities
  • Conducting on-site audit procedures
  • Writing concise audit reports
  • Documenting factual audit findings
  • Executing audit follow-up tasks

How to Prepare

Review the BRCGS internal audit requirements and practice applying audit principles to food safety scenarios. Focus on the 4 domains by studying audit planning, reporting, and follow-up techniques. Use the provided official guides to understand the expectations for internal auditors. Ensure you are familiar with the 75% passing score requirement and prepare to manage your time effectively during the 960-minute duration of the assessment. You must address all 4 domains to succeed in this professional certification.

Understand the roles and responsibilities of auditors: Study Guidance

This domain covers the specific duties and professional expectations required of an internal auditor within the food safety environment, ensuring that candidates understand the ethical standards and the scope of responsibilities necessary for effective and compliant auditing practices.

Be able to plan and conduct an internal audit: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the practical steps needed to organize and execute an internal audit from start to finish, including the development of audit checklists and the systematic scheduling of activities to ensure coverage of all food safety requirements.

Know how to write concise, accurate and factual audit reports: Study Guidance

This domain addresses the requirements for documenting audit results in a clear, factual, and professional manner, emphasizing the importance of reporting non-conformities accurately to facilitate effective communication and ensure that all findings are documented with the necessary level of detail.

  • Ensuring report accuracy
  • Conciseness in documentation

Be able to undertake audit follow-up activities: Study Guidance

This domain covers the necessary actions taken after an audit to verify that corrective measures are implemented, focusing on the verification of corrective actions and the formal process of closing out audit findings to maintain long-term food safety compliance.

  • Verification of corrective actions
  • Closing out audit findings
