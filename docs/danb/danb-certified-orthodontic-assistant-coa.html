What This Exam Validates

The DANB Certified Orthodontic Assistant COA exam covers evaluation, radiation health and safety, orthodontic procedures, patient management, and infection control protocols. Registered candidates answer a total of 215 questions during the testing session. The assessment features a duration of 165 minutes and requires a minimum passing scaled score of 400 on each component exam.

Who Should Take This Exam

Candidates seeking the COA credential include dental assistants with approved work experience, graduates of CODA-accredited programs, or professionals holding current CDA certification who perform orthodontic assisting duties.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Evaluation
  • Radiation Health and Safety
  • Orthodontic Procedures
  • Patient Management

  • Process Instruments

How to Prepare

Carefully review the official candidate handbook and all exam outlines before your test date. Utilize approved study materials, instructional guides, and practice tests for the ICE and OA components to prepare for answering the 215 total questions within the strict 165 minutes duration limit provided by the testing center.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The certification exam contains a total of 215 questions that all candidates must carefully complete within the officially allotted testing duration provided by the testing center.

What is the passing score?

Candidates must achieve a minimum passing scaled score of 400 on each component exam in order to successfully earn their professional dental assisting certification credentials today.

How long is the exam?

The official test duration is exactly 165 minutes, which provides candidates with adequate time to finish answering all sections of the test completely and carefully.

What are the eligibility requirements for the exam?

Eligibility pathways include combinations of approved work experience, education from a CODA-accredited dental assisting program, current DANB CDA certification, and current hands-on CPR, BLS, or ACLS certification.
