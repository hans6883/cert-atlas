What This Exam Validates

The ASCP Histotechnician (HT) Board Certification is administered by the American Society for Clinical Pathology to evaluate your professional knowledge. The certification exam evaluates your understanding across a total of 100 questions within a 150-minute time frame using computer adaptive testing methods to measure your competence and readiness for clinical practice.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is intended for histotechnicians seeking formal credentialing. Candidates should prepare to demonstrate theoretical and procedural knowledge in tissue preparation, staining methods, and laboratory safety.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Tissue Fixation
  • Tissue Processing
  • Paraffin Embedding
  • Microtomy Sectioning
  • Staining Techniques
  • Laboratory Safety Operations

How to Prepare

Review all designated content areas including fixation parameters, automated processing instruments, and staining reagents to build a strong foundation. Practice answering both theoretical and procedural questions under simulated testing conditions. Understand the underlying principles of tissue preparation, quality assurance protocols, and safety practices to prepare for the 100-question examination.

Domain Study Guidance

Fixation: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on tissue morphology, various fixation procedures, parameters like size and volume, appropriate chemical reagents, and related laboratory instrumentation such as microwaves used in diagnostic settings.

  • Morphology and anatomy
  • Fixation procedures
  • Parameters and reagents

Processing: Study Guidance

This domain covers tissue preservation techniques, processing procedures designed for light microscopy and delicate frozen sections, automated laboratory instrumentation, and chemical reagents required for sample dehydration and clearing.

  • Cell and component preservation
  • Processing procedures
  • Instrumentation maintenance

Embedding/Microtomy: Study Guidance

This domain addresses paraffin embedding and frozen section procedures, gelatin or adhesive mounting methods, routine quality control measures, and the mechanical operation of microtomy instrumentation used in producing tissue sections.

  • Paraffin and frozen sections
  • Quality control
  • Instrumentation components

Staining: Study Guidance

This domain encompasses extensive staining techniques for cell nuclei, cytoplasm, various carbohydrates, and microorganisms, alongside coverslipping procedures, biological dyes, and optical instrumentation utilized by histotechnicians during daily practice.

  • H&E and special stains
  • Mounting procedures
  • Reagents and dyes

Laboratory Operations: Study Guidance

This domain includes safety regulations, laboratory mathematics such as calculating percent solutions and serial dilutions, and the proper operational guidelines for utilizing ancillary laboratory equipment safely and efficiently.

  • Safety and hazardous materials
  • Laboratory mathematics
  • Ancillary equipment

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive prepared for the computer adaptive testing format. Manage your time across the 100 questions within the 150-minute limit to ensure every question is answered.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The official certification examination is composed of exactly 100 questions that are delivered continuously via computer adaptive testing platforms to carefully evaluate your professional clinical competence.

What is the passing score?

To successfully achieve this professional credential, candidates must attain the established passing score requirement of 400/999 on their official examination report provided by the testing administration board.

How long is the exam?

Candidates are given a total time allowance of a 150-minute time frame to read, analyze, and complete all 100 questions presented during the official board certification testing session.

What content areas are covered on the exam?

The board certification examination extensively covers five distinct content areas including fixation, processing, embedding, microtomy, staining, and laboratory operations to ensure a thorough technical skills evaluation.
