What This Exam Validates

The AMCB Certified Midwife Exam Practice Test is a detailed assessment designed to evaluate your clinical knowledge and readiness across six distinct domains. Certified by the American Midwifery Certification Board (AMCB), this structured evaluation consists of 175 questions and provides a 240-minute timeframe to test your overall proficiency in professional midwifery practices, patient evaluations, and standard healthcare delivery.

Who Should Take This Exam

This assessment is intended for individuals pursuing midwifery credentials through the American Midwifery Certification Board (AMCB). Candidates preparing for clinical practice certification should complete this practice test.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Antepartum care management
  • Intrapartum labor monitoring
  • Postpartum recovery support
  • Newborn assessment procedures
  • Gynecological evaluations

How to Prepare

Review each domain weight carefully to allocate your study time effectively throughout your preparation period. Focus heavily on antepartum and intrapartum care, which make up significant portions of the test questions. Use official resources provided directly by the American Midwifery Certification Board (AMCB) to guide your daily practice and ensure complete alignment with all official testing requirements.
