HACCP Certified Professional (NEHA) Credential

NEHA

Not an examination

NEHA does not offer an examination for HACCP Certified Professional (NEHA): it is a training programme. No exam blueprint, domain weights, exam code or practice test apply. Verified on the issuer's site September 18, 2026. issuer page

What This Exam Validates

The HACCP Certified Professional exam is a valuable credential for food safety practitioners. Administered by NEHA, the assessment consists of a total of 120 questions that must be answered within a strict duration of 120 minutes. Candidates must achieve a passing score of a 650 scaled score across all 12 domains of the curriculum.

Who Should Take This Exam

Food safety professionals, retail managers, and public health inspectors seeking validation of their operational knowledge in hazard analysis, sanitation practices, and regulatory requirements across diverse food service environments.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Causes and prevention of foodborne illness
  • Pathogens and microorganisms
  • Potentially hazardous foods
  • Contamination, allergens and food defense
  • Food hazard controls
  • Personnel training and education
  • Facilities and equipment
  • Cleaning and sanitizing

How to Prepare

Review each of the 12 domains systematically to master the material before test day. Focus study efforts on food hazard controls, pathogen characteristics, and HACCP principles. Practice applying regulatory requirements to retail and food service environments to comfortably meet the 650 scaled score passing requirement during your attempt.

Domain Study Guidance

Domain 1: Study Guidance

This specific domain covers the causes and prevention methods related to foodborne illness outbreaks in various food establishments, ensuring candidates understand fundamental epidemiological concepts and active managerial control strategies required for public safety.

  • Identifying illness triggers
  • Preventing biological and chemical risks

Domain 2: Study Guidance

This domain focuses intensely on foodborne pathogens and non-pathogenic microorganisms relevant to retail operations, teaching candidates how various bacterial, viral, and parasitic agents multiply, survive, and impact the overall safety of consumable food supplies.

  • Pathogen identification
  • Microorganism growth parameters

Domain 3: Study Guidance

This domain examines potentially hazardous foods and the specific controls needed for safe handling, requiring professionals to recognize time and temperature control for safety food items and implement strict protocols during preparation.

  • High-risk food identification
  • Safety measures for vulnerable foods

Domain 4: Study Guidance

This domain addresses contamination types, major food allergens, and food defense strategies, educating candidates on how to prevent cross-contamination, manage allergen disclosures accurately, and protect food products from intentional adulteration threats.

  • Allergen management
  • Defense protocols

Domain 5: Study Guidance

This domain details food hazard controls used to minimize safety risks in food processing and service environments, guiding candidates through the practical application of preventive measures and critical limits designed to eliminate identified hazards.

  • Control measure implementation
  • Hazard mitigation

Domain 6: Study Guidance

This domain outlines personnel training and education requirements for food service workers, ensuring that all staff members receive appropriate instruction regarding personal hygiene, disease transmission vectors, and safe food handling responsibilities.

  • Staff training methods
  • Hygiene education

Domain 7: Study Guidance

This domain reviews design and maintenance standards for facilities and food equipment, emphasizing the importance of choosing smooth, easily cleanable materials and arranging physical layouts to facilitate effective sanitation and prevent pest harborages.

  • Equipment cleanability
  • Facility layout standards

Domain 8: Study Guidance

This domain explores cleaning and sanitizing procedures for surfaces and equipment in retail settings, explaining the chemical concentrations, contact times, and mechanical steps necessary to remove soil and destroy harmful vegetative microorganisms effectively.

  • Sanitization methods
  • Cleaning schedules

Domain 9: Study Guidance

This domain examines pest control management and prevention protocols in food establishments, teaching candidates how to inspect facilities for signs of infestation, seal entry points, and eliminate the food and water sources that attract pests.

  • Pest exclusion
  • Monitoring techniques

Domain 10: Study Guidance

This domain covers receiving, storage, cooking, and cooling procedures for temperature control, helping professionals master the critical temperature danger zone rules and safe holding parameters required to prevent rapid bacterial growth during product processing.

  • Temperature logs
  • Storage safety

Domain 11: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on Hazard Analysis Critical Control Point principles and plan execution, providing deep insights into establishing critical limits, monitoring procedures, corrective actions, verification steps, and detailed record-keeping systems for operations.

  • Critical control points
  • HACCP plan development

Domain 12: Study Guidance

This domain reviews regulatory requirements and inspection procedures for food operations, preparing candidates to understand the legal frameworks, compliance standards, and regulatory agency interactions expected of certified food safety professionals.

  • Inspection compliance
  • Regulatory standards

Exam-Day Guidance

Manage your time carefully across the 120 questions during the 120 minutes of the assessment. Read each prompt carefully, keeping the 650 scaled score threshold in mind.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam contains a total of 120 questions distributed across 12 distinct domains covering broad food safety, sanitation standards, and hazard analysis principles for certified professionals.

What is the passing score?

The passing score for this certification exam is a 650 scaled score. Candidates must demonstrate professional proficiency across all of the tested content domains to earn the credential.

How long is the exam?

Candidates are given a total duration of 120 minutes to complete the 120 questions on the official certification exam administered by NEHA during their testing appointment at approved centers.

What certifying body offers this credential?

This professional certification is officially offered, overseen, and administered by NEHA for qualified practitioners seeking advanced credentials in food safety management and industry standards across the country.
