What This Exam Validates

The NCLEX-RN (National Council Licensure Examination for Registered Nurses) is certified by NCSBN and evaluates nursing candidates across eight distinct domains, including management of care, safety and infection prevention, pharmacological therapies, and physiological adaptation, to help ensure safe and effective entry-level nursing practice for all candidates taking the examination.

Who Should Take This Exam

Candidates seeking licensure as a registered nurse who have completed an approved nursing education program should take this examination to demonstrate entry-level clinical competence and safety.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Management of care
  • Safety and infection prevention
  • Pharmacological therapies
  • Physiological adaptation

How to Prepare

Review the official test plan and study all eight content domains. Focus heavily on high-weight areas such as management of care and pharmacological therapies. Practice answering clinical scenario questions and check with NCSBN for the current certification path to ensure adequate preparation for the 300-minute duration.

Domain Study Guidance

Management of Care: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on professional management of care principles, accounting for 18.0 percent of the total exam content to ensure that candidates understand effective nursing leadership, delegation, and care coordination.

  • Nursing management principles
  • Care coordination

Safety and Infection Prevention and Control: Study Guidance

This category covers safety and infection prevention and control measures, representing 13.0 percent of the exam content to ensure candidates can maintain secure healthcare environments and properly protect patients from potential hazards.

  • Infection control practices
  • Patient safety measures

Health Promotion and Maintenance: Study Guidance

This domain addresses health promotion and maintenance strategies, making up 9.0 percent of the test plan to evaluate how nurses educate individuals on wellness, preventive health practices, and developmental stages across the lifespan.

  • Health promotion strategies
  • Patient education

Psychosocial Integrity: Study Guidance

This section encompasses psychosocial integrity concepts, contributing 9.0 percent to the overall weight by testing the nurse's ability to provide emotional support, manage mental health challenges, and address therapeutic communication needs effectively.

  • Psychosocial support
  • Mental health concepts

Basic Care and Comfort: Study Guidance

This area pertains to basic care and comfort, spanning 9.0 percent of the examination to ensure that registered nurses can competently assist patients with activities of daily living, personal hygiene, and proper nutritional support.

  • Assistance with activities of daily living
  • Nutritional support

Pharmacological and Parenteral Therapies: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on pharmacological and parenteral therapies, accounting for 16.0 percent of the exam to evaluate medication administration safety, dosage calculations, and the continuous monitoring of intravenous and complex drug regimens.

  • Medication administration
  • Parenteral therapy monitoring

Reduction of Risk Potential: Study Guidance

This category covers the reduction of risk potential, representing 12.0 percent of the test content by measuring the nurse's capacity to anticipate diagnostic test outcomes, recognize potential complications, and implement preventative clinical interventions.

  • Diagnostic tests
  • Potential complications

Physiological Adaptation: Study Guidance

This final domain addresses physiological adaptation, making up 14.0 percent of the total exam weight to assess how nurses manage severe alterations in body systems, unexpected clinical deterioration, and acute medical emergencies.

  • Alterations in body systems
  • Medical emergencies

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive at the testing center prepared for a 300-minute examination. Manage your time carefully across the 8 domains and read each question carefully before selecting an answer.

Frequently asked questions

How many domains are on the exam?

The exam is divided into 8 distinct domains, each carrying a specific weight percentage ranging from 9.0 percent to 18.0 percent of the total test plan.

What is the passing score?

The passing score for this examination is established at or above the official passing standard determined by the governing board of the NCSBN to ensure nursing competency.

How long is the exam?

Candidates taking this professional nursing examination are allotted a total duration of exactly 300 minutes to complete all test questions presented during the testing session without exceptions.

Who administers the NCLEX-RN exam?

The examination is officially certified and administered under the regulatory authority of the NCSBN for all nursing candidates seeking professional licensure in the modern healthcare field today.
