What This Exam Validates

The VCAP-DCV Deploy certification exam is officially published and supported by VMware (Broadcom). It evaluates advanced technical skills across four domains covering installation, configuration, setup, performance-tuning, optimization, upgrades, troubleshooting, repairing, administrative, and operational tasks required for modern enterprise virtualization environments. Candidates must demonstrate proficiency in managing complex vSphere infrastructures to meet the established standards set by the certification program for deployment professionals during the 205-minute examination period, which costs 350 USD.

Who Should Take This Exam

This advanced certification is specifically designed for experienced virtualization administrators and infrastructure engineers with hands-on experience deploying, configuring, and managing complex VMware vSphere enterprise environments who want to validate their advanced deployment capabilities.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Installing and configuring ESXi
  • Setting up vCenter Server
  • Performance-tuning and upgrades
  • Troubleshooting and repairing
  • Administrative and operational tasks

How to Prepare

Review the official VMware (Broadcom) documentation for ESXi installation guidelines, vCenter Server deployment procedures, and advanced auto deploy configurations. Practice hands-on labs focusing on management, configuration, storage settings, system maintenance, and component upgrades to ensure readiness for the practical tasks required during the 205-minute examination period, keeping in mind the registration fee of 350 USD.

Domain Study Guidance

Installing, Configuring, and Setup: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on successfully installing, configuring, and setting up core vSphere components including standalone ESXi hosts, distributed vCenter Server appliances, advanced networking configurations, and shared storage elements within a virtualized data center environment.

  • ESXi installation and setup
  • vCenter Server deployment
  • Auto Deploy configuration

Performance-tuning, Optimization, and Upgrades: Study Guidance

This domain covers performance-tuning methodologies, infrastructure optimization strategies, and systematic upgrade paths for ESXi hosts, vCenter Server instances, and all related virtualization and hardware infrastructure components to ensure high availability and efficient resource utilization.

  • ESXi upgrade processes
  • vCenter Server upgrades
  • Performance optimization

Troubleshooting and Repairing: Study Guidance

This domain involves advanced troubleshooting procedures and repairing complex issues related to boot sequences, software upgrades, failed deployments, faulty configurations, and core enterprise services that maintain the stability of the virtualized infrastructure environment.

  • Troubleshooting installation errors
  • Resolving boot issues
  • Repairing cluster communication

Administrative and Operational Tasks: Study Guidance

This domain addresses administrative and operational tasks such as certificate management, authentication services setup, reliable backups, and proper licensing compliance across the enterprise architecture to maintain security and operational integrity of the system.

  • Managing certificates
  • Authentication services
  • Backup and restore operations

Exam-Day Guidance

Manage your time efficiently across the 205-minute duration of the examination. Read every individual lab prompt carefully and verify all system configurations before proceeding to the next task to ensure accuracy and successful completion of the practical requirements.

Frequently asked questions

How much does the exam cost?

The official exam registration fee is priced at 350 USD. Please check directly with VMware (Broadcom) for registration details, scheduling procedures, and payment policies to ensure you have the most current information regarding your specific testing location and requirements.

What is the passing score?

The required passing score is 300 on a scaled scoring system ranging from 100 to 500 points for all qualified candidates. You must achieve this threshold to earn the certification and validate your technical deployment skills.

How long is the exam?

The exam has an exact duration of 205 minutes to complete all required practical tasks and administrative questions successfully. Ensure you pace yourself throughout the session to address every lab requirement within the allotted time frame provided.

How long is the certification valid?

The certification validity period is exactly 2 years, and successful recertification or renewal is required to maintain an active certified status. Please monitor your certification expiration date to ensure you remain compliant with the current program standards.
