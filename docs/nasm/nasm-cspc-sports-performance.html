What This Exam Validates

The NASM Certified Sports Performance Coach certification is administered by NASM to validate professional expertise across six core domains. This credential covers coaching, sport science, assessments, training models, program design, and nutrition. It serves as a benchmark for individuals seeking to demonstrate their ability to apply evidence-based strategies in athletic settings, ensuring they possess the necessary knowledge to support athlete development and improve overall physical performance outcomes effectively through structured training and conditioning programs.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is intended for fitness professionals, strength coaches, and athletic practitioners who work directly with athletes and want to build expertise in sports performance, physiological testing, and the creation of structured training program designs.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Coaching and Sport Psychology
  • Introduction to Sport Science
  • Assessments, Testing, and Monitoring
  • Components of the Integrated Training Model
  • Program Design
  • Nutrition and Supplementation

How to Prepare

To prepare, review the six domains of the exam. Focus on understanding sport science principles, physiological testing methods, and creating structured training programs. Utilize official resources and practice questions to gauge your readiness before test day. Ensure you are comfortable with the application of these concepts in real-world athletic scenarios to maximize your performance during the 90-minute testing session.

Domain Study Guidance

Coaching and Sport Psychology: Study Guidance

Master the principles of coaching psychology, motivation, team dynamics, and mental strategies used to optimize athlete performance and support overall psychological well-being during high-pressure competitive situations and various training cycles throughout the year.

  • Motivational techniques
  • Team dynamics

Introduction to Sport Science: Study Guidance

Learn the concepts of sport science, physiological responses to exercise, and how movement mechanics influence physical performance, ensuring you understand the biological basis for various athletic training and conditioning interventions used in professional sports.

  • Exercise physiology basics
  • Movement mechanics

Assessments, Testing, and Monitoring: Study Guidance

Understand protocols for athletic testing, functional assessments, and continuous physical monitoring to track progress and evaluate training efficacy, allowing for data-driven adjustments to an athlete's specific strength and conditioning plan for better results.

  • Testing protocols
  • Monitoring methods

Components of the Integrated Training Model: Study Guidance

Examine the components of the integrated training model, integrating various modalities to build strength and conditioning regimens that address the unique physical requirements and performance goals of diverse athletic populations in many different environments.

  • Integrated training components
  • Modality selection

Program Design: Study Guidance

Design structured training programs tailored to specific athletic goals, incorporating periodization and safe progression strategies to ensure athletes reach their peak physical condition while minimizing the risk of overtraining or injury during the season.

  • Program design principles
  • Periodization models

Nutrition and Supplementation: Study Guidance

Review nutritional guidelines, energy requirements, and supplementation practices that support athletic recovery and performance, helping athletes maintain the fuel necessary for intense training sessions and optimal competition day output levels for their specific sport.

  • Sports nutrition fundamentals
  • Energy requirements

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive prepared for a 90-minute testing session. Manage your time effectively across the 100 questions to ensure you complete all sections before the allotted time expires for your certification exam.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The examination consists of 100 questions that cover all six domains of sports performance, requiring candidates to demonstrate their knowledge of coaching, science, and program design principles.

What is the passing score?

Candidates must achieve a passing score of 70% on the examination to successfully earn the certification and demonstrate their competency in the field of sports performance coaching.

How long is the exam?

Test takers are given 90 minutes to complete the examination, which requires careful time management to ensure all 100 questions are answered within the designated testing window.

What is the policy for certification path updates?

This exam is current; please check with NASM for the current certification path and any potential updates regarding requirements or testing procedures for the sports performance credential.
