What This Exam Validates

The Certified in Nonprofit Management exam, administered by the Nonprofit Leadership Alliance, evaluates knowledge across six core modules. These modules cover nonprofit leadership, program sustainability, financial management, marketing, fundraising, and industry trends. The exam serves as a requirement for professionals seeking to demonstrate their expertise in managing nonprofit organizations effectively and ethically within the modern sector. Candidates must demonstrate proficiency in these specific areas to earn their professional credential and validate their management skills.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is designed for nonprofit professionals who possess relevant work experience and a background in strategic planning, organizational budgeting, or program direction within the nonprofit sector and wish to validate their professional expertise.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Nonprofit management and leadership
  • Long-term program sustainability
  • Budgeting and financial oversight
  • Marketing and communications strategy
  • Fundraising and resource development
  • Innovation and sector trends

How to Prepare

Candidates should review the official study guide provided through the Nonprofit Leadership Alliance. The guide is organized into 6 modules. We recommend that you allocate study time per module based on your existing knowledge. Utilize the included case studies and practice quizzes to test your understanding of the material before sitting for the 100-question exam to ensure you are fully prepared for the assessment.

Domain Study Guidance

Module 1: Management and Leadership of the Nonprofit Sector: Study Guidance

This module covers the historical development and primary roles of nonprofit organizations, human resource planning techniques, and the specific governance responsibilities held by the board of directors in nonprofit settings for effective leadership.

  • Nonprofit history
  • Board roles

Module 2: Designing Programs for Long-term Sustainability: Study Guidance

This module focuses on the process of determining community needs and applying various planning and evaluation models to ensure long-term program sustainability and organizational impact within the nonprofit sector environment for all stakeholders.

  • Community needs assessment
  • Sustainability strategies

Module 3: Nonprofit Budgeting and Financial Management: Study Guidance

This module addresses financial management topics, including various budgeting processes, compliance with IRS regulations, and the board's role in maintaining financial oversight and transparency for the organization to ensure long-term fiscal health.

  • Budgeting processes
  • Financial oversight

Module 4: Nonprofit Communications and Marketing: Study Guidance

This module covers core marketing functions, the development of marketing plans, and effective strategies for advocacy and public policy processes to support the mission and goals of the nonprofit organization in the community.

  • Marketing plans
  • Public policy

Module 5: Nonprofit Fundraising and Resource Development: Study Guidance

This module explores fundraising functions, understanding donor motivation, implementing effective cultivation strategies, and maintaining high-quality stewardship practices to ensure the ongoing financial health and resource development of the nonprofit organization for future growth.

  • Fundraising concepts
  • Stewardship

Module 6: Innovation and Trends in the Nonprofit Sector: Study Guidance

This module examines current sector issues, innovation challenges, and the key differences between domestic nonprofits and global nongovernmental organizations to prepare leaders for the evolving landscape of the nonprofit industry and global trends.

  • Current trends
  • Global comparisons

Exam-Day Guidance

The exam is a 120-minute timed assessment. Ensure you are prepared to answer 100 multiple-choice or true/false questions. Manage your time effectively across the six modules to ensure you meet the required passing score.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of 100 multiple-choice or true/false questions. You have 120 minutes to complete the assessment, so please manage your time carefully to ensure you answer every question before the timer expires.

What is the passing score?

To successfully pass the exam and earn your certification, you must achieve a score of 76% or higher. Please review all study materials to ensure you meet this required performance standard.

How long is the exam?

The exam is a 120-minute timed assessment. You should plan to arrive early and ensure you are ready to focus for the full duration of the testing period to complete all sections.

Where can I find the current certification path?

For information regarding the current certification path, check with the Nonprofit Leadership Alliance. They provide the up-to-date guidance on requirements, study resources, and any changes to the professional certification process.
