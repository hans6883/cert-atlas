What This Exam Validates

The ABD Dermatology Board Certification evaluates professional knowledge and clinical competencies required for modern dermatology practice. Certified and overseen by the American Board of Dermatology (ABD), the evaluation spans exactly 376 minutes and consists of precisely 200 questions distributed across four primary clinical domains for candidates seeking complete professional validation.

Who Should Take This Exam

Residents and practitioners seeking professional board certification in dermatology from the American Board of Dermatology (ABD). Candidates must demonstrate clinical readiness across medical, surgical, and pediatric specialties.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Medical dermatology evaluation and management
  • Pediatric skin disorders and congenital conditions
  • Dermatologic surgery and procedural safety
  • Dermatopathology and microscopic biopsy analysis

How to Prepare

Review all core competencies across the four designated subject domains. Carefully allocate your study time according to the official topic weights, prioritizing medical dermatology which constitutes 55.0 percent of the entire content. Check with American Board of Dermatology (ABD) for any additional updates and guidance.

Domain Study Guidance

Medical Dermatology: Study Guidance

This domain covers medical dermatology topics, representing 55.0 percent of the entire evaluation. It focuses heavily on clinical management, therapeutic interventions, and clinical diagnosis of various common and complex skin conditions.

  • Medical dermatology evaluation
  • Cutaneous disease management

Pediatric Dermatology: Study Guidance

This domain covers pediatric dermatology topics, representing 15.0 percent of the entire evaluation. It focuses heavily on skin disorders and congenital conditions affecting infants, children, and adolescents during their developmental stages.

  • Pediatric skin disorders
  • Congenital cutaneous conditions

Surgical Dermatology: Study Guidance

This domain covers surgical dermatology topics, representing 15.0 percent of the entire evaluation. It focuses heavily on procedural techniques, operative safety protocols, and skin surgery interventions performed by practicing specialists.

  • Dermatologic surgery methods
  • Procedural safety and outcomes

Dermatopathology: Study Guidance

This domain covers dermatopathology topics, representing 15.0 percent of the entire evaluation. It focuses heavily on microscopic analysis, laboratory evaluation, and pathologic interpretation of skin biopsy specimens and tissue samples.

  • Microscopic skin pathology
  • Biopsy specimen interpretation

Exam-Day Guidance

Manage your time effectively across the 200 questions. Ensure you are familiar with the testing format and arrive prepared for the total duration of 376 minutes.
