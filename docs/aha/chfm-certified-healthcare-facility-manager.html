What This Exam Validates

The Certified Healthcare Facility Manager certification examination encompasses five major content areas including compliance, healthcare project management, maintenance and operations, finance management, and administration. The American Hospital Association Certification Center conducts this credentialing program to test professional competence, technical knowledge, and operational proficiency in managing modern healthcare facilities across various clinical environments during the 120 minutes allowed.

Who Should Take This Exam

Healthcare facility managers, engineering professionals, and supervisors with specified degrees and years of associated engineering and management experience in a healthcare setting qualify to take this certification examination.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Compliance and regulatory standards
  • Healthcare project management
  • Maintenance and operations
  • Finance management
  • Facility administration

How to Prepare

Begin your preparation by carefully reviewing the official examination content outline and guidelines provided in the handbook to understand every single tested concept. Complete the self-assessment examination to identify your current knowledge gaps, and study all listed source resources carefully to ensure you are fully prepared for test day success.

Domain Study Guidance

Compliance: Study Guidance

This domain focuses heavily on building codes, safety regulations, and environmental standards that must be strictly met in all accredited healthcare facilities to ensure patient safety and maintain regulatory compliance.

  • Building Codes
  • Occupational Safety and Health Administration
  • Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services

Healthcare Project Management: Study Guidance

This domain covers pre-construction risk assessments, detailed architectural design reviews, and careful construction coordination within complex, active healthcare environments designed to protect both patients and staff members from potential environmental hazards.

  • Pre-Construction Risk Assessment
  • Infection Control Risk Assessment
  • Interim Life Safety Measures

Maintenance and Operations: Study Guidance

This operational domain addresses the safe day-to-day operation and maintenance of building systems, designated utility management programs, and preventive maintenance strategies required in modern hospitals to sustain continuous facility operations without interruption.

  • Utility management program
  • HVAC and refrigeration systems
  • Emergency power supply systems

Finance Management: Study Guidance

This financial domain involves managing operational, construction, capital, and utility budgets while evaluating equipment and service bids to ensure fiscal responsibility and cost effectiveness within the hospital facility department.

  • Operations and maintenance budgets
  • Construction projects budgets
  • Capital and utilities budgets

Administration: Study Guidance

This administrative domain deals with departmental administration, routine policy revision, long-range capital improvement planning, and human resource management to maintain high organizational standards across the entire healthcare facility environment.

  • Reviewing policies and procedures
  • Managing human resource functions
  • Long-range capital improvement plan

Exam-Day Guidance

Report to the PSI test center with proper identification. On-site security rules apply, and calculators are permitted during the 120 minutes allowed for the exam containing 110 questions.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The certification examination is composed of a total of 110 multiple-choice questions designed to test your professional knowledge across all required healthcare facility management domains.

What is the passing score?

The official examination uses the Modified Angoff method to determine the required passing standard, and the specific minimum passing score is not published in the candidate handbook.

How long is the exam?

A candidate is allowed exactly 120 minutes, or two full hours, in which to complete the entire examination at authorized PSI test centers located around the world.

Who administers the examination?

The American Hospital Association Certification Center contracts with PSI Services to assist in the development, administration, scoring, score reporting, and security analysis of the examination.
