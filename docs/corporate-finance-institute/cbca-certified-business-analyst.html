What This Exam Validates

The CBCA Certified Business and Credit Analyst certification, administered by the Corporate Finance Institute, evaluates proficiency in commercial banking and credit analysis. The exam covers six distinct domains, including credit analysis, credit risk, accounting, qualitative business analysis, loan structure, security and pricing, and financial modeling. It provides a structured assessment for professionals working in commercial lending and credit management roles who wish to validate their technical expertise in the financial services sector through this formal testing process.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is designed for professionals in commercial banking, credit analysis, and lending. It is suitable for those managing credit portfolios, performing detailed financial statement analysis, or evaluating borrower risk who wish to demonstrate their technical competence in credit risk management and commercial lending practices.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Credit analysis techniques
  • Credit risk assessment
  • Accounting principles
  • Qualitative business analysis
  • Loan structure and pricing
  • Financial modeling

How to Prepare

Review the core concepts of credit risk management and financial statement analysis. Focus on the six domains, noting that credit analysis and credit risk each account for 25% of the exam. Practice applying accounting principles and loan pricing models to real-world scenarios. Ensure familiarity with the 50 total questions and the 180-minute time limit to manage your pace effectively during the assessment. Candidates should utilize all provided study materials to ensure they are prepared for the exam format.

Domain Study Guidance

Credit Analysis: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the evaluation of creditworthiness and the assessment of repayment capacity for commercial borrowers. It requires candidates to understand how to analyze financial data to determine the ability of a business to meet its debt obligations.

  • Credit risk rating systems
  • Borrower repayment capacity

Credit Risk: Study Guidance

This domain covers the identification, measurement, and monitoring of credit risk within a bank's loan portfolio. Candidates must demonstrate an understanding of how to manage risk rating process controls and maintain portfolio health through oversight and systematic risk management techniques.

  • Risk rating process controls
  • Portfolio risk management

Accounting: Study Guidance

This domain addresses the application of accounting standards and financial statement analysis to assess borrower health. It emphasizes the importance of interpreting financial statements to identify potential accounting issues that could impact the credit profile of a prospective borrower.

  • Financial statement analysis
  • Accounting issues in credit

Qualitative Business Analysis: Study Guidance

This domain examines non-quantitative factors that influence the credit risk profile of a business relationship. Candidates will learn how to evaluate qualitative considerations and management quality to form a view of the borrower beyond the raw financial data provided.

  • Qualitative considerations
  • Management quality

Loan Structure, Security & Pricing: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the structural elements of credit facilities, including collateral and pricing strategies. It covers the components of loan structure and credit pricing, ensuring that candidates can design facilities that align with the risk profile of the borrower.

  • Loan structure
  • Credit pricing

Financial Modeling: Study Guidance

This domain covers the use of financial models to support credit analysis and decision-making processes. It includes the application of financial modeling techniques and the integration of automated scoring systems to improve the accuracy of credit-related business decisions.

  • Financial modeling techniques
  • Automated scoring systems

Exam-Day Guidance

The exam consists of 50 questions to be completed in 180 minutes. You must achieve a passing score of 70% to earn the certification. Manage your time across the six domains to ensure all sections are addressed before the time expires during your testing session.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The CBCA Certified Business and Credit Analyst exam consists of 50 total questions. Candidates should be prepared to answer these questions within the allotted time to ensure they cover all required domains of the certification assessment successfully.

What is the passing score?

To successfully pass the exam and earn the certification, you must achieve a score of 70%. This threshold ensures that all certified professionals have demonstrated a level of proficiency in commercial banking and credit analysis required for the industry.

How long is the exam?

The exam has a duration of 180 minutes. This time limit is designed to allow candidates enough time to demonstrate their knowledge across all six domains while maintaining a steady pace throughout the entire testing experience for the certification.
