What This Exam Validates

The Certified Revenue Management Executive (CRME) certification, offered by HSMAI, provides professional knowledge in revenue optimization. The program covers eleven domains, ranging from revenue strategy and economics to performance analysis and distribution. It is intended for experienced professionals who demonstrate tactical and strategic expertise within their organizations. The certification process requires an approved application followed by an online exam consisting of 50 questions to ensure candidates meet the required standards for professional excellence in the hospitality industry.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is designed for revenue management professionals. Candidates must demonstrate eligibility through a points-based application process, assessing their professional experience, leadership, and industry contributions before they are approved to take the exam.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Developing revenue strategy
  • Applying economic principles
  • Integrating departmental functions
  • Analyzing market segments
  • Managing pricing and inventory
  • Evaluating performance metrics
  • Optimizing distribution channels

How to Prepare

Applicants should study the official guide, Evolving Dynamics: From Revenue Management to Revenue Strategy. Review the eleven domains and utilize the provided practice exam to assess readiness. Candidates may also participate in review sessions or instructor-led online training. Ensure the application is submitted and approved before scheduling the exam. Preparation is a necessary step for candidates to achieve the 70% passing score required for certification.

This domain addresses the necessity of aligning revenue management functions with other hotel departments, fostering collaboration to ensure that all operational areas work together toward shared profitability and guest satisfaction goals for the business.

Exam-Day Guidance

The exam is administered online. Ensure you have a stable internet connection and a quiet environment. You have 60 minutes to complete the 50 questions. Manage your time effectively across all eleven domains to ensure you address every section within the allotted duration.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The CRME certification exam consists of exactly 50 questions. These questions are designed to test your practical knowledge of the functions performed in revenue management as outlined in the official study guide provided by the organization.

What is the passing score?

To successfully earn the CRME certification, you must achieve a passing score of 70% on the online exam. This benchmark ensures that all certified professionals demonstrate a high level of competency in their revenue management roles.

How long is the exam?

The exam is a 60-minute assessment. You must complete the exam after having your application approved by HSMAI, so please plan your study schedule and testing date accordingly to meet this specific program requirement for all candidates.

What is the certification path?

The certification process involves an application review followed by an examination. Please check with HSMAI for the current certification path and any updates regarding eligibility requirements or the application process for new applicants.
