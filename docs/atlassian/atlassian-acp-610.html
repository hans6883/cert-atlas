Historical ACP-610 Scope

The Atlassian Certified Project Administrator for Jira Cloud exam was a formal certification administered by Atlassian that evaluated candidate knowledge across five distinct domains with a total of 75 questions. Because this exam officially retired on 2025-09-11, candidates should instead direct their preparation toward the verified successor exam, ACP-620, which represents the designated path forward.

Who ACP-610 Was For

Project administrators and Jira practitioners aiming to demonstrate cloud project configuration skills. Because this credential is retired, candidates must register for the active successor certification instead.

  • Project creation and configuration
  • Board and backlog management
  • Workflows and automation
  • Permissions and notifications
  • Reports and dashboards

How to Reuse Your Preparation

Preparation strategies should target the ACP-620 successor exam rather than attempting to study for this retired test. Although core knowledge of Jira Cloud configuration, workflows, and board management transfers directly, candidates must review current cloud documentation to learn fresh features and updated interface patterns before testing.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

During its active lifecycle, the examination consisted of a total of 75 multiple choice and multiple select questions designed to carefully evaluate candidate knowledge across all five distinct domains.

What is the passing score?

Candidates were required to achieve a minimum passing score of 70 in order to successfully pass the examination and earn the designated credential from Atlassian during its active period.

How long is the exam?

The active examination had an allocated total duration of 120 minutes, giving all registered candidates adequate time to complete every assigned question within the testing session successfully.

What is the price of the exam?

The standard registration price for taking the examination was established at $250 USD for all eligible participating candidates who registered through the official platform during its operational lifecycle.
