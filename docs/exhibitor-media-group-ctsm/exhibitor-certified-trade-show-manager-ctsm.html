What This Exam Validates

The Certified Trade Show Manager (CTSM) program, administered by Exhibitor Media Group, provides professional certification for trade show and corporate event marketers. The curriculum covers seven domains, including measurement, planning, and global marketing. Candidates complete coursework, an exam, and an applied learning portfolio to earn their credentials. This program is structured for professionals seeking to develop their expertise in event marketing through established industry standards and educational requirements.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is for trade show and corporate event marketing professionals. It is suitable for those seeking to gain structured knowledge within their organizations and expand their professional capabilities through specialized training and development.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Measurement and analytics
  • Event planning and execution
  • Marketing and sales strategies
  • Management and leadership
  • Global exhibit marketing
  • Experience and event design
  • Personal and career development

How to Prepare

Candidates move through the program at their own pace. You can access coursework at conferences or online through available portals. If you meet specific industry criteria, you may request course substitutions. Utilize the provided resources and guidance to assist with your progress through the required and elective study components.

Domain Study Guidance

Measurement & Analytics: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the use of data to evaluate trade show performance and marketing effectiveness. It covers how to collect, interpret, and apply metrics to demonstrate the return on investment for various corporate event marketing activities.

  • Performance metrics
  • Data analysis techniques

Planning & Execution: Study Guidance

This domain covers the logistical and operational aspects of managing trade show projects. It emphasizes the importance of detailed planning, resource allocation, and the execution strategies required to deliver successful events within a professional corporate environment.

  • Project planning
  • Execution strategies

Marketing & Sales: Study Guidance

This domain addresses the integration of trade shows into broader marketing and sales initiatives. It explores how to align event objectives with overall company goals and effectively manage sales leads generated during the trade show experience.

  • Marketing alignment
  • Sales lead management

Management & Leadership: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on leadership skills and management practices within the event marketing industry. It provides insights into leading teams, managing vendor relationships, and applying effective management techniques to ensure the success of complex marketing projects.

  • Team leadership
  • Management techniques

Global Exhibit Marketing: Study Guidance

This domain covers the complexities of marketing exhibits on a global scale. It examines the unique challenges of international marketing, including cultural considerations, regulatory requirements, and the development of a cohesive global exhibit strategy for brands.

  • International marketing
  • Global exhibit strategy

Exhibits, Experiences & Events: Study Guidance

This domain explores the design and implementation of exhibits, experiences, and events. It focuses on creating engaging environments that capture attendee attention while effectively communicating brand messages through innovative design and high-quality event production techniques.

  • Experience design
  • Event production

Personal & Career: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the professional growth and career advancement of the trade show manager. It provides guidance on long-term career planning, networking, and continuous professional development to maintain relevance in the evolving event marketing industry.

  • Career planning
  • Professional development

Exam-Day Guidance

The exam is a 180-minute assessment. Ensure you are prepared to demonstrate your knowledge of the core curriculum. Manage your time effectively to complete the exam following your coursework completion and portfolio preparation.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the CTSM exam?

The CTSM exam has a duration of 180 minutes. Candidates are expected to complete the exam after finishing the required classroom study and the applied learning portfolio to earn their professional certification.

What are the core domains?

The program curriculum is structured around 7 distinct domains covering essential topics such as measurement, planning, marketing, leadership, global strategy, experience design, and professional development.

How can I submit substitution requests?

If you have qualifying industry background, you may submit a substitution request to Exhibitor Media Group to determine if your prior professional history meets the criteria for credit toward your certification requirements.

What is the current certification path?

Check with Exhibitor Media Group (CTSM) for the current certification path and any updates regarding program requirements, course availability, and administrative guidelines for candidates pursuing their credentials.
