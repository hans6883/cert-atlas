What This Exam Validates

The Certified Six Sigma White Belt (CSSWB) is offered by ASQ and confirms a candidate’s grasp of fundamental Six Sigma concepts, lean principles, basic process improvement methods, a quality‑focused mindset, and elementary problem‑solving tools. The exam is linear, consists of multiple‑choice questions, costs $0, and the certification remains valid for 3 years, after which renewal is required.

Who Should Take This Exam

Professionals new to quality management, production supervisors, engineers, or anyone interested in learning Six Sigma and lean basics should consider the CSSWB to demonstrate basic knowledge and support career growth in continuous improvement.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Six Sigma fundamentals
  • Lean waste reduction
  • Process improvement steps
  • Quality mindset principles
  • Basic problem‑solving tools

How to Prepare

Start with the ASQ certification page to review the 5 domain titles. Study each area using ASQ’s free resources, focusing on DMAIC, waste types, PDCA, customer‑centric quality, and basic tools like cause‑and‑effect diagrams. Practice multiple‑choice questions, join an online study group, and schedule a quiet, uninterrupted session before the exam.

Domain Study Guidance

Introduction to Six Sigma: Study Guidance

Understand the definition of Six Sigma, its goal of reducing variation and defects, the DMAIC cycle, and how it fits within an organization’s quality strategy, including the roles of champions, belts, and the data‑driven approach.

  • DMAIC phases
  • Six Sigma goals
  • Key roles (Champion, Belt)

Lean Fundamentals: Study Guidance

Learn the core principles of Lean, focusing on waste elimination, value‑stream mapping, the seven (or eight) types of waste, and how Lean complements Six Sigma to improve flow and efficiency.

  • Types of waste (DOWNTIME)
  • Value‑stream mapping
  • Lean vs. Six Sigma

Process Improvement Concepts: Study Guidance

Explore systematic improvement methods such as the PDCA cycle, continuous improvement, and basic statistical tools that support analyzing and enhancing processes, including root‑cause analysis and measurement of process capability to drive measurable performance gains.

  • PDCA cycle
  • Root‑cause analysis
  • Process capability measurement

Quality Mindset: Study Guidance

Adopt a quality‑first perspective that emphasizes customer satisfaction, defect prevention, data‑driven decision making, and continuous learning across all organizational levels. It encourages every employee to act as an agent of change and to align daily work with quality goals.

  • Customer focus
  • Defect prevention
  • Employee empowerment

Basic Problem-Solving Tools: Study Guidance

Familiarize with fundamental tools such as cause‑and‑effect diagrams, basic statistical process control, simple data collection techniques, and the use of visual controls to identify and resolve issues. These tools support quick, low‑cost improvements.

  • Cause‑and-effect diagram
  • Statistical process control basics
  • Visual controls

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive early, ensure a quiet environment, have a reliable internet connection, keep a calculator handy, read each question carefully, and manage time to answer all multiple‑choice items within the allotted period.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

ASQ does not publish an exact question count for the White Belt exam. The assessment is presented in a linear format and uses only multiple‑choice items, so candidates should be ready for an unspecified number of questions while focusing on the 5 domain areas.

What is the passing score?

ASQ does not disclose a numeric passing score for the White Belt. Candidates must demonstrate competency across the five domains to meet the certification criteria, and the result is reported as pass or fail based on that standard.

How long is the exam?

The official ASQ page does not specify a fixed time limit for the White Belt exam. Test‑takers should allocate enough time to work through the linear, multiple‑choice format, typically planning for a sufficient session to ensure completion.

What does it cost to take the exam?

According to the ASQ certification page, the Certified Six Sigma White Belt exam is offered at no charge, listed as $0 in the exam price. This makes it accessible for individuals seeking to begin their Six Sigma journey without a fee.
