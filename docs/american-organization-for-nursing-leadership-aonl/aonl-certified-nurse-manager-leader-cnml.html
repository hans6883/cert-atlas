What This Exam Validates

The CNML - Certified Nurse Manager and Leader certification is administered by the American Organization for Nursing Leadership (AONL). It assesses nurse managers across five core domains including Communication and Relationship Building, Health Care Environment and Clinical Principles, Leadership, Professionalism, and Business Skills and Principles to ensure complete administrative readiness for healthcare leaders.

Who Should Take This Exam

Nurse managers and aspiring healthcare leaders seeking official validation of their administrative, clinical leadership, and operational competencies should take this certification exam to demonstrate their professional expertise.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Communication and Relationship Building
  • Health Care Environment & Clinical Principles
  • Leadership
  • Professionalism
  • Business Skills and Principles

Domain Study Guidance

Communication and Relationship Building: Study Guidance

This domain carefully covers communication strategies, relationship building, and interpersonal dynamics required for nurse leaders to successfully interact with staff, patients, and multidisciplinary healthcare teams within complex organizational structures.

  • Relationship building
  • Communication methods

Health Care Environment & Clinical Principles: Study Guidance

This domain focuses intensely on health care environment settings and core clinical principles relevant to management roles, ensuring that nurse leaders understand patient care delivery systems, safety regulations, and clinical quality standards.

  • Health care environment
  • Clinical principles

Leadership: Study Guidance

This domain addresses core leadership practices, team direction, and organizational management competencies that empower nurse managers to guide their departments effectively, foster positive work cultures, and navigate complex healthcare operational challenges.

  • Leadership principles
  • Team direction

Professionalism: Study Guidance

This domain examines professional standards, ethics, and accountability expected of certified nurse leaders, ensuring that candidates adhere to the highest codes of conduct, legal guidelines, and professional nursing values in their daily practice.

  • Professional standards
  • Ethics

Business Skills and Principles: Study Guidance

This domain covers standard business skills, financial principles, and operational management required in health care organizations, preparing managers to handle budgeting, resource allocation, strategic planning, and cost-effective department administration successfully.

  • Business skills
  • Financial principles

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The certification exam consists of a total of 115 questions spanning all five of the core content domains established by the American Organization for Nursing Leadership.

What is the passing score?

The passing standard for the assessment is specifically set at 75 scored items in order to demonstrate adequate competency for all certification applicants and candidates.

How long is the exam?

Candidates taking this healthcare management assessment are given a total duration of 120 minutes to complete the evaluation and successfully answer all required questions within the designated testing time limit without interruption.

Who administers the certification?

The certification program is officially administered, governed, and maintained directly by the American Organization for Nursing Leadership (AONL) for qualified nursing professionals seeking formal career recognition and professional advancement.
