PCQI: Preventive Controls Qualified Individual Credential

Food Safety Preventive Controls Alliance (FSPCA)

Not an examination

Food Safety Preventive Controls Alliance (FSPCA) does not offer an examination for PCQI: Preventive Controls Qualified Individual: it is a training programme. No exam blueprint, domain weights, exam code or practice test apply. Verified on the issuer's site September 19, 2026. issuer page

What This Exam Validates

The PCQI: Preventive Controls Qualified Individual certification, administered by the Food Safety Preventive Controls Alliance (FSPCA), validates your ability to develop and apply risk-based preventive controls. The program ensures you can oversee the preparation of food safety plans, perform hazard analysis, and manage verification and validation procedures in compliance with U.S. food safety regulations for human food manufacturing and processing. This certification allows professionals to demonstrate expertise in maintaining high standards of food safety and regulatory compliance within their respective manufacturing facilities, ensuring that all safety protocols meet the 1320 minutes of required training standards across the 5 distinct domains of study.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is for individuals responsible for performing or overseeing the preparation of food safety plans. It is suitable for those who need to meet the requirements for a preventive controls qualified individual through a standardized curriculum recognized by the FDA.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Developing food safety plans
  • Conducting hazard analysis
  • Implementing preventive controls
  • Monitoring and verification
  • Managing recordkeeping
  • Applying regulatory requirements

How to Prepare

Review the FSPCA participant manual and course materials to understand the standardized curriculum. Engage with the 5 domains by practicing the development of a food safety plan using the provided templates and worksheets. Utilize the official FSPCA resources and participate in the required training delivery methods, such as instructor-led or asynchronous learning, to ensure you are prepared for the application of these food safety principles. The training curriculum requires a minimum of 1320 minutes of contact time to complete for all candidates.

Domain Study Guidance

Domain 1: Study Guidance

This domain covers the initial steps for creating a food safety plan, including the resources needed to begin the process and the preliminary requirements for facility operations and management to ensure compliance with the 1320 minutes of training.

  • Food safety plan overview
  • Preliminary development steps

Domain 2: Study Guidance

This section focuses on identifying biological, chemical, and physical hazards and determining the appropriate preventive controls to minimize or eliminate these risks within a food facility for consumer safety across the 5 distinct domains of study.

  • Hazard analysis techniques
  • Preventive control determination

Domain 3: Study Guidance

This domain addresses the ongoing management of preventive controls, including monitoring procedures, verification and validation activities, and the execution of corrective actions when specific food safety issues arise during operations in a manufacturing facility environment.

  • Monitoring and verification
  • Corrective action procedures

Domain 4: Study Guidance

This domain covers the requirements for maintaining accurate records to demonstrate that the food safety system is operating as intended and to provide necessary data for official regulatory review processes during any facility audit or inspection.

  • Recordkeeping procedures
  • Documentation standards

Domain 5: Study Guidance

This domain explains the regulatory landscape, specifically the requirements for cGMP, hazard analysis, and risk-based preventive controls for human food as mandated by the FDA for all food manufacturing facilities operating within the United States.

  • Regulatory overview
  • Compliance requirements

The provided documentation does not specify the exact number of questions on the exam. Please consult the Food Safety Preventive Controls Alliance (FSPCA) for detailed information regarding the structure and format of the assessment before you begin your training.

How long is the training?

The training curriculum requires a total of 1320 minutes to complete. Please verify specific testing timeframes with your FSPCA course provider to ensure you are prepared for the full duration of the assessment session and the associated requirements for your professional certification path.
