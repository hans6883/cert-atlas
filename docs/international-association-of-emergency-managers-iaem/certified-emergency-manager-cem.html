What This Exam Validates

The Certified Emergency Manager examination is a comprehensive professional credential administered by the International Association of Emergency Managers (IAEM). It thoroughly evaluates qualified professionals across eight essential operational domains, including risk management, program preparedness, mitigation strategies, incident response coordination, recovery operations, technologies, administrative practices, and professional development.

Who Should Take This Exam

Emergency management personnel, emergency responders, and officials responsible for making decisions regarding incidents across all levels of government, nongovernmental organizations, and the private sector worldwide.

How to Prepare

Review the official exam guides and relevant documentation provided by the International Association of Emergency Managers (IAEM). Focus your study across all eight domains, ensuring complete familiarity with resource management, command structures, and communication procedures before taking the test to achieve a passing score of 500 on a scale of 200 to 800.

Domain Study Guidance

Conduct Risk Management Duties: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the 6.45% of the exam covering risk management duties, hazard identification, and related methodological practices required by emergency management professionals during their daily work across various complex response environments.

  • Risk assessment practices
  • Hazard identification

Manage Preparedness Programs: Study Guidance

This domain covers 21.65% of the exam, emphasizing the management of preparedness programs, detailed operational plans, and resource management preparedness across various sectors and emergency response organizations to ensure readiness.

  • Preparedness program planning
  • Resource management preparedness

Manage Mitigation Programs: Study Guidance

This domain represents 6.24% of the exam content focused on managing mitigation programs, long-term mitigation strategies, and the successful implementation of programs designed to reduce disaster risks in vulnerable communities.

  • Mitigation strategies
  • Program implementation

Coordinate Incident Responses: Study Guidance

This domain addresses the 20.24% of the exam focused on coordinating incident responses using command structures, Incident Command System operations, and Unified Command coordination during major emergencies and complex disaster events.

  • Incident Command System operations
  • Unified Command coordination

Manage Recovery Operations: Study Guidance

This domain involves 11.14% of the exam related to managing recovery operations, post-incident activities, and providing necessary post-incident support for affected communities trying to rebuild after experiencing severe natural or man-made disasters.

  • Recovery operations management
  • Post-incident support

Manage Emergency Management-based Technologies: Study Guidance

This domain pertains to 5.01% of the exam involving emergency management-based technologies, technology use during incidents, and effective information management systems utilized by response teams during crisis operations.

  • Technology use in incidents
  • Information management systems

Perform Administrative Duties: Study Guidance

This domain covers 18.73% of the exam concentrating on performing administrative duties, administrative procedures, and maintaining proper documentation standards throughout emergency operations and agency programs to ensure full compliance.

  • Administrative procedures
  • Documentation standards

Engage in Professional Development Activities: Study Guidance

This domain accounts for 10.54% of the exam focusing on professional development activities, professional growth, and adherence to established professional ethics and conduct within the emergency management field for all practitioners.

  • Professional development
  • Ethics and conduct

Exam-Day Guidance

Manage your time carefully across the 120 questions during the 150-minute test duration to ensure you answer every question successfully before your testing time expires.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The certification exam consists of a total of 120 questions that span eight different domains of emergency management and professional practice for all registered candidates.

What is the passing score?

The passing score required to pass this certification exam is 500 on a scale ranging from 200 to 800 for all test takers seeking professional credentials.

How long is the exam?

The total test duration allocated for candidates to complete all of the examination questions is 150 minutes in length from start to finish during your testing appointment.

Who certifies the exam?

The official certification program is administered and certified by the International Association of Emergency Managers (IAEM) for all qualified emergency management professionals globally across the industry to recognize standards of practice.
