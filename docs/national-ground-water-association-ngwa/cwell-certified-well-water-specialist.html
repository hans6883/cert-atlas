What This Exam Validates

The National Ground Water Association Certified Well Driller Examination evaluates professional competence across various drilling methods and techniques through 7 distinct exam modules. Candidates must achieve a passing score of 70% to demonstrate their knowledge in well construction, water systems, and related operations. Starting with Domain 1 on general drilling principles, candidates also test on method-specific domains to establish technical proficiency under National Ground Water Association standards.

Who Should Take This Exam

Well drillers and contractors seeking professional certification through the National Ground Water Association. Candidates should have practical experience in ground water systems and drilling operations to ensure they meet the requirements for this industry-recognized credential.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • General drilling practices
  • Cable tool drilling
  • Air rotary drilling
  • Mud rotary drilling
  • Reverse circulation drilling
  • Jetting and driving wells
  • Augering and monitoring

How to Prepare

Plan your study time carefully in advance using short, manageable blocks. Take detailed notes, consult regularly with experienced coworkers, supervisors, or equipment manufacturers, and review the National Ground Water Association exam guides and recommended bookstore texts to ensure readiness. Consistent practice and engagement with industry materials will help you master the required technical concepts for the examination.

Domain Study Guidance

General drilling exam: Study Guidance

This initial domain covers fundamental principles, general industry practices, site preparation steps, and basic water well construction methods that remain applicable across the entire drilling sector for all candidates preparing for certification.

  • General drilling practices
  • Site preparation

Exam A/B: Cable tool drilling: Study Guidance

Focuses on the specific techniques, mechanical equipment operation, maintenance routines, and safety procedures used in traditional cable tool drilling operations for water well installation projects within the drilling industry for candidates.

  • Cable tool equipment operation
  • Tool maintenance

Exam C/D: Air rotary drilling: Study Guidance

Addresses the mechanics, operational parameters, direct air usage, drilling fluids management, and necessary safety measures required for successful and stable air rotary drilling rig operations in various geological formations across projects.

  • Air rotary rig operation
  • Borehole stability

Exam E/F: Mud rotary drilling: Study Guidance

Covers fluid circulation systems, mud property management, equipment operations, and precise well completion procedures that are specific to mud rotary drilling methods and challenging formations encountered during water well projects today.

  • Mud rotary equipment
  • Well completion

Exam G: Reverse circulation drilling: Study Guidance

Examines large-diameter well construction techniques using reverse circulation drilling methods, standard pump setups, and formation sampling procedures required for industrial and municipal water projects managed by professional well drillers nationwide.

  • Reverse circulation principles
  • Formation sampling

Exam H: Jetting and driving wells: Study Guidance

Focuses on the distinct procedures involved in jetting and driving shallow wells for effective water extraction, including driving well points and installing small-diameter water systems safely and efficiently across various field sites.

  • Jetting techniques
  • Shallow well installation

Exam M: Augering and monitoring: Study Guidance

Involves augering methods and the installation of monitoring wells for environmental, hydrogeological, and geological assessments, ensuring accurate site sampling preparation and regulatory compliance for all professional drilling operations.

  • Auger rig operation
  • Monitoring well installation

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive early at the test center and bring a government-issued ID. Personal items and outside materials are prohibited in the testing room during this closed-book exam. Ensure you are prepared to follow all center policies to maintain the integrity of your testing experience.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The official documentation provided across all 7 exam modules by the National Ground Water Association does not specify the exact total number of questions featured. Candidates should focus on mastering the core drilling domains and safety practices outlined in the study guides.

What is the passing score?

Candidates taking the National Ground Water Association Certified Well Driller Examination must achieve a minimum passing score of 70% across the 7 modules to pass successfully. This requirement ensures that all certified professionals demonstrate a high level of competency in well drilling techniques and industry standards.

How long is the exam?

The official source documentation published for candidates across the 7 modules does not specify a strict time duration limit for completing this professional certification examination. Candidates are encouraged to manage their time effectively during the test to ensure they have sufficient opportunity to address all questions presented.

What is the certifying body for this exam?

The exam is administered and certified by the National Ground Water Association for qualified professionals working within the water well drilling industry. This organization sets the standards for professional practice and ensures that certified individuals meet the necessary requirements for safe operations.
