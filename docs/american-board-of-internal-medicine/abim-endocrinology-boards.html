What This Exam Validates

The American Board of Internal Medicine administers this certification to assess clinical expertise in endocrinology, diabetes, and metabolism. The exam covers eight distinct domains, including adrenal and pituitary disorders, reproductive health, and metabolic conditions. It serves as a formal evaluation of a physician's ability to diagnose and manage complex endocrine diseases, ensuring practitioners meet the standards required for board certification in this medical field while demonstrating knowledge of patient care protocols and clinical management strategies.

Who Should Take This Exam

Physicians seeking board certification in endocrinology, diabetes, and metabolism should take this exam. It is intended for medical professionals who have completed their internal medicine training and are pursuing formal recognition of their clinical expertise in managing various endocrine and metabolic disorders.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Managing adrenal and pituitary disorders
  • Treating lipid, obesity, and nutrition issues
  • Addressing female and male reproductive health
  • Controlling diabetes mellitus and hypoglycemia
  • Diagnosing calcium and bone disorders
  • Managing thyroid conditions

Domain Study Guidance

Adrenal Disorders: Study Guidance

This domain covers the diagnosis and management of adrenal gland conditions and associated hormonal imbalances, requiring an understanding of clinical presentations and diagnostic testing for patients with adrenal insufficiency, hypercortisolism, and various adrenal tumors.

  • Adrenal insufficiency
  • Hypercortisolism

Pituitary Disorders: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the evaluation and treatment of pituitary gland disorders and related hormonal regulation, emphasizing the clinical management of pituitary adenomas, hypopituitarism, and the specific protocols required for effective prolactinoma management in diverse patient populations.

  • Pituitary adenomas
  • Hypopituitarism

Lipids, Obesity, and Nutrition: Study Guidance

This domain addresses metabolic health, specifically lipid management, obesity, and nutritional interventions, requiring candidates to demonstrate proficiency in dyslipidemia management, evidence-based obesity treatment strategies, and the nutritional assessment of patients with complex metabolic health profiles.

  • Dyslipidemia management
  • Obesity treatment strategies

Female Reproduction: Study Guidance

This domain covers reproductive endocrine health in females, including hormonal cycles and related disorders, focusing on the clinical evaluation of menstrual irregularities, the diagnosis of polycystic ovary syndrome, and the systematic approach to infertility evaluation in clinical practice.

  • Menstrual irregularities
  • Polycystic ovary syndrome

Male Reproduction: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on male reproductive endocrinology, including testosterone regulation and related clinical issues, requiring candidates to understand the diagnostic and therapeutic approaches for hypogonadism, erectile dysfunction, and the management of male infertility within an endocrine context.

  • Hypogonadism
  • Erectile dysfunction

Diabetes Mellitus and Hypoglycemia: Study Guidance

This domain is the largest, focusing on the management of diabetes mellitus and hypoglycemia, requiring mastery of insulin therapy, advanced glucose monitoring techniques, and the acute management of hypoglycemia to ensure optimal patient outcomes in clinical settings.

  • Insulin therapy
  • Glucose monitoring

Calcium and Bone Disorders: Study Guidance

This domain covers the diagnosis and treatment of calcium metabolism and bone density disorders, focusing on the clinical management of osteoporosis, the evaluation of hypercalcemia, and the identification and treatment of vitamin D deficiency in patients.

  • Osteoporosis management
  • Hypercalcemia

Thyroid Disorders: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on thyroid function, including hyperthyroidism, hypothyroidism, and thyroid nodules, requiring candidates to demonstrate expertise in thyroid hormone replacement therapy, the management of thyroiditis, and the systematic evaluation of thyroid nodules in clinical practice.

  • Thyroid hormone replacement
  • Thyroiditis
