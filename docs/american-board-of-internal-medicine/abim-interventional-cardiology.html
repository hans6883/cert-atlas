What This Exam Validates

The ABIM Interventional Cardiology certification examination, administered by the American Board of Internal Medicine, extensively measures medical expertise across nine distinct subject domains. Candidates complete exactly 220 questions during an extended 600-minute evaluation covering case selection, procedural techniques, and thorough patient management strategies across diverse clinical healthcare settings for certification.

Who Should Take This Exam

Cardiologists and medical professionals seeking board certification in interventional cardiology. Candidates require clinical experience in catheter-based procedures, patient assessment, and the management of coronary and noncoronary cardiovascular diseases.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Case Selection and Management
  • Procedural Techniques
  • Complications of Coronary Intervention
  • Catheter-Based Management of Noncoronary Disease
  • Basic Science
  • Anatomy and Pathology

  • Cardiac Imaging and Assessment

How to Prepare

Extensively review the nine distinct exam domains, focusing intently on specific procedural weights such as twenty percent for case selection and twenty percent for procedural techniques. Practice clinical scenarios involving coronary complications, pharmacology, and imaging assessments to adequately prepare for all 220 questions during your study period.
