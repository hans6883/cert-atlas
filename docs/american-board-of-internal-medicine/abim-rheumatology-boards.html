What This Exam Validates

The ABIM Rheumatology Board Certification, administered by the American Board of Internal Medicine, assesses clinical proficiency in diagnosing and managing complex rheumatic conditions. The exam covers 13 distinct domains, ranging from basic clinical sciences to specific connective tissue disorders. It serves as the standard for verifying professional competence in rheumatology, ensuring practitioners possess the knowledge required to treat complex autoimmune and musculoskeletal diseases while maintaining high standards of patient care throughout their professional medical practice.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is for physicians who have completed their internal medicine and rheumatology training. It is intended for those seeking formal recognition of their clinical expertise in managing arthritis and related systemic inflammatory conditions within a professional clinical setting.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Managing rheumatoid arthritis
  • Diagnosing crystal-induced arthropathies
  • Treating systemic lupus erythematosus
  • Evaluating metabolic bone disease
  • Addressing vasculitides
  • Managing nonarticular musculoskeletal disorders

How to Prepare

Review the 13 exam domains, focusing on the highest-weighted areas like Other Rheumatic and Connective Tissue Disorders and Rheumatoid Arthritis. Utilize the official American Board of Internal Medicine exam guide to understand the scope of clinical topics. Practice applying diagnostic criteria for autoimmune diseases and review current treatment protocols for inflammatory joint conditions to prepare for the 240 questions included in the assessment, which requires 600 minutes to complete.

Domain Study Guidance

Basic and Clinical Sciences: Study Guidance

This domain covers the fundamental scientific principles and clinical applications necessary for rheumatology practice, ensuring that candidates understand the underlying mechanisms of disease and the appropriate application of scientific knowledge in a clinical setting.

  • Basic science concepts
  • Clinical application
  • Pathophysiology

Crystal-induced Arthropathies: Study Guidance

This section focuses on the identification and management of various arthropathies caused by crystal deposits, requiring candidates to demonstrate proficiency in diagnostic techniques and the implementation of effective treatment protocols for these specific joint conditions.

  • Crystal identification
  • Treatment protocols
  • Joint aspiration

Infections and Related Arthritides: Study Guidance

This domain addresses infections that manifest as arthritis or related joint inflammation, requiring candidates to distinguish between infectious and non-infectious causes while developing appropriate management strategies for patients presenting with these complex clinical symptoms.

  • Infectious arthritis
  • Differential diagnosis
  • Antibiotic therapy

Metabolic Bone Disease: Study Guidance

This section covers the diagnosis and management of conditions affecting bone metabolism, focusing on the evaluation of bone density and the treatment of metabolic disorders to prevent fractures and maintain skeletal health in patients.

  • Bone density
  • Metabolic disorders
  • Calcium homeostasis

Osteoarthritis and Related Disorders: Study Guidance

This domain examines the clinical presentation and management of osteoarthritis and related degenerative joint disorders, emphasizing the importance of understanding cartilage degradation and implementing joint management strategies to improve patient mobility and quality of life.

  • Cartilage degradation
  • Joint management
  • Pain relief

Rheumatoid Arthritis: Study Guidance

This section focuses on the diagnosis, treatment, and long-term management of rheumatoid arthritis, requiring candidates to be proficient in identifying joint inflammation and utilizing disease-modifying therapies to slow disease progression and improve patient outcomes.

  • Joint inflammation
  • Disease-modifying therapies
  • Biologic agents

Spondyloarthritis: Study Guidance

This domain covers the spectrum of spondyloarthritis conditions affecting the spine and joints, requiring candidates to recognize the signs of spinal inflammation and implement appropriate management plans for patients with these chronic inflammatory conditions.

  • Spinal inflammation
  • Spondyloarthropathy
  • Radiographic assessment

Other Rheumatic and Connective Tissue Disorders (ORCT): Study Guidance

This section addresses a broad range of connective tissue disorders not categorized elsewhere, requiring candidates to understand complex connective tissue pathology and manage the systemic symptoms that often accompany these challenging and diverse clinical conditions.

  • Connective tissue pathology
  • Systemic symptoms
  • Autoimmune markers

Lupus Erythematosus: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the clinical management of systemic lupus erythematosus, requiring candidates to identify autoimmune markers and manage organ involvement to provide care for patients living with this complex and multi-system autoimmune disease.

  • Autoimmune markers
  • Organ involvement
  • Immunosuppressive therapy

Nonarticular and Regional Musculoskeletal Disorders: Study Guidance

This section covers regional musculoskeletal disorders that do not primarily involve joint articulation, requiring candidates to diagnose soft tissue disorders and provide effective treatment for patients experiencing localized or regional pain in the musculoskeletal system.

  • Soft tissue disorders
  • Regional pain
  • Physical therapy

Nonrheumatic Systemic Disorders: Study Guidance

This domain addresses systemic conditions that present with nonrheumatic features, requiring candidates to recognize systemic manifestations and understand the impact of these conditions on various organ systems to provide holistic care for their patients.

  • Systemic manifestations
  • Organ system impact
  • Multidisciplinary care

Vasculitides: Study Guidance

This section focuses on the diagnosis and treatment of various forms of vasculitis, requiring candidates to identify vessel inflammation and apply current vasculitis classification systems to guide effective treatment and improve long-term patient health outcomes.

  • Vessel inflammation
  • Vasculitis classification
  • Immunotherapy

Miscellaneous Topics: Study Guidance

This domain covers miscellaneous topics relevant to the practice of rheumatology, ensuring that candidates stay informed about emerging topics and general rheumatology principles that are necessary for maintaining a high standard of clinical practice today.

  • Emerging topics
  • General rheumatology
  • Practice management

Exam-Day Guidance

The exam consists of 240 questions. Manage your time effectively across the 600-minute duration to ensure all domains are addressed. Read each clinical scenario carefully to identify key diagnostic indicators and apply your knowledge to select the most appropriate clinical management strategy.
