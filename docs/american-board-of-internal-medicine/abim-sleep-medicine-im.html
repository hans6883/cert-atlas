What This Exam Validates

The ABIM Sleep Medicine certification examination, administered by the American Board of Internal Medicine, validates clinical expertise across 9 distinct medical domains. Candidates face a total of 240 questions during a duration of 600 minutes, comprehensively assessing key competencies in sleep science, pathology, and diagnostic instrumentation for board certification.

Who Should Take This Exam

Internal medicine physicians, fellows, and licensed medical professionals specializing in sleep medicine disorders who are seeking official board certification through the American Board of Internal Medicine.

How to Prepare

Review core medical guidelines, clinical textbooks, and peer-reviewed literature covering all 9 exam domains to prepare effectively for the board certification process. Focus heavily on high-weight clinical areas such as sleep-related breathing disorders, insomnia, and normal sleep variants. Practice pacing yourself carefully for the demanding 600-minute testing session.

Domain Study Guidance

Normal Sleep and Variants: Study Guidance

This domain covers normal sleep physiology, complex architecture, and common variants encountered routinely during diagnostic evaluations and patient clinical assessments performed by qualified medical professionals seeking proper board certification status.

  • Sleep stages and architecture
  • Physiologic changes during sleep

Circadian Rhythm Sleep-Wake Disorders: Study Guidance

This section focuses on circadian rhythm sleep-wake disorders, evaluating alignment issues and dysfunctions between internal biological clocks and external environmental schedules encountered by patients in clinical practice settings during routine medical evaluations.

  • Delayed sleep-wake phase disorder
  • Shift work disorder management

Insomnia: Study Guidance

Candidates address the diagnosis, underlying etiology, and advanced treatment approaches for managing both chronic and acute insomnia conditions effectively in clinical practice for various patient populations seeking thorough medical help.

  • Cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia
  • Pharmacologic management options

Central Disorders of Hypersomnia: Study Guidance

This domain examines central disorders of hypersomnia, including narcolepsy and various other medical conditions marked primarily by severe excessive daytime sleepiness affecting daily functioning and overall patient well-being during clinical evaluations.

  • Narcolepsy type 1 and type 2
  • Multiple sleep latency testing

Parasomnias: Study Guidance

This section covers parasomnias, focusing on undesirable physical behaviors, movements, or experiential phenomena occurring during various transitions of the sleep cycle observed during standard clinical evaluations and detailed patient monitoring sessions.

  • Non-REM sleep parasomnias
  • REM sleep behavior disorders

Sleep-Related Movements: Study Guidance

Practitioners explore sleep-related movement disorders that significantly disrupt normal restorative sleep cycles and compromise overall physical comfort during the night for patients experiencing chronic restlessness and associated symptoms in practice.

  • Restless legs syndrome
  • Periodic limb movement disorder

Sleep-Related Breathing Disorders: Study Guidance

This domain details sleep-related breathing disorders, which represent the largest individual domain weight on the overall certification evaluation administered by the American Board of Internal Medicine for qualified internal medicine medical professionals.

  • Obstructive sleep apnea
  • Central sleep apnea syndromes

Sleep in Other Disorders: Study Guidance

Candidates evaluate sleep disturbances that occur secondarily to other complex medical, psychiatric, or primary neurological conditions affecting overall patient health and requiring coordinated multidisciplinary clinical management strategies during professional medical practice.

  • Sleep disruption in neurological diseases
  • Medical comorbidities affecting sleep

Instrumentation and Testing: Study Guidance

This final domain focuses directly on instrumentation, standard testing equipment, and technical protocols utilized daily in diagnostic sleep medicine laboratories by certified physicians and allied healthcare professionals during extensive evaluations.

  • Polysomnography setup and calibration
  • Home sleep apnea testing devices

Exam-Day Guidance

Manage your time effectively across the full 600-minute duration. Pace yourself strategically to answer all 240 questions without rushing through complex clinical scenarios during the test.

Frequently asked questions
