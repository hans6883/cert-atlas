What This Exam Validates

The TRUE Zero Waste Advisor Certification exam, administered by Green Business Certification Inc. (GBCI), comprehensively covers fundamental zero waste principles, facility operations, diversion metrics, and detailed reporting requirements. It evaluates advisors on implementing sustainable materials management practices across modern facilities to ensure strong sustainability performance, waste reduction, and effective environmental stewardship.

Who Should Take This Exam

Professionals and facility managers seeking to guide organizations toward zero waste goals, reduce carbon footprints, and implement sustainable materials management practices should take this certification.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Zero waste facility overview
  • TRUE certification role
  • Redesign and reduce strategies
  • Bin right sizing
  • Composting and recycling
  • Diversion and zero waste reporting
  • Zero waste metrics
  • Audits and hazard prevention

How to Prepare

Carefully review the TRUE rating system requirements and understand waste diversion calculations completely before your scheduled test date. Focus heavily on the core practices of reduction, reuse, composting, and recycling alongside reporting policies to ensure complete readiness and success on the examination.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The TRUE Zero Waste Advisor Certification exam consists of a total of 75 questions covering various important domains of zero waste and professional facility management principles.

What is the passing score?

To successfully pass the official examination and earn the recognized TRUE Advisor credential, candidates must achieve a minimum passing score of 77% on test day.

How long is the exam?

Candidates are given a total allotted duration of exactly 90 minutes to complete all questions included on the official examination appointment at the testing center.

Who administers the TRUE Zero Waste Advisor exam?

The TRUE Zero Waste Advisor Certification exam is administered directly by Green Business Certification Inc., which manages professional credentials under established global sustainability and environmental frameworks for advisors worldwide.
