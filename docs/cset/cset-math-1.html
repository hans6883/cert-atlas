What This Exam Validates

The CSET Mathematics Subtest I computer-based examination evaluates prospective educators on fundamental mathematical subjects including number and quantity, alongside advanced algebra concepts. Certified by CSET, this subtest forms a notable part of the official credentialing process for aspiring mathematics educators seeking professional licensure across authorized testing centers during their certification journey.

Who Should Take This Exam

Candidates who are actively pursuing a Single Subject Teaching Credential in mathematics take this computer-based test to comprehensively demonstrate their relevant knowledge in algebra and number theory.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Real and complex number systems
  • Number theory and induction
  • Algebraic structures
  • Polynomial equations and inequalities
  • Functions and linear algebra

How to Prepare

Carefully review the complex number and quantity systems alongside abstract algebraic structures and polynomial equations before your test session. Practice solving challenging mathematical problems using mathematical induction, the Euclidean Algorithm, and various functions without relying on a calculator to ensure full readiness for all 52 questions.

Domain Study Guidance

Number and Quantity: Study Guidance

Focuses primarily on the core properties of real and complex numbers, proportional relationships, dimensional analysis, divisibility rules, mathematical induction, the Euclidean Algorithm, and the Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic for candidates.

  • Properties of real and complex number systems
  • Basic properties of natural numbers and divisibility
  • The Euclidean Algorithm and mathematical induction

Algebra: Study Guidance

Covers notable algebraic structures, fields and rings, advanced polynomial equations, the Rational Root Theorem, general properties of functions, linear algebra principles, vectors, and matrices for prospective teachers preparing for certification.

  • Real and complex numbers as fields
  • Polynomial equations and inequalities
  • General properties of functions and linear algebra

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive at your designated computer-based testing center on time. Manage your 150 minutes across the 52 questions carefully, keeping in mind that calculators are strictly not allowed.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The official computer-based examination contains a total of 52 questions, which are presented to candidates in both multiple-choice and constructed-response formats during the testing period.

What is the passing score?

The required passing score for this certification test is established exactly at 220, which candidates must achieve on the official scale to successfully pass the subtest.

How long is the exam?

This computer-based examination has a total duration of 150 minutes, providing candidates with adequate time to complete all required questions within the designated testing session.

How much does the exam cost?

The exam price is set at $99 per subtest, and candidates pursuing a Single Subject credential take three subtests for a combined total cost of $297.
