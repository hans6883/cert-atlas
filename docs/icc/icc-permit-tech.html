What This Exam Validates

The ICC Permit Technician certification is a structured credential designed to test your knowledge across multiple operational domains including building codes and ordinances, plan review and permits, construction inspection procedures, customer service and communication, and records management and office procedures. Candidates must master these topics carefully to successfully navigate the evaluation process established by the International Code Council for code administration professionals.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification examination is intended specifically for permit technicians, administrative support staff, and municipal code enforcement professionals who work directly within building departments and related governmental regulatory agencies.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Building Codes and Ordinances
  • Plan Review and Permits
  • Construction Inspection Procedures
  • Customer Service and Communication
  • Records Management and Office Procedures

How to Prepare

To prepare effectively for your upcoming certification exam, carefully review the official exam guide and associated study documentation provided directly by the International Code Council. Focus your study sessions on mastering building codes, permit processing workflows, inspection scheduling protocols, and office records management procedures so that you feel fully confident when answering the total questions presented during your test.

Domain Study Guidance

Building Codes and Ordinances: Study Guidance

Building Codes and Ordinances covers standard regulations, legal aspects, and administrative provisions governing local municipal building standards, ensuring that all candidates understand the regulatory framework required for modern construction oversight and code compliance enforcement operations.

  • Building codes
  • Ordinances
  • Administrative provisions

Plan Review and Permits: Study Guidance

Plan Review and Permits involves processing incoming permit applications, reviewing detailed construction documents, and managing the official issuance process to ensure compliance with adopted municipal codes before any construction work officially begins.

  • Plan review procedures
  • Permit applications
  • Issuance processes

Construction Inspection Procedures: Study Guidance

Construction Inspection Procedures focuses on tracking job progress, scheduling field inspections efficiently, and coordinating closely with municipal inspection staff to maintain accurate timelines and ensure that all mandated site visits occur as required.

  • Inspection scheduling
  • Job tracking
  • Coordination

Customer Service and Communication: Study Guidance

Customer Service and Communication addresses professional interactions with contractors, property owners, and the general public, emphasizing clear information delivery regarding complex permit regulations, municipal building requirements, and administrative policies.

  • Public communication
  • Customer service
  • Information delivery

Records Management and Office Procedures: Study Guidance

Records Management and Office Procedures entails maintaining extensive office files, archiving vital permit documentation systematically, and handling municipal records securely in accordance with standard local government document retention policies and administrative guidelines.

  • Records management
  • Office procedures
  • Document archiving

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive promptly at Pearson VUE or an approved ICC testing site for your computer-based exam. Manage your time carefully across all multiple-choice questions during the allotted duration.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The examination consists of a total of 60 multiple-choice questions delivered entirely in a secure computer-based testing format at designated testing centers established for candidates.

What is the passing score?

The passing score for this evaluation is set at 75, utilizing the standard percentage scoring scale applied across all official International Code Council examinations administered today.

How long is the exam?

Candidates are given a total duration of 120 minutes to complete all sections of the computer-based test at the designated testing center facility during their scheduled appointment.

How much does the exam cost?

The registration exam price is established at $174.0, which covers the cost of administering the computer-based certification test through authorized testing channels and designated assessment locations.
