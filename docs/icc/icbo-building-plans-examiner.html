What This Exam Validates

The ICC Building Plans Examiner Certification is offered by the International Code Council to evaluate building plan examination skills. The assessment tests candidates across 7 distinct domains, featuring a total of 80 multiple-choice questions administered during a structured duration of 210 minutes to verify professional code compliance competency and technical knowledge.

Who Should Take This Exam

Professionals pursuing a career as a building plans examiner. Candidates need practical knowledge of construction documents, public safety requirements, and code compliance principles set by the International Code Council.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • General Administration
  • Building Planning
  • Footings and Foundations
  • Floor Construction
  • Wall Construction and Coverings
  • Roof/Ceiling Construction

How to Prepare

Review the complete content outline to understand each domain weight and focus area. Study all recommended reference materials, building codes, related appendices, and detailed footnotes. Domain Study Guidance

General Administration: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on general administration rules, code compliance agency creation, permit administration procedures, and the specific statutory duties and responsibilities assigned to the building official during plan review.

  • Code compliance agency
  • Permit administration
  • Department records

Building Planning: Study Guidance

This domain covers standard building planning principles, including height limitations, specific area requirements, and precise occupancy classifications necessary for safe and legally compliant structural designs under current regulations.

  • Building planning rules
  • Occupancy classifications
  • Heights and areas

Footings and Foundations: Study Guidance

This domain specifically addresses footings and foundations, underlying soil properties, and foundational structural design requirements needed to ensure stability and proper load-bearing safety for the bases of buildings across various projects.

  • Foundation standards
  • Footing design
  • Soil properties

Floor Construction: Study Guidance

This domain deals with standard floor construction standards, approved materials, and necessary structural support systems mandated by modern building codes to maintain structural integrity across different building levels.

  • Floor assemblies
  • Structural supports
  • Material compliance

Wall Construction and Coverings: Study Guidance

This particular domain covers wall construction methodologies, exterior and interior wall coverings, and all associated fire safety and structural performance standards required for both exterior and interior partitions.

  • Wall assemblies
  • Exterior coverings
  • Interior finishes

Roof/Ceiling Construction: Study Guidance

This domain examines roof and ceiling construction assemblies, structural dead and live loads, and rooftop structures to ensure weather resistance and structural safety at the top of buildings.

  • Roof assemblies
  • Ceiling construction
  • Rooftop structures

Public Safety and Special Construction: Study Guidance

This domain focuses heavily on public safety and special construction requirements, representing a massive portion of the exam content and testing standard life safety systems across various building projects.

  • Public safety rules
  • Special construction
  • Life safety systems

Exam-Day Guidance

Bring your government-issued photo identification with your first and last legal name matching your registration. Arrive prepared to manage your time across the 210-minute testing period.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of a total of 80 multiple-choice questions presented in a standard four-option format, with exactly one correct option scored for every question.

What is the passing score?

The passing score is evaluated on a pass-fail basis, with a scaled passing score typically established at 70 or 75 by the Code Council and the Exam Development Committee.

How long is the exam?

The official exam has a strict total duration of 210 minutes allocated for candidates to successfully complete all questions across the 7 distinct content domains.

Who should take the ICC Building Plans Examiner Certification?

Professionals seeking validation of their skills in reviewing building plans, checking construction documents, and enforcing code compliance should take this exam. Check with International Code Council for the current certification path.
