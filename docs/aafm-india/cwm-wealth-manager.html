What This Exam Validates

The CWM Chartered Wealth Manager certification, administered by AAFM India, covers twenty distinct curriculum domains ranging from wealth management concepts to advanced portfolio strategies. It evaluates professional knowledge in financial systems, investment vehicles, insurance, legalities, tax laws, and relationship management for finance practitioners seeking professional credentialing and standard career advancement.

Who Should Take This Exam

Private bankers, wealth managers, retail branch managers, corporate account managers, family office professionals, brokerage analysts, investment advisors, financial planners, investment managers, stock market professionals, and portfolio managers with financial services experience or appropriate graduate education.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Concept of Wealth Management
  • Indian and Global Financial System
  • Investment Vehicles
  • Measuring Risk And Returns
  • Insurance and Risk Management
  • Role in Banking
  • Legalities and Tax Laws
  • Portfolio Management

How to Prepare

Review all twenty domains using official curriculum materials and guides from AAFM India to properly prepare for the certification test. Focus your study efforts on core investment products, asset allocation principles, modern portfolio theory, and international tax planning strategies. For complete guidance on certification requirements, check with AAFM India.

Concept of Wealth Management: Study Guidance

This domain covers the fundamental concepts of wealth management, carefully distinguishing retail banking from wealth and private banking, while exploring client relationships and high net worth psychology for advisory professionals.

  • Understanding the relationship component
  • Exploring high net worth psychology

Indian and Global Financial System: Study Guidance

This domain examines the structure and operation of Indian and global financial systems, corporate finance principles, capital markets, and debt versus equity instruments utilized by modern financial practitioners across various market environments.

  • Capital markets operations
  • Debt versus equity

Investment Vehicles In Wealth Management: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on investment vehicles used in wealth management, protection versus accumulation, mutual funds, unit trusts, bonds, Sukuk instruments, and structured savings plans designed specifically for diverse investors.

  • Bonds and Sukuk instruments

Measuring Investment Risk And Returns: Study Guidance

This domain addresses the calculation and evaluation of investment risk and returns within wealth management portfolios and financial planning frameworks to ensure proper asset allocation and effective client goal alignment.

This domain explores insurance concepts and risk management, including term life insurance, Takaful, annuities, and other life products utilized for wealth protection and long-term financial security planning among clients.

Tax Laws For Wealth Management: Study Guidance

This domain reviews tax laws applicable to wealth management, investment strategies, and asset structures to help financial advisors minimize tax liabilities for individual clients and family groups during planning.

Life Cycle Management: Study Guidance

This domain examines behavioural finance concepts, client psychology, and decision-making patterns of high net worth investors to better understand how emotional biases affect financial choices and advisory engagements.

This domain focuses on relationship management by a wealth manager, advanced selling techniques, lead generation, client meetings, and communication tools necessary for building long-term client trust and loyalty.

  • Debt management strategies

Portfolio Management Strategies: Study Guidance

This domain explores portfolio management strategies, modern portfolio theory, risk, diversification, model portfolios, and structured products to build structured investment solutions aligned with specific client risk tolerances.

How many questions are on the exam?

The examination consists of a total of 85 questions designed to test your knowledge across the twenty curriculum domains covered by AAFM India during your preparation.

What is the passing score?

The passing score for the examination is 50% (80/160), which corresponds to the required marks needed to successfully earn the CWM designation from AAFM India.

How long is the exam?

The exam has a duration of 180 minutes, providing candidates with three hours to complete all 85 questions within the designated testing period administered by AAFM India.

What organization certifies the CWM Chartered Wealth Manager?

The CWM Chartered Wealth Manager certification is administered by AAFM India, which provides the official curriculum materials, guides, and testing requirements needed for professional certification.
