What This Exam Validates

The Exos Performance Specialist exam, administered by Exos, measures professional proficiency in the Exos Training System. It covers 19 distinct domains, including pillar preparation, movement skills, energy systems development, and nutrition. The exam consists of 150 multiple-choice questions that candidates must complete within 240 minutes. It serves as a formal assessment for individuals seeking to demonstrate their expertise in applying performance-based training methodologies to athletes and clients in a professional environment.

Who Should Take This Exam

This exam is designed for strength and conditioning coaches, personal trainers, and performance specialists. Candidates should have experience in movement-based training and a strong grasp of the Exos methodology to effectively apply these principles in a professional setting.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Exos Training System application
  • Pillar and movement preparation
  • Plyometrics and speed development
  • Strength and power programming
  • Energy systems development
  • Regeneration and nutrition strategies
  • Movement and performance testing

How to Prepare

Review the 19 domains outlined in the Exos Training System. Study the specific modules for any topics where you lack familiarity. Because the exam includes 150 questions to be finished in 240 minutes, practice managing your time during study sessions. Ensure you understand the integration of pillar preparation, plyometrics, and energy systems development, as these form the core of the performance model. Consistent review of the methodology is required for success.

Domain Study Guidance

Exos Training System: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the core Exos Training System framework, emphasizing the methodology and the integration of various training methods to ensure optimal performance outcomes for diverse clients and athletes in a professional setting.

  • System methodology
  • Integration of methods

Pillar Preparation: Study Guidance

This section covers the mobility and stability requirements of the hips, torso, and shoulders, focusing on pillar priming and nervous system activation to prepare the body for more intense training loads and complex movement patterns.

  • Pillar priming
  • Nervous system activation

Movement Preparation: Study Guidance

This domain addresses the systematic preparation of the body for movement through specific warm-up protocols, including hip activation and dynamic stretching, which are used for enhancing performance and reducing the risk of injury during training sessions.

  • Hip activation
  • Dynamic stretching

Plyometrics: Study Guidance

This domain examines the strategic use of plyometrics to improve speed and strength qualities, focusing on the stretch-shortening cycle and efficient force transfer to maximize athletic output during explosive movements and high-intensity training phases.

  • Stretch-shortening cycle
  • Force transfer

Acceleration: Study Guidance

This section covers the mechanics and training of acceleration phases, focusing on force initiation and movement mechanics to ensure athletes can effectively transition from a static position into high-speed movement with optimal efficiency and power.

  • Force initiation
  • Movement mechanics

Absolute Speed: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on developing maximum velocity and absolute speed, emphasizing speed mechanics and neural activation to help athletes reach their peak performance potential during linear sprinting and high-velocity movement tasks in their respective sports.

  • Speed mechanics
  • Neural activation

Multidirectional Speed: Study Guidance

This domain addresses speed development in multiple planes of motion, focusing on reactive movement and change of direction skills, which are used by athletes to perform effectively in dynamic, unpredictable sport environments requiring rapid adjustments.

  • Reactive movement
  • Change of direction

Medicine Ball: Study Guidance

This section covers the use of medicine balls for power development, focusing on rotational power and force production to enhance an athlete's ability to generate and transfer energy through the kinetic chain during sport-specific movements.

  • Rotational power
  • Force production

Strength/Power: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on strength and power training through a movement-based approach, emphasizing multi-planar training and strength qualities to build a foundation that supports athletic performance and long-term physical development for all types of clients.

  • Multi-planar training
  • Strength qualities

Energy Systems Development: Study Guidance

This domain addresses the development of anaerobic and aerobic capacities, focusing on energy system demands and sport-specific conditioning to ensure athletes are physiologically prepared for the metabolic requirements of their specific athletic endeavors and competition schedules.

  • Energy system demands
  • Sport-specific conditioning

Regeneration: Study Guidance

This section covers strategies for optimizing recovery and adaptation, focusing on fueling strategies and psychological recovery to ensure that athletes can sustain high-performance levels over time while minimizing the negative impacts of fatigue and stress.

  • Fueling strategies
  • Psychological recovery

Testing: FMS Integration: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on integrating functional movement screens into practice, emphasizing screening protocols and data application to identify movement limitations and tailor training programs that address individual needs and improve overall movement quality and performance.

  • Screening protocols
  • Data application

Testing: Movement Skills: Study Guidance

This section covers testing protocols for evaluating movement skills, focusing on movement assessment and skill evaluation to provide coaches with actionable data that informs program design and tracks progress in athletic development and movement proficiency.

  • Movement assessment
  • Skill evaluation

Testing: Strength/Power: Study Guidance

This domain addresses testing methods for strength and power qualities, focusing on performance metrics and testing standards to accurately measure an athlete's physical capabilities and ensure that training interventions are effectively driving desired performance improvements.

  • Performance metrics
  • Testing standards

Testing: ESD: Study Guidance

This section focuses on testing energy systems development capacities, emphasizing ESD testing and capacity measurement to evaluate an athlete's metabolic conditioning and ensure that training programs are appropriately challenging and aligned with specific performance goals.

  • ESD testing
  • Capacity measurement

Nutrition: Study Guidance

This domain covers nutrition as a lifestyle and fuel for the body, focusing on food as fuel and nutritional habits to support health, recovery, and performance, providing a holistic approach to athlete wellness and long-term success.

  • Food as fuel
  • Nutritional habits

Motivation Science: Study Guidance

This section addresses the science of motivation in a coaching context, focusing on goal setting and athlete engagement to foster a positive training environment that encourages commitment, consistency, and high levels of effort from all athletes.

  • Goal setting
  • Athlete engagement

Coaching Science: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the application of coaching science to performance, emphasizing coaching cues and communication strategies to effectively deliver instruction, provide feedback, and guide athletes through complex training movements and high-intensity performance sessions.

  • Coaching cues
  • Communication strategies

Putting It All Together: Study Guidance

This final domain covers the synthesis of all training domains into a cohesive program, focusing on program design and system integration to create effective training plans that address all aspects of athletic performance and development.

  • Program design
  • System integration

Exam-Day Guidance

The exam is 240 minutes long and consists of 150 multiple-choice questions. Once you start, you cannot stop. Ensure you are prepared to complete the entire assessment in one sitting. Check with Exos for the current certification path regarding retakes.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The Exos Performance Specialist exam consists of 150 multiple-choice questions, which are designed to test your knowledge of the Exos Training System and its practical application to various athletic and client-based training scenarios.

What is the passing score?

The official documentation provided by Exos does not specify a numerical passing score for this certification. Candidates should focus on mastering the 19 domains to ensure they are prepared for all potential questions.

How long is the exam?

You are allotted 240 minutes to complete the 150 questions on the exam. It is important to manage your time effectively during the test to ensure you have enough time to review all items.

How do I retake the exam?

If you do not pass the exam, you should check with Exos for the current certification path. They provide specific guidance on retake policies and procedures for candidates who need to re-attempt the assessment.
