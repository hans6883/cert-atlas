What This Exam Validates

The Certified Food Scientist (CFS) credential, offered by the Institute of Food Technologists (IFT), validates the applied scientific knowledge of food professionals. The exam covers eight domains, including product development, food safety, and regulatory compliance. This assessment ensures that candidates demonstrate the expertise to maintain standards within the food industry throughout their professional careers, confirming they possess the necessary skills to deliver safe and quality food products to consumers while adhering to industry benchmarks. The exam consists of 120 questions that evaluate your applied scientific knowledge across the eight domains defined by the Institute of Food Technologists.

Who Should Take This Exam

Food professionals with degrees in food science, engineering, chemistry, or related sciences should take this exam. Candidates must meet specific eligibility pathways based on their educational background and professional work experience in the food science and technology sector.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • New product development
  • Quality assurance systems
  • Food chemistry analysis
  • Regulatory compliance
  • Microbiological risk assessment
  • Food safety planning
  • Engineering process control
  • Sensory evaluation methods

Review the official exam content outline to understand the weight of each domain. Utilize the provided resource list, which includes textbooks on food chemistry, engineering, and microbiology. Consistent study of these core materials is the standard approach for success in this 180 minute assessment.

Domain Study Guidance

Product Development: Study Guidance

This domain covers the development of new products or reformulations from concept to commercialization, utilizing knowledge of raw materials, quality, safety, processing, and sensory attributes to ensure success in the marketplace for various food companies.

  • Technical feasibility assessment
  • Prototype design and testing
  • Commercialization strategies

Quality Assurance and Control: Study Guidance

This area focuses on developing and maintaining quality assurance and control systems, methods, and practices to ensure food products meet established requirements and satisfy all necessary safety standards for the consumer in the global food market.

  • Quality systems programs
  • Statistical process control
  • Audit preparation

Food Chemistry and Food Analysis: Study Guidance

This domain involves analyzing and interpreting chemical components, structural properties of molecules, and chemical processes occurring during postharvest handling, processing, and storage to maintain the integrity of food products for consumers in the modern food industry.

  • Chemical composition assays
  • Shelf-life stability testing
  • Nutritional analysis

Regulatory: Study Guidance

This area covers the application of knowledge required to develop or maintain foods in compliance with national and international regulations, ensuring that all products meet legal and safety requirements for public distribution and consumer protection globally.

  • Legal standards and identity claims
  • Labeling requirements
  • Regulatory filings

Food Microbiology: Study Guidance

This domain addresses the impact of microflora on food systems, including assessing microbial risk and determining methods to control that risk to ensure the safety of the final food products for the public and industry stakeholders.

  • Microbiological sampling plans
  • Pathogen detection methods
  • Growth kinetics

Food Safety: Study Guidance

This area focuses on the development and application of programs to minimize chemical, physical, and microbiological contamination risks in food products through safety planning and hazard identification strategies for the manufacturing environment and supply chain.

  • HACCP food safety plans
  • Sanitation protocols
  • Risk mitigation

Food Engineering: Study Guidance

This domain involves applying engineering principles of physical and chemical conversions to produce finished food products from raw materials while maintaining efficiency and quality throughout the entire manufacturing process for the industry and global food supply.

  • Process configuration
  • Thermal processing
  • Mass balance

Sensory Evaluation & Consumer Testing: Study Guidance

This area covers the principles and methods of sensory and consumer testing, including statistical analysis to ensure products meet consumer preferences and expectations in the food market environment for various global brands and retail food sectors.

  • Sensory attribute identification
  • Consumer preference mapping
  • Statistical data analysis

Exam-Day Guidance

Log in to the portal at your scheduled time. Ensure your testing area is clear of materials except for a calculator and beverage. Have your government-issued photo ID ready for verification. You must accept the nondisclosure agreement to begin the exam.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of 120 questions. These questions are designed to evaluate your applied scientific knowledge across the eight domains defined by the Institute of Food Technologists to ensure you meet the professional standards required for the certification.

What is the passing score?

The exam uses a scaled scoring system ranging from 100 to 900. To pass the certification, you must achieve a score of 650 or above, which demonstrates your proficiency in the required food science domains as established by the Institute of Food Technologists.

How long is the exam?

Your testing appointment is for a duration of 180 minutes. This time begins after you have verified your identity, confirmed technical requirements, and completed the necessary system checks at your designated testing location or through the remote proctoring portal.

What is the status of the certification?

The certification is a professional credential for food scientists. If you have questions regarding the current certification path or potential future updates to the program, please check with Institute of Food Technologists (IFT) for the current certification path.
