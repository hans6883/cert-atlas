What This Exam Validates

The American Culinary Federation Certified Executive Chef examination measures advanced knowledge, technical capability, and practical leadership skills required for professional culinarians. Certified and administered by the American Culinary Federation, candidates are evaluated across six core competency domains including advanced cooking, food safety, baking, nutrition, financial operations, and personnel management to demonstrate professional excellence in the kitchen environment.

Who Should Take This Exam

Culinarians with extensive work experience as a Chef de Cuisine, Executive Sous Chef, Pastry Chef, or chef in charge of food production supervising a team of full-time employees in a professional kitchen.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Advanced Cooking
  • Safety and Sanitation
  • Baking and Pastry
  • Nutrition
  • Finance and Operations Management
  • Team Management and Leadership

How to Prepare

Review all recommended reference materials and complete the required education courses covering nutrition, food safety, cost control, beverage management, and supervisory management. Practice multiple-choice questions regularly and ensure all work history and documentation are ready for initial application approval. Candidates should dedicate significant time to mastering the specific technical requirements outlined in the official candidate handbook to ensure readiness for the 100 question exam.

Domain Study Guidance

Advanced Cooking: Study Guidance

This domain covers product identification, advanced cooking methods, kitchen design, equipment utilization, food fabrication, plate presentation, and recipe development for professional culinary environments to ensure candidates demonstrate high level technical proficiency in their daily kitchen operations.

  • Product Identification
  • Cooking Methods
  • Techniques

Safety and Sanitation: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on safe food and product handling procedures, local laws and regulations, and strict HACCP compliance standards to ensure operational safety and sanitation within a professional kitchen environment at all times for all staff.

  • Food Handling
  • Product Handling
  • HACCP Compliance

Baking and Pastry: Study Guidance

This domain examines professional baking methods, technical pastry skills, precise formulas, equipment, quality ingredients, and artistic presentation standards required of executive pastry leaders to maintain high quality output in a professional setting for all guests.

  • Baking Methods
  • Formulas
  • Presentation

Nutrition: Study Guidance

This domain addresses food allergies, dietary considerations, nutritional guidelines, and healthy menu planning principles needed to accommodate diverse modern customer nutritional requirements while maintaining the integrity of the culinary experience for all guests in the dining room.

  • Allergies
  • Dietary Considerations
  • Guidelines

Finance and Operations Management: Study Guidance

This domain includes budget development, reading financial statements, purchasing strategies, receiving procedures, operational policies, menu design, and marketing techniques for profitable restaurant management and effective oversight of kitchen financial performance and operational success for the entire business.

  • Budget Development
  • Purchasing
  • Receiving

Team Management and Leadership: Study Guidance

This domain covers effective personnel management, team development strategies, federal employment laws, labor regulations, professional ethics, and talent recruitment practices for executive kitchen leaders to foster a productive and legally compliant work environment for their culinary staff.

  • Personnel Management
  • Team Development

Exam-Day Guidance

Bring a photo identification, a pen, scratch paper, and a basic calculator. Check cell phones completely off and ensure a stable internet connection if taking the exam through the online learning center to avoid any technical interruptions.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The written exam consists of exactly 100 multiple-choice questions administered through the online learning center. Candidates are given a total of 90 minutes to complete all items during the session.

What is the passing score?

The official passing score is determined using a modified Angoff Technique to ensure fairness. Candidates must meet the required threshold to pass the written certification examination successfully.

How long is the exam?

Candidates have 90 minutes to complete the 100 question multiple-choice written exam. This time limit is strictly enforced for all candidates taking the certification test online.

What is the cost of certification?

Please check with American Culinary Federation (ACF) for the current certification path and associated fees. Costs may vary based on membership status and specific exam requirements for each candidate.
