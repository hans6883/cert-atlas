What This Exam Validates

The CHPN Certified Hospice and Palliative Nurse examination is administered by the Hospice and Palliative Credentialing Center. It measures professional knowledge across multiple core domains, featuring exactly 150 questions to evaluate your ability to provide exceptional care for patients and their families during vital healthcare moments throughout the certification testing process.

Who Should Take This Exam

Registered nurses actively working in hospice and palliative care settings who want to validate their clinical expertise and professional practice through formal credentialing programs and advanced certification.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Life-Limiting Conditions
  • Pain Management
  • Symptom Management
  • Care of the Patient and Family
  • Education and Advocacy
  • Interdisciplinary/Collaborative Practice
  • Professional Issues

How to Prepare

Carefully review all official exam domains to focus your study time efficiently, allocating significant preparation effort to both pain management and symptom management domains. Practice with sample questions that reflect authentic clinical scenarios, keeping in mind the 180 minutes testing window, the 150 questions format, and overall candidate requirements to achieve success.

Domain Study Guidance

Life-Limiting Conditions: Study Guidance

This domain focuses intensely on understanding various life-limiting conditions and their clinical trajectories to anticipate patient care needs effectively while delivering consistent support throughout the entire illness timeline without any gaps whatsoever.

  • Disease trajectories
  • Pathophysiology of terminal illnesses

Pain Management: Study Guidance

This important section covers both pharmacological and non-pharmacological interventions for managing pain associated with serious illnesses, ensuring nurses can evaluate and relieve patient suffering successfully across various clinical settings during daily practice.

  • Pain assessment strategies
  • Opioid and adjuvant medications

Symptom Management: Study Guidance

This domain addresses the thorough assessment and relief of non-pain symptoms that affect comfort, daily functioning, and overall quality of life for individuals dealing with serious health conditions every single day of treatment.

  • Respiratory and gastrointestinal symptom management
  • Comfort measures

Care of the Patient and Family: Study Guidance

This area centers on direct nursing care strategies addressing the physical, emotional, and spiritual needs of patients and their families during difficult times of care delivery and transition phases throughout treatment courses.

  • Family-centered care
  • End-of-life support

Education and Advocacy: Study Guidance

This content explores how to effectively educate patients, families, and communities while advocating strongly for quality end-of-life care and informed healthcare decision making within various practice environments and clinical settings daily.

  • Patient and family education
  • Advocacy strategies

Interdisciplinary/Collaborative Practice: Study Guidance

This domain highlights vital coordination with interdisciplinary teams to deliver cohesive, structured care across various healthcare settings and clinical environments while maintaining high standards of communication among all healthcare professionals involved.

  • Team communication
  • Collaborative care planning

Professional Issues: Study Guidance

This section examines the legal, ethical, and professional standards that govern hospice and palliative nursing practice to ensure safe, legal, and accountable patient care throughout every career stage and clinical healthcare environment.

  • Ethical decision making
  • Regulatory standards

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive at the testing center early with your required identification documents, and manage your time carefully across the 180 minutes allowed for the test session.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The official examination consists of exactly 150 questions designed to test your professional knowledge comprehensively across all seven distinct domains of hospice and palliative nursing practice today.

What is the passing score?

You should check directly with the Hospice and Palliative Credentialing Center for specific and up-to-date details regarding the current passing score requirements and official certification policies.

How long is the exam?

Candidates are given a total of exactly 180 minutes to complete the entire examination session successfully under standard testing center conditions and official administrative rules for certification.

Who administers the CHPN exam?

The CHPN certification examination is administered directly by the Hospice and Palliative Credentialing Center for qualified registered nurses seeking professional validation and formal clinical credentialing.
