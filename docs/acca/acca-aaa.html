What This Exam Validates

The Advanced Audit and Assurance exam, certified by the ACCA, evaluates professional competence in audit and assurance engagements. It covers the regulatory environment, professional ethics, quality management, and advanced audit procedures. This linear exam requires candidates to demonstrate technical knowledge and professional judgment across various audit scenarios. As the exam is scheduled for retirement on 2027-06-30, candidates are directed to the SAA successor for future certification paths. Note that renewal is required for this professional qualification to maintain active status.

Who Should Take This Exam

This exam is for accounting professionals pursuing advanced audit qualifications. Candidates should possess a strong understanding of audit principles and professional standards, as the assessment focuses on the application of these concepts in complex, real-world audit and assurance scenarios.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Navigating regulatory environments
  • Applying professional ethics
  • Managing audit quality
  • Executing advanced audit procedures
