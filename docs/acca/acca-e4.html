What This Exam Validates

The ACCA Finance and Investment certification assesses professional knowledge in corporate finance, investment appraisal, working capital management, business valuations, and risk management. Certified by ACCA, the computer-based examination requires candidates to apply technical skills to evaluate financial and investment strategies effectively over the course of 180 minutes to achieve a passing score.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification suits accounting professionals, finance candidates, and practitioners seeking to validate their expertise in investment decision-making, working capital strategies, business valuations, and risk management techniques.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Finance and investment environment
  • Working capital management
  • Investment appraisal
  • Business finance
  • Business valuations
  • Risk management

How to Prepare

Review the official syllabus and study guide carefully to prepare for the assessment. Practice calculating the weighted average cost of capital, applying discounted cash flow methods, and analyzing working capital strategies. Review technical articles and tackle scenario-based questions across all domains to ensure complete readiness.

Domain Study Guidance

Finance and investment environment: Study Guidance

This specific domain covers the finance and investment environment, focusing heavily on strategic objectives, economic policies, financial markets, and institutional frameworks that govern modern corporate financial decisions across various global markets.

  • Strategic environment
  • Economic environment
  • Financial markets and institutions

Working capital: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on working capital management, examining inventory management, accounts receivable, accounts payable, cash management, and overall funding strategies to ensure proper liquidity and operational efficiency within organizations.

  • Nature, elements and importance of working capital
  • Management of inventories, accounts receivable, accounts payable and cash
  • Working capital needs and funding strategies

Investment appraisal: Study Guidance

This section explores investment appraisal techniques, incorporating inflation, taxation, risk, uncertainty, and specific investment decisions to help financial professionals evaluate capital expenditure projects accurately and make sound corporate choices.

  • Investment appraisal techniques
  • Inflation and taxation in DCF
  • Risk and uncertainty in investment appraisal
  • Specific investment decisions

Business finance: Study Guidance

This domain examines business finance deeply, covering diverse sources of finance, the cost of capital, relative costs, and complex capital structure theories that guide long-term corporate funding and financial planning.

  • Sources of finance
  • The cost of capital
  • Capital structure theories and practical considerations

Business valuations: Study Guidance

This section details business valuations by addressing equity valuations, debt valuations, financial asset assessments, and efficient market hypotheses to equip candidates with methods for determining accurate asset worth.

  • Nature and purpose of the valuation of business and financial assets
  • Valuation of equity
  • Valuation of debt and other financial assets
  • Efficient Market Hypothesis (EMH)

Risk management: Study Guidance

This domain focuses intensely on risk management, analyzing foreign currency risk, interest rate fluctuations, and distinct hedging techniques to protect corporate assets against unexpected financial volatility and market movements.

  • Nature and types of risk
  • Exchange rate differences and interest rate fluctuations
  • Hedging techniques for foreign currency risk
  • Hedging techniques for interest rate risk

Exam-Day Guidance

Manage your 180-minute exam time effectively across all sections. Section A contains objective test questions, Section B includes case questions, and Section C features constructed response questions.

The examination consists of multiple sections containing numerous question types, including objective test questions in Section A, case questions in Section B, and constructed response questions in Section C.

The computer-based examination has a total duration of 180 minutes, providing candidates with ample time to complete all of the required sections successfully during the testing session.
