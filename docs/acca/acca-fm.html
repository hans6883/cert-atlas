What This Exam Validates

The Financial Management (FM/F9) exam, certified by the ACCA, covers the financial management function, financial analysis, financing decisions, investments, and business valuations. It evaluates technical proficiency in managing corporate finance, including working capital, risk management, and capital structure. As this exam is scheduled for retirement on 2027-06-30, students must prepare for the successor exam, E4, to ensure their professional certification path remains current and aligned with ACCA requirements. This assessment ensures that candidates possess the skills to navigate financial environments while maintaining the standards expected by the ACCA organization for all its certified finance professionals.

Who Should Take This Exam

This exam is intended for accounting students and finance professionals seeking to demonstrate competency in corporate financial management. Candidates should possess practical experience in financial analysis and investment appraisal to succeed in this 180-minute assessment.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Financial management function oversight
  • Quantitative financial analysis
  • Strategic financing decision-making
  • Investment appraisal techniques
  • Business and asset valuation

How to Prepare Before Retirement

Review the official technical articles and topic explainers provided by the ACCA. Practice both Objective Test Questions and Constructed Response tasks to build speed. Ensure you understand the application of the Capital Asset Pricing Model and working capital management. Given the 202 dollar price, focus on mastering the syllabus early. Since the exam retires on 2027-06-30, prioritize the E4 curriculum for long-term planning. Consistent practice and review of all core financial concepts are needed for achieving success on this professional certification exam.

Domain Study Guidance

Financial management function: Study Guidance

This domain covers the role and purpose of the financial management function within an organization, focusing on how financial managers contribute to business objectives, stakeholder value, and the economic environment in which the firm operates for long-term success.

  • Role of financial management
  • Economic environment impact

Financial analysis: Study Guidance

This domain involves the detailed analysis of financial data to assess business performance, liquidity, and solvency, providing insights into the overall financial health and operational efficiency of an organization for stakeholders and internal management decision-making processes.

  • Financial statement analysis
  • Performance evaluation

Financing decisions: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on identifying and evaluating alternative sources of business finance, including debt and equity, while determining the optimal capital structure to support long-term growth and minimize the cost of capital for the firm in various scenarios.

  • Sources of finance
  • Capital structure theory

Investments: Study Guidance

This domain covers investment appraisal techniques, including net present value and internal rate of return, to assist in long-term project decision-making while accounting for the impacts of inflation and taxation on future cash flows for the organization.

  • Investment appraisal methods
  • Inflation and taxation impact

Business valuations: Study Guidance

This domain addresses the principles of business and asset valuations, utilizing various models to determine the value of equity and debt in different scenarios, ensuring accurate financial reporting and strategic decision-making capabilities for the business entity.

  • Asset-based valuation
  • Cash flow-based valuation
