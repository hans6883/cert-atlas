What This Exam Validates

Advanced Performance Management is a professional-level certification provided by the ACCA. It focuses on the application of strategic planning and control models to monitor organizational performance. The exam evaluates how candidates analyze external influences, measure performance, and apply management information systems. It serves as a final professional assessment, requiring candidates to demonstrate high-level analytical skills in complex business environments before the exam is retired in favor of SPI. This assessment ensures that students are prepared for the demands of modern financial leadership roles.

Who Should Take This Exam

This exam is intended for professional accounting students aiming to demonstrate advanced expertise in performance management. Candidates should possess a strong grasp of financial reporting and management accounting principles to navigate the complex, linear-format assessments required by the ACCA at this professional level.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Strategic planning and control
  • Analyzing external influences
  • Performance measurement techniques
  • Data-driven performance evaluation

How to Prepare Before Retirement

Candidates should review technical articles and past exam papers to understand the requirement format. Practice applying models to scenarios. Focus on integrating knowledge from previous levels, as the exam requires synthesis of skills. Ensure familiarity with the 195-minute time limit and the mix of objective test questions and constructed response tasks to manage the exam pace effectively. Consistent practice with past papers is a standard method for achieving the required 50 score.

Domain Study Guidance

Strategic planning and control: Study Guidance

This domain covers the use of strategic planning and control models to monitor organizational performance. It requires candidates to apply theoretical frameworks to real-world business scenarios to ensure organizational objectives are met through effective planning and monitoring processes.

  • Strategic planning models

External influences: Study Guidance

This domain examines how external factors impact organizational performance. Candidates must identify and evaluate how various external influences affect business strategy and performance measurement, requiring an understanding of the broader economic and competitive environment in which organizations operate today.

  • External influence analysis
  • Environmental impact assessment

Performance measurement: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the development and application of performance measurement systems. It involves selecting appropriate metrics and indicators to evaluate organizational success, ensuring that performance data aligns with strategic goals and provides actionable insights for management teams.

  • Performance indicator selection
  • Measurement system design

Exam-Day Guidance

The exam is a 195-minute linear assessment. Candidates must manage their time across objective test questions and constructed response sections. Ensure you read all requirements carefully, as the format demands precise application of knowledge to the provided scenarios to succeed.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of a mix of Objective Test Questions and Constructed Response questions. While the specific number of questions is not fixed, candidates must be prepared to address both formats within the 195-minute duration to demonstrate their full competency in the subject matter.

What is the passing score?

To successfully pass the Advanced Performance Management exam, candidates are required to achieve a minimum score of 50. This threshold reflects the professional standard expected of students at this level of the ACCA qualification and ensures mastery of the core performance management concepts.

How long is the exam?

The total duration for the exam is 195 minutes. Candidates should plan their time carefully to ensure they have sufficient opportunity to address all sections of the assessment, including the objective test questions and the more complex constructed response tasks provided during the session.

What is the replacement for this exam?

The ACCA has officially scheduled the retirement of this exam for 2027-06-30. Following this date, the official successor exam will be SPI. Students are encouraged to plan their studies accordingly to ensure a smooth transition to the new qualification path provided by the ACCA.
