Bloomberg ESG Certificate Credential

Bloomberg

Not an examination

Bloomberg does not offer an examination for Bloomberg ESG Certificate: it is a training programme. No exam blueprint, domain weights, exam code or practice test apply. Verified on the issuer's site September 19, 2026. issuer page

What This Exam Validates

The Bloomberg ESG Certificate, offered by Bloomberg, validates knowledge across four core areas: ESG Data and Analysis, Sustainable Finance, ESG Reporting Standards, and ESG Integration in Investment. Delivered as self‑paced e‑learning with embedded assessments, the program costs $149.0 and does not require renewal. Learners progress through 4 modules, each concluding with a practical assessment that confirms competency before moving to the next topic. The two question types include Multiple Choice and Embedded Assessment formats, and the curriculum covers four distinct domains of ESG practice.

Who Should Take This Exam

Financial analysts, portfolio managers, ESG researchers, and sustainability officers who already work with investment products or corporate reporting will benefit from the Bloomberg ESG Certificate, especially those seeking to deepen practical ESG analysis skills and broaden their understanding of sustainable finance frameworks.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Analyze ESG data sets
  • Apply sustainable finance concepts
  • Interpret ESG reporting frameworks
  • Integrate ESG factors into investment decisions

How to Prepare

Start by reviewing Bloomberg’s ESG module outlines on the certification page. Allocate time to each of the four domains, using Bloomberg’s own tutorials and the third‑party libguide for supplemental examples. Complete the embedded assessments after each lesson to ensure you meet the completion‑based criteria. Reinforce learning by comparing Bloomberg’s methodology with standards such as SASB, GRI and IFRS, which are referenced in the official guide, and schedule regular review sessions to retain key concepts.

Domain Study Guidance

Domain 1: Study Guidance

This domain covers the collection, processing, and interpretation of ESG data. It emphasizes understanding metric definitions, data sources, and quality controls needed to produce reliable ESG analytics for investment decisions.

  • Key ESG metrics
  • Data sourcing techniques
  • Data quality assessment

Domain 2: Study Guidance

Sustainable Finance examines how ESG considerations are integrated into financing activities, including green bonds, impact investing, and the evaluation of systemic financial risks within broader global capital markets.

  • Green financing instruments
  • Climate risk assessment
  • Impact investment strategies

Domain 3: Study Guidance

ESG Reporting Standards focuses on the frameworks and guidelines that companies use to disclose sustainability information, such as SASB, GRI, and the IFRS Sustainability Standards, which are increasingly mandatory for global corporate reporting.

  • SASB and GRI standards
  • IFRS sustainability disclosures
  • Materiality determination

Domain 4: Study Guidance

ESG Integration in Investment explores practical methods for embedding ESG factors into portfolio construction, risk management, and performance measurement and long‑term sustainable value creation for institutional and retail investors.

  • ESG factor weighting
  • Portfolio risk analytics
  • Performance attribution

Exam-Day Guidance

Log in early, ensure a stable internet connection, have Bloomberg terminal access ready, review each module’s key points, complete assessments without rushing, and keep the $149.0 receipt saved on your device for future reference.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The Bloomberg ESG Certificate uses embedded assessments within each self‑paced module rather than a fixed set of exam questions, so a specific question count is not published. Question types include Multiple Choice and Embedded Assessment formats.

What is the passing score?

Passing is completion‑based; you must successfully finish each module’s embedded assessment. There is no single unified pass/fail score across the program, nor any additional external exam requirement.

How long is the exam?

The program is self‑paced online, allowing you to progress through the four modules at your own speed without a set time limit or any scheduled exam window.

How much does the Bloomberg ESG Certificate cost?

The certification costs $149.0 and can be purchased directly through Bloomberg’s professional education portal, with payment processed via credit card or corporate invoice, and the fee includes lifetime access to all course materials.

Does the Bloomberg ESG Certificate require renewal?

No renewal is required for the Bloomberg ESG Certificate. Once you complete the 4 modules and their embedded assessments, the credential remains valid without any recurring fees or recertification cycle.
