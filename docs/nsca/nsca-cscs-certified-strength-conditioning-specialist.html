What This Exam Validates

The NSCA CSCS certification, administered by the NSCA, evaluates professional competence in strength and conditioning. The exam covers seven domains, including exercise science, sport psychology, nutrition, program design, exercise technique, program implementation, and organization and administration. It ensures practitioners possess the knowledge to safely and effectively train athletes across various disciplines, following established professional standards and guidelines for performance and safety in athletic environments. This credential validates the ability to apply scientific principles to improve athletic performance through the assessment of core competencies across the entire field of strength and conditioning.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is for strength and conditioning professionals working in collegiate, academic, or military settings. Candidates should have experience in coaching athletes and managing training programs, requiring a deep understanding of both scientific principles and practical application in athletic environments.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Applying exercise science principles
  • Designing evidence-based training programs
  • Teaching proper exercise techniques
  • Implementing athlete monitoring procedures
  • Managing facility safety and administration
  • Providing nutritional guidance within scope

How to Prepare

Review the detailed content outline to understand the seven domains. Focus on the practical application of exercise science and biomechanics. Practice identifying appropriate training variables, exercise order, and periodization strategies. Use the provided sample questions to gauge your understanding of the cognitive levels required, and ensure you are familiar with the NSCA standards and guidelines for professional practice. Consistent study of these core areas is required for success on the 220 questions contained within the exam, which requires a passing score of 70 or higher.

Domain Study Guidance

EXERCISE SCIENCE: Study Guidance

This domain covers the physiological and mechanical principles of human movement. It focuses on anatomy, bioenergetics, and the scientific research methods used to evaluate athlete performance and physiological adaptations for training in various athletic settings.

  • Muscle and neuromuscular anatomy
  • Bioenergetics and metabolism

SPORT PSYCHOLOGY: Study Guidance

This domain addresses the psychological factors that influence athletic performance and mental health. It covers motivational theories, mental skills training, and recognizing when to refer athletes for mental health concerns during training sessions.

  • Motivational theories
  • Mental skills for performance

NUTRITION: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on nutritional factors affecting health and performance. It emphasizes evidence-based approaches to body composition and the safety and efficacy of dietary supplements for athletes in various training programs and competitive environments.

  • Nutritional factors for performance
  • Supplement efficacy and safety

PROGRAM DESIGN: Study Guidance

This domain involves creating training programs based on needs analysis. It covers selecting exercises, determining intensity and volume, and applying periodization strategies for various athletic goals and the long-term development of athletes in training.

  • Needs analysis
  • Periodization strategies

EXERCISE TECHNIQUE: Study Guidance

This domain covers the instruction and evaluation of movement techniques. It includes resistance training, speed, agility, plyometrics, and energy system development for athletes to ensure safe and effective performance during their daily training sessions.

  • Resistance training technique
  • Speed and agility drills

PROGRAM IMPLEMENTATION: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the practical execution of training sessions and athlete monitoring. It includes coaching, data collection, and interpreting assessment results to adjust training programs for optimal athletic performance and safety in the field.

  • Coaching training sessions
  • Testing and monitoring procedures

ORGANIZATION AND ADMINISTRATION: Study Guidance

This domain covers the administrative responsibilities of a strength and conditioning professional. It focuses on facility safety, risk management, and adhering to professional codes and policies to maintain a safe training environment for all athletes.

  • Scope of practice
  • Facility safety and maintenance

Exam-Day Guidance

The exam consists of 220 questions to be completed within 240 minutes. Manage your time effectively across the seven domains, ensuring you address both scientific foundations and practical application sections within the allotted time to achieve a passing score.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of 220 total questions covering seven distinct domains of strength and conditioning knowledge. Candidates must answer these questions within the provided time limit to demonstrate their professional competency in the field.

What is the passing score?

To pass the exam, candidates must achieve a scaled score of 70 or higher. This score reflects the necessary level of knowledge required to practice as a certified strength and conditioning specialist in professional settings.

How long is the exam?

The exam has a total duration of 240 minutes. Candidates should plan their time carefully to ensure they have enough time to complete all sections of the test effectively and accurately within the testing window.

Where can I find the current certification path?

The NSCA CSCS is a current certification. For information regarding the long-term certification path or updates, please check with NSCA to ensure you have the most accurate and up-to-date information regarding your professional development.
