What This Exam Validates

The CSCS Certified Strength and Conditioning Specialist Practical Exam is certified and administered by the NSCA. It evaluates professional competencies across four key operational areas, which include exercise technique, program design, organization, and evaluation methods distributed across a total of 125 questions to test applied knowledge during the 150-minute duration.

Who Should Take This Exam

Fitness professionals, strength coaches, and sports science practitioners aiming to validate their applied knowledge in training, testing, and program administration should take this practical exam.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Exercise Technique
  • Program Design
  • Organization and Administration
  • Testing and Evaluation

How to Prepare

Review practical conditioning concepts across all four major domains very carefully and systematically to ensure complete understanding. Focus heavily on mastering technique demonstrations, training program layouts, facility administration protocols, and testing procedures required to meet standard NSCA requirements before your scheduled exam day finally arrives at the testing center for the assessment.

Domain Study Guidance

Exercise Technique: Study Guidance

This movement mechanics domain covers movement execution and safe spotting procedures for various lifts and physical conditioning modalities, ensuring proper athlete safety during all training sessions administered by professionals.

  • Lifting postures
  • Spotting methods

Program Design: Study Guidance

This domain focuses intensely on creating safe and effective training programs tailored specifically to unique physical conditioning goals and diverse athletic populations to maximize performance outcomes consistently across every training cycle.

  • Workout variables
  • Progression plans

Organization and Administration: Study Guidance

This administrative section addresses facility setup, daily safety procedures, and management duties directly related to overseeing professional strength and conditioning environments successfully under standard operational guidelines established by experts.

  • Facility rules
  • Safety protocols

Testing and Evaluation: Study Guidance

This final evaluation domain involves selecting, properly administering, and evaluating physical tests designed to accurately measure athlete performance and ongoing fitness progress over time using standardized measurement tools and protocols.

  • Test administration
  • Data collection

Exam-Day Guidance

Manage your time effectively across the 125 questions during the 150-minute test duration, keeping the scaled score requirement of 70 or higher clearly in mind.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The certification examination consists of exactly 125 questions covering four distinct domains of professional strength and conditioning practices designed to test applied knowledge across all candidates seeking credentials.

What is the passing score?

All candidates must achieve a minimum scaled score of 70 or higher in order to successfully pass the official examination administered by the organization during their scheduled testing appointment.

How long is the exam?

You are given a total of 150 minutes to complete all questions included on the test assessment during your scheduled testing appointment at the designated center location.

What body certifies this exam?

The certification examination is officially certified and administered by the NSCA for qualified strength and conditioning professionals seeking formal credential validation today through standard testing procedures.
