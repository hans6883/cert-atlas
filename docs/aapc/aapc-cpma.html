What This Exam Validates

Developed by AAPC, the Certified Professional Medical Auditor (CPMA) validates expertise in reviewing medical records and billing practices. The exam spans five domains: E/M documentation and audit, surgical documentation and audit, diagnostic services audit, compliance and regulatory issues, and quality improvement. Candidates must demonstrate ability to apply coding guidelines, detect compliance risks, and drive quality initiatives within health‑care organizations.

Who Should Take This Exam

Medical auditors, coding specialists, compliance officers, and health‑care managers who regularly review E/M, surgical, or diagnostic documentation should pursue CPMA to formalize their audit expertise and demonstrate advanced competency.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • E/M documentation audit
  • Surgical documentation audit
  • Diagnostic services audit
  • Compliance and regulatory knowledge
  • Quality improvement techniques

How to Prepare

Review AAPC’s official study guides and complete the practice exams on the AAPC training portal. Focus on each domain’s key concepts, memorize coding rules, and practice audit scenarios. Use flashcards for compliance statutes and quality‑improvement metrics. Schedule timed mock exams to build stamina for the 340‑minute, 100‑question linear format. Join a study group to discuss tricky cases and reinforce learning.

Domain Study Guidance

E/M Documentation and Audit: Study Guidance

Focuses on evaluating Evaluation and Management notes for completeness, accuracy, and appropriate level of service. Candidates must assess documentation against coding criteria, identify gaps, and apply correct E/M codes consistently.

  • E/M coding guidelines
  • Documentation completeness checklist
  • Audit case studies

Surgical Documentation and Audit: Study Guidance

Examines operative reports, postoperative notes, and related records to ensure surgical services are documented and coded correctly. Review of procedure descriptions, implant tracking, and compliance with surgical coding standards is required.

  • Surgical coding tables
  • Operative report audit checklist
  • Implant documentation rules

Diagnostic Services Audit: Study Guidance

Targets laboratory, radiology, and other diagnostic claims. Candidates must verify that test orders match clinical indications, assess proper use of CPT codes, and detect overutilization or unbundling errors within the billing cycle.

  • Diagnostic CPT coding
  • Clinical indication verification
  • Utilization review methods

Compliance and Regulatory: Study Guidance

Focuses on federal fraud and abuse statutes, OIG guidance, and payer regulations. Candidates must recognize kickback risks, self‑referral rules, and exclusion criteria, and apply safe‑harbor provisions during audits to ensure compliance.

  • Federal fraud statutes
  • Safe harbor criteria
  • Exclusion database checks

Quality Improvement: Study Guidance

Addresses methods for measuring and enhancing care quality through audit data. Candidates learn to develop performance metrics, conduct root‑cause analysis, and implement corrective action plans that align with regulatory expectations.

  • Performance metric design
  • Root cause analysis
  • Corrective action planning

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive early, bring a valid photo ID, and have your confirmation email ready. The linear exam lasts 340 minutes with 100 multiple‑choice questions; pace yourself, flag uncertain items, and review answers before submitting.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The CPMA exam contains 100 multiple‑choice questions presented in a linear format. All questions must be answered in the allotted 340‑minute window, and there is no penalty for guessing.

What is the passing score?

A score of 70 percent or higher is required to pass the CPMA certification. The exam is scored on a scale that reflects mastery of the five domain areas.

How long is the exam?

The CPMA test is a 340‑minute, linear exam lasting just under six hours. It is administered in one continuous session, allowing you to manage time across all 100 questions.

What are the renewal requirements for CPMA?

CPMA certification is valid for two years. Renewal requires meeting AAPC’s continuing education criteria, which includes completing the required number of credits and paying the renewal fee before expiration.
