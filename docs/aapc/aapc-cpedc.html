What This Exam Validates

The Certified Pediatric Emergency Department Coder (CPEDC) is offered by AAPC. It tests knowledge of well‑child preventive visits, pediatric procedural coding, neonatal and newborn coding, and diagnosis coding specific to children. The linear, multiple‑choice exam includes 100 questions to be completed in 340 minutes. A passing score of 70% earns a two‑year certification and recognized credential.

Who Should Take This Exam

Medical coders, billing specialists, or health information technicians who regularly code pediatric emergency department services, including well‑child visits, procedures, and newborn encounters, should consider CPEDC. Prior experience with ICD‑10‑CM and pediatric coding guidelines is recommended.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Well‑child preventive visit coding
  • Pediatric procedure coding
  • Neonatal and newborn coding
  • Pediatric diagnosis coding

How to Prepare

Review the AAPC CPEDC study guide and complete the practice exams on the AAPC training portal. Focus on ICD‑10‑CM conventions for pediatric diagnoses and the CPT codes used in emergency procedures. Use the CDC ICD‑10‑CM browser to verify current newborn codes. Schedule regular study sessions, take timed quizzes to build stamina for the 340‑minute linear format, and join a pediatric coding study group for peer feedback.

Domain Study Guidance

Well Child Visits and Preventive Care: Study Guidance

Covers coding of routine pediatric wellness examinations, immunizations, growth assessments, and preventive counseling using appropriate ICD‑10‑CM and CPT codes. Emphasizes accurate documentation of age‑specific services and ensures compliance with payer policies.

  • Well‑child visit codes
  • Immunization coding
  • Growth chart documentation
  • Preventive counseling codes

Pediatric Procedures: Study Guidance

Focuses on coding of common emergency department procedures performed on children, such as laceration repair, fracture reduction, airway management, and procedural sedation. Requires selection of correct CPT/HCPCS codes and appropriate modifiers.

  • Laceration repair coding
  • Fracture reduction coding
  • Airway management coding
  • Procedural sedation coding

Neonatal and Newborn Coding: Study Guidance

Addresses coding for newborn assessments, delivery types, complications, and early interventions. Includes selection of ICD‑10‑CM codes for birth weight, gestational age, and neonatal conditions, as well as CPT codes for newborn screening and procedures.

  • Birth weight and gestational age codes
  • Neonatal condition coding
  • Newborn screening CPT codes
  • Delivery type coding

Diagnosis Coding for Pediatrics: Study Guidance

Focuses on accurate assignment of ICD‑10‑CM diagnosis codes for pediatric illnesses, injuries, and chronic conditions encountered in the emergency setting. Emphasizes proper use of laterality, severity, and external cause codes for children.

  • Pediatric illness coding
  • Injury coding for children
  • Chronic condition coding
  • Laterality and severity modifiers

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive early, bring a valid photo ID and your AAPC membership card if applicable. Have a calculator and scratch paper ready. Manage time by pacing roughly three minutes per question, and flag any items to review before submitting.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The CPEDC exam consists of 100 multiple‑choice questions presented in a linear format. All questions must be answered within the allotted 340‑minute testing window, and the total score determines whether you meet the 70 % passing threshold.

What is the passing score?

A candidate must achieve at least 70 % correct answers to pass the Certified Pediatric Emergency Department Coder exam. Scores are calculated from the 100‑question total, so a minimum of 70 correct responses is required.

How long is the exam?

The CPEDC exam is a 340‑minute, linear test lasting just over five and a half hours. You will answer 100 multiple‑choice items within this time, after which the exam is automatically submitted.

Is the exam available to non‑members?

Non‑members can register for the CPEDC exam at the standard price of $399, while AAPC members receive a reduced rate of $299. Registration is completed through the AAPC website, and the exam remains current and active.
