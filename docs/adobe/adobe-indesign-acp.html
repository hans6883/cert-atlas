What This Exam Validates

Adobe’s 9A0-413 exam validates the Adobe Certified Professional – InDesign credential. Candidates answer 40 multiple‑choice and performance‑based tasks within a 50‑minute, linear computer‑based test. The exam measures ability to set project requirements, navigate InDesign’s interface, apply typography and graphic techniques, and prepare files for print or digital publishing. A score of 66 % or higher is required to pass.

Who Should Take This Exam

Graphic designers, layout artists, and publishing professionals with at least a year of hands‑on InDesign experience should take this exam to demonstrate proficiency in document creation, typography, image integration, and export workflows.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Define project scope
  • Navigate InDesign workspace
  • Apply advanced typography
  • Manage images and graphics
  • Prepare print and digital outputs

How to Prepare

Review Adobe’s official InDesign user guide and focus on each domain’s weight. Practice creating documents, applying paragraph and character styles, and exporting PDFs within the 50‑minute time limit using sample projects. Use Certiport’s practice tests to become familiar with multiple‑choice format and performance tasks. Track progress against the 13 % to 30 % domain weights, and schedule the exam when comfortable with all areas.

Domain Study Guidance

Setting Project Requirements: Study Guidance

This domain covers how to gather and define client needs, establish document specifications, set page size, margins, bleed, and create project plans before building an InDesign file, including workflow considerations and file organization.

  • Define client objectives
  • Set page size and margins
  • Create bleed and slug settings
  • Plan file organization

Understanding Adobe InDesign: Study Guidance

Focuses on the InDesign interface, workspace customization, tool panels, preferences, and core functions such as creating documents, using master pages, and managing templates. Mastery enables efficient navigation and setup for any layout project.

  • Navigate workspace
  • Customize toolbars
  • Use master pages
  • Manage templates

Working with Text and Typography: Study Guidance

Covers text frame creation, threading, applying paragraph and character styles, kerning, tracking, leading, glyphs, ligatures, and advanced typographic features like drop caps, numbering, and text variables. Emphasis on precise typographic control for print and digital output.

  • Create and thread text frames
  • Apply paragraph and character styles
  • Adjust kerning and tracking
  • Use drop caps and numbering

Working with Images and Graphics: Study Guidance

Focuses on placing, linking, and embedding images, using the Links panel, adjusting resolution, applying effects, working with transparency, color swatches, gradients, and managing vector graphics. Includes best practices for image preparation and color management.

  • Place and link images
  • Adjust resolution and color
  • Apply transparency and effects
  • Manage vector graphics

Printing, Exporting, and Publishing: Study Guidance

Encompasses preparing documents for print, setting printer's marks, bleeds, and color separations, exporting to PDF, EPUB, HTML, and JPEG, and using preflight tools. Also covers digital publishing options and workflow integration with Creative Cloud.

  • Set printer's marks and bleeds
  • Export to PDF and EPUB
  • Run preflight checks
  • Use digital publishing workflows

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive early, bring a valid ID, and ensure a stable internet connection for the online proctor. Review the exam timer, answer all 40 questions, and double‑check any performance tasks before submitting. Keep calm and manage time wisely.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The 9A0-413 exam consists of 40 questions, combining multiple‑choice items and performance‑based tasks that assess practical InDesign skills. All questions must be answered within the allotted time.

What is the passing score?

A candidate must achieve at least 66 % correct responses to pass the Adobe Certified Professional – InDesign exam. Scores are calculated from the weighted domains and reported immediately after completion.

How long is the exam?

The exam is a linear, computer‑based test lasting 50 minutes. During this time you will answer all 40 items, and the timer is visible on screen.

How much does the exam cost?

The standard price for the 9A0-413 exam is $180 when booked through Certiport or Pearson VUE, as noted in Adobe’s official exam guide. Additional fees may apply for rescheduling or retakes, so verify current pricing on the testing provider’s site.
