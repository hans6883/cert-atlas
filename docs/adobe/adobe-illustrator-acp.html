What This Exam Validates

The Adobe Certified Professional in Illustrator certification exam comprehensively validates your professional knowledge and practical competencies in using Adobe Illustrator, the premier vector graphics application. Certified directly by Adobe, this complete test evaluates candidates across four distinct domains using exactly 40 questions presented in a linear, computer-based testing format designed to measure your capabilities effectively.

Who Should Take This Exam

Graphic designers, digital artists, and students seeking to validate their vector illustration skills. Candidates should have practical experience using Adobe Illustrator to create logos, icons, drawings, and typography for print, web, video, and mobile outputs.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Setting Project Requirements
  • Understanding Adobe Illustrator
  • Organizing Documents
  • Working with Illustrations

How to Prepare

Carefully review all official exam objectives and take extensive CertPREP practice tests powered by GMetrix to fully experience the official computer-based testing format. Gain ample hands-on practice with core vector tools, artboards, and layers to adequately prepare for multiple-choice questions and performance-based tasks during your studies.

Domain Study Guidance

Setting Project Requirements: Study Guidance

This domain specifically covers project purpose, target audience needs, intellectual property regulations, copyright terms, digital graphics terminology, color representation, and general design principles for visual communication projects without missing any core concepts.

  • Target audience and demographics
  • Copyright and licensing rules
  • Color modes such as CMYK and RGB

Understanding Adobe Illustrator: Study Guidance

This section focuses heavily on document creation, modification settings for intended outputs, artboard management, asset placement, linking procedures, saving documents, and properly exporting both vector and raster graphics for different publication mediums.

  • Document settings and presets
  • Artboard scaling and arrangement
  • Links panel and asset exporting

Organizing Documents: Study Guidance

Candidates explore workspace customization, preference configurations, basic design aids like rulers and guides, color swatches, gradients, brushes, symbols, patterns, and complex layer hierarchy management techniques to maintain organized project files.

  • Workspace customization
  • Swatches, brushes, and symbols
  • Layer options and sublayers

Working with Illustrations: Study Guidance

Centers on creating and modifying objects, paths, and text using core tools like the Pen tool, shape tools, image trace, type tools, and adjustments to appearance and object position.

  • Pen tool and path refinement
  • Type tools and paragraph formatting
  • Clipping masks and object alignment

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive at your test center or launch your online proctored session ready for a linear exam format. Manage your time across the 40 questions within the 50-minute time limit.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The certification exam consists of 40 questions presented in a linear, computer-based format. Question types include traditional multiple choice questions and performance-based tasks specific to Adobe Certified Professional exams.

What is the passing score?

The passing score for the Adobe Certified Professional — Illustrator certification exam is 66. Candidates must successfully meet or exceed this required score to earn their official credential.

How long is the exam?

The certification exam has a strict total duration of 50 minutes. Candidates should carefully pace themselves throughout the testing session to complete all questions within this designated time frame.

How much does the exam cost?

The standard price for taking the certification exam is $180. Official vouchers or direct test registrations can be purchased conveniently through Certiport or Pearson VUE platforms.
