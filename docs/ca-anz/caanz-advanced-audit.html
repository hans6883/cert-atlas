What This Exam Validates

The Advanced Audit Capstone Module examination evaluates professional competence across five specific domains. Certified by CA ANZ, this thorough examination requires candidates to demonstrate extensive expertise in integrated case analysis, advanced financial reporting judgements, audit and assurance, tax planning, and professional ethics over a structured testing period lasting precisely 180 minutes.

Who Should Take This Exam

Candidates pursuing membership pathways with CA ANZ who have completed prerequisite modules and need to demonstrate advanced competence in auditing, financial reporting, and professional ethics should take this exam.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Integrated case analysis
  • Advanced financial reporting judgements
  • Advanced audit and assurance
  • Tax planning and complex structures
  • Professional ethics and judgement

How to Prepare

Candidates should carefully review all relevant auditing standards, advanced financial reporting frameworks, and complex tax regulations applicable to Australia and New Zealand. Practice analyzing multifaceted integrated case studies and applying professional skepticism and strong ethical principles to realistic business scenarios before taking the formal assessment.

Domain Study Guidance

Integrated Case Analysis: Study Guidance

Covers integrated case analysis across complex business scenarios, requiring candidates to synthesize financial, operational, and strategic information to evaluate reporting and auditing implications completely across multiple operational areas during the evaluation process.

  • Case synthesis
  • Information evaluation
  • Strategic analysis

Advanced Financial Reporting Judgements: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on advanced financial reporting judgements, addressing complex accounting treatments, standard interpretations, and financial statement disclosures under applicable frameworks for professional accountants actively working in practice today.

  • Accounting treatments
  • Standard interpretations
  • Financial disclosures

Advanced Audit and Assurance: Study Guidance

Examines advanced audit and assurance procedures, risk assessment methodologies, evidence gathering, and compliance with auditing standards during financial statement audits performed by qualified professionals operating within modern corporate structures.

  • Audit procedures
  • Risk assessment
  • Evidence gathering

Tax Planning and Complex Structures (AU/NZ): Study Guidance

Addresses tax planning strategies and complex corporate structures within the Australian and New Zealand regulatory environments, ensuring proper compliance and strategic advisory capabilities for practitioners navigating intricate financial legislation.

  • Tax planning
  • Complex structures
  • Regulatory compliance

Professional Ethics and Judgement: Study Guidance

Tests professional ethics and judgement, ensuring candidates apply ethical codes, independence rules, and professional skepticism to challenging professional situations encountered frequently in modern auditing environments and complex business operations.

  • Ethical codes
  • Professional skepticism
  • Independence rules

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive at the testing location or log into the remote platform well before the 180 minute duration begins. Ensure all permitted reference materials and calculation tools comply with CA ANZ guidelines.

Frequently asked questions

How much does the exam cost?

The exam price is $700.0 USD, though fees vary approximately between AUD $1,050 and $1,200 depending on specific enrollment conditions. Candidates should check the CA ANZ website for current fee details.

What is the passing score?

The passing score for the examination is set at 50 points. Candidates must achieve this minimum threshold to successfully pass the capstone module evaluation and progress further.

How long is the exam?

The examination has a total duration of 180 minutes. Candidates are advised to manage their time effectively across all sections of the test to ensure complete responses.

Is a renewal required for this certification?

No, renewal is not required for this certification once successfully completed. Candidates can maintain their credential status without undergoing periodic recertification exams for this specific module.
