Exam Domains

1 Strategy 40%
  • Identify the responsibilities of Center of Excellence and Innovation (CoEI)
  • Identify the ServiceNow recommended sequence of implementation of the Center of Excellence and Innovation (CoEI) including key outcomes for each stage
  • Identify the services and options available in the ServiceNow Support Portal
  • Given a case study: For a department's identified business objectives, identify the ServiceNow Platform capabilities that can assist in accomplishing the objectives
  • For a department's identified business objectives, identify the KPIs that measure the value/outcomes aligned to the objectives
  • Identify how to respond to pitfalls and successes
  • Identify the gaps in Center of Excellence and Innovation (CoEI) components
  • Given a scenario: Including a request from a department, identify the steps to design a solution to achieve the business objectives
  • About a product line, identify the data and information that is needed and how it is used
  • About ServiceNow family or store release, identify how to determine to take the update or skip it
  • About an end of year freeze, identify what should be considered when determining release dates
  • About an issue/defect which has emerged on the platform, identify actions to be taken to resolve the issue as quickly as possible
  • Identify the approach for capturing ideas/requirements which may lead to implementation by project team, operations team, and/or platform team
  • About new features that have been added to the platform, identify actions to be taken to avoid future issues
  • Identify the correct SCRUM events/ceremonies
  • About managing your ServiceNow team's Agile development processes, identify the platform capabilities to employ
  • Identify the good and bad user experience, customer experience, and employee experience
  • Identify appropriate oversight and methods of testing.
  • Identify the dependencies on data for particular processes
  • Identify the leading security practices (i.e., regulatory/compliance control; implementation; data and encryption)
  • What would the best practice approach and scope be to ensure the success of a citizen development program
2 People 10%
  • Given a case study: Identify the ServiceNow Platform capabilities that can assist in accomplishing the objectives
  • Identify the KPIs that measure the value/outcomes aligned to the objectives
  • Given a scenario: Identify the most likely candidate for a sponsor and the methods to use to engage with the sponsor
  • Identify best practices for dealing with that type of stakeholder.
  • Identify the scalable team structure that will serve the organization in supporting the ServiceNow implementation
3 Process 10%
  • Given a scenario: Identify the Agile role that most accurately fits the responsibilities
  • Identify the code promotion process that follows ServiceNow recommended best practice for code promotion
4 Technology 20%
  • Given a scenario: Identify the implication on platform maintenance requirements
  • Identify the implications an implementation can have on platform maintenance
  • Identify the sequence of activities to successfully perform an upgrade
  • Identify the contents and schedule of patches
  • Identify how to support the information being gathered
  • Identify the suitable implementation model (domain separation, single production instance, multiple production instance, on-premises implementation) for the ServiceNow platform
5 Data 10%
  • Given a scenario: Identify the ServiceNow capabilities that allow the requirement to be met while ensuring optimum system performance.
  • Identify how to govern the data for CMDB or CSDM
6 ServiceNow Governance 10%
  • Given a scenario: Identify how to create ServiceNow governance around a licensing review.
  • Identify how to ensure technical governance using coding best practices.
