What This Exam Validates

The ServiceNow Robotic Process Automation RPA certification assessment evaluates your knowledge of designing, configuring, and deploying automated bots across the platform. This linear format certification exam consists of exactly 35 total questions, spanning four distinct domains covering foundational principles, bot development lifecycles, execution mechanics, platform integration, and exception handling mechanisms.

Who Should Take This Exam

ServiceNow developers and automation professionals configuring enterprise workflows will benefit from taking this exam. Experience with building bots and integrating automation components across platform systems is required.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • RPA Fundamentals and Architecture
  • Bot Development Lifecycle
  • Attended and Unattended Bots
  • Bot Run Executions
  • Platform Integration and Exception Handling

How to Prepare

Carefully review official ServiceNow documentation, training courses, and learning paths before testing. Focus your study sessions extensively on bot development lifecycles, execution configuration, and error management strategies. Practice setting up robotic process automation components carefully and examine how they successfully integrate with existing platform elements and enterprise infrastructure.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of exactly 35 linear questions presented as multiple choice and multiple select items designed to test your knowledge across four distinct domains.

What is the passing score?

The passing score uses a scaled score model where the exact cut score is not publicly disclosed by ServiceNow and is not always 70% for candidates taking this certification test.

What does the exam cost?

The exam pricing is included with ServiceNow University access. Check with ServiceNow directly for current pricing and enrollment details before registering for your test to ensure you have all updated information regarding fees.

Is a renewal required?

No renewal is required for this certification. If you need further details on long-term paths or updates, check with ServiceNow for the current certification path.
