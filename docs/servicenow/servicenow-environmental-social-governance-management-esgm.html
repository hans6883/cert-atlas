What This Exam Validates

The ServiceNow Environmental, Social, Governance Management (ESGM) certification validates your ability to manage organizational environmental, social, and governance commitments, track metrics, and report progress using the ServiceNow platform. Certified by ServiceNow, this linear 29-question exam covers functional capabilities such as program set-up, entity frameworks, data collection, greenhouse gas accounting, Sustainable IT, and regulatory reporting frameworks across 7 structured domains.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification exam is ideal for practitioners, administrators, and implementation specialists who are directly responsible for configuring ESG programs, managing metric definitions, setting up entity frameworks, and generating disclosures within ServiceNow.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • ESG capabilities in ServiceNow
  • Material topics and goals
  • Entity frameworks
  • GRC metrics data collection
  • Greenhouse gas accounting
  • Sustainable IT dashboards
  • ESG regulatory reporting

How to Prepare

Review official ServiceNow documentation, implementation guides, and best practice collections carefully. Focus your study sessions on setting up material topics, configuring GRC metrics, mastering greenhouse gas accounting procedures, and successfully operating disclosure workflows within the ServiceNow ESG Workspace to ensure you are fully ready for test day.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The certification exam consists of exactly 29 questions delivered in a linear format utilizing both multiple choice and multiple select question types to test your practical platform knowledge.

What is the passing score?

The passing score utilizes a scaled score model where the exact cut score is not publicly disclosed by ServiceNow and is not always set to a flat percentage like 70%.

How much does the exam cost?

The certification exam is included with your ServiceNow University access; you should check directly with ServiceNow for any current pricing details, policy changes, or account update requirements.

What certification path applies after this exam?

This certification is current and active for platform administrators. If you need details on long-term credential paths or future requirements, check with ServiceNow for the official certification path.
