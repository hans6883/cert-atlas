Historical CTA Scope

The ServiceNow Certified Technical Architect (CTA) exam, certified by ServiceNow, covered architecture, governance, and data strategies. This exam is officially retired as of 2026-05-03. The ServiceNow Certified Technical Architect (CTA) is the direct successor to this credential. Candidates seeking certification should focus on the requirements and objectives established for the successor CTA, as the original exam is no longer available for registration or completion by any candidates. The exam consisted of 47 questions.

Who CTA Was For

This certification is intended for technical architects responsible for platform governance, data strategy, and integration design. Candidates should possess extensive experience in managing ServiceNow instances and implementing complex architectural solutions across the platform.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • CMDB and CSDM modeling
  • Technical governance frameworks
  • Leading testing practices
  • Go-live preparation strategies
  • Security architecture design
  • Platform data and integration management

Frequently asked questions

How many questions were on the exam?

The retired exam consisted of 47 questions. These questions were presented in a linear format, requiring candidates to demonstrate their knowledge across nine distinct domains during the testing session.

What was the passing score?

The passing score for the exam was based on a scaled score. The specific cut score was not publicly disclosed and was not always 70% for all candidates.

How long was the exam?

The exam was a 90-minute exam. Candidates were expected to complete the 47 questions within this duration, managing their time effectively across the various technical domains covered in the assessment.

How long is the certification valid for?

The certification validity was 2 years. After this period, renewal was required to maintain the credential status, ensuring that architects remained current with the latest platform standards and best practices.
