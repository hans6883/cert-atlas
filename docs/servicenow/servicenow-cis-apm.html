Historical CIS-APM Scope

The ServiceNow Certified Implementation Specialist – Application Portfolio Management exam covered topics including application inventory, rationalization, and portfolio management. This certification was provided by ServiceNow to validate professional expertise. The exam officially retired on 2025-06-30. There is no successor exam for this credential. Candidates interested in current ServiceNow certifications should consult the official ServiceNow learning portal to identify relevant paths, as this specific exam is no longer available for registration, scheduling, or completion by any individuals.

Who CIS-APM Was For

This exam was intended for professionals implementing Application Portfolio Management within the ServiceNow platform. It required knowledge of application inventory, rationalization processes, and the CSDM maturity model. Since the exam is retired, individuals should focus on current platform implementation credentials instead.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Managing application inventory
  • Executing rationalization processes
  • Applying CSDM maturity models
  • Configuring technology portfolio management
  • Utilizing application scoring profiles
  • Performing data certification

How to Reuse Your Preparation

Because this exam is retired, do not prepare for it. Focus your efforts on current ServiceNow implementation paths. Frequently asked questions

How many questions were on the exam?

The exam consisted of 30 questions in total. These questions were designed to test the candidate's knowledge of the ServiceNow Application Portfolio Management suite before the program was officially retired by the vendor for all global participants.

What was the passing score?

The exam used a scaled score. The cut score was not publicly disclosed and was not always 70%. Candidates were evaluated based on their performance across all domains during the testing session to determine if they met the required standards.

How long was the exam?

The exam had a duration of 60 minutes. Candidates were expected to complete all sections within this timeframe, as the exam was administered under standard testing conditions before its retirement date to ensure consistency for every single test taker.

Is there a replacement for this exam?

ServiceNow has retired this exam and has not named a successor. The official status of this credential is None, and there is no direct replacement path currently offered by the organization for those seeking to validate these specific skills.
