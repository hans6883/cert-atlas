What This Exam Validates

The Foundation Level — Agile Tester (CTFL-AT) certification, provided by the ISTQB, covers testing principles and practices within Agile software development environments. It addresses the role of the tester in Agile teams, including test-driven development, user story creation, and the use of various testing tools. The exam evaluates knowledge across three specific domains, with the largest portion focusing on fundamental Agile testing principles, practices, and processes. This certification ensures that professionals understand the unique challenges and methodologies required to maintain high quality standards while working in fast-paced, iterative development cycles common in modern software engineering.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is for software testers and team members working in Agile environments. It is suitable for those who need to understand how to apply testing techniques, collaborate within a whole-team approach, and contribute to quality in iterative development projects.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Agile software development concepts
  • Whole-team testing approaches
  • Collaborative user story creation
  • Continuous integration practices
  • Agile testing methods and techniques
  • Quality risk assessment in Agile

How to Prepare Before Retirement

Review the official ISTQB study documentation to understand the learning objectives for each domain. Focus on the 55% weight assigned to fundamental Agile testing principles. Practice identifying the differences between traditional and Agile testing, and ensure you are familiar with the terminology related to Agile methods, techniques, and tools. Use the provided syllabus to guide your study, as all content is examinable. Candidates should allocate sufficient time to master the core concepts and apply them to real-world scenarios.

Domain Study Guidance

Agile Software Development: Study Guidance

This domain covers the fundamentals of Agile software development, including the Agile Manifesto and the whole-team approach. It accounts for 15% of the exam and emphasizes the importance of early and frequent feedback mechanisms for project success.

  • Agile Manifesto values and principles
  • Benefits of the whole-team approach

Fundamental Agile Testing Principles, Practices, and Processes: Study Guidance

Focusing on fundamental Agile testing principles, practices, and processes, this domain represents 55% of the exam. It details the role of the tester and how testing activities differ from traditional approaches to ensure product quality throughout the lifecycle.

  • Testing in Agile versus traditional projects
  • Communicating test status and product quality
  • Agile tester skills and team roles

Agile Testing Methods, Techniques, and Tools: Study Guidance

This domain covers Agile testing methods, techniques, and tools, making up 30% of the exam. It explores specific testing strategies and the application of various tools in Agile projects to improve efficiency and overall software reliability.

  • Test-driven and behavior-driven development
  • Quality risk assessment and effort estimation
  • Exploratory testing in Agile

Exam-Day Guidance

The exam is a 60-minute linear test consisting of 40 multiple-choice questions. You must achieve a passing score of 65% to earn the certification. Ensure you manage your time effectively across the three domains to address all questions within the allotted period.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of 40 multiple-choice questions in total. Candidates should be prepared to answer these questions within the time limit provided, ensuring they cover all three domains of the syllabus to achieve the required passing score for certification.

What is the passing score?

A passing score of 65% is required to pass the exam. This percentage reflects the minimum level of knowledge required to demonstrate proficiency in Agile testing principles, practices, and techniques as defined by the official ISTQB certification standards for this specific level.

How long is the exam?

The exam has a duration of 60 minutes. During this time, candidates must complete all 40 questions. It is recommended that test-takers manage their time carefully to ensure they have enough time to review their answers before the final submission of the test.

What is the cost of the exam?

The approximate price is $250, though fees vary by national member board. Candidates should check with their local or regional ISTQB member board to confirm the exact pricing and registration procedures applicable to their specific location before scheduling their exam appointment.
