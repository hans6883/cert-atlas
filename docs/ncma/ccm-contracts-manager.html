What This Exam Validates

The NCMA Certified Contracts Manager Exam evaluates professional knowledge and operational competencies across leadership, management, guiding principles, pre-award, award, post-award, and learning. Candidates are tested on their ability to navigate complex business relationships, regulatory requirements, and various contract life cycle phases. This official evaluation validates professional capabilities for practitioners operating within the broader discipline of contract management according to established industry standards and guidelines.

Who Should Take This Exam

Contract managers, buyers, sellers, and procurement professionals with occupational experience seeking to validate their contract management proficiency and professional skills across the entire contract life cycle.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Leadership and team dynamics
  • Management and situational assessment
  • Guiding principles and regulatory compliance
  • Pre-award solicitation development
  • Offer preparation and sales planning
  • Contract formation and pricing analysis
  • Post-award performance and closure

How to Prepare

Carefully review the Contract Management Standard and the Contract Management Body of Knowledge to prepare for the assessment. Focus your study sessions on the seven domains, fundamental contract principles, professional standards of conduct, and specific job tasks across pre-award, award, and post-award life cycle phases to ensure complete readiness before taking the exam.

Domain Study Guidance

Leadership: Study Guidance

This domain covers leadership proficiency, personal character, collaboration strategies, emotional intelligence, and long-term vision required for successful contract managers working within modern organizational environments and complex professional settings.

  • Skills and roles
  • Contract principles
  • Standards of conduct
  • Regulatory compliance

Management: Study Guidance

This domain addresses targeted management skills, commercial business acumen, professional judgment, situational assessment methodologies, and team dynamics necessary for effectively handling diverse operational challenges throughout every stage of the contracting process.

  • Situational assessment
  • Team dynamics
  • Communication and documentation

Guiding Principles: Study Guidance

This domain focuses intensely on the fundamental principles of contracting, including offer, acceptance, legal consideration, and the mutual intent to create a binding relationship recognized under applicable laws and standard commercial practices.

  • General contracting concepts
  • Terms and conditions

Pre-Award: Study Guidance

This domain involves buyer and seller processes related to developing formal solicitations and preparing competitive offers during the initial pre-award phase of the contract lifecycle in order to establish clear commercial arrangements.

  • Develop solicitation
  • Develop offer
  • Plan sales
  • Request offers

Award: Study Guidance

This domain encompasses contract formation processes including price or cost analysis, strategic negotiation planning, systematic source selection, and effective management of disagreements or disputes between contracting parties during the award phase.

  • Analyze price or cost
  • Plan negotiations
  • Select source
  • Manage disagreements

Post-Award: Study Guidance

This domain covers active contract performance execution, business relationship management, quality assurance, change management protocols, and formal contract closeout procedures to ensure full compliance and successful project delivery during post-award.

  • Perform contract
  • Close contract

Learn: Study Guidance

This specific domain involves continuous lifelong learning activities designed to advance individual professional competence and enhance overall organizational capability within the dynamic and ever-evolving field of contract management practice.

  • Continuous learning
  • Process improvement

Exam-Day Guidance

Manage your time effectively across 180 total questions during the 240-minute duration. Read each question carefully and apply your knowledge of contract management principles and regulatory compliance.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The official certification examination is comprised of 180 total questions spanning all of the subject domains defined within the Contract Management Standard and related guides.

What is the passing score?

To achieve a successful result and earn certification, candidates must meet or exceed the official passing score requirement established at exactly 72.2% on the evaluation.

How long is the exam?

Candidates are granted a total duration of 240 minutes to complete all questions, manage their time effectively, and review their answers before submitting the examination.

What organization certifies this exam?

This professional certification examination is officially administered, governed, and certified by the National Contract Management Association, which is widely recognized in the industry as NCMA.
