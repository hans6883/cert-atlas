What This Exam Validates

The Arizona Driver's License Knowledge Test evaluates your understanding of essential motor vehicle laws, road regulations, and safety practices required for new drivers. Administered by DMV / State Driver Licensing, this closed-book assessment contains exactly 30 multiple-choice questions to determine whether applicants are qualified to safely operate vehicles on state roadways during daily travel at a $10 fee.

Who Should Take This Exam

New drivers and individuals relocating to Arizona who need to obtain an instruction permit or driver license. This test is required for applicants seeking standard operator credentials through DMV / State Driver Licensing offices.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Traffic Laws and Regulations
  • Road Signs and Signals
  • Safe Driving Practices
  • Right-of-Way Rules
  • Speed Limits and Distances

How to Prepare

Review the official driver license manual and customer service guide in detail before your exam date. Study the specific sections covering traffic laws, road signs, and driving practices. Practice answering questions about road regulations and utilize available sample tests to prepare for the computer-based or paper test.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The Arizona Driver's License Knowledge Test consists of 30 multiple-choice questions administered at a DMV office either on a computer or on paper in a closed-book format.

What is the passing score?

The passing score for the examination is 80 percentage, meaning you must answer enough questions correctly to meet this threshold on the computer-based or paper test.

How much does the exam cost?

The exam is associated with a $10 new driver's license fee when applying for your credential through DMV / State Driver Licensing offices during the standard registration process.

What format is the exam?

The exam format consists of multiple-choice questions administered as a closed-book test, delivered either through a computer-based system or via a paper test at a DMV office.

Exam Details

Question TypesMultiple Choice
FormatComputer-based or paper test at DMV office; closed-book
Online ProctoringNot available
ID RequirementsValid proof of identity, Social Security number, and proof of residency required. Requirements vary by state. Acceptable documents typically include a birth certificate or passport (identity), Social Security card or W-2 (SSN), and utility bill or bank statement (residency). REAL ID-compliant licenses require additional documentation.
RenewalRequired -- License renewal required every 4–8 years depending on state. Renewal typically does not require re-taking the written test unless the license has been expired for an extended period (usually 1–4 years, varies by state). Many states offer online or mail-in renewal for standard licenses.
Retake PolicyMost states require a 1-day wait between attempts. Some states allow same-day retake. Fees may apply for retakes after the first 2–3 attempts.
