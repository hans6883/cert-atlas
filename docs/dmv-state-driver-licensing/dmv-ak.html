What This Exam Validates

The Alaska Driver's License Knowledge Test is certified by the DMV / State Driver Licensing authority. It evaluates your understanding of traffic laws, road signs, safe driving practices, right-of-way rules, and speed limits to ensure the safe operation of motor vehicles on all public roads and highways across the state during your everyday travels. This examination consists of exactly 20 multiple choice questions and requires a passing score of 80 percentage to successfully earn your credentials.

Who Should Take This Exam

This exam is required for new drivers seeking an instruction permit or provisional license, out-of-state drivers establishing residency, and individuals whose driving privileges have expired for over one year.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Traffic Laws and Regulations
  • Road Signs and Signals

How to Prepare

Study the official driver manual in depth to master all required subject areas. Focus your study sessions on state traffic laws, highway sign recognition, and safe driving principles. You can take the written test online or at a DMV office after paying the required $15.00 fee.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The test consists of a total of exactly 20 multiple choice questions covering traffic laws, road signs, safe driving practices, right-of-way rules, and speed limits across the entire state examination.

What is the passing score?

You must achieve a passing score of 80 percentage on the knowledge test to successfully pass the examination, meet state standards, and earn your official driving privileges.

How long is the certification valid?

The resulting certification and driver's license are valid for a total of 5 years before a formal renewal process is required by the state licensing authority.

What is the fee for an instruction permit?

The fee for an original instruction permit is $15.00, and you should check with DMV / State Driver Licensing for the current certification path details and payment requirements.

Exam Details

Question TypesMultiple Choice
FormatComputer-based or paper test at DMV office; closed-book
Online ProctoringNot available
ID RequirementsValid proof of identity, Social Security number, and proof of residency required. Requirements vary by state. Acceptable documents typically include a birth certificate or passport (identity), Social Security card or W-2 (SSN), and utility bill or bank statement (residency). REAL ID-compliant licenses require additional documentation.
RenewalRequired -- License renewal required every 4–8 years depending on state. Renewal typically does not require re-taking the written test unless the license has been expired for an extended period (usually 1–4 years, varies by state). Many states offer online or mail-in renewal for standard licenses.
Retake PolicyMost states require a 1-day wait between attempts. Some states allow same-day retake. Fees may apply for retakes after the first 2–3 attempts.
