Historical DA0-001 Scope

CompTIA Data+ (DA0-001) is a certification administered by CompTIA covering data concepts and environments, data mining, data analysis, visualization, and data governance, quality, and controls. This exam is retired as of 2026-07-16. Candidates seeking certification must prepare for DA0-002 (CompTIA Data+ (V2)) instead, which serves as the direct replacement path for this retired credential. The retired exam is no longer available for testing.

Who DA0-001 Was For

This retired exam targeted professionals seeking to validate data management, mining, analysis, and governance competencies. Candidates should now focus their efforts on the successor credential, DA0-002 (CompTIA Data+ (V2)), to demonstrate their professional expertise.
