Retired exam

220-1202 has been retired

CompTIA's current A+ Core 2 exam is 220-1202 (exam version V15, launched March 25, 2025); the 220-1102 (V14) exam is no longer offered on comptia.org - the A+ pages and sitemap carry only V15, and the site prints no 220-1102 retirement date. Successor: 220-1202 (same exam, next version).
