Historical XK0-005 Scope

The CompTIA Linux+ certification covers multiple topics associated with Linux systems. Retired as of 2026-01-13, the evaluation was structured around four distinct domains including System Management, Security, Scripting Containers and Automation, and Troubleshooting.
