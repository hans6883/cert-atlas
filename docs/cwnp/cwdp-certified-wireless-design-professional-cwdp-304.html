What This Exam Validates

Certified by CWNP, the CWDP-304 examination evaluates professional knowledge and technical skills required to successfully manage the entire wireless local area network design life cycle from initial concept to final validation. Candidates face a total of 60 questions over a duration of 90 minutes, with a required passing score of 70% or 80% for instructors.

Who Should Take This Exam

Wireless networking professionals holding a valid CWNA certification who design, implement, and validate enterprise-grade wireless local area networks and want to earn credit toward the CWNE credential.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Define WLAN specifications
  • Design wireless architectures
  • Deploy WLAN infrastructure
  • Validate and optimize RF
  • Manage project documentation
  • Perform client performance testing

How to Prepare

Review official exam objectives and study all four core knowledge domains in complete detail to ensure deep comprehension. Ensure you fully understand business requirements, technical requirements, architecture types, deployment procedures, and validation methodologies before taking the test to achieve ultimate success on your certification journey.

Domain Study Guidance

Define Specifications for the WLAN: Study Guidance

This domain carefully covers gathering business and technical requirements, managing project documentation, identifying requirement areas, and evaluating environmental factors needed to accurately specify a wireless local area network project.

  • Collect business requirements and constraints
  • Collect and define technical requirements
  • Gather information on environmental factors

Design the WLAN: Study Guidance

Focuses on wireless local area network architectures, equipment selection, design tools and methodologies, vendor features, quality of service implementation, and security solutions for secure roaming across enterprise networks.

  • Define WLAN architectures and select appropriate architecture
  • Produce a design to meet requirements
  • Create, distribute, and communicate design documentation

Deploy the WLAN: Study Guidance

This specific section addresses implementation understanding, deployment tasks for different architectures, infrastructure configuration, and installation audits designed specifically to maintain quality assurance within wireless local area network deployments across various enterprise environments.

  • Ensure proper understanding and implementation of the design
  • Recommend or perform important deployment tasks
  • Perform an installation audit for quality assurance

Validate and Optimize the WLAN: Study Guidance

This vital area involves confirming system operational status, performing radio frequency validation and client performance testing, and recommending physical, radio frequency, and application remediation for wireless networks to ensure optimal performance.

  • Confirm the WLAN system is operational
  • Perform an RF validation survey
  • Perform client performance testing

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive at the testing center or log into the remote proctored platform on time. Manage your 90 minutes carefully to answer all 60 questions successfully.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The certification examination consists of a total of exactly 60 questions covering the four distinct knowledge domains of wireless design in detailed coverage to fully test your competencies.

What is the passing score?

Candidates must achieve a passing score of 70% on the examination, while instructors are strictly required to score 80% or higher in order to successfully pass the test.

How long is the exam?

The total examination duration is officially set at precisely 90 minutes, during which you must complete all questions presented during your scheduled testing session without any exceptions.

What certification is required before taking the exam?

To earn the CWDP certification after passing the exam, you must hold a current and valid CWNA credential. Check with CWNP for the current certification path.
