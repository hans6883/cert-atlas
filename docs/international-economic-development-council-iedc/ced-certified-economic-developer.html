What This Exam Validates

The CEcD Certified Economic Developer certification is administered by the International Economic Development Council (IEDC). This credential validates professional knowledge across the field of economic development. The assessment process includes multiple-choice and short-answer sections, written essays, and an oral interview to measure a candidate's ability to apply economic development principles to real-world community challenges and business growth strategies. Candidates must demonstrate proficiency across these 3 distinct formats to earn the designation and prove their mastery of the field.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is for economic development professionals seeking to demonstrate their expertise. Candidates typically have experience in business retention, expansion, downtown development, and brownfields redevelopment, and are looking to validate their skills through the IEDC certification process to advance their careers.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Economic development strategy
  • Business retention and expansion
  • Brownfields redevelopment
  • Downtown development
  • Cluster analysis
  • E-commerce integration

How to Prepare

Review the IEDC Economic Development Reference Guide to understand key concepts like business climate, cluster development, and downtown revitalization. Focus on applying these concepts to practical scenarios, as the exam requires both technical knowledge and the ability to articulate strategies for job creation, wealth building, and community improvement. Practice writing clear, concise responses for the essay portion and prepare for the oral interview by discussing your professional experience. Candidates should utilize the 2023 guides to ensure they are prepared for all 3 assessment formats.
