What This Exam Validates

The LEED AP Homes Accreditation exam, certified by the U.S. Green Building Council (USGBC), measures professional expertise in residential green building practices and sustainable design frameworks. Over a designated testing period of 120 minutes, participating candidates are required to answer 100 questions covering various sustainability domains including location, water conservation, energy efficiency, and indoor environmental quality.

Who Should Take This Exam

Professionals involved in residential green building projects who possess experience with sustainable design, construction, and certification processes should take this accreditation to demonstrate advanced knowledge.

Domain Study Guidance

LEED Process: Study Guidance

This domain covers the fundamental administrative processes, team coordination methodologies, and documentation requirements that are necessary to successfully navigate the complex certification workflow for residential green building projects.

  • Project registration steps
  • Documentation standards

Location and Transportation: Study Guidance

This section specifically focuses on site selections that actively promote environmentally responsible land-use patterns, successfully reduce urban sprawl, and provide convenient access to alternative sustainable transportation options for everyday residents.

  • Site selection criteria
  • Alternative transportation access

Sustainable Sites: Study Guidance

This area addresses site design principles that minimize adverse environmental effects on local ecosystems, soil stability, and natural vegetation during and after the construction process is completed by project teams.

  • Erosion and sedimentation control
  • Landscaping preservation

Water Efficiency: Study Guidance

This domain examines indoor and outdoor water use reduction strategies, strict plumbing fixture requirements, and efficient water management practices tailored specifically for residential properties and communities seeking formal certification.

  • Water-conserving fixtures
  • Prescriptive and performance pathways

Energy and Atmosphere: Study Guidance

This segment explores building energy performance standards, modeling software usage, renewable generation techniques, and prescriptive methods designed to reduce fossil fuel consumption and carbon emissions across all residential structures.

  • Energy modeling software
  • Prescriptive efficiency requirements

Materials and Resources: Study Guidance

This domain carefully reviews material selection criteria, waste reduction protocols, recycling practices, and proactive strategies to assess and mitigate long-term structural durability risks associated with sustainable residential construction projects.

  • Reclaimed and recycled materials
  • Construction waste management

Indoor Environmental Quality: Study Guidance

This area focuses on source removal, ventilation protocols, pollutant control mechanisms, and comfort measures designed to guarantee high indoor air quality for all building occupants residing within the certified residential properties.

  • Source control strategies
  • Mechanical fresh-air ventilation

Innovation: Study Guidance

This section rewards project planning that integrates advanced sustainable design techniques, exemplary performance levels, or novel practices that are not explicitly covered elsewhere in the rating system framework.

  • Exemplary performance credits
  • Innovative design strategies

Regional Priority: Study Guidance

This domain highlights specific environmental priorities identified by local USGBC chapters and the LEED International Roundtable in order to address pressing regional concerns and unique environmental conditions effectively during certification.

  • Local environmental priorities
  • Regional credit selection

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive at the testing center fully prepared to complete the 100-question test within the allotted 120 minutes. Manage your time carefully across all items to ensure every question receives a proper response.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of 100 questions covering various aspects of residential green building design, construction, and certification processes established by the U.S. Green Building Council.

What is the passing score?

The passing score for the accreditation test is 170 out of 200 possible points. Candidates must meet or exceed this score to earn the credential.

How long is the exam?

Test takers are given a total of 120 minutes to complete the 100 questions on the accreditation test administered by the U.S. Green Building Council.

What certifying body administers the exam?

The U.S. Green Building Council, commonly referred to as the USGBC, administers the accreditation program and provides the candidate handbooks, official guidelines, and reference materials needed for preparation.
