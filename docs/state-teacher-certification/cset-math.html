What This Exam Validates

The CSET Mathematics exam is a certification assessment provided by State Teacher Certification. It evaluates candidate competency across four distinct mathematical domains: Number and Quantity, Algebra, Geometry, and Statistics, Probability, and Calculus. This exam utilizes a variety of formats, including multiple-choice questions, constructed-response items, essays, and portfolio-based performance assessments, to measure the specific mathematical knowledge required for teaching licensure in the state. Candidates must demonstrate proficiency across these areas to earn their professional credential.

Who Should Take This Exam

This exam is intended for individuals seeking professional teacher certification in mathematics. Candidates should possess a strong background in mathematical theory and application, as the assessment requires demonstrating proficiency across multiple domains to meet state requirements for educators.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Number and Quantity analysis
  • Algebraic problem solving
  • Geometric reasoning
  • Statistical analysis
  • Probability models
  • Calculus operations

How to Prepare

Review the four domains of the exam using official study documentation. Practice answering both selected-response and constructed-response questions to become familiar with the format. Ensure you understand the specific requirements for the portfolio-based performance assessment. Utilize resources from State Teacher Certification to align your study plan with the exam structure and content expectations. Consistent practice with these diverse question types is important for success on the day of the assessment.

Domain Study Guidance

Number and Quantity: Study Guidance

This domain covers the fundamental properties of numbers and quantities. It focuses on the relationships between different number sets and the application of quantitative reasoning to solve various mathematical problems effectively and accurately for professional teaching standards.

  • Number systems
  • Quantitative relationships

Algebra: Study Guidance

This domain addresses algebraic expressions, equations, and functions. It requires the manipulation of variables and the application of algebraic properties to model and solve various mathematical scenarios encountered in secondary education curricula for students and teachers.

  • Algebraic expressions
  • Equation solving

Geometry: Study Guidance

This domain involves the study of geometric shapes, properties, and spatial relationships. It focuses on proofs, theorems, and the application of geometric principles to analyze two-dimensional and three-dimensional figures within a coordinate plane for classroom instruction.

  • Geometric theorems
  • Spatial analysis

Statistics, Probability, and Calculus: Study Guidance

This domain covers statistical methods, probability theory, and calculus concepts. It requires analyzing data sets, calculating probabilities, and applying derivatives and integrals to solve problems involving rates of change and accumulation in real-world contexts for educators.

  • Statistical analysis
  • Probability models
  • Calculus operations

Exam-Day Guidance

The exam is delivered via computer-based testing at approved centers, with some online proctored options available. Arrive prepared to engage with multiple question types, including essays and performance-based tasks, within the testing environment to demonstrate your full competency.

Frequently asked questions

How much does the exam cost?

The price for the CSET Mathematics exam is $99. This fee covers the entire assessment process as determined by the certifying body and must be paid in full before you can schedule your testing appointment.

What is the passing score?

The passing score is determined by a scaled score. The specific scale varies by state and the exam program requirements, so you should consult the official documentation for your specific jurisdiction to understand the threshold.

How long is the certification valid?

The certification is valid for 5 years. After this period, a renewal is required to maintain your status as a certified teacher, ensuring that your professional knowledge remains current with state standards.

What question types are included?

The exam includes selected-response multiple choice questions, constructed-response items, essays, and a portfolio-based performance assessment to evaluate your knowledge across the 4 required mathematical domains and pedagogical applications.
