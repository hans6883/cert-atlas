What This Exam Validates

CPSM Exam 1 — Foundation of Supply Management is an official certification examination administered by ISM to evaluate professional procurement and supply management practitioners across five distinct domains. These key areas include contracting, financial management, international supply operations, strategic leadership, and effective supplier relationship management methodologies required for modern industry professionals.

Who Should Take This Exam

Supply management professionals with extensive procurement experience who wish to demonstrate their advanced expertise across manufacturing, service, and public sector organizations globally today, ensuring strong career advancement potential.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Contracting and Negotiation
  • Cost and Finance
  • International Supply Management
  • Leadership and Strategy
  • Supplier Relationship Management

How to Prepare

Carefully review the official exam guide completely and successfully complete multiple practice tests to identify any weaker content areas scoring below target levels. Build a focused study plan utilizing approved learning systems and diverse reference materials to ensure complete understanding of all tested domains.

Domain Study Guidance

Contracting and Negotiation: Study Guidance

This domain focuses primarily on supplier contracting, sourcing plans, category management, the preparation and execution of complex negotiations, legal compliance requirements, and primary contract administration procedures for supply professionals.

  • Assess stakeholder needs
  • Create category management plans
  • Prepare negotiation strategies
  • Manage contracts and legal reviews

Cost and Finance: Study Guidance

This domain carefully covers cost management program strategies, detailed cost-benefit analyses, extensive spend analysis, and accurate tracking mechanisms for both cost savings and cost avoidance across the entire organization.

  • Develop cost management strategies
  • Perform cost-benefit analyses
  • Conduct spend analysis
  • Track cost savings

International Supply Management: Study Guidance

This domain addresses global supply management concepts, international sourcing requirements, cross-border logistics, and the specific regulatory challenges associated with successfully operating supply chains across international borders and regions.

  • Analyze international sources
  • Manage global supply requirements

Leadership and Strategy: Study Guidance

This domain involves strategic planning initiatives, leadership principles, departmental budgeting techniques, and aligning all internal supply management operational activities closely with overarching organizational objectives and long-term business goals.

  • Implement strategic sourcing plans
  • Administer departmental budgets

Supplier Relationship Management: Study Guidance

This domain centers entirely on developing effective supplier relationships, creating appropriate supplier qualification plans, conducting performance evaluations, holding formal business reviews, and successfully rationalizing the broader supply base.

  • Develop supplier qualification plans
  • Conduct supplier performance evaluations
  • Manage relationships with suppliers

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive at the designated test center early with required identification matching your registration name. Manage your 180 minutes carefully across all available test questions to achieve success.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The certification exam contains a total of 165 multiple-choice questions administered via a linear computer-based testing format at authorized testing centers for all registered candidates.

What is the passing score?

Candidates taking this certification test must achieve a minimum passing score of 70 to successfully demonstrate competency and pass the examination according to established guidelines.

How long is the exam?

Candidates are given a total duration of 180 minutes to complete the linear computer-based test within the allocated testing window provided by the official administration.

How much does the exam cost?

The standard exam price is $595 for active ISM members and $765 for non-members registering for the professional certification program through the official channels provided by the organization.
