Historical SCWI Scope

The Senior Certified Welding Inspector exam, certified by the American Welding Society, was officially retired on 2025-04-01. It previously covered advanced inspection methods, welding metallurgy, and advanced codes and standards. Every candidate looking to advance their professional credentials must now look toward the verified successor path consisting of WPQ1,WPQ2,WCQA,NDEC for their future certification journey.

Who SCWI Was For

Active Certified Welding Inspectors and experienced inspection professionals holding extensive welding inspection experience, who are now preparing to progress through the verified successor credentials of WPQ1,WPQ2,WCQA,NDEC to maintain their professional standing.

How to Reuse Your Preparation

Do not prepare to sit for this retired exam because the American Welding Society has officially discontinued it. Instead, build your preparation around the verified successor path WPQ1,WPQ2,WCQA,NDEC. Review technical reference materials and study guide documents to master procedure qualifications, welder performance, and NDE coordination as required by the new standards.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The examination contains a total of 150 multiple choice and open-book multiple choice questions delivered across a linear computer-based testing format for all registered candidates seeking certification.

What is the passing score?

The examination uses a pass/fail grading scale, and specific passing thresholds are not publicly disclosed by the American Welding Society under any circumstances for any of their exams.

How long is the exam?

Candidates are given a total duration of 120 minutes to complete the linear computer-based examination at approved test centers and designated testing facilities for their professional certification requirements.

What is the exam price?

The exam price is set at $1,095 for active members and $1,395 for non-members. You should verify current fees directly at the official website before registration.
