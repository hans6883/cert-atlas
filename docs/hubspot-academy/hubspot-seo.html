Historical HubSpot-SEO Scope

The HubSpot Academy SEO Certification covered SEO fundamentals, on-page and technical SEO, keyword research, link building, and reporting. This exam is retired as of 2026-06-23. HubSpot Academy now offers AEO Fundamentals; Implementing AEO With HubSpot as the official successor. Candidates should focus their efforts on this new path, as the previous certification is no longer available for completion or renewal. The certification was valid for 2 years, requiring periodic renewal to maintain active status for professionals in the field.

Who HubSpot-SEO Was For

This retired certification was intended for marketers, content creators, and website managers. Professionals seeking to validate their search engine optimization skills should now pursue the AEO Fundamentals; Implementing AEO With HubSpot certification to demonstrate current industry knowledge and expertise in modern search strategies.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions were on the exam?

The retired exam consisted of 60 questions in total. Candidates were expected to demonstrate their knowledge across all five domains to successfully complete the assessment and earn their certification credential before the official retirement date.

What was the passing score?

The passing score for the exam was 75. Achieving this score required a solid understanding of SEO fundamentals, technical optimization, keyword research, link building, and reporting analytics as outlined in the official HubSpot Academy training materials.

How long was the exam?

The exam had a duration of 75 minutes. This time limit was designed to test the candidate's ability to apply their knowledge of search engine optimization principles efficiently and accurately within the constraints of the testing environment.

Was there a cost to take the certification?

The exam was free, though it required a free HubSpot account to access the materials and the assessment. This ensured that all interested professionals could pursue the certification without any financial barriers to their professional development.
