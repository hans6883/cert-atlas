What This Exam Validates

The NBRC Clinical Simulation Examination is a computer-based assessment administered by the NBRC at PSI testing centers. It evaluates clinical decision-making through 22 clinical simulation questions. This exam is scheduled for retirement on 2027-12-31, and the NBRC identifies the Respiratory Therapy Examination as the official successor path for candidates pursuing professional certification in the field of respiratory therapy. This assessment remains a standard for those currently seeking to demonstrate their expertise in the field.

Who Should Take This Exam

This exam is intended for respiratory therapy professionals seeking certification. Candidates must meet all eligibility requirements established by the NBRC. Following the retirement of this exam, the Respiratory Therapy Examination will serve as the primary path forward for those pursuing professional certification.

How to Prepare Before Retirement

Review clinical practice guidelines and patient-ventilator assessment protocols. Familiarize yourself with the computer-based format at PSI testing centers. Because this exam retires on 2027-12-31, focus your study efforts on the content areas required for the Respiratory Therapy Examination. Utilize official NBRC resources to understand the transition to the new examination format and ensure your knowledge aligns with current standards for professional practice.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The NBRC Clinical Simulation Examination consists of exactly 22 clinical simulation questions. Candidates should be prepared to navigate these scenarios within the allotted time to demonstrate their clinical decision-making skills effectively during the testing session.

What is the passing score?

The exam utilizes a pass/fail scoring system for the Clinical Simulation Examination component. Candidates must demonstrate sufficient clinical competency to meet the requirements for certification as established by the NBRC standards for this specific assessment.

How long is the exam?

The exam is a 240-minute session administered at PSI testing centers. This duration is designed to allow candidates sufficient time to work through the clinical simulation scenarios and provide appropriate responses for each patient case presented.

What is the cost of the exam?

The price for the exam is $200. Candidates should ensure they are prepared for the examination process and understand the associated costs before scheduling their appointment at an authorized PSI testing center location.
