What This Exam Validates

The CHMP: Certified Hazardous Materials Practitioner examination is officially administered by the Institute of Hazardous Materials Management (IHMM). It evaluates candidates across five distinct domains, including the identification, safe handling, and compliant transport of hazardous materials, emergency management protocols, waste sampling techniques, site remediation procedures, and overall professional project management responsibilities.

Who Should Take This Exam

Environmental practitioners, safety professionals, and waste management personnel with hands-on experience handling hazardous substances seeking professional certification from the Institute of Hazardous Materials Management (IHMM).

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Hazardous materials transport
  • Emergency response planning
  • Waste sampling techniques
  • Site investigation procedures
  • Remediation management
  • Project administration

How to Prepare

To prepare for this certification, candidates should review all five test domains systematically according to the official blueprint. Focus your study on the handling, identification, and transport rules, emergency response procedures, waste sampling protocols, site investigation methods, and project management practices required by the Institute of Hazardous Materials Management (IHMM).

This section addresses the precise sampling and laboratory analysis of hazardous materials and waste streams, accounting for exactly fifteen point zero percent of the exam and requiring an understanding of containment protocols and testing requirements.

Site Investigation and Remediation: Study Guidance

This subject area examines site investigation and environmental remediation practices, making up fourteen point zero four percent of the evaluation by focusing on assessment methodologies, contamination detection, and cleanup execution techniques.

Program and Project Management: Study Guidance

This final domain concentrates on program and project management principles, representing sixteen point nine two percent of the test and emphasizing effective planning, resource allocation, and regulatory compliance oversight throughout project lifecycles.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of exactly 120 questions covering five distinct domains administered by the Institute of Hazardous Materials Management (IHMM) during your scheduled testing session.

What is the passing score?

The passing score uses a scaled pass/fail approach, and a fixed raw passing score is not published by the Institute of Hazardous Materials Management (IHMM) for candidates.

How long is the exam?

Candidates are given a total of 180 minutes to complete the examination administered by the Institute of Hazardous Materials Management (IHMM) during their testing appointment.

Who administers the certification?

The certification is administered by the Institute of Hazardous Materials Management (IHMM). Check with them directly for the current certification path and any updated handbook guidelines.
