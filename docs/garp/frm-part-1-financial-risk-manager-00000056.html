What This Exam Validates

The FRM Part 1 - Financial Risk Manager exam is administered by GARP. It assesses knowledge across four domains: Foundations of Risk Management, Quantitative Analysis, Financial Markets and Products, and Valuation and Risk Models. This 240-minute exam consists of 100 questions, providing a standardized assessment of the technical skills needed to manage financial risk in professional settings. Candidates must demonstrate proficiency in these four core areas to earn the designation, ensuring they possess the necessary expertise to navigate complex financial environments and mitigate risks effectively.

Who Should Take This Exam

This exam is intended for individuals pursuing the Financial Risk Manager certification. It is suitable for professionals seeking to demonstrate their technical knowledge of risk management, quantitative methods, and financial product valuation within the global financial industry.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Risk management principles
  • Quantitative analysis techniques
  • Financial market operations
  • Product valuation methods
  • Risk model application
  • Statistical analysis

Domain Study Guidance

Foundations of Risk Management: Study Guidance

This domain covers the basic principles and concepts of risk management. It provides the framework for understanding how various risks are identified, measured, and managed within modern financial institutions and professional organizations to ensure stability.

  • Risk management framework
  • Basic risk concepts

Quantitative Analysis: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on the mathematical and statistical tools required for risk analysis. It covers the quantitative methods used to measure and model various types of financial risk in practice for professional applications and analysis.

  • Statistical tools
  • Risk measurement methods

Financial Markets and Products: Study Guidance

This domain examines the structure and function of financial markets and the characteristics of various financial products. It represents a significant portion of the exam weight and requires a deep technical understanding of market operations.

  • Financial market structure
  • Product characteristics

Valuation and Risk Models: Study Guidance

This domain covers the models used for the valuation of financial instruments and the assessment of risk. It focuses on the application of these models in real-world financial risk scenarios for professional practitioners and analysts.

  • Valuation models
  • Risk assessment techniques
