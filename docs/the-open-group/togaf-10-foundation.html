What This Exam Validates

The TOGAF 10 Foundation exam evaluates knowledge of core enterprise architecture concepts, the Architecture Development Method, and architecture governance. Certified by The Open Group, this complete certification validates an understanding of standard enterprise architecture terminology and structure to help professionals succeed in their careers by demonstrating baseline comprehension of standard methodologies.

Who Should Take This Exam

Enterprise architects, IT consultants, and project managers seeking to validate their understanding of standard enterprise architecture frameworks and methodology principles within organizational settings across various industries.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Mastering core architecture concepts
  • Understanding ADM phases and cycle
  • Applying ADM techniques and guidelines
  • Implementing architecture governance structures
  • Managing architecture content and repositories

How to Prepare

Review official source documentation and study the core components of the architecture development cycle in detail. Focus on understanding repository management, phase versions, scoping dimensions, and how enterprise architecture governance operates across different organizational structures to prepare for the test successfully.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The exam consists of a total of 40 questions designed to test your knowledge of enterprise architecture concepts, methods, and governance principles established by the governing organization.

What is the passing score?

The passing score required to achieve certification is 60%. Candidates must meet or exceed this percentage threshold across the evaluation to successfully pass the examination.

How long is the exam?

You are given 60 minutes to complete all questions on the exam. Proper time management during this period is vital for addressing every item on the test.

Who certifies the exam?

The exam is certified and administered by The Open Group, which sets the official curriculum standards, framework definitions, and evaluation metrics for all participating candidates.
