What This Exam Validates

The Global Industrial Cyber Security Professional certification, offered by GIAC, validates extensive knowledge across ten detailed domains of industrial control systems, operational technology security architecture, threat modeling, endpoint hardening, risk management, and incident response to protect modern infrastructure and connected production systems effectively during everyday facility operations across various industrial sectors.

Who Should Take This Exam

Industrial control system engineers, information technology professionals, plant operators, and security analysts aiming to secure operational technology environments and manage risks across infrastructure systems successfully.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Endpoint hardening
  • ICS architecture
  • Threat modeling
  • Network protocols
  • Disaster recovery
  • Wireless security

How to Prepare

Carefully review official exam documentation, domain lists, and reference materials covering operational technology security, industrial control systems, and risk management frameworks to successfully master the ten exam domains before sitting for the test, ensuring you meet the required passing score during your testing session without any unexpected complications.

Domain Study Guidance

Hardening & Protecting Endpoints: Study Guidance

This domain focuses heavily on securing endpoint devices, executing hardening procedures, and protecting operating systems used within unique industrial control environments and operational technology networks against potential intrusions.

  • Hardening endpoints
  • Protecting devices

ICS Components & Architecture: Study Guidance

Candidates explore industrial control system components, underlying architectures, and typical operational topologies to understand how different hardware and software layers interact within the overall production framework and enterprise structure.

  • ICS components
  • System architecture

ICS Overview & Concepts: Study Guidance

This section covers the fundamental concepts, basic principles, and operational overviews of industrial control systems, establishing a strong baseline for understanding broader technology deployments across various manufacturing and utility sectors.

  • ICS concepts
  • System overview

ICS Program & Policy Development: Study Guidance

This domain addresses the systematic development of governance programs, security policies, and administrative guidelines specifically tailored for complex industrial cybersecurity and operational technology environments operating under strict regulatory demands.

  • Program development
  • Policy creation

Intelligence Gathering & Threat Modeling: Study Guidance

Learners focus on gathering actionable intelligence and performing threat modeling exercises designed to identify vulnerabilities and predict adversary movements within industrial settings while maintaining high levels of system availability.

  • Intelligence gathering
  • Threat modeling

PERA Level 0 & 1 Technology Overview and Compromise: Study Guidance

This topic examines Purdue Enterprise Reference Architecture Level 0 and Level 1 physical technologies, underlying hardware components, and associated compromise vectors that threaten fundamental industrial processes and field devices.

  • PERA Level 0 and 1
  • Technology compromise

PERA Level 2 & 3 Technology Overview and Compromise: Study Guidance

This domain covers Purdue Enterprise Reference Architecture Level 2 and Level 3 supervisory control technologies, networking components, and the specific compromise vectors affecting these intermediate layers within the automation pyramid.

  • PERA Level 2 and 3
  • Technology compromise

Protocols, Communications, & Compromises: Study Guidance

Registered candidates analyze communication protocols utilized across industrial systems, reviewing their unique messaging structures alongside respective security vulnerabilities and potential compromise methods during active facility operations in modern industrial plant environments.

  • Protocols and communications
  • Compromise analysis

Risk Based Disaster Recovery & Incident Response: Study Guidance

This area focuses on risk-based disaster recovery planning, business continuity strategies, and structured incident response procedures tailored specifically for responding to operational emergencies in industrial environments and energy plants.

  • Disaster recovery
  • Incident response

Wireless Technologies & Compromises: Study Guidance

Registered candidates examine wireless communication technologies deployed in modern industrial settings, thoroughly evaluating their architectural designs, implementation challenges, and associated security compromises impacting remote site connectivity during critical operational tasks.

  • Wireless technologies
  • Security compromises
