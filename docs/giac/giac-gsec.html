ID RequirementsGovernment-issued photo ID required. ID must match the name on the exam registration. Acceptable forms include passport, driver's license, or national ID card.
RenewalRequired -- Certifications are valid for 4 years. Renewal requires 36 CPE (Continuing Professional Education) credits earned over the 4-year period, plus a $429 renewal fee. Alternatively, candidates may retake the current exam to renew.
PrerequisitesHands-on experience in information security tasks in IT systems
Retake PolicyTwo free practice tests are included with each exam registration. Candidates have a 4-month window to take the exam after registration. Retake fees apply after the first failed attempt. Contact GIAC for specific retake waiting periods.
