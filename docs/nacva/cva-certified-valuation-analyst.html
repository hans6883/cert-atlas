What This Exam Validates

The CVA Certified Valuation Analyst credential is administered by NACVA and evaluates knowledge across nine specific domains, featuring a total of 400 questions to be completed during 300 minutes. Candidates must demonstrate proficiency in valuation approaches, professional responsibilities, fundamental analysis, and business valuation concepts required for financial analysts seeking formal recognition through this credential program.

Who Should Take This Exam

Financial analysts, accountants, and valuation professionals seeking official certification from NACVA to establish recognized expertise in business valuation engagements, methodologies, industry standards, and professional practices.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Overview principles
  • Professional standards
  • Engagement planning
  • Qualitative analysis
  • Quantitative analysis
  • Valuation approaches
  • Cost of capital
  • Discounts and premiums

How to Prepare

Review NACVA professional standards and candidate handbooks carefully while establishing a disciplined and thorough study routine over many weeks of preparation. Focus extensive preparation time on quantitative analysis, valuation approaches, and cost of capital concepts, because these specific subject sections represent large percentage weights on the official certification examination.

Domain Study Guidance

OVERVIEW: Study Guidance

This domain carefully covers overview principles and introductory framework concepts that are fundamentally required for valuation analysts entering the profession and beginning their practice within the financial industry environment successfully.

  • Overview concepts
  • Initial framework

PROFESSIONAL RESPONSIBILITIES AND STANDARDS: Study Guidance

This specific domain focuses heavily on professional responsibilities, strict ethics, and various regulatory standards governing valuation practitioners throughout all phases of their daily business engagements and reporting duties as outlined by the organization.

  • Professional responsibilities
  • Ethical standards

ENGAGEMENT ACCEPTANCE AND PLANNING: Study Guidance

This domain carefully addresses engagement acceptance procedures, detailed scoping requirements, and effective planning processes necessary for managing client projects successfully from initial initiation through final delivery of reports.

  • Engagement acceptance
  • Planning processes

QUALITATIVE ANALYSIS: Study Guidance

This domain deals with qualitative analysis techniques used to accurately assess business history, competitive industry environments, and broader economic outlook conditions impacting subject companies during all stages of valuations.

  • Qualitative analysis methods
  • Economic outlook

QUANTITATIVE ANALYSIS: Study Guidance

This domain strongly emphasizes quantitative analysis methods, complex financial statement adjustments, and earning capacity evaluations vital for reliable business valuation determinations across diverse operational environments and business structures.

  • Quantitative analysis
  • Financial adjustments

VALUATION APPROACHES: Study Guidance

This domain covers asset-based, market, and income valuation approaches and analytical methods which constitute the largest weighted portion of the entire certification examination content for all participating candidates.

  • Valuation approaches
  • Valuation methods

COST OF CAPITAL CONCEPTS AND METHODOLOGY, AND OTHER PRICING MODELS: Study Guidance

This domain explores complex cost of capital concepts, computational methodologies, and alternative pricing models required to determine appropriate discount rates for business valuation assignments and financial calculations accurately.

  • Cost of capital
  • Pricing models

DISCOUNTS, PREMIUMS, AND OTHER ADJUSTMENTS: Study Guidance

This specific domain examines discounts, premiums, and other necessary adjustments for non-controlling ownership interests and lack of marketability within private company valuation reports prepared for various financial advisory clients.

  • Discounts
  • Premiums

SPECIAL PURPOSE VALUATION: Study Guidance

This domain addresses unique special purpose valuation topics and alternative applications that fall outside standard ongoing business valuation operating engagements and standard reporting formats currently utilized by practitioners.

  • Special purpose valuation
  • Specialized applications

Exam-Day Guidance

Manage your testing time effectively across the 400 total questions during the strictly enforced 300 minutes allotted for the official certification examination session to ensure success.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the exam?

The certification examination consists of exactly 400 questions covering nine different domains of valuation knowledge, professional standards, and analytical techniques required by NACVA for candidates.

How long is the exam?

Candidates are given a total duration of 300 minutes to complete all questions within the official testing environment provided for the credential program administration by the organization.

Who administers the exam?

The credential and its associated certification examination are administered directly and exclusively by NACVA, known formally as the National Association of Certified Valuators and Analysts organization.

What is the certification status?

This certification path is active and current; please check with NACVA for any official updates regarding testing requirements and candidate handbook revisions before applying for the credential.
