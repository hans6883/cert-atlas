What This Exam Validates

The PMI Professional in Business Analysis (PMI-PBA) is a professional credential offered by PMI. It assesses a practitioner's competence in business analysis activities, including needs assessment, planning, analysis, traceability, monitoring, and evaluation. The exam ensures that professionals can effectively manage requirements and deliver solutions that meet business needs. It is developed using industry practices to reflect the role of a business analysis professional, ensuring that candidates possess the skills to drive project success through analysis and stakeholder engagement. This credential confirms your ability to handle the 200 questions presented during the testing session.

Who Should Take This Exam

This certification is for professionals who perform business analysis activities on projects. It is suitable for individuals who collaborate with stakeholders to define business problems, elicit requirements, and evaluate solution performance against established business goals and objectives to ensure organizational alignment.

Skills You Should Be Ready to Demonstrate

  • Needs assessment and problem analysis
  • Requirements management planning
  • Elicitation and decomposition of requirements
  • Traceability and lifecycle monitoring
  • Solution evaluation and gap analysis

How to Prepare

Review the PMI-PBA Examination Content Outline to understand the five domains and their respective task weights. Study the current edition of the PMBOK Guide alongside other business analysis titles. Focus on mastering the tools and techniques listed in the knowledge and skills section, such as decision-making, elicitation, and valuation methods. Practice applying these concepts to real-world project scenarios to ensure you can handle the 200 questions on the exam. Consistent study and practical application are standard methods for success.

Domain Study Guidance

Needs Assessment: Study Guidance

This domain covers understanding business problems or opportunities and evaluating inputs to develop effective solutions. It focuses on defining the business case and identifying stakeholders to ensure alignment with organizational goals and objectives.

  • Problem and opportunity analysis
  • Value proposition determination

Planning: Study Guidance

This domain involves preparing for business analysis activities. It includes establishing policies, procedures, and tools for requirements management, change control, and document versioning to create a roadmap for successful solution delivery and project execution.

  • Requirements management plan development
  • Requirements traceability strategy

Analysis: Study Guidance

This domain centers on requirements management, including elicitation, analysis, and specification. It covers how to decompose requirements, obtain stakeholder sign-off, and validate that requirements are measurable, actionable, and aligned with the project scope.

  • Requirements elicitation and analysis
  • Prioritization and baseline creation

Traceability and Monitoring: Study Guidance

This domain focuses on managing the lifecycle of requirements. It includes continuous monitoring, documenting status, and communicating changes to stakeholders to maintain the integrity of requirements and associated artifacts throughout the project duration.

  • Requirements status tracking
  • Lifecycle monitoring

Evaluation: Study Guidance

This domain covers assessing how well the delivered solution fulfills requirements and meets business needs. It involves testing, identifying gaps, and obtaining final stakeholder sign-off before deployment to ensure the solution provides value.

  • Solution validation against acceptance criteria
  • Gap and delta analysis

Exam-Day Guidance

Arrive prepared for a 240-minute session. The exam consists of 200 questions, which include various items to test your knowledge. Manage your time effectively across the five domains to ensure you address all requirements and tasks within the allotted duration.
